# 260-10  Notice to Commonwealth, State or Territory

If the third party is the Commonwealth, a State or a Territory, the Commissioner may give the notice to a person who: Authorised Version C2026C00170 registered 28/04/2026 Part 4-15 Collection and recovery of tax-related liabilities and other amounts Section 260-15

> <a id="s260-10-a"></a>
> **(a)**  is employed by the Commonwealth, or by the State or Territory (as appropriate); and

> <a id="s260-10-b"></a>
> **(b)**  has the duty of disbursing public money under a law of the Commonwealth, or of the State or Territory (as appropriate).
