# 260-15  Indemnity

An amount that the third party pays to the Commissioner under this Subdivision is taken to have been authorised by:

> <a id="s260-15-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the debtor; and

> <a id="s260-15-b"></a>
> **(b)**  any other person who is entitled to all or a part of the amount; and the third party is indemnified for the payment.
