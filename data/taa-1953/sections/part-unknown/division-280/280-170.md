# 280-170  Objecting against remission decision

You may object, in the manner set out in Part IVC, against a decision of the Commissioner not to remit an amount of *shortfall interest charge you are liable to pay on an additional amount of income tax, *petroleum resource rent tax, *excess non-concessional contributions tax, *Division 293 tax or *Division 296 tax, if the amount of the charge that was not remitted is more than 20% of the additional amount.
