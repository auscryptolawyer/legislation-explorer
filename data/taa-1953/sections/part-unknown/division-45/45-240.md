# 45-240  Commissioner may remit general interest charge

The Commissioner may, if he or she is satisfied that because special circumstances exist it would be fair and reasonable to do so, remit the whole or any part of any *general interest charge payable under subsection 45-230(2) or 45-232(2) or subsection 45-235(2) or (3).
