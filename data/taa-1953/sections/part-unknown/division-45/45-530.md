# 45-530  How Commissioner works out benchmark instalment rate

<a id="s45-530-1"></a>
**(1)**  Your benchmark instalment rate for the variation year is the percentage worked out to 2 decimal places (rounding up if the third decimal place is 5 or more) using the formula: Your *benchmark tax ´ 100 Variation year instalment income However, your benchmark instalment rate is a nil rate if either component of the formula is nil.

<a id="s45-530-2"></a>
**(2)**  For the purposes of the formula in subsection (1): variation year instalment income means so much of the trust’s assessable income for the variation year as the Commissioner determines is *instalment income for that year. 
*For definition, see section 995-1 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997.
