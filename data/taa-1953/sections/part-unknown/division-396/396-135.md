# 396-135  Application of penalty to false or misleading self-certification

For the purposes of applying Part 4-25 (Charge and penalties) in relation to a statement that is, or that relates to, a self-certification Authorised Version C2026C00170 registered 28/04/2026 Part 5-25 Record-keeping and other obligations relating to taxpayers Section 396-136 (within the meaning of the *CRS) that a Reporting Financial Institution is required to obtain when applying, under subsection 396-105(3), the due diligence procedures described in the CRS:

> <a id="s396-135-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the CRS is treated as permitting the self-certification; and

> <a id="s396-135-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the CRS is treated as being a *taxation law (but not an *Excise Act).

> **Note:** You are liable to an administrative penalty under subsection 284-75(4) if you give a self-certification that is false or misleading in a material particular.
