# 97-20  What this Subdivision is about

The Commissioner must give you a determination stating:

> <a id="s97-20-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the amount by which your non-concessional contributions exceed your non-concessional contributions cap; and

> <a id="s97-20-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a proxy amount for your associated earnings on this excess; and

> <a id="s97-20-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the total amount that can be released from your superannuation interests in relation to this excess and those earnings. Table of sections Operative provisions 97-25      Excess non-concessional contributions determinations 97-30      Associated earnings 97-35      Review 
*For definition, see section 995-1 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997.
