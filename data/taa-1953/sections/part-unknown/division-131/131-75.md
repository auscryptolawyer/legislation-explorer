# 131-75  Income tax treatment of amounts released—proportioning

rule does not apply Section 307-125 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 (the proportioning rule) does not apply to a payment made as required or permitted under this Subdivision.

> **Note:** The income tax treatment of released amounts is also affected by Subdivision 292-B, and section 303-15, of that Act.
