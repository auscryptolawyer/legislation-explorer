# 105-145  Commissioner must give things in writing


<a id="s105-145-1"></a>
**(1)**  Any notice, approval, direction, authority or declaration that the Commissioner may give, or must give, to you under an *indirect tax law must be in writing.

<a id="s105-145-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, this does not prevent the Commissioner giving any of those things to you by electronic transmission if a provision of an *indirect tax law allows the Commissioner to do so. Authorised Version C2026C00170 registered 28/04/2026 Part 3-10 Indirect taxes Section 110-1
