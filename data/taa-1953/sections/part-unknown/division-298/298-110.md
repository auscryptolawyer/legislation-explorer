# 298-110  Civil double jeopardy

If an entity is ordered to pay a civil penalty under the civil penalty provisions in respect of particular conduct, the entity is not liable to a civil penalty under some other provision of a *Commonwealth law in respect of that conduct. Authorised Version C2026C00170 registered 28/04/2026
