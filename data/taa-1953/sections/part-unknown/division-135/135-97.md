# 135-97  Defined benefit interests—releasing amounts to pay

Division 296 debt account discharge liability The exclusion of *defined benefit interests from subsection 135-75(4) and paragraph 135-85(c) is to be disregarded for a release authority issued under item 4 of the table in subsection 135-10(1) (about Division 296 debt account discharge liability). 
*For definition, see section 995-1 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997.
