# 358-15  When a public ruling ceases to apply


<a id="s358-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  A *public ruling may specify the time at which it ceases to apply.

<a id="s358-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  If a *public ruling does not do this, it applies until it is withdrawn. Authorised Version C2026C00170 registered 28/04/2026 Part 5-5 Rulings Section 358-20 Withdrawing public rulings
