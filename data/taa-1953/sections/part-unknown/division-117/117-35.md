# 117-35  Commissioner may disregard scheme in making determinations

For the purposes of making a determination under this Subdivision, the Commissioner may: 
*For definition, see section 995-1 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997.

> <a id="s117-35-a"></a>
> **(a)**  treat a particular event that actually happened as not having happened; and

> <a id="s117-35-b"></a>
> **(b)**  treat a particular event that did not actually happen as having happened and, if appropriate, treat the event as:

> > <a id="s117-35-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  having happened at a particular time; and

> > <a id="s117-35-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  having involved particular action by a particular entity; and

> <a id="s117-35-c"></a>
> **(c)**  treat a particular event that actually happened as:

> > <a id="s117-35-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  having happened at a time different from the time it actually happened; or

> > <a id="s117-35-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  having involved particular action by a particular entity (whether or not the event actually involved any action by that entity).
