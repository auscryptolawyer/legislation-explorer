# 45-913  Sections 45-705 and 45-740 do not apply to members of MEC

groups In applying Subdivision 45-Q to members of a *MEC group, the Subdivision has effect as if:

> <a id="s45-913-a"></a>
> **(a)**  section 45-705 had no effect and section 45-915 had effect instead; and 
*For definition, see section 995-1 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997.

> <a id="s45-913-b"></a>
> **(b)**  section 45-740 had no effect and section 45-920 had effect instead.
