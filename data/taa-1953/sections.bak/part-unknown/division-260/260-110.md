# 260-110  Offence

A person must not fail to comply with subsection 260-105(2), (4),

<a id="s260-110-5"></a>
**(5)**  or (6). Penalty: 10 penalty units.

> **Note:** Chapter 2 of the Criminal Code sets out the general principles of criminal responsibility.

> **Note:** See section 4AA of the Crimes Act 1914 for the current value of penalty units.
