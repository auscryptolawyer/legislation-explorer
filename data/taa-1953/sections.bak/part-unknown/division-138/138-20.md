# 138-20  What this Subdivision is about

Your FHSS maximum release amount comprises your eligible non-concessional contributions, 85% of your eligible concessional contributions, and your associated earnings. There are limits on the amount of contributions that may be eligible for release. 
*For definition, see section 995-1 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997.
