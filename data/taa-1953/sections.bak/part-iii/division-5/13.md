# 13 Limitation of action to enforce payment of fine

Notwithstanding anything contained in any other law of the
               Commonwealth or in a law of a State or Territory, where a
               defendant who has not entered a plea in relation to a prescribed
               taxation offence is, in the defendant's absence, convicted of the
               prescribed taxation offence and the order of the court includes the
               imposition of a fine:
                 (a) a warrant for commitment of the defendant to prison for
                     default in payment of the fine shall not be issued until the
                     expiration of 6 months after the date of the conviction; and
                 (b) the service of a summons upon the defendant in relation to
                     the prescribed taxation offence does not, in proceedings
                     for the recovery of the fine, operate as a bar to the
                     defendant showing that the defendant did not receive the
                     summons, unless the court is satisfied that the defendant
                     wilfully absented himself or herself from the hearing.
