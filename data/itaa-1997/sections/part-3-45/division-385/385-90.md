# 385-90 What this Subdivision is about

You can elect to exclude from your assessable income the profit on a forced disposal or death of live stock that you held as assets of a primary production business you carry on in Australia. The excluded profit is then brought into your assessable income over a 5 year period in one of 2 ways.
