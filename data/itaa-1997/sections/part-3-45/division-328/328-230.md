# 328-230 Estimate where deduction denied

This Subdivision applies to you as if you had estimated that you will not use, or have *installed ready for use, a *depreciating asset at all for a *taxable purpose during an income year if a provision of Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Rules for particular industries and occupations Part 3-45 Small business entities Division 328 Section 328-235 this Act outside this Division denies a deduction for the asset for that year.
