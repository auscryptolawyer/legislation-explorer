# 20-5 Other provisions that reverse the effect of deductions

The table lists other provisions that reverse the effect of certain kinds of deductions. Provisions of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 are identified in normal text. The other provisions, in bold, are provisions of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936.
