# 116-105 Conservation covenants

If *CGT event D4 happens because you enter into a *conservation covenant over land you own and you can deduct an amount under Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Capital proceeds Division 116 Section 116-110 Division 31 because you enter into the covenant, the *capital proceeds from the event are the amount you can deduct.

> **Note:** To get a deduction under Division 31, you must not receive money, property or other material benefit for entering into the covenant.
