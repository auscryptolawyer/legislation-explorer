# 210-85 What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision: • creates a venture capital sub-account for each PDF; and • identifies when venture capital credits and debits arise in the sub-account and the amount of those credits and debits; and • identifies when there is a venture capital surplus or deficit in the sub-account; and • creates a liability to pay venture capital deficit tax if the account is in deficit at certain times.
