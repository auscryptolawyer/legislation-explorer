# 719-4 What this Subdivision is about

A MEC group and a potential MEC group each consist of certain Australian-resident entities that are wholly-owned subsidiaries of a foreign top company. A company that is a first-tier subsidiary of the top company is a tier-1 company. A MEC group cannot be formed unless there are at least 2 tier-1 companies of the top company that are eligible to be members of the group.
