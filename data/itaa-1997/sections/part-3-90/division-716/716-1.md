# 716-1 What this Division is about

Some items of assessable income, and some deductions, are in effect spread over 2 or more income years. This Division apportions the assessable income or deduction for each of those income years among periods within the income year when an entity is, or is not, a subsidiary member of a consolidated group. This Division also apportions in a similar way some items of assessable income, and some deductions, for a single income year.
