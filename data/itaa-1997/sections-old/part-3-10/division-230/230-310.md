# 230-310  Aligning tax classification of gain or loss from hedging financial arrangement with tax classification of hedged item


<a id="s230-310-1"></a>
**(1)**  The object of this section is to better align, in particular circumstances, the tax classification of a gain or loss you make from a [[*hedging financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hedging-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hedging-financial-arrangement) with the tax classification of the [[*hedged item*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hedged-item)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hedged-item).

<a id="s230-310-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section applies if:

> <a id="s230-310-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you make a gain or loss from a [[*hedging financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hedging-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hedging-financial-arrangement) for an income year; and

> <a id="s230-310-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*hedging financial arrangement election*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hedging-financial-arrangement-election)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hedging-financial-arrangement-election) applies to the arrangement.

<a id="s230-310-3"></a>
**(3)**  Subject to subsection (4):

> <a id="s230-310-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if you make a gain from the arrangement—your assessable income includes the gain in accordance with subsection 230-15(1); and

> <a id="s230-310-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if you make a loss from the arrangement—you may deduct the loss in accordance with subsections 230-15(2) and (3).

> **Note:** Section 230-300 tells you how to allocate the gain or loss to an income year or years.
