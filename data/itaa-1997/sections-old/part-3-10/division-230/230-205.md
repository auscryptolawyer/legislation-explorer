# 230-205  Objects of this Subdivision

The objects of this Subdivision are:

> <a id="s230-205-a"></a>
> **(a)**  to allow you to align the tax treatment of gains and losses from [[*financial arrangements*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement) with the accounting treatment that applies where assets and liabilities are classified or designated as at fair value through profit or loss; and

> <a id="s230-205-b"></a>
> **(b)**  to facilitate efficient price-making; and

> <a id="s230-205-c"></a>
> **(c)**  to achieve the above objects without allowing you to obtain an inappropriate tax benefit. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            323 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026
