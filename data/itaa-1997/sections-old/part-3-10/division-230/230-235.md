# 230-235  Splitting financial arrangements into 2 financial arrangements


<a id="s230-235-1"></a>
**(1)**  If: 330                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Financial transactions Part 3-10 Taxation of financial arrangements Division 230 Section 230-240

> <a id="s230-235-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement) is constituted only in part by an asset or liability mentioned in paragraph 230-220(1)(c); and

> <a id="s230-235-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*fair value election*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fair-value-election)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fair-value-election) would apply to the arrangement if it were constituted solely by that asset or liability; the provisions of this Division (other than this section) apply to the arrangement as if it were instead 2 separate financial arrangements.

<a id="s230-235-2"></a>
**(2)**  The 2 separate [[*financial arrangements*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement) are:

> <a id="s230-235-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  one consisting of the part referred to in paragraph (1)(a); and

> <a id="s230-235-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  one consisting of the remaining part.
