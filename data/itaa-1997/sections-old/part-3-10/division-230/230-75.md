# 230-75  Apportionment when financial benefit provided or obligation ceases


<a id="s230-75-1"></a>
**(1)**  Apply subsection (2) in working out whether you make, or will make, a gain or loss (and the amount of the gain or loss) at a time when: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            283 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s230-75-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you provide a particular [[*financial benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) under the [[*financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement); or

> <a id="s230-75-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  one of your obligations under a financial arrangement ceases. The gain or loss is to be calculated in nominal (and not *present value) terms.

<a id="s230-75-2"></a>
**(2)**  You must have regard to the extent to which the [[*financial benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) that you have received, or are to receive or might receive, under the [[*financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement) are reasonably attributable, at the time mentioned in subsection (1), to the benefit or obligation referred to in paragraph (1)(a) or (b).

<a id="s230-75-3"></a>
**(3)**  Any attribution made under subsection (2) must reflect appropriate and commercially accepted valuation principles that properly take into account:

> <a id="s230-75-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the nature of the rights and obligations under the [[*financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement); and

> <a id="s230-75-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the risks associated with each [[*financial benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit), right and obligation under the arrangement; and

> <a id="s230-75-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the time value of money.

> **Note:** Generally, no financial benefit you have received, or are to receive or might receive, under a financial arrangement is reasonably attributable to an amount you provide that is in the nature of interest.
