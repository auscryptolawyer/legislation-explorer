# 230-500  Comparable foreign accounting and auditing standards

The regulations may:

> <a id="s230-500-a"></a>
> **(a)**  specify that particular standards that apply under a [[*foreign law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law) are to be taken for the purposes of this Division to be comparable to the [[*accounting principles*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-accounting-principles)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-accounting-principles); and

> <a id="s230-500-b"></a>
> **(b)**  specify that particular standards that apply under a foreign law are to be taken for the purposes of this Division to be comparable to the [[*auditing principles*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-auditing-principles)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-auditing-principles). 406                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Financial transactions Part 3-10 Taxation of financial arrangements Division 230 Section 230-505
