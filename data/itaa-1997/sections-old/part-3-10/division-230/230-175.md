# 230-175  Running balancing adjustments

Overestimate of financial benefit to be received

<a id="s230-175-1"></a>
**(1)**  You are taken for the purposes of this Division to make a loss from a [[*financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement) if:

> <a id="s230-175-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a provision of this Subdivision has applied on the basis that you were sufficiently certain, at a particular time, to receive a [[*financial benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) of, or of at least, a particular amount under the arrangement; and

> <a id="s230-175-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  when you receive the benefit (or the time comes for you to receive the benefit), the amount you receive (or are to receive) is nil or is less than the amount estimated. The amount of the loss is equal to the difference between the amount estimated and the amount you receive (or are to receive). You are taken to have made the loss for the income year in which you receive the benefit (or in which the time comes for you to receive the benefit). (1A) Subsection (1) does not apply to the extent that the difference results from:

> <a id="s230-175-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an impairment (within the meaning of the [[*accounting principles*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-accounting-principles)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-accounting-principles)) of:

> > <a id="s230-175-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the [[*financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement); or

> > <a id="s230-175-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a financial asset or financial liability that forms part of the arrangement; or

> <a id="s230-175-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you writing off, as a bad debt, a right to a [[*financial benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) (or a part of a financial benefit). 310                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Financial transactions Part 3-10 Taxation of financial arrangements Division 230 Section 230-175 Underestimate of financial benefit to be received

**(2)**  You are taken for the purposes of this Division to make a gain from a [[*financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement) if:

> <a id="s230-175-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a provision of this Subdivision has applied on the basis that you were sufficiently certain at a particular time to receive a [[*financial benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) of, or of at least, a particular amount under the arrangement; and

> <a id="s230-175-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  when you receive the benefit, or the time comes for you to receive the benefit, the amount you receive, or are to receive, is more than the amount estimated. The amount of the gain is equal to the difference between the amount estimated and the amount you receive or are to receive. You are taken to have made that gain in the income year in which you receive the benefit or in which the time comes for you to receive the benefit. (2A) Subsection (2) does not apply to the extent that the difference results from the reversal of an impairment loss (within the meaning of the [[*accounting principles*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-accounting-principles)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-accounting-principles)) that resulted from an impairment (within the meaning of the accounting principles) of:

> <a id="s230-175-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement); or

> <a id="s230-175-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a financial asset or financial liability that forms part of the arrangement. Overestimate of financial benefit to be provided
