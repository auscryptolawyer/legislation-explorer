# 250-290  Balancing adjustment under Subdivision 40-D in some circumstances


<a id="s250-290-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if:

> <a id="s250-290-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  this Division applies to you and an asset; and

> <a id="s250-290-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) period for the [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) preferred use of the asset ends because a particular event happens; and

> <a id="s250-290-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the event would have been a [[*balancing adjustment event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event) for the asset for the purposes of Subdivision 40-D if this Division had not applied to you and the asset when the event happened.

<a id="s250-290-2"></a>
**(2)**  A balancing adjustment is made under Subdivision 40-D as if:

> <a id="s250-290-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the event were a [[*balancing adjustment event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event) for the asset; and

> <a id="s250-290-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) of the asset, just before the event happened, were the adjustable value worked out under subsection 250-285(1); and

> <a id="s250-290-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  sections 40-290, 40-291, 40-292 and 40-293 did not apply. 570                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Financial transactions Part 3-10 Assets put to tax preferred use Division 250 Section 250-295
