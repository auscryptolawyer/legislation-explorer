# 250-135  Level of expected financial benefits test

Effective guarantee or indemnity for value of asset

<a id="s250-135-1"></a>
**(1)**  You lack a predominant economic interest in an asset at a particular time if the asset has a *guaranteed residual value at that time. Likely financial benefits exceeding 70% limit

<a id="s250-135-2"></a>
**(2)**  You also lack a predominant economic interest in an asset at a particular time if, at that time:

> <a id="s250-135-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) under which the asset is *put to the tax preferred use (either alone or together with any other arrangement in relation to the [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) preferred use of the asset or the *provision of [[*financial benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) in relation to the tax preferred use of the asset) is a *debt interest; or

> <a id="s250-135-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the sum of the present values of the [[*expected financial benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-expected-financial-benefits)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-expected-financial-benefits) that *members of the tax preferred sector have provided, or are or are reasonably likely to provide, to you (or a *connected entity) in relation to the tax preferred use of the asset exceeds 70% of:

> > <a id="s250-135-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the *market value of the asset if subparagraph 250-15(d)(i) applies; or 536                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Financial transactions Part 3-10 Assets put to tax preferred use Division 250 Section 250-140

> > <a id="s250-135-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  so much of the market value of the asset as is attributable to the expenditure referred to subparagraph 250-15(d)(ii) if that subparagraph applies.
