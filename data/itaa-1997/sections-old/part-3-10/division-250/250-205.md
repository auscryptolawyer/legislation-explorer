# 250-205  Gains are assessable and losses deductible

Gains

<a id="s250-205-1"></a>
**(1)**  Your assessable income includes a gain you make from the [[*financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement). Losses

<a id="s250-205-2"></a>
**(2)**  You can deduct a loss you make from the [[*financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement), but only to the extent that:

> <a id="s250-205-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you make it in gaining or producing your assessable income; or

> <a id="s250-205-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you necessarily make it in carrying on a *business for the purpose of gaining or producing your assessable income. 550                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Financial transactions Part 3-10 Assets put to tax preferred use Division 250 Section 250-210
