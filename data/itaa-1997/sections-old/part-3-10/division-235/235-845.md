# 235-845  Interactions with other provisions


<a id="s235-845-1"></a>
**(1)**  Section 106-50 (about absolutely entitled beneficiaries) does not apply to an [[*instalment trust asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-instalment-trust-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-instalment-trust-asset).

<a id="s235-845-2"></a>
**(2)**  Section 106-60 (about securities, charges and encumbrances) does not apply to an [[*instalment trust asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-instalment-trust-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-instalment-trust-asset).

<a id="s235-845-3"></a>
**(3)**  Nothing in this Subdivision limits Division 247 (which is about capital protected borrowings).
