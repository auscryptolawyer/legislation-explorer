# 247-5  Object of Division

The object of this Division is to ensure that amounts for [[*capital protection*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-protection)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-protection) under all relevant [[*capital protected borrowings*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-protected-borrowing)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-protected-borrowing) are treated (for the borrower) under this Act as a payment for a put option.
