# 245-185  Relevant cost bases of investments in associated entities are reduced last

If your [[*CGT assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that are subject to reduction under section 245-175 include investments in, or in relation to, an [[*associate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate) of yours (including *membership interests, or *debt interests, in your associate), the:

> <a id="s245-185-a"></a>
> **(a)**  [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base; and

> <a id="s245-185-b"></a>
> **(b)**  *reduced cost base; of those assets are not subject to reduction under section 245-175 until the [[*total net forgiven amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-total-net-forgiven-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-total-net-forgiven-amount) (if any) remaining has been applied, to the maximum extent possible, in reduction of the cost bases of your other CGT assets.
