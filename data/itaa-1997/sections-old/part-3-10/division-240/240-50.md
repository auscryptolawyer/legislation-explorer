# 240-50  Extent to which deductions are allowable to notional buyer


<a id="s240-50-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*notional buyer*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-buyer)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-buyer) is only entitled to deduct [[*notional interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-interest) for an income year to the extent that the notional buyer would, apart from this Division, have been entitled to deduct [[*arrangement payments*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement-payment) for that income year if no part of those payments were capital in nature.

<a id="s240-50-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*notional buyer*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-buyer)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-buyer) is entitled to deduct [[*notional interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-interest) for [[*arrangement payment periods*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement-payment-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement-payment-period), and parts of arrangement payment periods, in the income year.
