# 240-55  Arrangement payments not to be deductions

The [[*notional buyer*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-buyer)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-buyer) is not entitled to deduct [[*arrangement payments*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement-payment) that the [[*notional buyer*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-buyer)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-buyer) makes under the [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement), but those payments are taken into account in calculating [[*notional interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-interest) that may be deducted under section 240-50.
