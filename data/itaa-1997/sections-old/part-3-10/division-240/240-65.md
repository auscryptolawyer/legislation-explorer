# 240-65  Arrangement payments

An arrangement payment is an amount that the [[*notional buyer*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-buyer)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-buyer) is required to pay under the [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) but does not include:

> <a id="s240-65-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an amount in the nature of a penalty payable for failure to make a payment on time; or

> <a id="s240-65-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*termination amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-amount).
