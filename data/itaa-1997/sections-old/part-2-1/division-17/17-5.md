# 17-5  GST and increasing adjustments

An amount is not assessable income, and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income), to the extent that it includes an amount relating to:

> <a id="s17-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  [[*GST*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst) payable on a [[*taxable supply*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply); or

> <a id="s17-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an [[*increasing adjustment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-increasing-adjustment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-increasing-adjustment) that relates to a [[*supply*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-supply)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-supply); or

> <a id="s17-5-c"></a>
> **(c)**  an [[*increasing adjustment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-increasing-adjustment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-increasing-adjustment) that:

> > <a id="s17-5-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  relates to an *acquisition; and

> > <a id="s17-5-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  arises in circumstances that also give rise to a [[*recoupment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment) that is included in assessable income. 104                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Assessable income Part 2-1 Effect of GST etc. on assessable income Division 17 Section 17-10
