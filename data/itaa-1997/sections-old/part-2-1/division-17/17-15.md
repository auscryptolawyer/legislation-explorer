# 17-15  Elements in calculation of amounts

In calculating an amount that may be included in assessable income:

> <a id="s17-15-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an element in the calculation that is an amount received or receivable is treated as not including an amount equal to any [[*GST*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst) payable on a [[*taxable supply*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply) related to the amount received or receivable, or any [[*increasing adjustment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-increasing-adjustment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-increasing-adjustment) related to that amount; and

> <a id="s17-15-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an element in the calculation that is an amount paid or payable is treated as not including an amount equal to any [[*input tax credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-input-tax-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-input-tax-credit) for an *acquisition related to the amount paid or payable, or any [[*decreasing adjustment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-decreasing-adjustment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-decreasing-adjustment) related to that amount.
