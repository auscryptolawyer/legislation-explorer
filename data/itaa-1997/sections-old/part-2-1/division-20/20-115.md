# 20-115  Working out the profit on the disposal


<a id="s20-115-1"></a>
**(1)**  The profit on the disposal is the amount by which the [[*consideration receivable*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consideration-receivable)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consideration-receivable) for the disposal exceeds: •     the amount it cost you to acquire the [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car); plus: •     any capital expenditure you incurred on the car after acquiring it.
