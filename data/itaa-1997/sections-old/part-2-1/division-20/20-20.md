# 20-20  Assessable recoupments

Exclusion

<a id="s20-20-1"></a>
**(1)**  An amount is not an assessable recoupment to the extent that it is [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income), or it is [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income) because of a provision outside this Subdivision. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            111 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Insurance or indemnity

<a id="s20-20-2"></a>
**(2)**  An amount you have received as [[*recoupment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment) of a loss or outgoing is an assessable recoupment if:

> <a id="s20-20-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you received the amount by way of insurance or indemnity; and

> <a id="s20-20-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you can deduct an amount for the loss or outgoing for the [[*current year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year), or you have deducted or can deduct an amount for it for an earlier income year, under any provision of this Act. Other recoupment

<a id="s20-20-3"></a>
**(3)**  An amount you have received as [[*recoupment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment) of a loss or outgoing (except by way of insurance or indemnity) is an assessable recoupment if:

> <a id="s20-20-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you can deduct an amount for the loss or outgoing for the [[*current year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year); or

> <a id="s20-20-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you have deducted or can deduct an amount for the loss or outgoing for an earlier income year; under a provision listed in section 20-30.
