# 727-250  Distribution by an entity to a member or beneficiary


<a id="s727-250-1"></a>
**(1)**  An [[*indirect value shift*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-value-shift)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-value-shift) does not have consequences under this Division if:

> <a id="s727-250-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*greater benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-greater-benefits)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-greater-benefits) consist entirely of:

> > <a id="s727-250-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a distribution of income or capital that the *losing entity makes to the *gaining entity; or

> > <a id="s727-250-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a right to a distribution of income or capital that the losing entity is to make to the gaining entity; 64                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Value shifting Part 3-95 Indirect value shifting affecting interests in companies and trusts, and arising from non-arm’s length dealings Division 727 Section 727-250 because the gaining entity holds *primary equity interests in the losing entity; and

> <a id="s727-250-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  either:

> > <a id="s727-250-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  an amount covered by one or more of subsections (2),

<a id="s727-250-3"></a>
**(3)**  and (4); or

> > <a id="s727-250-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the total of 2 or more such amounts; equals or exceeds the amount of the distribution. Conditions

<a id="s727-250-2"></a>
**(2)**  This subsection covers an amount that the assessable income, [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) or [[*non-assessable non-exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income) of the gaining entity for any income year includes because of the distribution or right.

<a id="s727-250-3"></a>
**(3)**  This subsection covers an amount by which the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base or *reduced cost base (or both) of some or all of the *primary equity interests referred to in subsection (1) changes because of the distribution or right.

<a id="s727-250-4"></a>
**(4)**  This subsection covers an amount that, because of the distribution or right, is taken into account:

> <a id="s727-250-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  under section 116-20 in working out the [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) of a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) that happens during any income year to some or all of the *primary equity interests referred to in subsection (1); or

> <a id="s727-250-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  in working out a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) that an entity makes from CGT event E4 or G1 happening during any income year to some or all of those primary equity interests; or

> <a id="s727-250-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  in working out whether a loss or gain is [[*realised for income tax purposes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realised-for-income-tax-purposes)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realised-for-income-tax-purposes) by a [[*realisation event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event) that happens to some or all of those primary equity interests (in their character as [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock) or [[*revenue assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)). **(5)**  If a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) makes a *distribution that is not otherwise a distribution of income or capital, this section applies as if the distribution were a distribution of income or capital the entity made.
