# 727-615  Reduction of loss on realisation event for affected interest in losing entity

If this Subdivision applies to a [[*realisation event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event) that happens to an [[*affected interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest) in the *losing entity, a loss that would, apart from this Division, be [[*realised for income tax purposes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realised-for-income-tax-purposes)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realised-for-income-tax-purposes) by the event is reduced by an amount that is reasonable having regard to:

> <a id="s727-615-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a reasonable estimate of the amount (if any) by which the [[*indirect value shift*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-value-shift)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-value-shift) has reduced the interest’s *market value; and

> <a id="s727-615-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if the interest is also an affected interest in the *gaining entity—a reasonable estimate of the extent (if any) to which the interest’s market value at the time of the realisation event still reflects the effect of the indirect value shift on the market value of [[*equity or loan interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest) in the gaining entity.
