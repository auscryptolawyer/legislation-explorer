# 727-470  Exceptions

Mere active participants

<a id="s727-470-1"></a>
**(1)**  An [[*equity or loan interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest) that an [[*active participant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-active-participant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-active-participant) in the *scheme owns in another active participant immediately before the [[*IVS time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time) is not an [[*affected interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest) in the *losing entity or in the *gaining entity unless one of the active participants is also covered by 1, 2, 3 or 4 in the table in subsection 727-530(1) (about who is an affected owner). Entity that is a small business entity, or satisfies the maximum net asset value test for small business relief

<a id="s727-470-2"></a>
**(2)**  An [[*equity or loan interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest) that an entity (the owner) owns immediately before the [[*IVS time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time) is not an [[*affected interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest) in the *losing entity or in the *gaining entity if the owner:

> <a id="s727-470-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  is a [[*small business entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-small-business-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-small-business-entity) for each income year that includes any of the [[*IVS period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-period); or

> <a id="s727-470-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  would satisfy the maximum net asset value test in section 152-15 throughout the [[*IVS period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-period).

<a id="s727-470-3"></a>
**(3)**  If the owner is not in existence for part of the [[*IVS period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-period), disregard that part in applying subsection (2). Interests in superannuation entities not covered

<a id="s727-470-4"></a>
**(4)**  An [[*equity or loan interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest) in an [[*affected owner*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-owner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-owner) is not an [[*affected interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest) in the *losing entity or in the *gaining entity if the affected owner is an entity listed in section 727-125 (about superannuation entities) in relation to the income year in which the [[*IVS time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time) happens. 80                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Value shifting Part 3-95 Indirect value shifting affecting interests in companies and trusts, and arising from non-arm’s length dealings Division 727 Section 727-520
