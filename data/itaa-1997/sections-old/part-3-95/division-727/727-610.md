# 727-610  Consequences of indirect value shift


<a id="s727-610-1"></a>
**(1)**  This Subdivision sets out the realisation time method of working out the consequences (if any) of an [[*indirect value shift*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-value-shift)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-value-shift).

<a id="s727-610-2"></a>
**(2)**  If those consequences are to be worked out using that method, this Subdivision applies to each [[*realisation event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event):

> <a id="s727-610-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  by which a loss would, apart from this Division, be [[*realised for income tax purposes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realised-for-income-tax-purposes)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realised-for-income-tax-purposes); and

> <a id="s727-610-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  that happens to an [[*affected interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest) in the *losing entity; and

> <a id="s727-610-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  that is the first realisation event that happens to that interest at or after the [[*IVS time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time); and

> <a id="s727-610-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  that happens:

> > <a id="s727-610-2-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  if the amount of the indirect value shift is $500,000 or more—at any time after the IVS time; or

> > <a id="s727-610-2-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  otherwise—within 4 years after the IVS time.

<a id="s727-610-3"></a>
**(3)**  If:

> <a id="s727-610-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  those consequences are to be worked out using that method; and

> <a id="s727-610-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the *gaining entity is a company or trust (except one listed in section 727-125 (about superannuation entities)) immediately before the [[*IVS time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time); this Subdivision applies to each [[*realisation event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event):

> <a id="s727-610-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  by which a gain would, apart from this Division, be [[*realised for income tax purposes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realised-for-income-tax-purposes)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realised-for-income-tax-purposes); and

> <a id="s727-610-3-d"></a>
> **(d)**  that happens to an [[*affected interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest) in the *gaining entity; and

> <a id="s727-610-3-e"></a>
> **(e)**  that is the first realisation event that happens to that interest at or after the IVS time. 88                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Value shifting Part 3-95 Indirect value shifting affecting interests in companies and trusts, and arising from non-arm’s length dealings Division 727 Section 727-615

<a id="s727-610-4"></a>
**(4)**  The consequences for the [[*affected interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest) depend on its character. There are consequences for the interest in its character as a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset). However, if the interest is also [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock) or a [[*revenue asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset), there are additional consequences for it in that character.

<a id="s727-610-5"></a>
**(5)**  In working out the consequences for an [[*affected interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest) in the *losing entity or *gaining entity, in the interest’s character as [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock), a [[*realisation event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event) is disregarded for the purposes of identifying under paragraph (2)(c) or (3)(e) the first realisation event that happens to that interest at or after the [[*IVS time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time), if:

> <a id="s727-610-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the realisation event consists of the ending of an income year; and

> <a id="s727-610-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the [[*value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value) of the interest as trading stock on hand of an entity at the end of the income year is the interest’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost); and

> <a id="s727-610-5-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the interest became part of the entity’s trading stock on hand during that income year, or the value of the interest as trading stock of the entity on hand at the start of the income year was also the interest’s cost.
