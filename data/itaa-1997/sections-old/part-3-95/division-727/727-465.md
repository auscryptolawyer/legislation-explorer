# 727-465  Affected interests in the gaining entity

If immediately before the [[*IVS time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time) the *gaining entity is a company or trust (except one listed in section 727-125 (about superannuation entities)), these are the affected interests in the gaining entity:

> <a id="s727-465-a"></a>
> **(a)**  each [[*equity or loan interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest) that an [[*affected owner*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-owner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-owner) owns in the gaining entity immediately before the [[*IVS time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time); and

> <a id="s727-465-b"></a>
> **(b)**  each equity or loan interest that: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             79 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 arising from non-arm’s length dealings Section 727-470

> > <a id="s727-465-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  an affected owner owns in another affected owner immediately before the IVS time; and

> > <a id="s727-465-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  is an [[*indirect equity or loan interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-equity-or-loan-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-equity-or-loan-interest) in the gaining entity; (except one covered by an exception in section 727-470).
