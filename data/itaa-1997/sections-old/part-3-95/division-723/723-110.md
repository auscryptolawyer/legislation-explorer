# 723-110  Direct and indirect roll-over replacement for underlying asset


<a id="s723-110-1"></a>
**(1)**  For an entity (the transferor) that owns a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), the CGT asset is a direct roll-over replacement for something (the underlying asset) that another entity owns if, and only if:

> <a id="s723-110-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happened to the underlying asset while the transferor owned it; and

> <a id="s723-110-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the other entity *acquired the underlying asset as a result of that CGT event; and

> <a id="s723-110-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  there was a [[*replacement-asset roll-over*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-replacement-asset-roll-over)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-replacement-asset-roll-over) for the CGT event; and

> <a id="s723-110-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the transferor received the CGT asset (or CGT assets including it) in respect of the CGT event as the replacement asset (or the replacement assets).

<a id="s723-110-2"></a>
**(2)**  For an entity (the transferor) that owns a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), the CGT asset is an indirect roll-over replacement for something (the underlying asset) that another entity owns if, and only if:

> <a id="s723-110-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happened to another CGT asset at a time when the transferor owned it and the other entity already owned the underlying asset; and

> <a id="s723-110-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  for the transferor, the other CGT asset was at that time:

> > <a id="s723-110-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a [[*direct roll-over replacement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-roll-over-replacement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-roll-over-replacement) for the underlying asset; or

> > <a id="s723-110-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  an indirect roll-over replacement for the underlying asset because of any other application or applications of this subsection; and

> <a id="s723-110-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  there was a [[*replacement-asset roll-over*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-replacement-asset-roll-over)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-replacement-asset-roll-over) for the CGT event; and

> <a id="s723-110-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the transferor received the first CGT asset (or CGT assets including it) in respect of the CGT event as the replacement asset (or the replacement assets). 10                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Value shifting Part 3-95 Direct value shifting affecting interests in companies and trusts Division 725 Section 725-1
