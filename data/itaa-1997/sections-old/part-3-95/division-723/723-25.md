# 723-25  Realisation event that is only a partial realisation


<a id="s723-25-1"></a>
**(1)**  Section 723-10 or 723-15 applies differently if:

> <a id="s723-25-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*realisation event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event) happens to some part of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) (the underlying asset) you own that, at the time of the event:

> > <a id="s723-25-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  is not a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset); or

> > <a id="s723-25-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  is an item of your [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock); or

> > <a id="s723-25-1-a-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  is a [[*revenue asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset) of yours; but not to the remainder of the underlying asset; or

> <a id="s723-25-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a realisation event consists of creating an interest in a CGT asset (also the underlying asset) you own that, at the time of the event, is covered by subparagraph (a)(i), (ii) or (iii).

<a id="s723-25-2"></a>
**(2)**  The section applies on the basis that:

> <a id="s723-25-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*realisation event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event) happens to the underlying asset; and

> <a id="s723-25-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the shortfall on creating the right referred to in paragraph 723-10(1)(e) or 723-15(1)(c); and

> <a id="s723-25-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the deficit on realisation referred to in paragraph 723-10(1)(g) or 723-15(1)(e); are each reduced by multiplying its amount by this fraction: *Market value of part *Market value of underlying asset
