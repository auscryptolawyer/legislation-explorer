# 725-65  Cause of the value shift


<a id="s725-65-1"></a>
**(1)**  It must be the case that one or more of the following:

> <a id="s725-65-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the target entity;

> <a id="s725-65-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the controller;

> <a id="s725-65-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  an entity that was an [[*associate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate) of the controller at some time during or after the *scheme period;

> <a id="s725-65-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  an [[*active participant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-active-participant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-active-participant) in the *scheme; Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             13 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 (either alone or together with one or more other entities) did under the scheme the one or more things:

> <a id="s725-65-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  to which the decrease in the *market value of the [[*down interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest) is reasonably attributable; and

> <a id="s725-65-1-f"></a>
> **(f)**  to which the increase in the market value of the [[*up interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest), or the issue of up interests at a [[*discount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount), is reasonably attributable, or that is or include the issue of up interests at a [[*discount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount). Active participants (if target entity is closely held)

<a id="s725-65-2"></a>
**(2)**  An entity (the first entity) is an active participant in the *scheme if, and only if:

> <a id="s725-65-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  at some time during the *scheme period, the target entity has fewer than 300 members (in the case of a company) or fewer than 300 beneficiaries (in the case of a trust); and

> <a id="s725-65-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the first entity has actively participated in, or directly facilitated, the entering into or carrying out of the *scheme (whether or not it did so at the direction of some other entity); and

> <a id="s725-65-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the first entity:

> > <a id="s725-65-2-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  owns a [[*down interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest) at the *decrease time; or

> > <a id="s725-65-2-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  owns an [[*up interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest) at the *increase time or has an up interest issued to it at a [[*discount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount) because of the [[*direct value shift*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-value-shift)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-value-shift). When an entity has 300 or more members or beneficiaries

<a id="s725-65-3"></a>
**(3)**  Section 124-810 (under which certain companies and trusts are not regarded as having 300 or more members or beneficiaries) also applies for the purposes of this Division.
