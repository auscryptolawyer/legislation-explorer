# 725-245  Table of taxing events generating a gain for interests as CGT assets

To the extent that the [[*direct value shift*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-value-shift)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-value-shift) is from [[*down interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest) of which you are an [[*affected owner*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-owner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-owner), and that are specified in an item in the table, to [[*up interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest) specified in that item, those up interests give rise to a taxing event generating a gain for you on each of those down interests. The gain is worked out under section 725-365.
