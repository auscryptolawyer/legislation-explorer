# 725-370  Uplifts in adjustable values of up interests under certain table items

Use the following method statement to work out the uplift in [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) of an [[*up interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest) under:

> <a id="s725-370-a"></a>
> **(a)**  item 1 or 2 of the table in subsection 725-250(2); or

> <a id="s725-370-b"></a>
> **(b)**  item 1 of the table in subsection 725-335(3). Method statement Step 1.   If the *market value of the [[*up interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest) increases because of the [[*direct value shift*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-value-shift)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-value-shift), group together all up interests of the kind referred to in the relevant item that:

> <a id="s725-370-a"></a>
> **(a)**  immediately before the *increase time, had the same [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) as the up interest; and

> <a id="s725-370-b"></a>
> **(b)**  sustained the same increase in market value as the up interest because of the [[*direct value shift*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-value-shift)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-value-shift). If the [[*up interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest) is issued at a [[*discount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount), group together all [[*up interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest) of the kind referred to in the relevant item that:

> <a id="s725-370-c"></a>
> **(c)**  immediately before the *increase time, had the same [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) as the up interest; and
