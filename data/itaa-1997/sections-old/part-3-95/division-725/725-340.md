# 725-340  Multiple trading stock or revenue asset consequences for the same down interest or up interest


<a id="s725-340-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*down interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest) or [[*up interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest) of which you are an [[*affected owner*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-owner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-owner) may be covered by 2 or more items in the table in subsection 725-335(3).

<a id="s725-340-2"></a>
**(2)**  If the [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) of the same [[*down interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest) or [[*up interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest) is decreased or uplifted under 2 or more items, it is decreased or uplifted by the total of the amounts worked out under those items.

> **Note:** If subsection 725-335(3A) is relevant, it will affect all the uplifts worked out under all those items.

<a id="s725-340-3"></a>
**(3)**  If for a particular [[*down interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest) there is a [[*taxing event generating a gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxing-event-generating-a-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxing-event-generating-a-gain) under an item, that taxing event is in addition to:

> <a id="s725-340-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  each taxing event generating a gain for that interest under any other item in the table; and

> <a id="s725-340-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  each decrease in the [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) of the interest under that or any other item in the table.
