# 725-320  Consequences for down interest or up interest as a revenue asset


<a id="s725-320-1"></a>
**(1)**  The consequences of the [[*direct value shift*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-value-shift)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-value-shift) for your [[*revenue assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset) are of one or more of these 3 kinds:

> <a id="s725-320-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*adjustable values*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) of [[*down interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest) of which you are an [[*affected owner*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-owner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-owner) are reduced (see subsection (2));

> <a id="s725-320-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the adjustable values of [[*up interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest) of which you are an affected owner are uplifted (see subsection (3));

> <a id="s725-320-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  one or more *taxing events generating a gain for down interests of which you are an affected owner (see subsection (5)). Effect of reduction or uplift of adjustable value

<a id="s725-320-2"></a>
**(2)**  If the [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) of a [[*down interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-down-interest) that is your [[*revenue asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset) is decreased under section 725-335, you are treated as if:

> <a id="s725-320-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  *immediately before the *decrease time, you had sold the interest to someone else for its [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) immediately before the decrease time; and

> <a id="s725-320-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  immediately afterwards, you had bought the interest back for the reduced adjustable value; and 34                                     Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Value shifting Part 3-95 Direct value shifting affecting interests in companies and trusts Division 725 Section 725-325

> <a id="s725-320-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  from the time when you bought it back, the interest continued to be a revenue asset, for the same reasons as it was a revenue asset before you sold it.

<a id="s725-320-3"></a>
**(3)**  If the [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) of an [[*up interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-up-interest) that is your [[*revenue asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset) is uplifted under section 725-335, you are treated as if:

> <a id="s725-320-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  *immediately before the *increase time, you had sold the interest to someone else for its [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) immediately before the increase time; and

> <a id="s725-320-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  immediately afterwards, you had bought the interest back for the uplifted adjustable value; and

> <a id="s725-320-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  from the time when you bought it back, the interest continued to be a revenue asset, for the same reasons as it was a revenue asset before you sold it.

<a id="s725-320-4"></a>
**(4)**  However, the uplift in [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) is taken into account only to the extent that the amount of the uplift is still reflected in the *market value of the interest when it is disposed of or otherwise realised. Taxing event generating a gain

<a id="s725-320-5"></a>
**(5)**  For each [[*taxing event generating a gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxing-event-generating-a-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxing-event-generating-a-gain) under an item in the table in subsection 725-335(3), the gain is included in your assessable income for the income year in which the *decrease time happens.
