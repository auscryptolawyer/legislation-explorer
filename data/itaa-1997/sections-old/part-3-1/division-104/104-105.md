# 104-105  Creating a trust over future property: CGT event E9


<a id="s104-105-1"></a>
**(1)**  CGT event E9 happens if:

> <a id="s104-105-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you agree for consideration that when property comes into existence you will hold it on trust; and

> <a id="s104-105-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  at the time of the agreement, no potential beneficiary under the trust has a beneficial interest in the rights created by the agreement.

<a id="s104-105-2"></a>
**(2)**  The time of the event is when you made the agreement.

<a id="s104-105-3"></a>
**(3)**  You make a capital gain if the *market value the property would have had if it had existed when you made the agreement is more than any [[*incidental costs*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-incidental-costs)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-incidental-costs) you incurred that relate to the event. You make a capital loss if that market value is less.

<a id="s104-105-4"></a>
**(4)**  The costs can include giving property: see section 103-5. However, they do not include an amount you have received as [[*recoupment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment) of them and that is not included in your assessable income, or an amount to the extent that you have deducted or can deduct it. 196                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 CGT events Division 104 Section 104-107A 104-107A AMIT—cost base reduction exceeds cost base: CGT event E10
