# 104-190  Replacement asset period (1A) If you choose a small business roll-over under Subdivision 152-E

for a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) that happens in relation to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) in an income year, the replacement asset period is the period:

> <a id="s104-190-a"></a>
> **(a)**  starting one year before the last CGT event in the income year for which you obtain the roll-over; and

> <a id="s104-190-b"></a>
> **(b)**  ending at the later of:

> > <a id="s104-190-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  2 years after that last CGT event; and 222                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 CGT events Division 104 Section 104-195

> > <a id="s104-190-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if the first-mentioned CGT event happened because you *disposed of the CGT asset—6 months after the latest time a possible [[*financial benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) becomes or could become due under a [[*look-through earnout right*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right) relating to the CGT asset and the disposal.

<a id="s104-190-1"></a>
**(1)**  The replacement asset period is modified if your [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) for the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) are increased under subsection 116-45(2) or 116-60(3) after the end of that period. Instead, you have until 12 months after you receive those additional proceeds to [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) a replacement asset, or incur [[*fourth element expenditure*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fourth-element-expenditure)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fourth-element-expenditure) in relation to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), or do both.

> **Note:** Section 116-45 applies if you do not receive your capital proceeds despite having taken all reasonable steps to get them, and section 116-60 applies if your capital proceeds are misappropriated by your employee or agent.

<a id="s104-190-2"></a>
**(2)**  The Commissioner may extend the replacement asset period, or that period as modified by subsection (1).
