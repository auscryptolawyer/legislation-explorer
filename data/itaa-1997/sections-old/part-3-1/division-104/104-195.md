# 104-195  Trust failing to cease to exist after roll-over under Subdivision 124-N: CGT event J4


<a id="s104-195-1"></a>
**(1)**  CGT event J4 happens if:

> <a id="s104-195-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  there is a roll-over under Subdivision 124-N for a trust *disposing of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) to a company under a trust restructure; and

> <a id="s104-195-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the trust fails to cease to exist:

> > <a id="s104-195-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  within 6 months after the start of the [[*trust restructuring period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trust-restructuring-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trust-restructuring-period); or

> > <a id="s104-195-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if that is not possible because of circumstances outside the control of the trustee—as soon as practicable after the end of that 6 month period; and

> <a id="s104-195-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the company owns the asset when the failure happens.

> *[[*Example*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example):** Circumstances would be outside the control of the trustee if the trustee is involved in litigation concerning the trust and cannot wind up the trust until the litigation is finished. <a id="s104-195-2"></a>
**(2)**  CGT event J4 also happens if:

> <a id="s104-195-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  there is a roll-over under Subdivision 124-N for an entity (the shareholding entity) receiving a [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share) in a company in exchange for a unit or interest in a trust under a trust restructure; and

> <a id="s104-195-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the trust fails to cease to exist:

> > <a id="s104-195-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  within 6 months after the start of the [[*trust restructuring period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trust-restructuring-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trust-restructuring-period); or

> > <a id="s104-195-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if that is not possible because of circumstances outside the control of the trustee—as soon as practicable after the end of that 6 month period; and

> <a id="s104-195-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the shareholding entity owns the share when the failure happens.

<a id="s104-195-3"></a>
**(3)**  The time of the event is when the failure to cease to exist happens.

<a id="s104-195-4"></a>
**(4)**  The company makes a capital gain if the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)’s *market value at the time the company *acquired the asset is more than its [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base at that time. The company makes a capital loss if that market value is less than the asset’s *reduced cost base at that time.

<a id="s104-195-5"></a>
**(5)**  This Part and Part 3-3 apply to the company from just after the time of the event as if the first element of the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base and *reduced cost base of the asset were its *market value at the time the company *acquired the asset.

<a id="s104-195-6"></a>
**(6)**  The shareholding entity makes a capital gain if the [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)’s *market value at the time the entity *acquired the share is more than its [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base at that time. The shareholding entity makes a capital loss if that market value is less than the share’s *reduced cost base at that time.

<a id="s104-195-7"></a>

<a id="s104-195-8"></a>
