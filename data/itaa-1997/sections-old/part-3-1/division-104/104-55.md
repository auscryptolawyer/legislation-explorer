# 104-55  Creating a trust over a CGT asset: CGT event E1


<a id="s104-55-1"></a>
**(1)**  CGT event E1 happens if you create a trust over a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) by declaration or settlement.

> **Note:** A change in the trustee of a trust does not constitute a change in the entity that is the trustee of the trust (see subsection 960-100(2)). This means that CGT event E1 will not happen merely because of a change in the trustee.

<a id="s104-55-2"></a>
**(2)**  The time of the event is when the trust over the asset is created.

<a id="s104-55-3"></a>
**(3)**  You make a capital gain if the [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) from the creation are more than the asset’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base. You make a capital loss if those capital proceeds are less than the asset’s *reduced cost base. Cost base rule

<a id="s104-55-4"></a>
**(4)**  If you are the trustee of the trust and no beneficiary is absolutely entitled to the asset as against you (disregarding any legal disability), the first element of the asset’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base and *reduced cost base in your hands is its *market value when the trust is created. Exceptions

<a id="s104-55-5"></a>
**(5)**  CGT event E1 does not happen if you are the sole beneficiary of the trust and:

> <a id="s104-55-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are absolutely entitled to the asset as against the trustee (disregarding any legal disability); and

> <a id="s104-55-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the trust is not a unit trust.

<a id="s104-55-6"></a>
**(6)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make is disregarded if you *acquired the asset before 20 September 1985. 178                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 CGT events Division 104 Section 104-60
