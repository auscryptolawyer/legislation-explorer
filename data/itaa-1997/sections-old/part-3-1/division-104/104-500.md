# 104-500  Loss of pre-CGT status of membership interests in entity becoming subsidiary member: CGT event L1


<a id="s104-500-1"></a>
**(1)**  CGT event L1 happens if, under section 705-57 (including in its application in accordance with Subdivisions 705-B to 705-E), there is a reduction in the [[*tax cost setting amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount) of assets of an entity that becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) or a [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group).

<a id="s104-500-2"></a>
**(2)**  The time of the event is just after the entity becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group.

<a id="s104-500-3"></a>
**(3)**  For the head company core purposes mentioned in subsection 701-1(2), the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) makes a capital loss equal to the reduction.
