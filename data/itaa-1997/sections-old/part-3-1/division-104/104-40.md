# 104-40  Granting an option: CGT event D2


<a id="s104-40-1"></a>
**(1)**  CGT event D2 happens if you grant an option to an entity, or renew or extend an option you had granted.

> **Note:** Some options are not covered: see subsections (6) and (7).

<a id="s104-40-2"></a>
**(2)**  The time of the event is when you grant, renew or extend the option.

<a id="s104-40-3"></a>
**(3)**  You make a capital gain if the [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) from the grant, renewal or extension of the option are more than the expenditure you incurred to grant, renew or extend it. You make a capital loss if those capital proceeds are less.

<a id="s104-40-4"></a>
**(4)**  The expenditure can include giving property: see section 103-5. However, it does not include an amount you have received as [[*recoupment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment) of it and that is not included in your assessable income, or an amount to the extent that you have deducted or can deduct it. Exceptions

<a id="s104-40-5"></a>
**(5)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make from the grant, renewal or extension of the option is disregarded if the option is exercised.

> **Note:** Section 134-1 sets out the consequences of an option being exercised.

> **Note:** A capital gain or capital loss you made for the 1997-98 income year or an earlier income year under former Part IIIA of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 is also disregarded where the option is exercised in the 1998-99 income year or a later one: see section 104-40 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997. 174                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 CGT events Division 104 Section 104-45

<a id="s104-40-6"></a>
**(6)**  This section does not apply to an option granted, renewed or extended by a company or the trustee of a unit trust to [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that is:

> <a id="s104-40-6-a"></a>
> **(a)**  *shares in the company or units in the unit trust; or

> <a id="s104-40-6-b"></a>
> **(b)**  debentures of the company or unit trust.

> **Note:** Section 104-30 deals with this situation.

<a id="s104-40-7"></a>
**(7)**  Nor does it apply to an option relating to a [[*personal use asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-use-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-use-asset) or a [[*collectable*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-collectable)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-collectable).
