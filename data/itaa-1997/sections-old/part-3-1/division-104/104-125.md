# 104-125  Lessee receives payment for changing lease: CGT event F4


<a id="s104-125-1"></a>
**(1)**  CGT event F4 happens if a lessee receives a payment from the lessor for agreeing to vary or waive a term of the lease. The payment can include giving property: see section 103-5.

<a id="s104-125-2"></a>
**(2)**  The time of the event is when the term is varied or waived.

<a id="s104-125-3"></a>
**(3)**  The lessee makes a capital gain if the [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) from the event are more than the lease’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base (at the time of the event). If the lessee makes a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain), the lease’s cost base is also reduced to nil.

> **Note:** The lessee cannot make a capital loss.

<a id="s104-125-4"></a>
**(4)**  On the other hand, if those [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) are less, the lease’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base is reduced by that amount at the time of the event.

> *[[*Example*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example):** On 1 January 1999 a lessee enters a lease. On 1 May 1999 the lessee agrees to waive a term. The lessor pays the lessee $1,000 for this. 206                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 CGT events Division 104 Section 104-130 If the lease’s cost base at the time of the waiver is $2,500, it is reduced from $2,500 to $1,500. If the lease’s cost base at the time of the waiver is $1,500, the lessee makes a capital gain of $500, and the cost base is reduced to nil. Exceptions

<a id="s104-125-5"></a>
**(5)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) the lessee makes is disregarded if:

> <a id="s104-125-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the lease was granted before 20 September 1985; or

> <a id="s104-125-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  for a lease that has been renewed or extended—the start of the last renewal or extension occurred before that day.
