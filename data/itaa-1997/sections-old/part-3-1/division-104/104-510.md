# 104-510  Where tax cost setting amounts for retained cost base assets exceeds joining allocable cost amount: CGT event L3


<a id="s104-510-1"></a>
**(1)**  CGT event L3 happens if:

> <a id="s104-510-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) or a [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group); and

> <a id="s104-510-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the sum of the [[*tax cost setting amounts*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount) for all [[*retained cost base assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retained-cost-base-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retained-cost-base-asset) that are taken into account under 246                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 CGT events Division 104 Section 104-515 paragraph 705-35(1)(b) in working out the tax cost setting amount of each reset cost base asset of the entity exceeds the group’s [[*allocable cost amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-allocable-cost-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-allocable-cost-amount) for the entity.

<a id="s104-510-2"></a>
**(2)**  The time of the event is just after the entity becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group.

<a id="s104-510-3"></a>
**(3)**  For the head company core purposes mentioned in subsection 701-1(2), the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) makes a capital gain equal to the excess.
