# 104-165  Exception for individuals

Choosing to disregard making a gain or loss

<a id="s104-165-2"></a>
**(2)**  If you are an individual, you can choose to disregard making a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or a [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) from all [[*CGT assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) covered by [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) I1.

<a id="s104-165-3"></a>
**(3)**  If you do so choose, each of those assets is taken to be [[*taxable Australian property*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property) until the earlier of: 214                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 CGT events Division 104 Section 104-170

> <a id="s104-165-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happening in relation to the asset, if the CGT event involves you ceasing to own the asset;

> <a id="s104-165-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you again becoming an Australian resident.

> **Note:** If you are an individual who was in Australia on 6 April 2006, and you remain an Australian resident from that day until you stop being one, and you were an Australian resident for less than 5 years during the 10 years before you stopped being one, see section 104-166 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997.
