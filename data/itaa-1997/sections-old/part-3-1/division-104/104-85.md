# 104-85  Disposal to beneficiary to end capital interest: CGT event E7


<a id="s104-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  CGT event E7 happens if the trustee of a trust (except a unit trust or a trust to which Division 128 applies) *disposes of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) of the trust to a beneficiary in satisfaction of the beneficiary’s interest, or part of it, in the trust capital.

> **Note:** Division 128 deals with the effect of death.

<a id="s104-85-2"></a>
**(2)**  The time of the event is when the disposal occurs. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                              189 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Trustee makes a capital gain or loss

<a id="s104-85-3"></a>
**(3)**  The trustee makes a capital gain if the *market value of the asset (at the time of the disposal) is more than its [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base. It makes a capital loss if that market value is less than the asset’s *reduced cost base. Exception for trustee

<a id="s104-85-4"></a>
**(4)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) the trustee makes is disregarded if it *acquired the asset before 20 September 1985. Beneficiary makes a capital gain or loss

<a id="s104-85-5"></a>
**(5)**  The beneficiary makes a capital gain if the *market value of the asset (at the time of the disposal) is more than the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base of the interest, or the part of it, being satisfied. The beneficiary makes a capital loss if that market value is less than the *reduced cost base of that interest or part. Exceptions for beneficiary

<a id="s104-85-6"></a>
**(6)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) the beneficiary makes is disregarded if:

> <a id="s104-85-6-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the beneficiary *acquired the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that is the interest (except by way of an assignment from another entity) for no expenditure; or

> <a id="s104-85-6-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the beneficiary acquired it before 20 September 1985; or

> <a id="s104-85-6-c"></a>
> **(c)**  all or part of the capital gain or capital loss the trustee makes from the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) is disregarded under Subdivision 118-B (about main residence). Expenditure can include giving property: see section 103-5.

> **Note:** For provisions affecting the application of Subdivision 118-B to the trustee, see sections 118-215 to 118-230.
