# 104-160  Individual or company stops being an Australian resident: CGT event I1


<a id="s104-160-1"></a>
**(1)**  CGT event I1 happens if you stop being an Australian resident. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            213 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

<a id="s104-160-2"></a>
**(2)**  The time of the event is when you stop being one.

<a id="s104-160-3"></a>
**(3)**  You need to work out if you have made a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or a [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) for each [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that you owned just before the time of the event, except one that is [[*taxable Australian property*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property):

> <a id="s104-160-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  covered by item 1 or 3 of the table in section 855-15; or

> <a id="s104-160-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  covered by item 4 of that table because it is an option or right to [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) covered by item 1 or 3 of that table.

<a id="s104-160-4"></a>
**(4)**  You make a capital gain if the *market value of the asset (at the time of the event) is more than its [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base. You make a capital loss if that market value is less than the asset’s *reduced cost base. (4A) If the asset is an *indirect Australian real property interest, or an option or right to acquire such an interest, this Part and Part 3-3 apply to the asset as if the first element of the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base and *reduced cost base of the asset (just after the time of the event) were its *market value at the time of the event. (4B) Subsection (4A) does not apply if the [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make is disregarded under subsection (5) or (6), or subsection 104-165(2). Exceptions

<a id="s104-160-5"></a>
**(5)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make is disregarded if you *acquired the asset before 20 September 1985.
