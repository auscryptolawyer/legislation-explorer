# 116-85  Section 47A of 1936 Act applying to rolled-over asset


<a id="s116-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  You reduce the [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) that happens in relation to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) you have if the conditions in this table are satisfied. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            407 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Conditions for reduction Item      Condition 1         You must have *acquired the asset from a company or [[*CFC*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cfc)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cfc) 2         Either:

> <a id="s116-85-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the company obtained a roll-over for the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) that resulted in your *acquisition of the asset; or

> <a id="s116-85-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the [[*CFC*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cfc)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cfc) obtained a roll-over for that event in applying Division 7 of Part X of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 for the purpose of working out the [[*attributable income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-attributable-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-attributable-income) of a company in relation to any entity except a roll-over under Subdivision 124-J (about Crown leases), 124-K (about depreciating assets) or 124-L (about prospecting and mining entitlements) 3         The company or [[*CFC*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cfc)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cfc) is taken, under section 47A of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936, to have paid you a dividend in relation to that event and some or all of the dividend is included in your assessable income under section 44 of that Act

> **Note:** For roll-overs: see Divisions 122, 124 and 126.

<a id="s116-85-2"></a>
**(2)**  The reduction is the lesser of:

> <a id="s116-85-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the amount of the dividend; and

> <a id="s116-85-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount of any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) that, apart from the roll-over, the company or [[*CFC*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cfc)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cfc) would have made from the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) if its [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) from the event had been the asset’s *market value (at the time of the event).

> **Note:** This section is disregarded in calculating the attributable income of a CFC: see section 410 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936.
