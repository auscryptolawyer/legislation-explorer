# 116-105  Conservation covenants

If [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) D4 happens because you enter into a [[*conservation covenant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-conservation-covenant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-conservation-covenant) over land you own and you can deduct an amount under 410                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Capital proceeds Division 116 Section 116-110 Division 31 because you enter into the covenant, the [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) from the event are the amount you can deduct.

> **Note:** To get a deduction under Division 31, you must not receive money, property or other material benefit for entering into the covenant.
