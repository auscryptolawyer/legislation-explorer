# 116-115  Farm-in farm-out arrangements


<a id="s116-115-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:

> <a id="s116-115-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) A1 is the *disposal of part of your interest in a *mining, quarrying or prospecting right; and

> <a id="s116-115-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the part is disposed of under a [[*farm-in farm-out arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-farm-in-farm-out-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-farm-in-farm-out-arrangement); and

> <a id="s116-115-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  you have received an [[*exploration benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-benefit) in respect of the event happening; in working out the [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) for the CGT event, treat as zero the *market value of the exploration benefit.

<a id="s116-115-2"></a>
**(2)**  If:

> <a id="s116-115-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) C2 arises as a result of an [[*exploration benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-benefit) being provided to you; and

> <a id="s116-115-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the exploration benefit is provided under a [[*farm-in farm-out arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-farm-in-farm-out-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-farm-in-farm-out-arrangement); in working out the [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) for the CGT event, treat as zero the *market value of the exploration benefit.
