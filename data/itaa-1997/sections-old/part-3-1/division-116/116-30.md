# 116-30  Market value substitution rule: modification 1

No capital proceeds

<a id="s116-30-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you received no [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event), you are taken to have received the *market value of the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that is the subject of the event. (The market value is worked out as at the time of the event.)

> *[[*Example*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example):** You give a CGT asset to another entity. You are taken to have received the market value of the CGT asset. There are capital proceeds

<a id="s116-30-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*capital proceeds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-proceeds) from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) are replaced with the *market value of the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that is the subject of the event if:

> <a id="s116-30-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  some or all of those proceeds cannot be valued; or

> <a id="s116-30-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  those capital proceeds are more or less than the market value of the asset and:

> > <a id="s116-30-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you and the entity that *acquired the asset from you did not deal with each other at *arm’s length in connection with the event; or

> > <a id="s116-30-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the CGT event is CGT event C2 (about cancellation, surrender and similar endings). (The market value is worked out as at the time of the event.) (2A) Subsection (2) does not apply if there is a partial roll-over for the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) because of section 124-150.
