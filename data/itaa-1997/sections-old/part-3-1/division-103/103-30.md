# 103-30  Reduction of cost base etc. by net input tax credits

Reduce the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base and [[*reduced cost base of a *CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-reduced-cost-base-of-a-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-reduced-cost-base-of-a-cgt-asset), and any other amount that could be involved in the calculation of an entity’s [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss), by the amount of any [[*net input tax credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-input-tax-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-input-tax-credit) of the entity in relation to that amount.

> *[[*Example*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example):** The other amount could be expenditure in the case of some CGT events (see, for example, CGT event D1).
