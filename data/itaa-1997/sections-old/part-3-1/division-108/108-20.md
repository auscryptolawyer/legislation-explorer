# 108-20  Losses from personal use assets must be disregarded


<a id="s108-20-1"></a>
**(1)**  In working out your [[*net capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain) or [[*net capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-loss) for the income year, any [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make from a [[*personal use asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-use-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-use-asset) is disregarded.

<a id="s108-20-2"></a>
**(2)**  A personal use asset is:

> <a id="s108-20-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) (except a [[*collectable*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-collectable)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-collectable)) that is used or kept mainly for your (or your [[*associate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate)’s) personal use or enjoyment; or

> <a id="s108-20-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an option or right to [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) of that kind; or

> <a id="s108-20-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  a debt arising from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) in which the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) the subject of the event was one covered by paragraph (a); or

> <a id="s108-20-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  a debt arising other than:

> > <a id="s108-20-2-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  in the course of gaining or producing your assessable income; or

> > <a id="s108-20-2-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  from your carrying on a *business.

> **Note:** There is an exemption for a personal use asset you acquire for $10,000 or less: see section 118-10.

> **Note:** A debt arising from a CGT event involving a CGT asset kept mainly for your personal use and enjoyment is a personal use asset to prevent any loss arising from the debt being a normal capital loss.

<a id="s108-20-3"></a>
**(3)**  A personal use asset does not include land, a [[*stratum unit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-stratum-unit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-stratum-unit) or a building or structure that is taken to be a separate [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) because of Subdivision 108-D.
