# 108-25  Sets of personal use assets


<a id="s108-25-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section sets out what happens if: 262                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 CGT assets Division 108 Section 108-30

> <a id="s108-25-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you own [[*personal use assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-use-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-use-asset) that are a set; and

> <a id="s108-25-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  they would ordinarily be *disposed of as a set; and

> <a id="s108-25-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  you dispose of them in one or more transactions for the purpose of trying to obtain the exemption in section 118-10.

<a id="s108-25-2"></a>
**(2)**  The set of [[*personal use assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-use-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-use-asset) is taken to be a single [[*personal use asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-use-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-use-asset) and each of your *disposals is a disposal of part of that asset.
