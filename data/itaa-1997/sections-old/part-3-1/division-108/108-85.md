# 108-85  Meaning of improvement threshold


<a id="s108-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  The improvement threshold for the 1997-98 income year is $89,992.

<a id="s108-85-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*improvement threshold*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-improvement-threshold)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-improvement-threshold) is indexed annually.

> **Note:** Subdivision 960-M shows you how to index amounts.

<a id="s108-85-3"></a>
**(3)**  The Commissioner must publish before the beginning of each [[*financial year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year) the [[*improvement threshold*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-improvement-threshold)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-improvement-threshold) for that year. 270                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Acquisition of CGT assets Division 109 Section 109-1
