# 118-230  Application of CGT events E5 and E7 in relation to main residence exemption and special disability trusts

If [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) E5 or E7 happens in relation to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) held by a trust that is or has been a *special disability trust, treat the lists of CGT events in paragraphs 118-110(2)(a) and 118-195(2)(a) as including a reference to that CGT event. Compulsory acquisitions of adjacent land only
