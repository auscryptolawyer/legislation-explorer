# 118-505  Exemption for certain foreign venture capital


<a id="s118-505-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) is disregarded if it is made from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happening in relation to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that is [[*venture capital equity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-equity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-equity) where the asset:

> <a id="s118-505-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  was *acquired by a [[*venture capital entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-entity); and

> <a id="s118-505-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  at the time of the CGT event:

> > <a id="s118-505-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  was owned by that entity; and

> > <a id="s118-505-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  had been owned by that entity for at least 12 months.

<a id="s118-505-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*venture capital entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-entity) must be registered under Part 7A of the Pooled Development Funds Act 1992 at the time of the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event). 520                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Exemptions Division 118 Section 118-510
