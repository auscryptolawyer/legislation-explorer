# 118-227  Amount of exemption available after the principal beneficiary’s death—cost base and reduced cost base


<a id="s118-227-1"></a>
**(1)**  If section 118-220 applies to you and:

> <a id="s118-227-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the applicable [[*dwelling*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling) was the deceased’s main residence just before the deceased’s death; and

> <a id="s118-227-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  that dwelling was not then being used for the [[*purpose of producing assessable income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income); and

> <a id="s118-227-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the trust referred to in paragraph 118-220(b) was then a *special disability trust; and

> <a id="s118-227-1-ca"></a>
> **(ca)**  the deceased was not an [[*excluded foreign resident*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-excluded-foreign-resident)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-excluded-foreign-resident) just before the deceased’s death; then:

> <a id="s118-227-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the first element of the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base, in your hands, is the CGT asset’s *market value just before the deceased’s death; and

> <a id="s118-227-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the first element of the CGT asset’s *reduced cost base, in your hands, is worked out similarly.

<a id="s118-227-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, if section 118-220 applies to you as trustee of an implied trust arising because of the deceased’s death, but subsection (1) does not, then:

> <a id="s118-227-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the first element of the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base, in your hands, is the CGT asset’s cost base just before the deceased’s death; and

> <a id="s118-227-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the first element of the CGT asset’s *reduced cost base, in your hands, is worked out similarly.

<a id="s118-227-3"></a>
**(3)**  If section 118-222 applies to you:

> <a id="s118-227-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the first element of the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base, in your hands, is the CGT asset’s cost base just before the earlier [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happened that resulted in you *acquiring the CGT asset or your [[*ownership interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) in it; and

> <a id="s118-227-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the first element of the CGT asset’s *reduced cost base, in your hands, is worked out similarly. 466                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Exemptions Division 118 Section 118-230
