# 118-320  Segregated current pension assets of a complying superannuation entity


<a id="s118-320-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) that a *complying superannuation entity makes from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happening in relation to a *segregated current pension asset is disregarded.

<a id="s118-320-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, subsection (1) does not apply to a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) if the capital gain would, if it were an amount of [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income) or [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income) received by the [[*complying superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-fund), be *non-arm’s length income. 476                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Exemptions Division 118 Section 118-350
