# 118-21  Carried interests

CGT events relating to carried interests not to be treated as income

<a id="s118-21-1"></a>
**(1)**  The modifications in subsections (2) and (3) apply if [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) K9 happens in relation to your entitlement to receive a payment of the [[*carried interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-carried-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-carried-interest) of a [[*general partner*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-partner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-partner) in a [[*VCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp), an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp) or an [[*AFOF*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-afof)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-afof) or a [[*limited partner*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-limited-partner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-limited-partner) in a [[*VCMP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vcmp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vcmp).

<a id="s118-21-2"></a>
**(2)**  These provisions do not apply to the CGT event:

> <a id="s118-21-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  sections 6-5 (about [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)), 8-1 (about amounts you can deduct), 15-15 and 25-40 (about profit-making undertakings or plans) and 118-20 (reducing capital gains if amount otherwise assessable);

> <a id="s118-21-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  sections 25A and 52 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (about profit-making undertakings or schemes).

<a id="s118-21-3"></a>
**(3)**  Section 6-10 (about [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)) does not apply to the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) except so far as that section applies in relation to section 102-5 (about net capital gains).
