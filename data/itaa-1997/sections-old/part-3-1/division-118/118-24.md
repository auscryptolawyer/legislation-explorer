# 118-24  Depreciating assets


<a id="s118-24-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) (that is also a [[*balancing adjustment event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)) that happens to a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) is disregarded if the asset was:

> <a id="s118-24-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an asset you [[*held*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held); or 422                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Exemptions Division 118 Section 118-25

> <a id="s118-24-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if you are a partner, an asset of the partnership; or

> <a id="s118-24-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if you are absolutely entitled to the asset as against the trustee of a trust (disregarding any legal disability), an asset of the trustee; where the decline in value of the asset was worked out under Division 40 (including that Division as it applies under Division 355), or the deduction for the asset was calculated under Division 328, or would have been if the asset had been used.

<a id="s118-24-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, subsection (1) does not apply to:

> <a id="s118-24-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make from [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) J2 or [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) K7 happening; or

> <a id="s118-24-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) for which you or another entity has deducted or can deduct amounts under Subdivision 40-F or 40-G.
