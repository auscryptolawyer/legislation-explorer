# 118-210  Trustee acquiring dwelling under will


<a id="s118-210-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if you are the trustee of a deceased estate and, under the deceased’s will, you [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) an [[*ownership interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) in a [[*dwelling*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling) for occupation by an individual.

<a id="s118-210-2"></a>
**(2)**  If a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happens to the interest in relation to the individual and you receive no money or property for it:

> <a id="s118-210-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make from the event is disregarded; and 460                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Exemptions Division 118 Section 118-210

> <a id="s118-210-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the first element of the [[*dwelling*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base and *reduced cost base in the hands of the individual is its cost base and reduced cost base in your hands at the time of the event; and

> <a id="s118-210-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the individual is taken to have *acquired it when you did.

<a id="s118-210-3"></a>
**(3)**  If:

> <a id="s118-210-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you receive money or property for the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happening or the event happens in relation to another entity; and

> <a id="s118-210-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the dwelling was the main residence of the individual from the time you *acquired the interest until the time of the event; you do not make a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) from the CGT event.

<a id="s118-210-4"></a>
**(4)**  However, if the [[*dwelling*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling) was the main residence of the individual during part only of that period, you make a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) worked out using the formula: Non-main residence days CG or CL amount ´ Days in that period where: CG or CL amount is the [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you would have made from the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) apart from this Subdivision. non-main residence days is the number of days in that period when the [[*dwelling*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling) was not the individual’s main residence.

<a id="s118-210-5"></a>
**(5)**  Only these [[*CGT events*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) are relevant:

> <a id="s118-210-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  CGT events A1, B1, C1, C2, E1, E2, E5, F2, K3, K4 and K6 (except one involving the forfeiting of a deposit); and

> <a id="s118-210-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a CGT event that involves the forfeiting of a deposit as part of an uninterrupted sequence of transactions ending in one of the events specified in paragraph (a) subsequently happening.

> **Note:** The full list of CGT events is in section 104-5.

<a id="s118-210-6"></a>
**(6)**  However, this section does not apply if, just before the deceased’s death, the deceased was an [[*excluded foreign resident*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-excluded-foreign-resident)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-excluded-foreign-resident).
