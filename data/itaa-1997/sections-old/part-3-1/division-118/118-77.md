# 118-77  Native title and rights to native title benefits


<a id="s118-77-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make is disregarded if:

> <a id="s118-77-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are an [[*Indigenous person*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indigenous-person)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indigenous-person) or an [[*Indigenous holding entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indigenous-holding-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indigenous-holding-entity); and

> <a id="s118-77-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you make the gain or loss because one of the following things happens in relation to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) mentioned in subsection (2):

> > <a id="s118-77-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you transfer the CGT asset to one or more entities that are either Indigenous persons or Indigenous holding entities;

> > <a id="s118-77-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  you create a trust, that is an Indigenous holding entity, over the CGT asset;

> > <a id="s118-77-1-b-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  your ownership of the CGT asset ends, resulting in [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) C2 happening in relation to the CGT asset.

<a id="s118-77-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*CGT assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) are as follows:

> <a id="s118-77-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  *native title;

> <a id="s118-77-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the right to be provided with a [[*native title benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-native-title-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-native-title-benefit).

> **Note:** Paragraph (a) does not require a determination of native title under the Native Title Act 1993. 432                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Exemptions Division 118 Section 118-80 Boat capital gains
