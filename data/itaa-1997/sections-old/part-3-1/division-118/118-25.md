# 118-25  Trading stock


<a id="s118-25-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make from a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) is disregarded if, at the time of the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event), the asset is:

> <a id="s118-25-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  your [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock); or

> <a id="s118-25-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if you are a partner, trading stock of the partnership; or

> <a id="s118-25-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if you are absolutely entitled to the asset as against the trustee of a trust (disregarding any legal disability), trading stock of the trustee.

<a id="s118-25-2"></a>
**(2)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make in these circumstances is disregarded:

> <a id="s118-25-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you start holding as [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock) a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) you already own but do not hold as trading stock; and

> <a id="s118-25-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you elect under paragraph 70-30(1)(a) to be treated as having sold the asset for its cost (worked out under that section).

> **Note:** Paragraph 70-30(1)(a) allows you to elect the cost of the asset, or its market value, just before it became trading stock.
