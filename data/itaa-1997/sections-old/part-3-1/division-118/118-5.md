# 118-5  Cars, motor cycles and valour decorations

A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make from any of these [[*CGT assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) is disregarded:

> <a id="s118-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car), motor cycle or similar vehicle;

> <a id="s118-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a decoration awarded for valour or brave conduct (unless you paid money or gave any other property for it).
