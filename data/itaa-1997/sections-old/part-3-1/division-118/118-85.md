# 118-85  Special disability trusts


<a id="s118-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make is disregarded if you make it from transferring a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) for no consideration to:

> <a id="s118-85-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a *special disability trust; or

> <a id="s118-85-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a trust that becomes a special disability trust as soon as practicable after the transfer.

<a id="s118-85-2"></a>
**(2)**  In working out whether the transfer was for consideration, disregard any interest in the trust.
