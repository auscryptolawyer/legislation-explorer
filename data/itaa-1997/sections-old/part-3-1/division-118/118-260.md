# 118-260  Partial exemption rules


<a id="s118-260-1"></a>
**(1)**  If section 118-245 applies to a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event), the amount of the [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) that you would have made apart from this section from the CGT event is increased by an amount that is reasonable having regard to the following:

> <a id="s118-260-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the extent that the [[*dwelling*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling) was not a main residence for the relevant period;

> <a id="s118-260-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the extent that the dwelling was used for the [[*purpose of producing assessable income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income) during the relevant period.

<a id="s118-260-2"></a>
**(2)**  In determining what is a reasonable increase, have regard to the principles in this Subdivision applicable to [[*CGT events*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happening in relation to a [[*dwelling*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling) or your [[*ownership interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) in it.
