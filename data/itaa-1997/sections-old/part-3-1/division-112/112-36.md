# 112-36  Acquisitions of assets involving look-through earnout rights

Consequences for cost base and reduced cost base

<a id="s112-36-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) because an entity *disposes of the CGT asset to you, and that disposal causes [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) A1 (the first CGT event) to happen:

> <a id="s112-36-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  neither the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base nor the *reduced cost base of the CGT asset includes the value of any [[*look-through earnout right*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right) relating to the CGT asset and the acquisition; and 314                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Modifications to cost base and reduced cost base Division 112 Section 112-36

> <a id="s112-36-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  include in the first element of the CGT asset’s cost base and reduced cost base any [[*financial benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) that you provide under such a look-through earnout right; and

> <a id="s112-36-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  reduce the first element of the CGT asset’s cost base and reduced cost base by an amount equal to the amount of any financial benefit that you receive under such a look-through earnout right. Remaking choices affected by the look-through earnout right

<a id="s112-36-2"></a>
**(2)**  Despite section 103-25, you may remake any choice you made under this Part or Part 3-3 for a later [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) involving the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) if:

> <a id="s112-36-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  after the later CGT event, you provide or receive a [[*financial benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) under such a [[*look-through earnout right*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right); and

> <a id="s112-36-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you remake the choice at or before the time you are required to lodge your [[*income tax return*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return) for the income year in which the financial benefit is provided or received. Amending assessments affected by the look-through earnout right

<a id="s112-36-3"></a>
**(3)**  The Commissioner may amend an assessment of a [[*tax-related liability*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-related-liability)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-related-liability) if:

> <a id="s112-36-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity provides or receives a [[*financial benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) under such a [[*look-through earnout right*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right); and

> <a id="s112-36-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount of the tax-related liability:

> > <a id="s112-36-3-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  depends on that entity’s taxable income for an income year in which a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event), involving the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), happens after the first CGT event but before the financial benefit is provided or received; or

> > <a id="s112-36-3-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  is otherwise affected by that right’s character as a look-through earnout right; and

> <a id="s112-36-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the Commissioner makes the amendment before the end of the 4-year period starting at the end of the income year in which the last possible financial benefit becomes or could become due under the look-through earnout right. The tax-related liability need not be a liability of that entity. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            315 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> **Note:** Subparagraph (b)(ii) covers changes to the amount of that tax-related liability that happen directly or indirectly because of subsection (1) or (2).

<a id="s112-36-4"></a>
**(4)**  If at a particular time a right is taken never to have been a [[*look-through earnout right*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right) because of subsection 118-565(2), the Commissioner may amend an assessment of a [[*tax-related liability*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-related-liability)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-related-liability) for up to 4 years after that time if:

> <a id="s112-36-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity provides or receives a [[*financial benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) under the right; and

> <a id="s112-36-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount of the tax-related liability:

> > <a id="s112-36-4-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  depends on that entity’s taxable income for an income year in which a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event), involving the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), happens after the first CGT event but before the financial benefit is provided or received; or

> > <a id="s112-36-4-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  was otherwise affected by that right’s character as a look-through earnout right before subsection 118-565(2) applied. The tax-related liability need not be a liability of that entity.

> **Note:** Subsection 118-565(2) restricts look-through earnout rights to rights to financial benefits over a period not exceeding 5 years from the end of the income year in which the first CGT event happens.

<a id="s112-36-5"></a>
**(5)**  If, after providing or receiving a [[*financial benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-benefit) under a right referred to in subsection (3) or (4):

> <a id="s112-36-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are dissatisfied with an assessment referred to in that subsection; and

> <a id="s112-36-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the Commissioner notifies you that the Commissioner has decided under that subsection not to amend your assessment; you may object against the assessment, to the extent that it does not take account of that right’s character (as a [[*look-through earnout right*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-look-through-earnout-right) or not such a right), in the manner set out in Part IVC of the Taxation Administration Act 1953. 316                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Modifications to cost base and reduced cost base Division 112 Section 112-37
