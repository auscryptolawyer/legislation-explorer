# 110-40  Assets acquired before 7.30 pm on 13 May 1997


<a id="s110-40-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section prevents some expenditure from forming part of one or more elements of the [[*cost base of a *CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost-base-of-a-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost-base-of-a-cgt-asset) *acquired at or before 7.30 pm, by legal time in the Australian Capital Territory, on 13 May 1997. (The expenditure mentioned in this section can include giving property: see section 103-5.)

> **Note:** For the cost base of a partnership interest you acquire at or before that time, see section 110-43. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             295 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

<a id="s110-40-2"></a>
**(2)**  Expenditure does not form part of the second or third element of the cost base to the extent that you have deducted or can deduct it.

<a id="s110-40-3"></a>
**(3)**  Expenditure does not form part of any element of the cost base to the extent of any amount you have received as [[*recoupment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment) of it, except so far as the amount is included in your assessable income.

<a id="s110-40-4"></a>
**(4)**  Subsection (2) does not apply in relation to amounts that you have deducted or can deduct under Division 243.
