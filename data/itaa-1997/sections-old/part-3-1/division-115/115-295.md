# 115-295  Maintaining records

A [[*listed investment company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-listed-investment-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-listed-investment-company) must maintain records showing the balance of its [[*LIC capital gains*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-lic-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-lic-capital-gain) available for distribution.
