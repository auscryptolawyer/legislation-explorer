# 115-15  Discount capital gain must be made after 21 September 1999

To be a [[*discount capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount-capital-gain), the [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) must result from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happening after 11.45 am (by legal time in the Australian Capital Territory) on 21 September 1999.
