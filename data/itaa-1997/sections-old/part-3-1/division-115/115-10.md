# 115-10  Who can make a discount capital gain?

To be a [[*discount capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount-capital-gain), the [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) must be made by:

> <a id="s115-10-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an individual; or

> <a id="s115-10-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a *complying superannuation entity; or 352                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 Discount capital gains and trusts’ net capital gains Division 115 Section 115-15

> <a id="s115-10-c"></a>
> **(c)**  a trust; or

> <a id="s115-10-d"></a>
> **(d)**  a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) in relation to a [[*discount capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount-capital-gain) from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) in respect of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that is a [[*complying superannuation asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset).
