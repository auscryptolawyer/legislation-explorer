# 115-25  Discount capital gain must be on asset acquired at least 12 months before


<a id="s115-25-1"></a>
**(1)**  To be a [[*discount capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount-capital-gain), the [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) must result from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happening to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that was *acquired by the entity making the capital gain at least 12 months before the CGT event.

> **Note:** Even if the capital gain results from a CGT event happening at least a year after the CGT asset was acquired, the gain may not be a discount capital gain, depending on the cause of the CGT event (see section 115-40) and the nature of the asset (see sections 115-45 and 115-50).

> **Note:** Section 115-30 or 115-34 may affect the time when the entity is treated as having acquired the CGT asset.
