# 315-150  Roll-over where assets transferred to lost policy holder


<a id="s315-150-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies in relation to a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) if:

> <a id="s315-150-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the CGT event happens in relation to an asset held by the trustee of a lost policy holders trust on behalf of a lost policy holder; and

> <a id="s315-150-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the CGT event happens because the lost policy holder (or, if the lost policy holder has died, the *legal personal representative of the lost policy holder or a beneficiary in the estate of the lost policy holder) either:

> > <a id="s315-150-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  is transferred the asset by the trustee; or

> > <a id="s315-150-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  becomes absolutely entitled to the asset.

> **Note:** The asset may be a demutualisation asset, or some other asset. Consequence for trustee

<a id="s315-150-2"></a>
**(2)**  Disregard a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) the trustee makes from the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event). Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                              9 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Consequence for lost policy holder

<a id="s315-150-3"></a>
**(3)**  The [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base of the asset in the hands of the trustee of the lost policy holders trust just before the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) becomes the first element of the cost base and *reduced cost base of the asset in the hands of the lost policy holder, *legal personal representative or beneficiary.

<a id="s315-150-4"></a>
**(4)**  The lost policy holder, *legal personal representative or beneficiary is taken to have *acquired the asset when the trustee of the lost policy holders trust acquired it.
