# 316-275  Franking credits to negate franking debits from refunds of tax paid before demutualisation


<a id="s316-275-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if a [[*franking debit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit) arises in the [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) of the *friendly society or a *wholly-owned subsidiary of the society because, on or after the demutualisation resolution day identified under subsection 316-70(4), the society or subsidiary [[*receives a refund of income tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-receives-a-refund-of-income-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-receives-a-refund-of-income-tax) that is wholly or partly attributable to a period before that day.

<a id="s316-275-2"></a>
**(2)**  A [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) arises in that account.

<a id="s316-275-3"></a>
**(3)**  The amount of the [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) is so much of the [[*franking debit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit) as is attributable to the period before that day.

<a id="s316-275-4"></a>
**(4)**  The [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) arises at the same time as the [[*franking debit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit) arises.
