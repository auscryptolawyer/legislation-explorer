# 832-560  Neutralising amount


<a id="s832-560-1"></a>
**(1)**  The neutralising amount for a [[*deducting hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch) is worked out by:

> <a id="s832-560-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  starting with the lesser of the amounts of each deduction or [[*foreign income tax deduction*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax-deduction)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax-deduction) to which the amount gives rise; and

> <a id="s832-560-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  reducing (but not below nil) the result from paragraph (a) by the amount of any [[*dual inclusion income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dual-inclusion-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dual-inclusion-income) that is available to be applied in working out the neutralising amount. Australian deduction—inclusions must be in Australia and in the other deducting country

<a id="s832-560-2"></a>
**(2)**  An amount of [[*dual inclusion income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dual-inclusion-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dual-inclusion-income) is available to be applied to reduce the [[*neutralising amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-neutralising-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-neutralising-amount) for a [[*deducting hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch) to which section 832-530 applies if:

> <a id="s832-560-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*deducting hybrid*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid) is eligible to apply the amount (see subsection 832-680(7)); and

> <a id="s832-560-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount is [[*subject to Australian income tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subject-to-australian-income-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subject-to-australian-income-tax) for the purposes of subsection 832-680(1) in the income year mentioned in subsection 832-530(1); and

> <a id="s832-560-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the amount is [[*subject to foreign income tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subject-to-foreign-income-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subject-to-foreign-income-tax) for the purposes of subsection 832-680(1) in the foreign country in which the [[*foreign income tax deduction*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax-deduction)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax-deduction) arose.

> **Note:** Section 832-680 modifies the meanings of subject to Australian income tax and subject to foreign income tax for the purpose of working out dual inclusion income. <a id="s832-560-3"></a>
**(3)**  An amount of [[*dual inclusion income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dual-inclusion-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dual-inclusion-income) is available to be applied to reduce the [[*neutralising amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-neutralising-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-neutralising-amount) for a [[*deducting hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch) that is an [[*offshore hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-offshore-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-offshore-hybrid-mismatch) if:

> <a id="s832-560-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*deducting hybrid*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid) is eligible to apply the amount (see subsection 832-680(7)); and

> <a id="s832-560-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount is [[*subject to foreign income tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subject-to-foreign-income-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subject-to-foreign-income-tax) for the purposes of subsection 832-680(1) in the foreign country in which one of the [[*foreign income tax deductions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax-deduction)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax-deduction) arose, and in the same *foreign tax period; and

> <a id="s832-560-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the amount is also subject to foreign income tax for the purposes of subsection 832-680(1) in the foreign country in which another of the foreign income tax deductions arose.
