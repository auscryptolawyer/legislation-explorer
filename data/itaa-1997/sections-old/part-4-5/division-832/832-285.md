# 832-285  Deduction not allowable—Australian primary response


<a id="s832-285-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies to an entity if:

> <a id="s832-285-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  apart from this section, the entity would be entitled to a deduction in an income year in respect of a payment; and 556                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Hybrid mismatch rules Division 832 Section 832-290

> <a id="s832-285-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the deduction is the [[*deduction component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-component) of a [[*hybrid payer mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch) to which the payment gives rise.

<a id="s832-285-2"></a>
**(2)**  So much of the deduction as does not exceed the [[*neutralising amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-neutralising-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-neutralising-amount) for the [[*hybrid payer mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch) is not allowable as a deduction.

> **Note:** The neutralising amount is worked out under section 832-330.
