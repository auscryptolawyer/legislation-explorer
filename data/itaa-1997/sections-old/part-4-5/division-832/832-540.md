# 832-540  When a deducting hybrid mismatch is an offshore hybrid mismatch


<a id="s832-540-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*deducting hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch) is an offshore hybrid mismatch if: 582                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Hybrid mismatch rules Division 832 Section 832-545

> <a id="s832-540-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the only [[*deduction components*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-component) of the mismatch are [[*foreign income tax deductions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax-deduction)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax-deduction); and

> <a id="s832-540-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the mismatch is not covered by [[*foreign hybrid mismatch rules*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid-mismatch-rules)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid-mismatch-rules) that correspond to this Subdivision, or by a law that has substantially the same effect as foreign hybrid mismatch rules that correspond to this Subdivision, in any country in which a foreign income tax deduction arose.

> **Note:** An offshore hybrid mismatch might give rise to an imported hybrid mismatch: see Subdivision 832-H.

<a id="s832-540-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount of the [[*offshore hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-offshore-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-offshore-hybrid-mismatch) is the [[*neutralising amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-neutralising-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-neutralising-amount) for the [[*deducting hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch).
