# 832-305  When a payment gives rise to a hybrid payer mismatch


<a id="s832-305-1"></a>
**(1)**  A payment gives rise to a hybrid payer mismatch if:

> <a id="s832-305-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the payment gives rise to a [[*hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-mismatch) under section 832-310; and 558                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Hybrid mismatch rules Division 832 Section 832-310

> <a id="s832-305-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  subsection (3) or (4) applies.

<a id="s832-305-2"></a>
**(2)**  The deduction component of the [[*hybrid payer mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch) is the [[*deduction component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-component) of the [[*deduction*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction)/non-inclusion mismatch mentioned in section 832-310. Control group

<a id="s832-305-3"></a>
**(3)**  This subsection applies if the following entities are in the same [[*Division 832 control group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-division-832-control-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-division-832-control-group):

> <a id="s832-305-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*hybrid payer*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer);

> <a id="s832-305-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  each entity that is a [[*liable entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-liable-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-liable-entity) in respect of the income or profits of the hybrid payer.

> **Note:** For the meaning of Division 832 control group, see section 832-205. Structured arrangement

<a id="s832-305-4"></a>
**(4)**  This subsection applies if the payment is made under a [[*structured arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-structured-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-structured-arrangement).

> **Note:** For the meaning of structured arrangement, see section 832-210.
