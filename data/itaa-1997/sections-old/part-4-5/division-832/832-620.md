# 832-620  Hybrid mismatch


<a id="s832-620-1"></a>
**(1)**  A payment gives rise to a hybrid mismatch if the payment is an *importing payment in relation to an [[*offshore hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-offshore-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-offshore-hybrid-mismatch).

> **Note:** For the meaning of offshore hybrid mismatch see sections 832-195, 832-300, 832-390, 832-465, and 832-540. Ordering rule

<a id="s832-620-2"></a>
**(2)**  A payment does not give rise to a hybrid mismatch under this section if it gives rise to a [[*hybrid financial instrument mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-financial-instrument-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-financial-instrument-mismatch), a [[*hybrid payer mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch), a [[*reverse hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-reverse-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-reverse-hybrid-mismatch), a [[*branch hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-branch-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-branch-hybrid-mismatch) or a [[*deducting hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deducting-hybrid-mismatch).

> **Note:** However, for an imported hybrid mismatch to arise, a different payment must have given rise to an offshore hybrid mismatch that is of one of these kinds.
