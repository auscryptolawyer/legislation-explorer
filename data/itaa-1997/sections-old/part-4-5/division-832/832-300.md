# 832-300  When a hybrid payer mismatch is an offshore hybrid mismatch


<a id="s832-300-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*hybrid payer mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch) is an offshore hybrid mismatch if:

> <a id="s832-300-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*deduction component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-component) of the mismatch is a [[*foreign income tax deduction*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax-deduction)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax-deduction); and

> <a id="s832-300-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  no amount becomes [[*subject to Australian income tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subject-to-australian-income-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subject-to-australian-income-tax) as a result of the application of section 832-290 in relation to the mismatch; and

> <a id="s832-300-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the mismatch is not covered by [[*foreign hybrid mismatch rules*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid-mismatch-rules)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid-mismatch-rules) that correspond to this Subdivision, or by a law that has substantially the same effect as foreign hybrid mismatch rules that correspond to this Subdivision.

> **Note:** An offshore hybrid mismatch might give rise to an imported hybrid mismatch: see Subdivision 832-H.

<a id="s832-300-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount of the [[*offshore hybrid mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-offshore-hybrid-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-offshore-hybrid-mismatch) is the [[*neutralising amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-neutralising-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-neutralising-amount) for the [[*hybrid payer mismatch*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hybrid-payer-mismatch).
