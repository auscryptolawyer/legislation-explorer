# 768-910  Income derived by temporary resident


<a id="s768-910-1"></a>
**(1)**  The following are [[*non-assessable non-exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income):

> <a id="s768-910-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income) you *derive directly or indirectly from a source other than an [[*Australian source*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-source)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-source) if you are a [[*temporary resident*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-temporary-resident)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-temporary-resident) when you derive it; Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            157 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s768-910-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  your [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income) (other than a [[*net capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain)) from a source other than an Australian source if you are a temporary resident when you derive it. This subsection has effect subject to subsections (3) and (5).

> **Note:** A capital gain or loss you make may be disregarded under section 768-915.

<a id="s768-910-2"></a>
**(2)**  For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b):

> <a id="s768-910-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if you have statutory income because a particular circumstance occurs, you derive the statutory income at the time when the circumstance occurs; and

> <a id="s768-910-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if you have statutory income because a number of circumstances occur, you derive the statutory income at the time when the last of those circumstances occurs. Exception to subsection (1)

<a id="s768-910-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, the following are not [[*non-assessable non-exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income) under subsection (1):

> <a id="s768-910-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income) you *derive directly or indirectly from a source other than an [[*Australian source*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-source)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-source) to the extent that it is remuneration, for employment undertaken, or services provided, while you are a [[*temporary resident*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-temporary-resident)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-temporary-resident);

> <a id="s768-910-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  your [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income) (other than a [[*net capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain)) from a source other than an Australian source to the extent that it relates to employment undertaken, or services provided, while you are a temporary resident;

> <a id="s768-910-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  an amount included in your assessable income under Division 86.

> **Note:** This subsection only makes an amount not non-assessable non-exempt income under subsection (1).
