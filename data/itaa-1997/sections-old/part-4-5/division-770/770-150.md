# 770-150  Meaning of foreign DMT tax


<a id="s770-150-1"></a>
**(1)**  A tax is a foreign DMT tax if it is any of the following: 174                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Foreign income tax offsets Division 770 Section 770-190

> <a id="s770-150-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  tax that is payable under a [[*foreign law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law) and is a [[*Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-qualified-domestic-minimum-top-up-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-qualified-domestic-minimum-top-up-tax);

> <a id="s770-150-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  tax that is payable under a foreign law and would be a Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax if paragraph (c) of the definition of Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax in the *GloBE Rules were disregarded;

> <a id="s770-150-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  tax that is payable under a foreign law specified by regulations made for the purposes of this paragraph.

<a id="s770-150-2"></a>
**(2)**  If regulations made for the purposes of this subsection specify a provision of this Act and a tax that is payable under a foreign law, for the purposes of that provision, that tax is also a foreign DMT tax.
