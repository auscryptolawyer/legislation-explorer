# 880-115  Sovereign entity’s capital gain from membership interest etc.—gain disregarded

Disregard a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) of a [[*sovereign entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-sovereign-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-sovereign-entity) from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) that happens in relation to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) if:

> <a id="s880-115-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the sovereign entity is covered by section 880-125; and

> <a id="s880-115-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the CGT asset is a *membership interest, *non-share equity interest or *debt interest in another entity; and

> <a id="s880-115-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the requirements in paragraphs 880-105(1)(c), (d) and (e) would be satisfied, on the assumptions that:

> > <a id="s880-115-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the capital gain were an amount of [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income) or [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income); and

> > <a id="s880-115-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the amount mentioned in subparagraph (i) became ordinary income or statutory income of the sovereign entity immediately before the time the CGT event happened; and

> > <a id="s880-115-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  references in those paragraphs to the test entity were references to the other entity mentioned in paragraph (b) of this section.
