# 830-45  Partner’s revenue and net capital losses from foreign hybrid not to exceed partner’s loss exposure amount


<a id="s830-45-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies to a [[*limited partner*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-limited-partner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-limited-partner) in a [[*foreign hybrid*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid) in relation to an income year if the sum of the following amounts:

> <a id="s830-45-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  any amount (a foreign hybrid revenue loss amount) allowable to the partner as a deduction under subsection 92(2) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 in respect of a [[*partnership*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-partnership)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-partnership) loss of the foreign hybrid for the income year;

> <a id="s830-45-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  any [[*foreign hybrid net capital loss amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid-net-capital-loss-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid-net-capital-loss-amount) of the partner in respect of the foreign hybrid for the income year; exceeds the partner’s [[*loss exposure amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-exposure-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-exposure-amount) for the income year. Reduction in foreign hybrid revenue loss amount or foreign hybrid net capital loss amount

<a id="s830-45-2"></a>
**(2)**  If this section applies, the amount mentioned in paragraph (1)(a) or (b), or each of the amounts mentioned in those paragraphs, is reduced so that in total they equal the partner’s [[*loss exposure amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-exposure-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-exposure-amount). The partner must choose how much of the reduction is applied to each of the amounts. Effect of reducing foreign hybrid net capital loss amount

<a id="s830-45-3"></a>
**(3)**  If the partner’s [[*foreign hybrid net capital loss amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid-net-capital-loss-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid-net-capital-loss-amount) in respect of the [[*foreign hybrid*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid) for the income year is reduced under subsection (2), the partner’s [[*net capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain) or [[*net capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-loss) for the income year is worked out by assuming that the [[*capital gains*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) and *capital losses taken into account in working out the partner’s foreign hybrid net capital loss amount were instead a capital loss equal to the foreign hybrid net capital loss amount after the reduction.
