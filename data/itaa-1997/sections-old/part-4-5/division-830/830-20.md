# 830-20  Treatment of company as a partnership

If a company is a [[*foreign hybrid company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid-company) in relation to an income year, the [[*foreign hybrid*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid) tax provisions apply as if the company were a partnership, and for that purpose the following provisions of this Subdivision have effect.
