# 830-120  End of CFC’s last statutory accounting period

If:

> <a id="s830-120-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a taxpayer is a partner in an entity that becomes a [[*foreign hybrid*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid) in relation to an income year; and

> <a id="s830-120-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity was a [[*CFC*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cfc)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cfc) at the end of the taxpayer’s preceding income year; and

> <a id="s830-120-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the last [[*statutory accounting period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-accounting-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-accounting-period) of the CFC did not end at the end of the taxpayer’s preceding income year; and

> <a id="s830-120-d"></a>
> **(d)**  if it had so ended, the taxpayer would have been an [[*attributable taxpayer*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-attributable-taxpayer)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-attributable-taxpayer) in relation to the CFC; for the purposes of working out the [[*attributable income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-attributable-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-attributable-income) of the CFC for the taxpayer in respect of the last statutory accounting period of the CFC, that statutory accounting period ends at the end of the taxpayer’s preceding income year.
