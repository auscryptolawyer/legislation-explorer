# 830-110  No disposal of assets etc. on entity becoming or ceasing to be a foreign hybrid

To avoid doubt, the fact that an entity becomes or ceases to be a [[*foreign hybrid*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid) in relation to an income year does not cause:

> <a id="s830-110-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) to happen to any [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) consisting of:

> > <a id="s830-110-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  any [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share) or interest in the entity; or

> > <a id="s830-110-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  any interest in an asset of the entity; or

> <a id="s830-110-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a disposal or any other event to happen to any other asset consisting of such a share or interest. 526                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Foreign hybrids Division 830 Section 830-115
