# 830-55  Meaning of foreign hybrid net capital loss amount

If:

> <a id="s830-55-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the sum of a partner’s *capital losses from [[*CGT events*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happening during an income year in relation to a [[*foreign hybrid*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-hybrid) or [[*CGT assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) of a foreign hybrid; exceeds:

> <a id="s830-55-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the sum of the partner’s [[*capital gains*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) from CGT events happening during the income year in relation to the foreign hybrid or CGT assets of the foreign hybrid; the partner has a foreign hybrid net capital loss amount in respect of the foreign hybrid for the income year equal to the excess.
