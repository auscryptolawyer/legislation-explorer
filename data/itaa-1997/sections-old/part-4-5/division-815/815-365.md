# 815-365  Exemptions

Exemptions for particular entities

<a id="s815-365-1"></a>
**(1)**  The Commissioner may, by notice in writing, exempt an entity from:

> <a id="s815-365-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  giving statements under section 815-355; or

> <a id="s815-365-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  giving statements of a particular kind under that section.

<a id="s815-365-2"></a>
**(2)**  A notice under subsection (1) is not a legislative instrument. General exemptions

<a id="s815-365-3"></a>
**(3)**  The Commissioner may, by legislative instrument, determine that section 815-355 does not apply to a specified class of entity. 294                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Cross-border transfer pricing Division 815 Section 815-370
