# 815-370  Meaning of country by country reporting entity (or CBC reporting entity)

An entity is a country by country reporting entity (or CBC reporting entity) for a period if:

> <a id="s815-370-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity is a [[*CBC reporting parent*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cbc-reporting-parent)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cbc-reporting-parent) for the period; or

> <a id="s815-370-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity is a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of a [[*CBC reporting group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cbc-reporting-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cbc-reporting-group) during the period and another member of that group is a CBC reporting parent for the period.
