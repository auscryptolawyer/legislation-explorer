# 820-587  Additional application of Subdivision 820-D to MEC group that includes foreign-controlled Australian ADI

Subdivision 820-D applies to the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of a [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group) as if it were an *outward investing entity (ADI) for a period that is all or part of an income year if:

> <a id="s820-587-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the head company is not an outward investing entity (ADI) for that period; and

> <a id="s820-587-b"></a>
> **(b)**  throughout that period, at least one [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the group is both a [[*foreign controlled Australian entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-controlled-australian-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-controlled-australian-entity) and an *ADI; and

> <a id="s820-587-c"></a>
> **(c)**  throughout that period, there is at least one [[*eligible tier-1 company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company) of the [[*top company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-top-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-top-company) for the group that:

> > <a id="s820-587-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  is a member of the group; and

> > <a id="s820-587-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  is not an ADI; and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            415 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> > <a id="s820-587-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  has no *wholly-owned subsidiary that is an ADI.
