# 820-427  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision sets out concepts concerning third party debt. These concepts are relevant to entities that choose to apply the third party debt test, that is:

> <a id="s820-427-a"></a>
> **(a)**  general class investors that make a choice, or that are taken to have made a choice, under subsection 820-46(4); and

> <a id="s820-427-b"></a>
> **(b)**  outward investing financial entities (non-ADI) that make a choice under subsection 820-85(2C); and

> <a id="s820-427-c"></a>
> **(c)**  inward investing financial entities (non-ADI) that make a choice under subsection 820-185(2C). <a id="s820-427-1"></a>
**(1)**  An entity’s third party earnings limit for an income year is the sum of each [[*debt deduction*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-debt-deduction)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-debt-deduction) of the entity for the income year (disregarding this Division) that is attributable to a *debt interest issued by the entity that satisfies the [[*third party debt conditions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-third-party-debt-conditions)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-third-party-debt-conditions) in relation to the income year. 398                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Thin capitalisation rules Division 820 Section 820-427A

<a id="s820-427-2"></a>
**(2)**  For the purposes of subsection (1), treat a [[*debt deduction*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-debt-deduction)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-debt-deduction) of an entity as being attributable to a *debt interest issued by the entity to the extent that:

> <a id="s820-427-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the debt deduction is directly associated with hedging or managing the interest rate risk in respect of the debt interest; and

> <a id="s820-427-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the debt deduction is not referable to an amount paid or payable, directly or indirectly, to an [[*associate entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate-entity) (see section 820-427D) of the entity.

<a id="s820-427-3"></a>
**(3)**  A *debt interest issued by an entity satisfies the third party debt conditions in relation to an income year if the following conditions are satisfied:

> <a id="s820-427-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity issued the debt interest to an entity that is not an [[*associate entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate-entity) (see section 820-427D) of the entity;

> <a id="s820-427-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the debt interest is not held at any time in the income year by an entity that is an associate entity of the entity;

> <a id="s820-427-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  disregarding recourse to minor or insignificant assets, the holder of the debt interest has recourse for payment of the debt to which the debt interest relates only to Australian assets that:

> > <a id="s820-427-3-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  are covered by subsection (4); and

> > <a id="s820-427-3-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  are not rights covered by subsection (5) (about credit support rights);

> <a id="s820-427-3-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the entity uses all, or substantially all, of the proceeds of issuing the debt interest to fund its commercial activities in connection with Australia that do not include:

> > <a id="s820-427-3-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  any *business carried on by the entity at or through its *overseas permanent establishments; and

> > <a id="s820-427-3-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the holding by the entity of any [[*associate entity debt*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate-entity-debt)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate-entity-debt), [[*controlled foreign entity debt*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-controlled-foreign-entity-debt)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-controlled-foreign-entity-debt) or [[*controlled foreign entity equity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-controlled-foreign-entity-equity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-controlled-foreign-entity-equity);

> **(e)**  the entity is an *Australian entity (see section 820-427E).
