# 820-55  Meaning of entity EBITDA and GR group EBITDA


<a id="s820-55-1"></a>
**(1)**  The entity EBITDA of an entity, for a period, is the sum of the following for the entity for the period:

> <a id="s820-55-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity’s net profit (disregarding tax expenses);

> <a id="s820-55-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity’s [[*adjusted net third party interest expense*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjusted-net-third-party-interest-expense)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjusted-net-third-party-interest-expense);

> <a id="s820-55-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity’s depreciation and amortisation expenses.

<a id="s820-55-2"></a>
**(2)**  The GR group EBITDA, for a period, of a [[*GR group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gr-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gr-group) for the period, is the sum of the following:

> <a id="s820-55-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the GR group’s net profit (disregarding tax expenses);

> <a id="s820-55-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the GR group’s [[*adjusted net third party interest expense*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjusted-net-third-party-interest-expense)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjusted-net-third-party-interest-expense);

> <a id="s820-55-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the GR group’s depreciation and amortisation expenses; as disclosed in:

> <a id="s820-55-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  if paragraph 820-53(2)(a) applies—the *audited consolidated financial statements for the [[*GR group parent*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gr-group-parent)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gr-group-parent) for the period for the GR group; or

> <a id="s820-55-2-e"></a>
> **(e)**  if paragraph 820-53(2)(b) applies—the [[*global financial statements*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-global-financial-statements)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-global-financial-statements) for the GR group parent for the period for the GR group.

<a id="s820-55-3"></a>
**(3)**  For the purposes of subsection (2), in working out the [[*GR group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gr-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gr-group)’s [[*GR group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gr-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gr-group) EBITDA for the period, if a [[*GR group member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gr-group-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gr-group-member) for the period of the GR group has an [[*entity EBITDA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-entity-ebitda)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-entity-ebitda) for the period of less than zero, disregard that entity EBITDA.
