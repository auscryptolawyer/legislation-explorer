# 820-881  Application

This Subdivision applies to:

> <a id="s820-881-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity (the relevant entity) that is a [[*general class investor*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-class-investor)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-class-investor), an *outward investing entity (non-ADI), or an *outward investing entity (ADI), for a period (the relevant period) that is all or a part of an income year; and

> <a id="s820-881-b"></a>
> **(b)**  each entity (controlled entity of the relevant entity) that is an [[*Australian controlled foreign entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-controlled-foreign-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-controlled-foreign-entity) of which:

> > <a id="s820-881-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the relevant entity is an [[*Australian controller*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-controller)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-controller); or Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                           463 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> > <a id="s820-881-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  an [[*associate entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate-entity) of the relevant entity is an Australian controller.
