# 820-30  Object of Division

The Object of this Division is to ensure that the following entities do not reduce their tax liabilities by using an excessive amount of [[*debt deductions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-debt-deduction)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-debt-deduction), in financing their Australian operations: 302                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Thin capitalisation rules Division 820 Section 820-31

> <a id="s820-30-a"></a>
> **(a)**  *Australian entities that operate internationally;

> <a id="s820-30-b"></a>
> **(b)**  Australian entities that are foreign controlled;

> <a id="s820-30-c"></a>
> **(c)**  *foreign entities that operate in Australia.

> **Note:** This Division applies in relation to debt deductions of an entity as reduced, if required, in accordance with Division 815 (about cross-border transfer pricing).
