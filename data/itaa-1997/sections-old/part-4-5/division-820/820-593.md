# 820-593  FRT disallowed amount cannot be applied for income year ending after the joining time

To the extent that the [[*FRT disallowed amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frt-disallowed-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frt-disallowed-amount) is not transferred under section 820-590 from the joining entity to the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the joined group, the FRT disallowed amount cannot be applied under paragraph 820-56(2)(b) by any entity in respect of an income year ending after the joining time. 420                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Thin capitalisation rules Division 820 Section 820-594
