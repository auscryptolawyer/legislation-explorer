# 775-200  Forex realisation event 4 does not apply

A [[*forex realisation gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-gain) or a [[*forex realisation loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-loss) you make as a result of forex realisation event 4 or 9 is disregarded to the extent to which the event happens because:

> <a id="s775-200-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you discharge your obligation under an [[*eligible security*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-security)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-security) issued by you under a [[*facility agreement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-facility-agreement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-facility-agreement); and

> <a id="s775-200-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you have made a choice for roll-over relief for the facility agreement, and that choice is in effect. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                              225 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026
