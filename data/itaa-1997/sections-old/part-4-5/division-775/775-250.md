# 775-250  Tax consequences of passing the limited balance test


<a id="s775-250-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*forex realisation gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-gain) or a [[*forex realisation loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-loss) you make as a result of forex realisation event 2 or 4 is disregarded if the event happens in relation to a [[*qualifying forex account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-qualifying-forex-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-qualifying-forex-account) that:

> <a id="s775-250-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you hold at the time of the event; and

> <a id="s775-250-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  passes the limited balance test at the time of the event.

<a id="s775-250-2"></a>
**(2)**  If CGT event C1 or C2 happens in relation to a [[*qualifying forex account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-qualifying-forex-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-qualifying-forex-account) that:

> <a id="s775-250-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you hold at the time of the event; and

> <a id="s775-250-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  passes the limited balance test at the time of the event; disregard so much of any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make as a result of the event as is attributable to a [[*currency exchange rate effect*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-currency-exchange-rate-effect)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-currency-exchange-rate-effect).

> **Note:** For currency exchange rate effect, see section 775-105.
