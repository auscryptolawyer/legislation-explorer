# 775-25  Certain forex realisation gains are non-assessable non-exempt income

A [[*forex realisation gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-gain) you make is [[*non-assessable non-exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income) to the extent that, if it had been a [[*forex realisation loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-loss), it would have been made in gaining or producing non-assessable non-exempt income.
