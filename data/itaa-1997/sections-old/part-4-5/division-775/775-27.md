# 775-27  Certain forex realisation gains are non-assessable non-exempt income

Sections 775-20 and 775-25 apply to a [[*forex realisation gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-gain) only if, had it been a [[*forex realisation loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-loss), it would have been disregarded under section 775-35.
