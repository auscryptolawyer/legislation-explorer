# 775-300  Tax consequences of choosing retranslation for arrangement


<a id="s775-300-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*forex realisation gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-gain) or [[*forex realisation loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forex-realisation-loss) you make as a result of forex realisation event 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 or 8 is disregarded if:

> <a id="s775-300-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the event happens in relation to an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) that you hold; and

> <a id="s775-300-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you have made a [[*foreign exchange retranslation election*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-exchange-retranslation-election)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-exchange-retranslation-election) that applies to the arrangement; and

> <a id="s775-300-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the election is in effect when the event happens.

<a id="s775-300-2"></a>
**(2)**  If:

> <a id="s775-300-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  CGT event C1 or C2 happens in relation to an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) that you hold at the time of the event; and

> <a id="s775-300-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you have made a [[*foreign exchange retranslation election*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-exchange-retranslation-election)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-exchange-retranslation-election) that applies to the arrangement; and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            251 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s775-300-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the election is in effect when the event happens; disregard so much of any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make as a result of the event as is attributable to a [[*currency exchange rate effect*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-currency-exchange-rate-effect)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-currency-exchange-rate-effect).

> **Note:** For currency exchange rate effect, see section 775-105.
