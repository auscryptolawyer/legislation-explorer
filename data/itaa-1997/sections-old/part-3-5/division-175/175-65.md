# 175-65  Capital loss injected into company because of available capital gain


<a id="s175-65-1"></a>
**(1)**  The Commissioner may [[*disallow*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-disallow)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-disallow) a [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) of a company for an income year to the extent that the company would not have made the loss if it had not also made some or all of a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) it made in that income year.

> **Note:** The disallowance may result in a tax loss for the income year: see section 175-75.

<a id="s175-65-2"></a>
**(2)**  The Commissioner cannot [[*disallow*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-disallow)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-disallow) any of the [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) if:

> <a id="s175-65-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*continuing shareholders*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-continuing-shareholders)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-continuing-shareholders) will benefit from any profit or advantage that has arisen or might arise directly or indirectly from the loss being made; and

> <a id="s175-65-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the Commissioner thinks that the extent to which they will benefit is fair and reasonable having regard to their respective [[*shareholding interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-shareholding-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-shareholding-interest) in the company.

> **Note:** Section 175-100 allows the Commissioner to disallow a capital loss of an insolvent company.

<a id="s175-65-3"></a>
**(3)**  The continuing shareholders are the individuals who had [[*shareholding interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-shareholding-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-shareholding-interest) in the company both immediately before the [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) was made, and immediately afterwards.
