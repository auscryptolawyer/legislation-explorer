# 170-15  Income company is taken to have incurred transferred loss


<a id="s170-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  If an amount of a [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss is transferred, the amount is taken to be a tax loss incurred by the [[*income company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-company) in the [[*loss year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year). 482                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Corporate taxpayers and corporate distributions Part 3-5 Treatment of certain company groups for income tax purposes Division 170 Section 170-20

<a id="s170-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, if the [[*loss year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year) is the same as the income year of the transfer, the [[*income company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-company) is taken to have incurred the [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss in the income year before the loss year.

> **Note:** This rule is needed because Division 36 allows a tax loss to be deducted only if it was incurred in an earlier income year.

<a id="s170-15-3"></a>
**(3)**  Despite subsection (1), if the [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss is transferred because the conditions in section 170-32 are met, the [[*income company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-company) is taken to have incurred the tax loss for the income year for which the first prior transferor mentioned in that section incurred the tax loss.

<a id="s170-15-4"></a>
**(4)**  Despite subsection (1), if the [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss is transferred because the condition in subsection 170-42(4) is met, the [[*income company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-company) is taken to have incurred the tax loss for the income year for which that subsection assumes the income company incurred the tax loss.
