# 170-35  The loss company


<a id="s170-35-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*loss company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-company):

> <a id="s170-35-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  must be an Australian resident and not a [[*prescribed dual resident*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-prescribed-dual-resident)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-prescribed-dual-resident); and

> <a id="s170-35-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  must not be a [[*dual resident investment company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dual-resident-investment-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dual-resident-investment-company) in either the [[*loss year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year) or the [[*deduction year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-year).

<a id="s170-35-2"></a>
**(2)**  If the [[*loss year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year) and the [[*deduction year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-year) are the same, it must be the case that the [[*loss company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-company) was not required to calculate the [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss: 488                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Corporate taxpayers and corporate distributions Part 3-5 Treatment of certain company groups for income tax purposes Division 170 Section 170-40

> <a id="s170-35-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  under section 165-70 (because of a change in ownership or control); or

> <a id="s170-35-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  under section 175-35 (because of injected income or deductions).

<a id="s170-35-3"></a>
**(3)**  Also, it must be the case that neither Subdivision 165-A nor Subdivision 175-A would have prevented the [[*loss company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-company) from deducting the [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss in the [[*deduction year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deduction-year) if it had had enough assessable income (including *assessable film income) to offset the tax loss.

> **Note:** Subdivision 165-A deals with the deductibility of a company’s tax loss for an earlier income year if there has been a change in the ownership or control of the company in the loss year or the income year. Subdivision 175-A is about the Commissioner preventing a company from getting certain tax benefits through its unused tax losses.

> **Note:** Division 707 affects the operation of Subdivision 165-A if the loss company incurred the tax loss because of a transfer under Subdivision 707-A.
