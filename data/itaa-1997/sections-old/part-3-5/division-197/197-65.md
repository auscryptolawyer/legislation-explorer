# 197-65  Choosing to untaint—further franking debits may arise

When this section applies

<a id="s197-65-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if:

> <a id="s197-65-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a company chooses to untaint its [[*share capital account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share-capital-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share-capital-account); and

> <a id="s197-65-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the applicable franking percentage (within the meaning of subsection (3)) is higher than the percentage that was the [[*benchmark franking percentage*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-benchmark-franking-percentage)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-benchmark-franking-percentage) in relation to the [[*franking period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-period) in which the transfer of an amount (the transferred amount) that is, or is part of, the [[*tainting amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tainting-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tainting-amount) occurred.

> **Note:** If paragraph (b) is satisfied in relation to 2 or more amounts, this section is to be applied separately in relation to each of those amounts (so a separate franking debit will arise in relation to each of those amounts). Franking debit arises in relation to making the choice

<a id="s197-65-2"></a>
**(2)**  A [[*franking debit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit) arises in the company’s [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) in relation to the transferred amount. The debit arises immediately before the end of the [[*franking period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-period) in which the choice to untaint is made.

<a id="s197-65-3"></a>
**(3)**  The amount of the [[*franking debit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit) is the amount by which the amount calculated in accordance with the following formula exceeds the amount of the franking debit that arose under section 197-45 in relation to the transferred amount: Applicable franking percentage Transferred amount  Applicable gross-up rate where: applicable franking percentage means:

> <a id="s197-65-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if, before the debit arises, the [[*benchmark franking percentage*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-benchmark-franking-percentage)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-benchmark-franking-percentage) for the [[*franking period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-period) in which the choice to untaint is made has already been set by section 203-30—that percentage; or

> <a id="s197-65-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—100%. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            593 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 applicable gross-up rate means the company’s *corporate tax gross-up rate for the income year in which the franking debit arises.
