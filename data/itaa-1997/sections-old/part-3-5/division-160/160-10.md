# 160-10  Amount of loss carry back tax offset


<a id="s160-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  The amount of the entity’s [[*loss carry back tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-carry-back-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-carry-back-tax-offset) for the [[*current year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year) is the lesser of the following amounts:

> <a id="s160-10-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the sum of the [[*loss carry back tax offset components*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-carry-back-tax-offset-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-carry-back-tax-offset-component) for:

> > <a id="s160-10-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the 2018-19 income year; and

> > <a id="s160-10-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the 2019-20 income year; and 286                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Corporate taxpayers and corporate distributions Part 3-5 Corporate loss carry back tax offset for 2020-21, 2021-22 or 2022-23 for businesses with turnover under $5 billion Division 160 Section 160-10

> > <a id="s160-10-1-a-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  if the current year is the 2021-22 income year—the 2020-21 income year; and

> > <a id="s160-10-1-a-iv"></a>
> > **(iv)**  if the current year is the 2022-23 income year—the 2021-22 income year and the 2020-21 income year;

> <a id="s160-10-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity’s [[*franking account balance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account-balance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account-balance) at the end of the current year. Meaning of loss carry back tax offset component

<a id="s160-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  For the purposes of working out the amount of the entity’s [[*loss carry back tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-carry-back-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-carry-back-tax-offset) for the [[*current year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year), the entity’s loss carry back tax offset component for an income year is:

> <a id="s160-10-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the entity does not, in its [[*loss carry back choice*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-carry-back-choice)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-carry-back-choice) for the current year, [[*carry back*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-carry-back)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-carry-back) any [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) losses to the income year— nil; or

> <a id="s160-10-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—so much of the entity’s [[*income tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax) liability for the income year as does not exceed:

> > <a id="s160-10-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  if, in its loss carry back choice for the current year, the entity carries back only one tax loss to the income year—the amount worked out at step 3 of the following method statement in relation to the tax loss; or

> > <a id="s160-10-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if, in its loss carry back choice for the current year, the entity carries back tax losses for 2, 3 or 4 [[*loss years*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year) to the income year—the sum of the amounts worked out at step 3 of the following method statement in relation to each of those tax losses. > *[[*Example*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example):** Company A (which is not a base rate entity) has at the end of the 2020-21 income year:

> <a id="s160-10-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a tax loss of $900,000 for that year and a franking account balance of $280,000; and

> <a id="s160-10-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  for the 2018-19 income year—an income tax liability of $120,000 and net exempt income of $5,000; and

> <a id="s160-10-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  for the 2019-20 income year—an income tax liability of $210,000. Company A chooses to carry back $405,000 of its tax loss for the 2020-21 year to the 2018-19 year and $495,000 of that loss to the 2019-20 year. Company A’s loss carry back tax offset for the 2020-21 year is $268,500, worked out as follows:

> <a id="s160-10-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an offset component for the 2018-19 income year of $120,000, calculated by starting with the $405,000 carried back, reducing that at step 2 by $5,000, and multiplying the result by 30%;

> <a id="s160-10-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an offset component for the 2019-20 income year of $148,500, calculated by starting with the $495,000 carried back and multiplying the result by 30%. The sum of the 2 components is $268,500 (which is less than Company A’s $280,000 franking account balance at the end of the 2020-21 year). If that sum had exceeded that balance, the amount of the offset would have been limited under paragraph (1)(b) of this section to that balance.
