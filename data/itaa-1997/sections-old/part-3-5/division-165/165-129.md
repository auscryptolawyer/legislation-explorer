# 165-129  Same people must control the voting power, or the company must satisfy the business continuity test


<a id="s165-129-1"></a>
**(1)**  Even if section 165-120 does not prevent a company from deducting a bad debt (or part of one), it cannot deduct the bad debt (or that part of it) if:

> <a id="s165-129-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  for some or all of the part of the [[*ownership test period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-test-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-test-period) that started at the end of the [[*first continuity period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-first-continuity-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-first-continuity-period), a person controlled, or was able to control, the voting power in the company (whether directly, or indirectly through one or more interposed entities); and

> <a id="s165-129-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  for some or all of the [[*first continuity period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-first-continuity-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-first-continuity-period), that person did not control, and was not able to control, that voting power (directly, or indirectly in that way); and 398                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Corporate taxpayers and corporate distributions Part 3-5 Income tax consequences of changing ownership or control of a company Division 165 Section 165-132

> <a id="s165-129-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  that person began to control, or became able to control, that voting power (directly, or indirectly in that way) for the purpose of:

> > <a id="s165-129-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  getting some benefit or advantage in relation to how this Act applies; or

> > <a id="s165-129-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  getting such a benefit or advantage for someone else; or for purposes including that purpose.

> **Note:** A person can still control the voting power in a company that is in liquidation etc.: see section 165-250.

> **Note:** Subdivision 167-B has special rules for working out voting power in a company whose shares do not all carry the same voting rights, or do not carry all of the voting rights in the company.

<a id="s165-129-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, that person’s control of the voting power, or ability to control it, does not prevent the company from deducting the bad debt (or that part of it) if the company satisfies the [[*business continuity test*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-business-continuity-test)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-business-continuity-test) for the [[*second continuity period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-second-continuity-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-second-continuity-period) (the business continuity test period).

<a id="s165-129-3"></a>
**(3)**  Apply the [[*business continuity test*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-business-continuity-test)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-business-continuity-test) to the *business that the company carried on immediately before the time (the test time) when the person began to control that voting power, or became able to control it. For the business continuity test: see Subdivision 165-E.
