# 165-65  How to calculate the company’s taxable income for the income year


<a id="s165-65-1"></a>
**(1)**  The company’s taxable income for the income year is calculated as follows.

<a id="s165-65-2"></a>
**(2)**  Add up the [[*notional taxable incomes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-taxable-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-taxable-income) (if any) worked out under section 165-50 or 165-75.

> **Note:** A notional loss for a period is not taken into account, but counts towards the company’s tax loss for the income year. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            321 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

<a id="s165-65-3"></a>
**(3)**  Add the [[*full year amounts*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-full-year-amounts)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-full-year-amounts) referred to in subsection 165-60(7) (if any) and any [[*net capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain) of the company for the income year.

<a id="s165-65-4"></a>
**(4)**  Subtract the company’s [[*full year deductions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-full-year-deductions)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-full-year-deductions) of these kinds:

> <a id="s165-65-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  deductions for bad debts under section 8-1 (about general deductions) or section 25-35 (about bad debts);

> <a id="s165-65-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  deductions, so far as they are allowable under Division 8 (which is about deductions) because Subdivision H (Period of deductibility of certain advance expenditure) of Division 3 of Part III of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 applies to the company in relation to the income year; unless they exceed the total of the [[*notional taxable incomes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-taxable-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-notional-taxable-income) and the [[*full year amounts*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-full-year-amounts)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-full-year-amounts). (If they equal or exceed that total, the company does not have a taxable income for the income year.)

<a id="s165-65-5"></a>
**(5)**  If an amount remains, subtract from it the company’s other [[*full year deductions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-full-year-deductions)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-full-year-deductions), in the order shown in subsection 165-55(5), unless they exceed the amount remaining. (If they equal or exceed that amount, the company does not have a taxable income for the income year.)

<a id="s165-65-6"></a>
**(6)**  If an amount remains, it is the company’s taxable income for the income year. Working out the company’s tax loss
