# 166-145  The ownership tests: substantial continuity of ownership


<a id="s166-145-1"></a>
**(1)**  There is substantial continuity of ownership of the company as between the start of the [[*test period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-test-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-test-period) and another time in the test period if (and only if) the conditions in this section are met.

> **Note:** Section 166-165, and Subdivision 166-E, affect how this section is applied. Voting power

<a id="s166-145-2"></a>
**(2)**  There must be persons (none of them companies or trustees) who had [[*more than 50% of the voting power*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-more-than-50-of-the-voting-power)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-more-than-50-of-the-voting-power) in the company at the start of the [[*test period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-test-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-test-period). Also, those persons must have had [[*more than 50% of the voting power*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-more-than-50-of-the-voting-power)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-more-than-50-of-the-voting-power) in the company immediately after the other time in the test period.

> **Note:** To work out who had more than 50% of the voting power, see section 165-150.

> **Note:** Subdivision 167-B has special rules for working out voting power in a company whose shares do not all carry the same voting rights, or do not carry all of the voting rights in the company. Rights to dividends

<a id="s166-145-3"></a>
**(3)**  There must be persons (none of them companies) who had rights to *more than 50% of the company’s dividends at the start of the [[*test period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-test-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-test-period). Also, those persons must have had rights to *more than 50% of the company’s dividends immediately after the other time in the test period.

> **Note:** Subdivision 167-A has special rules for working out rights to dividends in a company whose shares do not all carry the same rights to dividends. Rights to capital distributions

<a id="s166-145-4"></a>
**(4)**  There must be persons (none of them companies) who had rights to *more than 50% of the company’s capital distributions at the start of the [[*test period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-test-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-test-period). Also, those persons must have had rights to *more than 50% of the company’s capital distributions immediately after the other time in the test period.

> **Note:** To work out who had rights to more than 50% of the company’s capital distributions, see section 165-160.

> **Note:** Subdivision 167-A has special rules for working out rights to capital distributions in a company whose shares do not all carry the same rights to capital distributions. When to apply the test

<a id="s166-145-5"></a>
**(5)**  To work out whether a condition in this section was satisfied at a time (the ownership test time), apply the alterative test for that condition.

> **Note:** For the alternative test, see subsections 165-150(2), 165-155(2) and 165-160(2). Conditions in subsections (3) and (4) satisfied by non-profit and mutual companies

<a id="s166-145-6"></a>
**(6)**  If the company is:

> <a id="s166-145-6-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*non-profit company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-profit-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-profit-company); or

> <a id="s166-145-6-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*mutual affiliate company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mutual-affiliate-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mutual-affiliate-company); or

> <a id="s166-145-6-c"></a>
> **(c)**  a [[*mutual insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mutual-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mutual-insurance-company); during the whole of the [[*test period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-test-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-test-period), the conditions in subsections (3) and (4) are taken to have been satisfied by the company. 440                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Corporate taxpayers and corporate distributions Part 3-5 Income tax consequences of changing ownership or control of a widely held or eligible Division 166 company Division 166 Section 166-165
