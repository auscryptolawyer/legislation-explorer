# 166-265  Persons who actually control voting power or have rights are taken not to control power or have rights

If any of sections 166-225, 166-230, 166-240, 166-245, 166-255 or 166-260 apply, the ownership tests in section 166-145 are also applied to the tested company as if, at the [[*ownership test time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-test-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-test-time):

> <a id="s166-265-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the persons who control, or are able to control, the voting power in the tested company (whether directly, or indirectly through one or more interposed entities) that is carried by each [[*voting stake*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-voting-stake)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-voting-stake) in the tested company mentioned in that section had not had that control; and

> <a id="s166-265-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the persons who have the right to receive for their own benefit (whether directly, or [[*indirectly*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirectly)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirectly) through one or more interposed entities):

> > <a id="s166-265-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  any *dividends that the tested company may pay in respect of each [[*dividend stake*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dividend-stake)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dividend-stake) in the tested company mentioned in that section; and

> > <a id="s166-265-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  any distributions of capital of the tested company in respect of each [[*capital stake*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-stake)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-stake) in the tested company mentioned in that section; had not had that right. 460                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Corporate taxpayers and corporate distributions Part 3-5 Income tax consequences of changing ownership or control of a widely held or eligible Division 166 company Division 166 Section 166-270
