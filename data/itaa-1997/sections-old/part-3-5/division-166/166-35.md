# 166-35  Companies can choose that this Subdivision is not to apply to them


<a id="s166-35-1"></a>
**(1)**  The company can choose that Subdivisions 165-B and 165-CB are to apply to it for the income year without the modifications made by this Subdivision.

<a id="s166-35-2"></a>
**(2)**  The company must choose on or before the day it lodges its [[*income tax return*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return) for the income year, or before a later day if the Commissioner allows.
