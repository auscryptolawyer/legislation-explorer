# 195-30  PDF cannot transfer net capital loss

If a company is a *PDF at the end of an income year for which it has a [[*net capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-loss), it cannot transfer any amount of the loss under Subdivision 170-B (which is about the transfer of net capital losses within certain wholly-owned groups of companies). 564                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Corporate taxpayers and corporate distributions Part 3-5 Special types of company Division 195 Section 195-35
