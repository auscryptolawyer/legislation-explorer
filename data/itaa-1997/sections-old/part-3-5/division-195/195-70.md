# 195-70  Previous tax losses can be deducted after ceasing to be a VCLP, an ESVCLP, an AFOF or a VCMP

This Subdivision does not prevent a *limited partnership that has ceased to be a [[*VCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp), an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp), an [[*AFOF*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-afof)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-afof) or a [[*VCMP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vcmp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vcmp) from deducting, in an income year, a [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss for a [[*loss year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year) that occurred before the partnership was a VCLP, ESVCLP, AFOF or VCMP.
