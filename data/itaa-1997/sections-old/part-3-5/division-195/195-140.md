# 195-140  Entry on Australian Business Register


<a id="s195-140-1"></a>
**(1)**  If a [[*CCIV sub-fund trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cciv-sub-fund-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cciv-sub-fund-trust) has an [[*ABN*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-abn)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-abn), the [[*Australian Business Registrar*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-business-registrar)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-business-registrar) must enter in the [[*Australian Business Register*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-business-register)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-business-register) in relation to the trust a statement that: 576                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Corporate taxpayers and corporate distributions Part 3-5 Special types of company Division 195 Section 195-140

> <a id="s195-140-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  indicates that the trust is taken to exist for tax purposes because of the application of section 195-110 to a *sub-fund of a [[*CCIV*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cciv)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cciv); and

> <a id="s195-140-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  sets out the sub-fund’s ARFN (within the meaning of the Corporations Act 2001).

> **Note:** ARFN is short for Australian Registered Fund Number.

<a id="s195-140-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*Australian Business Registrar*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-business-registrar)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-business-registrar) must take reasonable steps to ensure that information entered in the [[*Australian Business Register*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-business-register)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-business-register) under this section is accurate.
