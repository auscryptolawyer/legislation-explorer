# 195-65  Tax losses cannot be transferred to a VCLP, an ESVCLP, an AFOF or a VCMP

A *limited partnership’s [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss for a [[*loss year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year) cannot be deducted in a later income year during which the partnership is a [[*VCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp), an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp), an [[*AFOF*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-afof)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-afof) or a [[*VCMP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vcmp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vcmp).
