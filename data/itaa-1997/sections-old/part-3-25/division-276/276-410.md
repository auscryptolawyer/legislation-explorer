# 276-410  Trustee taxed on excess in determined member component (character relating to tax offset)


<a id="s276-410-1"></a>
**(1)**  An *AMIT has an excess under this subsection for an income year equal to the amount (if any) by which:

> <a id="s276-410-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*determined member component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-member-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-member-component) of a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the AMIT of a character relating to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for the income year; exceeds:

> <a id="s276-410-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the [[*member component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member-component) of the member of that character for the income year. 62                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Particular kinds of trusts Part 3-25 Australian managed investment trusts: attribution managed investment trusts Division 276 Section 276-415 Liability to tax

<a id="s276-410-2"></a>
**(2)**  The trustee is liable to pay tax at the rate declared by the Parliament on the amount that is the sum of each excess of the *AMIT under subsection (1) for the income year.

> **Note:** The tax is imposed by the Income Tax (Attribution Managed Investment Trusts—Offsets) Act 2016 and the rate of the tax is set out in that Act.
