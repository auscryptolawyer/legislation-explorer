# 276-85  Member’s assessable income or tax offsets for determined member components—specific rules


<a id="s276-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section makes clarifications and modifications of the operation of section 276-80 in respect of a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of an *AMIT in respect of an income year.

<a id="s276-85-2"></a>
**(2)**  For the purposes of this Act, if an amount is included in the [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)’s assessable income because of the operation of this section, treat that amount as being so included because of the operation of subsection 276-80(2). Discount capital gains

<a id="s276-85-3"></a>
**(3)**  Subsection (4) applies if the [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) has, for the income year, a [[*determined member component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-member-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-member-component) of the character of:

> <a id="s276-85-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*discount capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discount-capital-gain) from a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that is [[*taxable Australian property*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property); or

> <a id="s276-85-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a discount capital gain from a CGT asset that is not taxable Australian property.

<a id="s276-85-4"></a>
**(4)**  For the purposes of section 276-80 and this section, treat the amount of the component as being double what it would be apart from this subsection. Franking credit gross-up

<a id="s276-85-5"></a>
**(5)**  Subsection (6) applies if the [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) has, for the income year, a [[*determined member component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-member-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-member-component) (the franking credit gross-up 38                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Particular kinds of trusts Part 3-25 Australian managed investment trusts: attribution managed investment trusts Division 276 Section 276-90 component) of the character of assessable income under subsection 207-20(1) (franking credit gross-up).

<a id="s276-85-6"></a>
**(6)**  For the purposes of subsection 207-20(1) (franking credit gross-up), treat the reference in that subsection to the amount of the [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) on the distribution as instead being a reference to the amount of the franking credit gross-up component. Limitation on circumstances in paragraph 276-80(2)(b)

<a id="s276-85-7"></a>
**(7)**  The circumstances mentioned in paragraph 276-80(2)(b) or (5)(b) do not include the following:

> <a id="s276-85-7-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the residence of the trustee of the *AMIT;

> <a id="s276-85-7-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the place of the central management and control of the AMIT.
