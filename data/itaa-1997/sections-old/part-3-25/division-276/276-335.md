# 276-335  Meaning of FITO allocation amount


<a id="s276-335-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if:

> <a id="s276-335-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the *AMIT has a *trust component of the character of [[*foreign income tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax) paid that counts towards a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) under Division 770; and

> <a id="s276-335-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the AMIT has a *trust component deficit for the income year of that character.

<a id="s276-335-2"></a>
**(2)**  The *AMIT has a FITO allocation amount for the income year equal to the sum of:

> <a id="s276-335-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  that *trust component deficit; and

> <a id="s276-335-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the product of:

> > <a id="s276-335-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  that trust component deficit; and

> > <a id="s276-335-2-b-ii"></a>
