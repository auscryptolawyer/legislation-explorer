# 276-315  Rounding adjustment surplus decreases trust component


<a id="s276-315-1"></a>
**(1)**  If the *AMIT has a [[*rounding adjustment surplus*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rounding-adjustment-surplus)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rounding-adjustment-surplus) of that character for the income year, decrease the amount of the *trust component by the amount of that rounding adjustment surplus.

<a id="s276-315-2"></a>
**(2)**  The *AMIT has a rounding adjustment surplus of a particular character for an income year if:

> <a id="s276-315-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the AMIT has an excess for the previous income year under subsection (3); and

> <a id="s276-315-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the excess results wholly or partly from the trustee of the AMIT rounding up amounts in working out [[*determined member components*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-member-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-member-component) for the previous income year. The amount of the rounding adjustment surplus is the amount of the excess, to the extent that it results from that rounding up.

<a id="s276-315-3"></a>
**(3)**  The *AMIT has an excess under this subsection for an income year equal to the amount (if any) by which:

> <a id="s276-315-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the sum of all the [[*determined member components*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-member-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-member-component) of all the *members of the AMIT of a particular character relating to Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             55 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 trusts Section 276-320 assessable income, [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) or [[*non-assessable non-exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income) for the income year; exceeds:

> <a id="s276-315-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the [[*determined trust component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-trust-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-determined-trust-component) of that character of the AMIT for the income year.
