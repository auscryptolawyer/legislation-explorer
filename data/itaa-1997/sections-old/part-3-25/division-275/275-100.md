# 275-100  Consequences of making choice—CGT to be primary code for calculating MIT gains or losses


<a id="s275-100-1"></a>
**(1)**  The modifications in subsection (2) apply if:

> <a id="s275-100-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happens at a time involving a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset); and

> <a id="s275-100-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the CGT asset is owned at that time by an entity that is a [[*managed investment trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-managed-investment-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-managed-investment-trust) in relation to the income year in which the time occurs; and

> <a id="s275-100-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the CGT event happens because the managed investment trust *disposes of, ceases to own or otherwise realises the asset; and

> <a id="s275-100-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the asset is covered by section 275-105; and

> <a id="s275-100-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the entity meets the requirement in section 275-110 at the time; and

> <a id="s275-100-1-f"></a>
> **(f)**  a choice under section 275-115 covering the entity is in force for the income year in which the time occurs. (1A) Without limiting paragraph (1)(b), if:

> <a id="s275-100-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*VCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp) or an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp) owns a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) at the time referred to in that paragraph; and

> <a id="s275-100-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  at that time, the [[*managed investment trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-managed-investment-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-managed-investment-trust) has an interest in the asset as a [[*limited partner*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-limited-partner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-limited-partner) of the VCLP or ESVCLP; for the purposes of that paragraph, the managed investment trust is taken to own the asset to the extent of that interest.

**(5)**  The modifications in subsection (6) apply if:

> <a id="s275-100-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity that is a [[*managed investment trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-managed-investment-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-managed-investment-trust) in relation to the income year *acquires a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) at a time in that income year; and

> <a id="s275-100-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the CGT asset is an item of [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock); and

> <a id="s275-100-5-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the CGT asset is not land (including an interest in land), or a right or option to acquire or *dispose of land (including an interest in land); and

> <a id="s275-100-5-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the entity incurs an outgoing in connection with acquiring the asset; and

> <a id="s275-100-5-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the asset is covered by section 275-105; and

> <a id="s275-100-5-f"></a>
> **(f)**  the entity meets the requirement in section 275-110 at the time; and

> <a id="s275-100-5-g"></a>
> **(g)**  a choice under section 275-115 covering the entity is in force for the income year in which the time occurs.
