# 900-135  Evidence on a payment summary


<a id="s900-135-1"></a>
**(1)**  If the nature and amount of a [[*work expense*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-work-expense)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-work-expense) are shown on your copy of a [[*payment summary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-summary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-summary) given to you by your employer, you can use the copy as written evidence of the expense.

> **Note:** This Division also applies to entities that are not employers, but pay (or are liable to pay) withholding payments covered by subsection 900-12(3).

<a id="s900-135-2"></a>
**(2)**  Expenses of the same nature need not be separately itemised; it is acceptable if they are totalled together on the [[*payment summary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-summary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-summary).
