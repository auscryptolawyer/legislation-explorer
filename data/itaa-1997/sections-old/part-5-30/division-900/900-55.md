# 900-55  Exception for overseas travel allowance expenses


<a id="s900-55-1"></a>
**(1)**  You can deduct a [[*travel allowance expense*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-travel-allowance-expense)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-travel-allowance-expense) for travel outside Australia without getting written evidence under the same conditions as for domestic [[*travel allowances*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-travel-allowance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-travel-allowance), except that you still have to get written evidence for losses or outgoings for accommodation.

<a id="s900-55-2"></a>
**(2)**  Consequently, in deciding whether the total of the losses or outgoings you claim is reasonable, the Commissioner must disregard losses or outgoings for accommodation. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                                9 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

<a id="s900-55-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, for overseas travel covered by a [[*travel allowance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-travel-allowance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-travel-allowance) you must still keep travel records if the travel involves you being away from your ordinary residence for 6 or more nights in a row: Subdivision 900-F tells you about travel records.
