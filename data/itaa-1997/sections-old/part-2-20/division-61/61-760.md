# 61-760  Who is entitled to the ESVCLP tax offset

General case

<a id="s61-760-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*limited partner*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-limited-partner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-limited-partner) of an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp) is entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for an income year if:

> <a id="s61-760-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the partner contributes to the ESVCLP during the income year; and

> <a id="s61-760-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the partner is not a trust or partnership. 416                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Tax offsets Part 2-20 Generally applicable tax offsets Division 61 Section 61-765 Members of trusts or partnerships

<a id="s61-760-2"></a>
**(2)**  A [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of a trust or partnership is entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for an income year if the trust or partnership would be entitled to a tax offset, under this section, for the income year if it were an individual. Trustees

<a id="s61-760-3"></a>
**(3)**  A trustee of a trust is entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for an income year if:

> <a id="s61-760-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the trust would be entitled to a tax offset, under this section, for the income year if it were an individual; and

> <a id="s61-760-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  in a case where the trustee has determined percentages under subsection 61-770(2) in relation to the *members of the trust—the sum of those percentages is not 100%; and

> <a id="s61-760-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the trustee is liable to be assessed or has been assessed, and is liable to pay [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax), on a share of, or all or a part of, the trust’s [[*net income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-income) under section 98, 99 or 99A of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 for that income year.
