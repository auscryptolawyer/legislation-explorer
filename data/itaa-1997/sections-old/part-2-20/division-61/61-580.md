# 61-580  Entitlement to a tax offset

Tax offset for MLS lump sums paid to you

<a id="s61-580-1"></a>
**(1)**  You are entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for the [[*current year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year) if:

> <a id="s61-580-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are an individual; and

> <a id="s61-580-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  [[*Medicare levy*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-medicare-levy)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-medicare-levy) surcharge is payable by you for the current year because of:

> > <a id="s61-580-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  section 8B, 8C or 8D of the Medicare Levy Act 1986; or

> > <a id="s61-580-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the A New Tax System (Medicare Levy Surcharge— Fringe Benefits) Act 1999; and

> <a id="s61-580-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  your assessable income or [[*exempt foreign employment income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-foreign-employment-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-foreign-employment-income) for the current year includes one or more [[*MLS lump sums*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mls-lump-sums)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mls-lump-sums) paid to you; and

> <a id="s61-580-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the total of the MLS lump sums paid to you is greater than or equal to one-eleventh of the total of the following amounts:

> > <a id="s61-580-1-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  your normal taxable income (within the meaning of section 159ZR of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936) for the current year, disregarding your *assessable FHSS released amount for the current year;

> > <a id="s61-580-1-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  your exempt foreign employment income for the current year;

> > <a id="s61-580-1-d-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  your *reportable fringe benefits total for the current year;

> > <a id="s61-580-1-d-iv"></a>
> > **(iv)**  the amounts that would be included in your assessable income for the current year if, and only if, subsection 271-105(1) (family trust distribution tax) in Schedule 2F to the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 were ignored;

> > <a id="s61-580-1-d-v"></a>
> > **(v)**  your *reportable superannuation contributions for the current year;

> > <a id="s61-580-1-d-vi"></a>
> > **(vi)**  your *total net investment loss for the current year. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             409 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> **Note:** The test in paragraph (d) is similar to the 10% test in paragraph 159ZRA(1)(b) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936, which also deals with a tax offset for lump sum payments in arrears. Tax offset for MLS lump sums paid to your spouse

<a id="s61-580-2"></a>
**(2)**  You are also entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for the [[*current year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year) if:

> <a id="s61-580-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  during all or part of the current year, you were married to an individual (within the meaning of section 3 of the Medicare Levy Act 1986 or section 7 of the A New Tax System (Medicare Levy Surcharge—Fringe Benefits) Act 1999); and

> <a id="s61-580-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the individual is entitled to a tax offset for the current year under subsection (1); and

> <a id="s61-580-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  [[*Medicare levy*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-medicare-levy)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-medicare-levy) surcharge is payable by you for the current year because of:

> > <a id="s61-580-2-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  section 8D of the Medicare Levy Act 1986; or

> > <a id="s61-580-2-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  Division 4 of Part 3 of the A New Tax System (Medicare Levy Surcharge—Fringe Benefits) Act 1999; (which are about Medicare Levy surcharge for individuals who are married); and

> <a id="s61-580-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  you are not entitled to a tax offset for the current year under subsection (1); and

> <a id="s61-580-2-e"></a>
> **(e)**  less of the Medicare levy surcharge referred to in paragraph (c) would be payable by you for the current year if the [[*MLS lump sums*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mls-lump-sums)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mls-lump-sums) paid to the individual referred to in paragraph (a) were disregarded. 410                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Tax offsets Part 2-20 Generally applicable tax offsets Division 61 Section 61-585
