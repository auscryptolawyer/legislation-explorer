# 122-170  Capital gain or loss disregarded

If the partners choose a roll-over for *disposing of their interests in all the assets of a *business to the company, a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) any partner makes from the disposal is disregarded.
