# 122-185  All interests acquired before 20 September 1985


<a id="s122-185-1"></a>
**(1)**  A partner is taken to have *acquired all of the *shares before 20 September 1985 if the partner acquired all the partner’s interests in the assets of the *business before that day and none of the assets is a [[*precluded asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-precluded-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-precluded-asset).

<a id="s122-185-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, if at least one of the assets is a [[*precluded asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-precluded-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-precluded-asset), the partner is taken to have *acquired a whole number of the *shares (but not all of them) before that day. The number is the greatest possible that (when expressed as a percentage of all the shares) does not exceed: • the total of the *market values of the partner’s interests in the assets that are not [[*precluded assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-precluded-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-precluded-asset), less any liabilities the company undertakes to discharge in respect of those interests; expressed as a percentage of: • the total of the market values of the partner’s interests in all the assets, less any liabilities the company undertakes to discharge in respect of those interests.

> **Note:** There are rules for working out what are the liabilities in respect of an interest: see section 122-145.

<a id="s122-185-3"></a>
**(3)**  The first element of the partner’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base and *reduced cost base of each other [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share) is the total of the *market values of the partner’s interests in the [[*precluded assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-precluded-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-precluded-asset) (less any liabilities the company undertakes to discharge in respect of those interests) divided by the number of the other shares.

<a id="s122-185-4"></a>
**(4)**  The *market value of an interest in an asset is worked out when the partner *disposed of it. The [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base or *reduced cost base of an interest is worked out at the same time.
