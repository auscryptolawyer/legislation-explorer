# 124-140  New statutory licences


<a id="s124-140-1"></a>
**(1)**  There is a roll-over if:

> <a id="s124-140-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  your ownership of one or more [[*statutory licences*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-licence)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-licence) (each of which is an original licence) ends, resulting in [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) C2 happening to the licence (or to each of the licences as part of an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)); and

> <a id="s124-140-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  as a result of the CGT event or events, you are issued one or more new licences (each of which is a new licence) for the original licence (or original licences); and

> <a id="s124-140-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the new licence authorises (or the new licences taken together authorise) substantially similar activity as that authorised by the original licence (or by the original licences taken together).

> **Note:** If there has been a capital improvement to the original licence: see section 108-75.

> **Note:** Subdivision 124-C of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997 modifies this roll-over for certain water-related licences. A separate roll-over for other water entitlements is provided in Subdivision 124-R of this Act. (1A) If:

> <a id="s124-140-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are a foreign resident just before the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happens (or just before one or more of the CGT events happens); or

> <a id="s124-140-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you are the trustee of a trust that is a [[*foreign trust for CGT purposes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-trust-for-cgt-purposes)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-trust-for-cgt-purposes) for the income year in which the event happens (or for an income year in which one or more of those events happens); there is no roll-over under this section unless the conditions in subsection (1B) are satisfied. > <a id="s124-140-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if there was only one original licence—the licence must be [[*taxable Australian property*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property) just before the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happens; and

> <a id="s124-140-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if there was more than one original licence—each original licence must be taxable Australian property just before the CGT event in relation to it happens; and

> <a id="s124-140-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if there is only one new licence—the licence must be taxable Australian property just after you [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) it; and

> <a id="s124-140-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  if there is more than one new licence—each new licence must be taxable Australian property just after you acquire it.

<a id="s124-140-2"></a>
**(2)**  The first element of the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base and *reduced cost base of the new licence includes any amount you paid to get it (which can include giving property: see section 103-5).

<a id="s124-140-3"></a>
**(3)**  A statutory licence is an authority, licence, permit or quota (except a lease or a [[*mining entitlement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-entitlement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-entitlement) or [[*prospecting entitlement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-prospecting-entitlement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-prospecting-entitlement)) granted by:

> <a id="s124-140-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an *Australian government agency under an [[*Australian law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-law); or

> <a id="s124-140-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a *foreign government agency under a [[*foreign law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law).
