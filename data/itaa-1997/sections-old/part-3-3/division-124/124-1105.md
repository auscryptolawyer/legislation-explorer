# 124-1105  Replacement water entitlements roll-over

Automatic roll-over for single water entitlements

<a id="s124-1105-1"></a>
**(1)**  There is a roll-over if:

> <a id="s124-1105-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  your ownership of a [[*water entitlement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-entitlement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-entitlement) (the original entitlement) ends, resulting in a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happening; and

> <a id="s124-1105-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  as a result of your ownership of the original entitlement ending, you [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) one or more water entitlements (each of which is a new entitlement); and

> <a id="s124-1105-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if you are a foreign resident just before your ownership of the original entitlement ends, or you are the trustee of a trust that is a [[*foreign trust for CGT purposes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-trust-for-cgt-purposes)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-trust-for-cgt-purposes) for the income year in which your ownership of the original entitlement ends:

> > <a id="s124-1105-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the original entitlement was [[*taxable Australian property*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property) just before you stopped owning it; and

> > <a id="s124-1105-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if there is only one new entitlement—the new entitlement is taxable Australian property just after you acquire it; and

> > <a id="s124-1105-1-c-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  if there is more than one new entitlement—each new entitlement is taxable Australian property just after you acquire it; and

> <a id="s124-1105-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  you have not chosen a roll-over in relation to the original entitlement under subsection (2). Elective roll-over for bundled water entitlements

<a id="s124-1105-2"></a>
**(2)**  There is a roll-over if:

> <a id="s124-1105-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  your ownership of more than one [[*water entitlement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-entitlement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-entitlement) (each of which is an original entitlement) ends, resulting in a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happening; and

> <a id="s124-1105-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  as a result of your ownership of the original entitlements ending, you [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) one or more water entitlements (each of which is a new entitlement); and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            113 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s124-1105-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if you are a foreign resident just before your ownership of the original entitlements ends, or you are the trustee of a trust that is a [[*foreign trust for CGT purposes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-trust-for-cgt-purposes)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-trust-for-cgt-purposes) for the income year in which your ownership of the original entitlements ends:

> > <a id="s124-1105-2-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  each original entitlement was [[*taxable Australian property*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property) just before you stopped owning it; and

> > <a id="s124-1105-2-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if there is only one new entitlement—the new entitlement is taxable Australian property just after you acquire it; and

> > <a id="s124-1105-2-c-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  if there is more than one new entitlement—each new entitlement is taxable Australian property just after you acquire it; and

> <a id="s124-1105-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  you choose to obtain the roll-over.

> **Note:** Section 103-25 tells you when the choice must be made. No roll-over if Subdivision 124-C applies

<a id="s124-1105-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, there is no roll-over in relation to a [[*water entitlement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-entitlement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-entitlement) under this section if there is a roll-over in relation to the water entitlement under Subdivision 124-C (statutory licences). Meaning of water entitlement

<a id="s124-1105-4"></a>
**(4)**  A water entitlement is a legal or equitable right that an entity owns that relates to water, including a right to:

> <a id="s124-1105-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  receive water; or

> <a id="s124-1105-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  take water from a water resource; or

> <a id="s124-1105-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  have water delivered; or

> <a id="s124-1105-4-d"></a>
> **(d)**  deliver water; and includes a right that must be owned by the entity in order to own a right covered by paragraph (a), (b), (c) or (d).

> *[[*Example*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example):** Philip owns a share in Big Pump Irrigation Ltd. The share provides Philip with the right to receive dividends, to participate in the running of the company and to have a separate contractual agreement with Big Pump Irrigation Ltd for the delivery of 1 megalitre of water. Philip has such an agreement. Philip’s agreement is a water entitlement.
