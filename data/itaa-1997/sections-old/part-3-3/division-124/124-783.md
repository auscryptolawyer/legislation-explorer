# 124-783  Meaning of significant stakeholder, common stakeholder, significant stake and common stake

Significant stakeholder

<a id="s124-783-1"></a>
**(1)**  An original interest holder is a significant stakeholder for an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) if it had:

> <a id="s124-783-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*significant stake*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-significant-stake)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-significant-stake) in the original entity just before the arrangement started; and

> <a id="s124-783-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a significant stake in the replacement entity just after the arrangement was completed.

<a id="s124-783-2"></a>
**(2)**  Also, if an original interest holder is an acquiring entity, any other original interest holder is a significant stakeholder for an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) if it:

> <a id="s124-783-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  had a [[*significant stake*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-significant-stake)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-significant-stake) in the original entity just before the [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) started; and

> <a id="s124-783-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is an [[*associate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate) of the replacement entity just after the arrangement was completed. Common stakeholder

<a id="s124-783-3"></a>
**(3)**  An original interest holder is a common stakeholder for an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) if it had:

> <a id="s124-783-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*common stake*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-common-stake)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-common-stake) in the original entity just before the arrangement started; and

> <a id="s124-783-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a common stake in the replacement entity just after the arrangement was completed.

<a id="s124-783-4"></a>
**(4)**  If an acquiring entity for an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) is an original interest holder, each other original interest holder that has a replacement interest is a common stakeholder for the arrangement.

<a id="s124-783-5"></a>
**(5)**  No original interest holder is a common stakeholder for an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) if either the original entity or the replacement entity had at least 300 *members (for a company) or 300 beneficiaries (for a trust) just before the arrangement started. 82                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: special topics Part 3-3 Replacement-asset roll-overs Division 124 Section 124-783 Significant stake

<a id="s124-783-6"></a>
**(6)**  An entity has a significant stake in a company at a time if the entity, or the entity and the entity’s *associates between them:

> <a id="s124-783-6-a"></a>
> **(a)**  have at that time *shares carrying 30% or more of the voting rights in the company; or

> <a id="s124-783-6-b"></a>
> **(b)**  have at that time the right to receive 30% or more of any *dividends that the company may pay; or

> <a id="s124-783-6-c"></a>
> **(c)**  have at that time the right to receive 30% or more of any distribution of capital of the company.

> *[[*Example*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example):** There are 4 shareholders in YZT Company: Sonja has 60%, Mario has 20%, Peter has 10% and Dave has 10%. Sonja, Mario and Peter are associates. They each have a significant stake in YZT because, on an associate inclusive basis, they each have a 90% stake in YZT. Dave does not have a significant stake because his total stake, on an associate inclusive basis, is 10%.

<a id="s124-783-7"></a>
**(7)**  An entity has a significant stake in a trust at a time if the entity, or the entity and the entity’s *associates between them, had at that time the right to receive 30% or more of any distribution to beneficiaries of the trust of income or capital of the trust.

<a id="s124-783-8"></a>
**(8)**  No original interest holder has a significant stake in a company that has at least 300 *members or a trust that has at least 300 beneficiaries if it is reasonable for the company or the trustee of the trust to conclude that this is the case on the information available to it.

> **Note:** There are some cases where a company or trust will not be regarded as having 300 members or beneficiaries: see section 124-810. Common stake

> <a id="s124-783-9-a"></a>
> **(a)**  had 80% or more of: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             83 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Section 124-783A

> > **(i)**  the voting rights in the original entity just before the arrangement started; and

> > <a id="s124-783-9-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the voting rights in the replacement entity just after the arrangement was completed; or

> <a id="s124-783-9-b"></a>
> **(b)**  had the right to receive 80% or more of:

> > <a id="s124-783-9-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  any *dividends that the original entity may pay just before the arrangement started; and

> > <a id="s124-783-9-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  any distribution of capital of the replacement entity just after the arrangement was completed.

<a id="s124-783-10"></a>
**(10)**  If the original entity and the replacement entity are trusts, an entity, or 2 or more entities, have a common stake in the original entity just before the [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) started and in the replacement entity just after the arrangement was completed if the entity or entities, and their *associates, between them:

> <a id="s124-783-10-a"></a>
> **(a)**  had, just before the arrangement started, the right to receive 80% or more of any distribution to beneficiaries of the original entity of income or capital of the original entity; and

> <a id="s124-783-10-b"></a>
> **(b)**  had, just after the arrangement was completed, the right to receive 80% or more of any distribution to beneficiaries of the replacement entity of income or capital of that entity. 124-783A Rights that affect stakes

**(1)**  An entity has a significant stake in another entity if:

> <a id="s124-783-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the first entity has one or more [[*stake options*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-stake-option)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-stake-option) in the other entity; and

> <a id="s124-783-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the first entity would have such a stake (under section 124-783) if the first entity acquired [[*stake interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-stake-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-stake-interest) in the other entity under any of those stake options. 84                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: special topics Part 3-3 Replacement-asset roll-overs Division 124 Section 124-783A

> **Note:** Paragraph (b) is satisfied if there are any circumstances (e.g. the first entity exercises some but not all of the stake options) in which the first entity would have a significant stake in the other entity, even if in other circumstances the first entity would not have such a stake.

<a id="s124-783-2"></a>
**(2)**  An entity, or 2 or more entities, have a common stake in the original entity just before the [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) started and in the replacement entity just after the arrangement was completed if:

> <a id="s124-783-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entities:

> > <a id="s124-783-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  had one or more [[*stake options*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-stake-option)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-stake-option) in the original entity before the arrangement started; or

> > <a id="s124-783-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  have one or more stake options in the replacement entity; and

> <a id="s124-783-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entities would have such stakes (under section 124-783) if:

> > <a id="s124-783-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the entities had acquired [[*stake interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-stake-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-stake-interest) in the original entity under any of the stake options mentioned in subparagraph (a)(i); or

> > <a id="s124-783-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the entities acquired stake interests in the replacement entity under some or all of the stake options mentioned in subparagraph (a)(ii).

<a id="s124-783-3"></a>
**(3)**  Something is a stake option an entity has in another entity if it gives the first entity, or its *associates, a right to acquire the following (stake interests):

> <a id="s124-783-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the other entity is a company:

> > <a id="s124-783-3-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  voting rights in the company; or

> > <a id="s124-783-3-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the right to receive any part of any *dividends that the company may pay; or

> > <a id="s124-783-3-a-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  the right to receive any part of any distribution of capital of the company;

> <a id="s124-783-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if the other entity is a trust—the right to receive any part of any distribution to beneficiaries of the trust of income or capital of the trust; and the acquisition could occur before the end of 5 years after the [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) was completed.
