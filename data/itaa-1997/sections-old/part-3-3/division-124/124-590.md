# 124-590  Part of original right excised


<a id="s124-590-1"></a>
**(1)**  There is a partial roll-over if you *acquired the original right on or after 20 September 1985 and:

> <a id="s124-590-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the land to which the new right relates is different in area to the land the subject of the original right because a part (the excised part) of the land to which the original right related was excised or you relinquished it; and 64                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: special topics Part 3-3 Replacement-asset roll-overs Division 124 Section 124-595

> <a id="s124-590-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you received a payment for the expiry or surrender of the original right. The payment can include giving property: see section 103-5.

> **Note:** Section 124-600 sets out the effect on your cost base.

<a id="s124-590-2"></a>
**(2)**  There is no roll-over for the excised part. The [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base of the excised part is so much of the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base of the relevant [[*Crown lease*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-crown-lease)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-crown-lease) as is attributable to the excised part. Its *reduced cost base is worked out similarly.

> **Note:** You may make a capital gain or loss on the excised part because of CGT event C2.
