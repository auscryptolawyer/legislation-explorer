# 124-875  Effect on the transferor and transferee

Capital gains and losses disregarded

<a id="s124-875-1"></a>
**(1)**  Any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) from [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) A1 happening to the transferor under the trust restructure is disregarded (even if [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) J4 applies).

> **Note:** The effect of the roll-over may be reversed if the transferor does not cease to exist within 6 months: see section 104-195. Cost base is transferred

<a id="s124-875-2"></a>
**(2)**  The first element of the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base and *reduced cost base (for the transferee) of each [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that the transferee *acquires under the trust restructure is the same as the cost base and reduced cost base of that asset (for the transferor) just before that acquisition.

> **Note:** For the cost base and reduced cost base of interests in the transferee: see Subdivision 124-A. 102                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: special topics Part 3-3 Replacement-asset roll-overs Division 124 Section 124-975 Pre-CGT assets retain their status

<a id="s124-875-3"></a>
**(3)**  If the transferor *acquired any of the [[*CGT assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) *disposed of to the transferee under the trust restructure before 20 September 1985, the transferee is taken to have acquired it before that day.

<a id="s124-875-4"></a>
**(4)**  However, subsection (3) is taken never to have applied to such an asset of the transferee if subsection 104-195(4) (CGT event J4) applies to the transferee in relation to the asset. Exception: trading stock

<a id="s124-875-5"></a>
**(5)**  This section does not apply to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) if:

> <a id="s124-875-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the asset was an item of [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock) of the transferor and becomes an item of trading stock of the transferee; or

> <a id="s124-875-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the asset was not an item of trading stock of the transferor but becomes an item of trading stock of the transferee when the transferee *acquires it. Exception: asset must be taxable Australian property for foreign resident transferee

<a id="s124-875-6"></a>
**(6)**  For a transferee that is a foreign resident, this section only applies to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that is [[*taxable Australian property*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-property) just after the transferee *acquires it under the trust restructure.
