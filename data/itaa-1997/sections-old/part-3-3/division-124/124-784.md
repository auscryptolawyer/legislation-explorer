# 124-784  Cost base of equity or debt given within acquiring group

Purpose

<a id="s124-784-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section allocates an appropriate [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base to equity issued, or new debt owed, under the [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement), by a member of a [[*wholly-owned group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-wholly-owned-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-wholly-owned-group) to another member (the recipient) of the group, if:

> <a id="s124-784-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the acquiring entity is a member of the group; and

> <a id="s124-784-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the cost base of an original interest was transferred or allocated under section 124-782 because the original interest holder is a [[*significant stakeholder*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-significant-stakeholder)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-significant-stakeholder) or a [[*common stakeholder*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-common-stakeholder)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-common-stakeholder) for the arrangement. Allocation of cost base

<a id="s124-784-2"></a>
**(2)**  The first element of the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base of the equity or debt for the recipient is that part of the cost base of the original interest transferred or allocated under section 124-782 as:

> <a id="s124-784-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  may be reasonably allocated to the equity or debt; and

> <a id="s124-784-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is not more than the *market value of the equity or debt just after the [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) was completed. 124-784A When arrangement is a restructure
