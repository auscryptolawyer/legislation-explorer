# 124-80  Other requirements if you receive an asset


<a id="s124-80-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you receive another [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) for the event happening, you can choose to obtain a roll-over only if these other requirements are satisfied.

> **Note:** The roll-over consequences are set out in section 124-90. 40                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: special topics Part 3-3 Replacement-asset roll-overs Division 124 Section 124-85

<a id="s124-80-2"></a>
**(2)**  The other asset cannot become an item of your [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock) just after you [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) it, nor can it be a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) whose decline in value is worked out under Division 40 or deductions for which are calculated under Division 328 nor can it be a [[*registered emissions unit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-registered-emissions-unit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-registered-emissions-unit).

<a id="s124-80-3"></a>
**(3)**  The *market value of the other asset (when you [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) it) must be more than the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base of the original asset just before the event happens.
