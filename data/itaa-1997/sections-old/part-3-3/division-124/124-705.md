# 124-705  Extension or renewal of prospecting or mining entitlement


<a id="s124-705-1"></a>
**(1)**  There is a roll-over if: 68                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: special topics Part 3-3 Replacement-asset roll-overs Division 124 Section 124-710

> <a id="s124-705-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you hold one or more [[*CGT assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that are [[*prospecting entitlements*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-prospecting-entitlement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-prospecting-entitlement) or [[*mining entitlements*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-entitlement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-entitlement) (the original entitlement); and

> <a id="s124-705-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the original entitlement expires or you surrender it; and

> <a id="s124-705-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  you are granted one or more new prospecting entitlements or mining entitlements (the new entitlement); and

> <a id="s124-705-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the new entitlement relates to the same land as the original entitlement.

> **Note:** The roll-over consequences are set out in Subdivision 124-A. They might be modified: see section 124-730.

> **Note:** If there has been a capital improvement to the entitlement: see section 108-75.

<a id="s124-705-2"></a>
**(2)**  The new entitlement must have been granted in one of these ways:

> <a id="s124-705-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  by renewing or extending the term of the original entitlement where the renewal or extension is mainly due to your having held the original entitlement; or

> <a id="s124-705-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  by consolidating, or consolidating and dividing, the original entitlement; or

> <a id="s124-705-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  by subdividing the original entitlement; or

> <a id="s124-705-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  by converting a [[*prospecting entitlement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-prospecting-entitlement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-prospecting-entitlement) to a [[*mining entitlement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-entitlement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-entitlement), or a mining entitlement to a prospecting entitlement; or

> <a id="s124-705-2-e"></a>
> **(e)**  by excising or relinquishing a part of the land to which the original entitlement related; or

> <a id="s124-705-2-f"></a>
> **(f)**  by expanding the area of that land.
