# 124-710  Meaning of prospecting entitlement and mining entitlement


<a id="s124-710-1"></a>
**(1)**  A prospecting entitlement is:

> <a id="s124-710-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an authority, licence, permit or entitlement under an [[*Australian law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-law) or [[*foreign law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law) to prospect or explore for *minerals in an area; or

> <a id="s124-710-1-aa"></a>
> **(aa)**  an authority, licence, permit or entitlement under an Australian law to prospect or explore for [[*geothermal energy resources*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-geothermal-energy-resources)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-geothermal-energy-resources) in an area; or Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             69 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s124-710-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a lease of land that allows the lessee to prospect or explore for minerals or geothermal energy resources on the land; or

> <a id="s124-710-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  an interest in a thing referred to in paragraph (a), (aa) or (b).

<a id="s124-710-2"></a>
**(2)**  A mining entitlement is:

> <a id="s124-710-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an authority, licence, permit or entitlement under an [[*Australian law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-law) or [[*foreign law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law) to mine for *minerals in an area; or

> <a id="s124-710-2-aa"></a>
> **(aa)**  an authority, licence, permit or entitlement under an Australian law to extract energy from [[*geothermal energy resources*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-geothermal-energy-resources)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-geothermal-energy-resources) in an area; or

> <a id="s124-710-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a lease of land that allows the lessee to mine for minerals, or extract energy from geothermal energy resources, on the land; or

> <a id="s124-710-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  an interest in a thing referred to in paragraph (a), (aa) or (b).
