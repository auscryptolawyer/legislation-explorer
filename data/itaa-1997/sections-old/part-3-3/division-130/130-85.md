# 130-85  Interests in employee share trusts

Scope

<a id="s130-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if:

> <a id="s130-85-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) an *ESS interest under an [[*employee share scheme*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme); and

> <a id="s130-85-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  Subdivision 83A-B or 83A-C applies to the ESS interest; and

> <a id="s130-85-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the ESS interest is, or arises because of, an interest you hold in an [[*employee share trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-trust). Application of Division 83A, Part 3-1 and this Part

<a id="s130-85-2"></a>
**(2)**  Division 83A (Employee share schemes), Part 3-1 (Capital gains and losses: general topics) and this Part apply as if you were absolutely entitled to the relevant [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share) or right:

> <a id="s130-85-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  from the time of acquisition of the *ESS interest; and

> <a id="s130-85-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  until you no longer have an ESS interest in the share or right. 206                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: special topics Part 3-3 Investments Division 130 Section 130-85

> **Note:** An interest you hold in an employee share trust may give rise to an ESS interest because of the operation of section 83A-320.

> **Note:** As a result of subsection (2) of this section, CGT event E5 might happen at the time of acquisition. This may result in the trustee making a capital gain. However, any capital gain made by the beneficiary would be disregarded under section 130-80.

<a id="s130-85-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, if this section applies to you because an [[*associate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate) of yours *acquired the *ESS interest, Division 83A, this Part and Part 3-3 apply as if your associate were absolutely entitled to the relevant [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share) or right (instead of you):

> <a id="s130-85-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  either:

> > <a id="s130-85-3-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  if Subdivision 83A-B applies to the ESS interest—from the time of acquisition; or

> > <a id="s130-85-3-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if Subdivision 83A-C applies to the ESS interest—from immediately after the *ESS deferred taxing point for the ESS interest; and

> <a id="s130-85-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  until your associate no longer has an ESS interest in the share or right.

> **Note:** Once the ESS interest has been taxed to you under Subdivision 83A-B or 83A-C, section 83A-305 (which treats the interest as having been acquired by you, rather than your associate) is no longer relevant. Subsection (3) of this section ensures that your associate then gets the same tax treatment as you would have, had you originally acquired the interest. This does not, however, imply a disposal from you to your associate. Meaning of employee share trust

<a id="s130-85-4"></a>
**(4)**  An employee share trust, for an [[*employee share scheme*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme), is a trust whose sole activities are:

> <a id="s130-85-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  obtaining *shares or rights in a company; and

> <a id="s130-85-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  ensuring that *ESS interests in the company that are beneficial interests in those shares or rights are provided under the employee share scheme to employees, or to *associates of employees, of:

> > <a id="s130-85-4-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the company; or

> > <a id="s130-85-4-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a [[*subsidiary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary) of the company; and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             207 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s130-85-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  other activities that are merely incidental to the activities mentioned in paragraphs (a) and (b).
