# 130-75  Objects of Subdivision

The objects of this Subdivision are:

> <a id="s130-75-a"></a>
> **(a)**  to recognise that:

> > <a id="s130-75-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  Division 83A contains the primary rules for taxing gains on *ESS interests acquired under [[*employee share schemes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme); and

> > <a id="s130-75-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  [[*capital gains*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) and *capital losses on such interests should usually be disregarded during the period in which Division 83A applies to them; and

> <a id="s130-75-b"></a>
> **(b)**  to align the treatment of ESS interests under Division 83A and the CGT provisions by, for example:

> > <a id="s130-75-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  turning off certain special CGT rules; and

> > <a id="s130-75-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  extending some of the deeming provisions of that Division into the CGT provisions; and

> <a id="s130-75-c"></a>
> **(c)**  to disregard [[*employee share trusts*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-trust) for most CGT purposes, by treating ESS interests owned by such trusts as being directly owned by the beneficiaries of the trusts. 204                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: special topics Part 3-3 Investments Division 130 Section 130-80
