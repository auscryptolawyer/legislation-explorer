# 130-80  ESS interests acquired under employee share schemes

Capital gains and losses

<a id="s130-80-1"></a>
**(1)**  Disregard any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) to the extent that it results from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) if:

> <a id="s130-80-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the CGT event happens in relation to an *ESS interest you [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) under an [[*employee share scheme*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme); and

> <a id="s130-80-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the CGT event is not CGT event E4, G1 or K8; and

> <a id="s130-80-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if Subdivision 83A-B applies to the interest—the time of the acquisition is the time when the CGT event happens; and

> <a id="s130-80-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  if Subdivision 83A-C applies to the interest:

> > <a id="s130-80-1-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the time of the acquisition is the time when the CGT event happens; or

> > <a id="s130-80-1-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the CGT event happens on or before the *ESS deferred taxing point for the ESS interest.

<a id="s130-80-2"></a>
**(2)**  Subsection (1) does not apply if:

> <a id="s130-80-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  Subdivision 83A-C applies to the *ESS interest; and

> <a id="s130-80-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happens because you forfeit or lose the ESS interest (other than by disposing of it) on or before the *ESS deferred taxing point for the interest. General acquisition rule

<a id="s130-80-3"></a>
**(3)**  Subsection 109-5(2) (about when you acquire a CGT asset) does not apply to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) and a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) if:

> <a id="s130-80-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the CGT asset is:

> > <a id="s130-80-3-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share); or

> > <a id="s130-80-3-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a right to acquire a beneficial interest in a share; and

> <a id="s130-80-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the CGT event is CGT event A1; and

> <a id="s130-80-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  you acquire an *ESS interest; and

> <a id="s130-80-3-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the ESS interest is a beneficial interest in the share or right; and

> <a id="s130-80-3-e"></a>
> **(e)**  Subdivision 83A-B or 83A-C (about employee share schemes) applies to the ESS interest. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            205 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Market value substitution rule

<a id="s130-80-4"></a>
**(4)**  Sections 112-20 and 116-30 (about the market value substitution rule) do not apply to the extent that they relate to:

> <a id="s130-80-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you acquiring an *ESS interest to which Subdivision 83A-C (about employee share schemes) applies; or

> <a id="s130-80-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you:

> > <a id="s130-80-4-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  forfeiting an ESS interest; or

> > <a id="s130-80-4-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  forfeiting or losing an ESS interest that is a beneficial interest in a right (without you having disposed of the interest or exercised the right); if Subdivision 83A-B or 83A-C applies to the ESS interest (ignoring section 83A-310); or

> <a id="s130-80-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  you acquiring an ESS interest that:

> > <a id="s130-80-4-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  is a beneficial interest in a right; and

> > <a id="s130-80-4-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  is an ESS interest to which the provisions referred to in paragraphs 83A-33(1)(a) to (c) (about start ups) apply.
