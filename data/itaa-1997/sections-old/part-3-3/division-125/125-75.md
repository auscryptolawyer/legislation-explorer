# 125-75  Exceptions to subsection 125-70(2)

Employee share schemes

<a id="s125-75-1"></a>
**(1)**  In working out whether the requirements in subsection 125-70(2) are met, disregard each of the [[*ownership interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) described in subsections (2) and (3) if, just before the [[*demerger*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger), those interests (taking into account either or both of their number and value) 136                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: special topics Part 3-3 Demerger relief Division 125 Section 125-75 represented not more than 3% of the total [[*ownership interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) in the entity.

<a id="s125-75-2"></a>
**(2)**  An [[*ownership interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest), in a company, that is owned by an entity is disregarded under subsection (1) if:

> <a id="s125-75-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity acquired a beneficial interest in the ownership interest under an [[*employee share scheme*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme); and

> <a id="s125-75-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  these provisions apply to the beneficial interest:

> > <a id="s125-75-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  Subdivision 83A-B and the provisions referred to in paragraphs 83A-33(1)(a) to (c); or

> > <a id="s125-75-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  Subdivision 83A-B and the provisions referred to in paragraphs 83A-35(1)(a) and (b); or

> > <a id="s125-75-2-b-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  Subdivision 83A-C; and

> <a id="s125-75-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the ownership interest is not a fully-paid ordinary [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share).

<a id="s125-75-3"></a>
**(3)**  An [[*ownership interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest), in a trust, that is owned by an entity is disregarded under subsection (1) if:

> <a id="s125-75-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  both of the following would apply if Division 83A (about employee share schemes) applied to ownership interests in trusts in the same way as it applies to *shares:

> > <a id="s125-75-3-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the entity acquired a beneficial interest in the ownership interest under an [[*employee share scheme*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme);

> > <a id="s125-75-3-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the provisions referred to in subparagraph (2)(b)(i), (ii) or (iii) apply to the beneficial interest; and

> <a id="s125-75-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the ownership interest is not a fully-paid unit. Adjusting instruments

<a id="s125-75-4"></a>
**(4)**  In working out whether the requirements in subsection 125-70(2) are met, disregard each of the [[*ownership interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) described in subsection (5) (adjusting instruments) if, just before the [[*demerger*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger), those interests represented not more than 10%, or such greater percentage (not exceeding 17%) as is prescribed, of the ownership interests in the entity. <a id="s125-75-5"></a>
**(5)**  An [[*ownership interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) in a [[*listed public company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-listed-public-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-listed-public-company) or a [[*listed widely held trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-listed-widely-held-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-listed-widely-held-trust) that is the *head entity of a [[*demerger group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger-group) is disregarded under subsection (4) if:

> <a id="s125-75-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the adjusting instrument was issued on terms that ensure that its value is not adversely affected by an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) undertaken by the company or trust in relation to other ownership interests in the company or trust; and

> <a id="s125-75-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if the adjusting instrument can be converted into an ordinary [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share) in the company or an ordinary unit in the trust, any conversion will occur on a basis:

> > <a id="s125-75-5-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  that is set out in the terms of the issue of the instrument; and

> > <a id="s125-75-5-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  that is adjusted to take into account a capital reduction or a capital reconstruction; and

> <a id="s125-75-5-c"></a>
> **(c)**  before conversion, the owner of the adjusting instrument does not have a right to participate in distributions of profit or capital except as set out in the terms of the issue of the instrument; and

> <a id="s125-75-5-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the adjusting instrument deals with the effect of a [[*demerger*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger) that happens to the demerger group on the value of the instrument.

> *[[*Example*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example):** Some examples of adjusting instruments are: •  convertible preference shares, including reset preference shares; •  convertible notes; •  partly paid shares where the paid-up amount is adjusted to reflect a capital reduction. Additional exceptions

<a id="s125-75-6"></a>

<a id="s125-75-7"></a>
**(7)**  However, the total percentage of [[*ownership interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) to be disregarded under this section must not exceed 20% of the ownership interests in the entity.
