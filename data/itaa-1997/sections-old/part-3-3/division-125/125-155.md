# 125-155  Certain capital gains or losses disregarded for demerging entity

Any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) a [[*demerging entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerging-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerging-entity) makes from [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) A1, [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) C2, [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) C3 or [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) K6 happening to its [[*ownership interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) in a [[*demerged entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerged-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerged-entity) under a [[*demerger*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger) is disregarded.

> **Note:** The full list of CGT events is in section 104-5.

> **Note:** This section will not apply if section 125-100 applies. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            143 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026
