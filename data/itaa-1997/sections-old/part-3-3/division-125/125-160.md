# 125-160  No CGT event J1

[[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) J1 does not happen to a [[*demerged entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerged-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerged-entity) or a member of a [[*demerger group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger-group) under a [[*demerger*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger).
