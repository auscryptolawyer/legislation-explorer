# 125-85  Cost base adjustments where CGT event happens but no roll-over chosen


<a id="s125-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  You must adjust the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base and *reduced cost base of an [[*ownership interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) you own in a company or trust if:

> <a id="s125-85-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*demerger*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger) happens to a [[*demerger group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger-group) of which the company or trust is a member; and

> <a id="s125-85-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you owned an original interest in the *head entity of the demerger group just before the demerger; and

> <a id="s125-85-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happens to the original interest and you [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) a new interest under the demerger; and

> <a id="s125-85-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  you do not choose a roll-over under this Subdivision for the original interest.

<a id="s125-85-2"></a>
**(2)**  The adjustments you must make are the same as the adjustments you would have to make under section 125-80 for the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) bases and *reduced cost bases of the remaining original interests and new interests just after the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) if you could have chosen a roll-over under this Subdivision for the [[*demerger*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger) and you had done so.
