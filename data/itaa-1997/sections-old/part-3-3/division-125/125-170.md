# 125-170  Reduced cost base reduction if demerger asset subject to roll-over


<a id="s125-170-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*reduced cost base of a *CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-reduced-cost-base-of-a-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-reduced-cost-base-of-a-cgt-asset) is reduced if:

> <a id="s125-170-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the *market value of the asset is reduced because of a [[*demerger*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger); and

> <a id="s125-170-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  after the demerger the asset is *acquired by an entity from another entity (the transferor) in a situation where the transferor obtained a roll-over for the disposal; and

> <a id="s125-170-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the reduction occurred when the transferor owned the asset.

<a id="s125-170-2"></a>
**(2)**  The *reduced cost base of the asset as determined under the roll-over is reduced just after the roll-over to the extent of the reduction in *market value caused by the [[*demerger*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-demerger).

> **Note:** The rules in section 125-165 and this section deal with any value shift that might occur under the demerger and avoid the need for the general value shifting regime to apply.

<a id="s125-170-3"></a>
