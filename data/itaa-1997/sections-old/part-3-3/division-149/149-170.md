# 149-170  Effect of demutualisation of interposed company


<a id="s149-170-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section modifies the treatment of evidence that an entity (the head entity) gives the Commissioner under section 149-55 as to the [[*ultimate owners*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ultimate-owner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ultimate-owner) who had *underlying interests in the asset at a particular time if another entity (the interposed company):

> <a id="s149-170-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  was:

> > <a id="s149-170-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a [[*mutual insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mutual-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mutual-insurance-company); or

> > <a id="s149-170-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a [[*mutual affiliate company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mutual-affiliate-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mutual-affiliate-company); at the end of the [[*starting day*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-starting-day)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-starting-day) (as determined under subsection 149-60(6)) for the head entity; and

> <a id="s149-170-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  has since stopped being a company of either of those kinds, but either:

> > <a id="s149-170-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  has continued in existence as a company covered by paragraph 149-50(1)(a) or (e) or a [[*publicly traded unit trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-publicly-traded-unit-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-publicly-traded-unit-trust); or

> > <a id="s149-170-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  has undergone a demutualisation in relation to which Division 316 (Demutualisation of friendly society health or life insurers) applied and has continued in existence as a company; and

> <a id="s149-170-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  when it stopped being an entity of either of those kinds (the stopping time), had more than 50 members.

<a id="s149-170-2"></a>
**(2)**  The head entity may require the Commissioner to treat the evidence on the assumption that an [[*ultimate owner*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ultimate-owner)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ultimate-owner) who:

> <a id="s149-170-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  immediately before the stopping time was a member of the interposed company; and

> <a id="s149-170-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  immediately after the stopping time had, through the interposed company, an *underlying interest in the asset; had the interest at all times from and including the end of the [[*starting day*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-starting-day)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-starting-day) until immediately after the stopping time.
