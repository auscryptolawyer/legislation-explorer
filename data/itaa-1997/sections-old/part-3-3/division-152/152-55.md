# 152-55  Meaning of significant individual

An individual is a significant individual in a company or a trust at a time if, at that time, the individual has a [[*small business participation percentage*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-small-business-participation-percentage)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-small-business-participation-percentage) in the company or trust of at least 20%.
