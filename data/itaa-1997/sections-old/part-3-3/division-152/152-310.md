# 152-310  Consequences of choice

Consequences in all cases

<a id="s152-310-1"></a>
**(1)**  If the individual, company or trust makes the choice mentioned in section 152-305 for any part of the [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) from the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), that part of the capital gain equal to its [[*CGT exempt amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-exempt-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-exempt-amount) is disregarded. Additional consequences in relation to company or trust

<a id="s152-310-2"></a>
**(2)**  Any payment or part of one the company or trust makes to comply with section 152-325:

> <a id="s152-310-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  is not assessable income, and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income), of the [[*CGT concession stakeholder*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-concession-stakeholder)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-concession-stakeholder) to whom it is made; and

> <a id="s152-310-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  cannot be deducted from the company’s or trust’s assessable income. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            275 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Additional consequences in relation to interposed entities

<a id="s152-310-3"></a>
**(3)**  If:

> <a id="s152-310-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity (the paying entity) receives a payment (whether directly or indirectly through one or more interposed entities) that a company or trust makes to comply with section 152-325; and

> <a id="s152-310-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the paying entity passes on the payment to the [[*CGT concession stakeholder*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-concession-stakeholder)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-concession-stakeholder) or another interposed entity; then:

> <a id="s152-310-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the payment cannot be deducted from the paying entity’s assessable income; and

> <a id="s152-310-3-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the payment received by the paying entity is not assessable income and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income).
