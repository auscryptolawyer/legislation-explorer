# 152-12  Special conditions for CGT event D1


<a id="s152-12-1"></a>
**(1)**  Paragraphs 152-10(1)(a) and (d) do not apply in the case of [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) D1.

<a id="s152-12-2"></a>
**(2)**  Instead, it is a basic condition that the right you create that triggers the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) must be inherently connected with a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) of yours that satisfies the active asset test (see section 152-35).
