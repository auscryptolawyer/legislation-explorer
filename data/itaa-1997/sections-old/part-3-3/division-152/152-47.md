# 152-47  Spouses or children taken to be affiliates for certain passively held CGT assets


<a id="s152-47-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if:

> <a id="s152-47-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  one entity (the asset owner) owns a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) (whether the asset is tangible or intangible); and

> <a id="s152-47-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  either:

> > <a id="s152-47-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the asset is used, or held ready for use, in the course of carrying on a *business in an income year by another entity (the business entity); or

> > <a id="s152-47-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the asset is inherently connected with a business that is carried on in an income year by another entity (the business entity); and

> <a id="s152-47-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the business entity is not (apart from this section) an [[*affiliate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affiliate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affiliate) of, or [[*connected with*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-connected-with)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-connected-with), the asset owner.

> **Note:** The meaning of connected with an entity is affected by section 152-78.

<a id="s152-47-2"></a>
**(2)**  For the purposes of this Subdivision, in determining whether the business entity is an [[*affiliate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affiliate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affiliate) of, or is [[*connected with*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-connected-with)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-connected-with), the asset owner, take the following to be affiliates of an individual:

> <a id="s152-47-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a *spouse of the individual;

> <a id="s152-47-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*child*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-child)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-child) of the individual, being a child who is under 18 years.

<a id="s152-47-3"></a>
**(3)**  If an entity is an [[*affiliate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affiliate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affiliate) of, or [[*connected with*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-connected-with)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-connected-with), another entity as a result of subsection (2), then the *spouse or [[*child*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-child)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-child) mentioned in that subsection is, in addition, taken to be an affiliate of the individual for the purposes of this Subdivision, and for the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             255 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 purposes of sections 328-110 to 328-125 to the extent that they relate to this Subdivision.

> *[[*Example*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example):** The spouse or child mentioned in subsection (2) is taken to be an affiliate of the individual for the purposes of working out which entities are affiliates of or connected with entities under section 152-48.

<a id="s152-47-4"></a>
**(4)**  To avoid doubt, subsection (2) applies:

> <a id="s152-47-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  for the purposes of reducing or disregarding, under this Division, any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) from any [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset); but

> <a id="s152-47-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  only while:

> > <a id="s152-47-4-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a *spouse remains a spouse; or

> > <a id="s152-47-4-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a [[*child*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-child)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-child) remains a child who is under 18 years.
