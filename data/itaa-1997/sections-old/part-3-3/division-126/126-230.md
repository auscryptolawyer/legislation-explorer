# 126-230  Beneficiaries’ entitlements not be discretionary etc.


<a id="s126-230-1"></a>
**(1)**  The conditions in subsections (2) and (3) must be met:

> <a id="s126-230-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if subsection 126-225(2) applies—at all times during the period:

> > <a id="s126-230-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  starting at the start time; and

> > <a id="s126-230-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  ending at the transfer time; and

> <a id="s126-230-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—at the transfer time. CGT event E4 is capable of happening

<a id="s126-230-2"></a>
**(2)**  The first condition is met at a particular time if, at that time, [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) E4 is capable of happening to all of the *membership interests in each of the trusts.

> **Note:** A roll-over cannot be chosen if either trust is a discretionary trust. Beneficiaries’ entitlements not discretionary

<a id="s126-230-3"></a>
**(3)**  The second condition is met at a particular time if, at that time, the manner or extent to which each beneficiary of each trust can 174                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: special topics Part 3-3 Same-asset roll-overs Division 126 Section 126-235 benefit from the trust is not capable of being significantly affected by the exercise, or non-exercise, of a power.

<a id="s126-230-4"></a>
**(4)**  However, if both trusts are [[*managed investment trusts*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-managed-investment-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-managed-investment-trust), disregard a power if the power’s existence at that time does not significantly affect the *market value at that time of each *membership interest in each of the trusts.
