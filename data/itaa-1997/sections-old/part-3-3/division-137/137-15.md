# 137-15  CGT event does not happen when a certain kind of granny flat arrangement is entered into

A [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) does not happen, to the extent it relates to creating a [[*granny flat interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-granny-flat-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-granny-flat-interest) in a [[*dwelling*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling) under an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) by entering into the arrangement at a particular time (the start time), if:

> <a id="s137-15-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the individual who holds, or who is to hold, the granny flat interest under the arrangement is [[*eligible for a granny flat interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-for-a-granny-flat-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-for-a-granny-flat-interest) at the start time; and

> <a id="s137-15-b"></a>
> **(b)**  another individual:

> > <a id="s137-15-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  holds an [[*ownership interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) in the dwelling at the start time; or

> > <a id="s137-15-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  agrees, under the arrangement, to [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) an ownership interest in a dwelling that is to be the dwelling in which the first-mentioned individual is to hold the granny flat interest; and

> <a id="s137-15-c"></a>
> **(c)**  at the start time, both individuals are parties to the arrangement; and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            221 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s137-15-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the arrangement:

> > <a id="s137-15-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  is in writing; and

> > <a id="s137-15-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  indicates an intention for the parties to the arrangement to be legally bound by it; and

> <a id="s137-15-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the arrangement is not of a commercial nature.
