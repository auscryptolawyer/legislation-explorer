# 137-10  Meaning of key terms


<a id="s137-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  An individual holds a granny flat interest in a [[*dwelling*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling) under an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) if the individual has a right to occupy the dwelling for life that has been conferred by the arrangement.

<a id="s137-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  An individual is eligible for a granny flat interest at a particular time if: 220                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: special topics Part 3-3 Granny flat arrangements Division 137 Section 137-15

> <a id="s137-10-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the individual reached [[*pension age*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pension-age)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pension-age) at or before that time; or

> <a id="s137-10-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the individual:

> > <a id="s137-10-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  needs, because of a disability, assistance to carry out most day-to-day activities; and

> > <a id="s137-10-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  is likely to continue to need that assistance, because of that disability, for at least 12 months after that time.

<a id="s137-10-3"></a>
**(3)**  This Subdivision applies:

> <a id="s137-10-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  to a [[*dwelling*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)’s [[*adjacent land*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjacent-land)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjacent-land) in a corresponding way to the way Subdivision 118-B applies to the adjacent land; or

> <a id="s137-10-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  to an [[*adjacent structure*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjacent-structure)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjacent-structure) of a flat or home unit in a corresponding way to the way Subdivision 118-B applies to the adjacent structure.

> **Note:** Subsections 118-120(1) and (5) provide that Subdivision 118-B (about main residences) applies to adjacent land and adjacent structures as if they were a dwelling.
