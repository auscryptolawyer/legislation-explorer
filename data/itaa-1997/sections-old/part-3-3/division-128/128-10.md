# 128-10  Capital gain or loss when you die is disregarded

When you die, a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) that results for a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) you owned just before dying is disregarded.

> **Note:** Section 104-215 sets out an exception to this rule if the CGT asset passes to a beneficiary in your estate who is: •    an exempt entity; or •    the trustee of a complying superannuation entity; or •    a foreign resident.
