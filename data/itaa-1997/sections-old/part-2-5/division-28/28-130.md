# 28-130  Replacing one car with another


<a id="s28-130-1"></a>
**(1)**  For the purposes of using the “log book” method, you may nominate one [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car) as having replaced another car with effect from a day specified in the nomination.

<a id="s28-130-2"></a>
**(2)**  After the nomination takes effect, the replacement [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car) is treated as the original car, and the original car is treated as a different car. This means that you do not need to repeat for the replacement car the steps you have already taken for the original car under this Subdivision.

<a id="s28-130-3"></a>
**(3)**  You must record the nomination in writing before you lodge your [[*income tax return*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return) for the income year in which the nomination takes effect. However, the Commissioner may allow you to do it later.

<a id="s28-130-4"></a>
**(4)**  You must retain the nomination document until the end of the period for which you must retain the last log book that you began to keep for the original [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car) before the day of effect of the nomination.

<a id="s28-130-5"></a>
**(5)**  Section 28-150 (which is about retaining log books) applies to the nomination document in the same way as it applies to that last log book.
