# 28-180  Car expenses related to award transport payments


<a id="s28-180-1"></a>
**(1)**  Subdivision 900-I (Award transport payments) allows certain losses or outgoings to be deducted without getting written evidence. The losses or outgoings are [[*transport expenses*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transport-expense)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transport-expense) related to an allowance or reimbursement paid or payable to you by your employer under an *industrial instrument that was in force on 29 October 1986. 238                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Rules about deductibility of particular kinds of amounts Part 2-5 Car expenses Division 28 Section 28-185

> **Note:** This Subdivision also applies to entities that are not employers, but pay (or are liable to pay) withholding payments covered by subsection 28-185(3).

<a id="s28-180-2"></a>
**(2)**  If that Subdivision lets you deduct [[*car expenses*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-expense)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-expense), or parts of [[*car expenses*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-expense)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-expense), without getting written evidence, you don’t need to use any of the 2 methods to calculate your deductions for those expenses or parts of expenses.

<a id="s28-180-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, your use of the 2 methods for other [[*car expenses*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-expense)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-expense) you incur for the [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car) for the income year is affected, unless you elect not to rely on Subdivision 900-I. Section 900-250 deals with this matter.
