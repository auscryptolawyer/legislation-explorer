# 28-35  Substantiation

To use this method, you do not need to substantiate the [[*car expenses*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-expense)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-expense) for the [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car).
