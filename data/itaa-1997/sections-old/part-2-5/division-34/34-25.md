# 34-25  Application to register the design


<a id="s34-25-1"></a>
**(1)**  The employer of an employee who has, or will have, a *non-compulsory [[*uniform*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-uniform)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-uniform) can apply to the [[*Industry Secretary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-industry-secretary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-industry-secretary) for the *design of the uniform to be registered.

> **Note:** This Division also applies to entities that are not employers: see Subdivision 34-A. Meaning of design of a uniform

<a id="s34-25-2"></a>
**(2)**  The design of a [[*uniform*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-uniform)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-uniform) includes features such as its colouring, construction, durability, ornamentation, pattern and shape. Form of application

<a id="s34-25-3"></a>
**(3)**  The application must be:

> <a id="s34-25-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  in writing; and

> <a id="s34-25-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  in a form approved in writing by the [[*Industry Secretary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-industry-secretary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-industry-secretary); and

> <a id="s34-25-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  accompanied by such information as the Industry Secretary requires. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            367 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026
