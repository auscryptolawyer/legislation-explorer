# 34-50  Changes to the Register

Removal of registration

<a id="s34-50-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*Industry Secretary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-industry-secretary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-industry-secretary) must remove an entry for a *design from the Register of Approved Occupational Clothing if requested to do so by the employer who applied for the design to be registered. 370                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Rules about deductibility of particular kinds of amounts Part 2-5 Non-compulsory uniforms Division 34 Section 34-55 Correcting errors and mistakes

<a id="s34-50-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*Industry Secretary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-industry-secretary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-industry-secretary) may correct a clerical error or an obvious mistake in an entry for a design in the Register and, if the Industry Secretary does so, the correction takes effect on the day on which the design to which the entry relates was registered.
