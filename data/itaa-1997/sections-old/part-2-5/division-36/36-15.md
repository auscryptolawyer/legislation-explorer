# 36-15  How to deduct tax losses of entities other than corporate tax entities


<a id="s36-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  Your [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss for a [[*loss year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-loss-year) is deducted in a later income year as follows if you are not a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) at any time during the later income year.

> **Note:** See section 36-17 for the deduction of a tax loss of an entity that is a corporate tax entity at any time during the later income year.

> **Note:** A tax loss can be deducted only to the extent that it has not already been utilised: see subsection 960-20(1). If you have no net exempt income

<a id="s36-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  If your total assessable income for the later income year exceeds your total deductions (other than [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) losses), you deduct the tax loss from that excess. If you have net exempt income

<a id="s36-15-3"></a>
**(3)**  If you have [[*net exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-exempt-income) for the later income year and your total assessable income (if any) for the later income year exceeds your total deductions (except [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) losses), you deduct the tax loss: 386                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Rules about deductibility of particular kinds of amounts Part 2-5 Tax losses of earlier income years Division 36 Section 36-17

> <a id="s36-15-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  first, from your net exempt income; and

> <a id="s36-15-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  secondly, from the part of your total assessable income that exceeds those deductions.

<a id="s36-15-4"></a>
**(4)**  However, if you have [[*net exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-exempt-income) for the later income year and those deductions exceed your total assessable income, then:

> <a id="s36-15-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  subtract that excess from your net exempt income; and

> <a id="s36-15-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  deduct the tax loss from any net exempt income that remains. To work out your net exempt income: see section 36-20. General

<a id="s36-15-5"></a>
**(5)**  If you have 2 or more [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) losses, you deduct them in the order in which you incurred them.
