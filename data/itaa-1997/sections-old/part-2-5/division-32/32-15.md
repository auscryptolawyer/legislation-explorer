# 32-15  No deduction for property used for providing entertainment

To the extent that you use property in providing [[*entertainment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-entertainment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-entertainment), your use of the property is taken not to be for the [[*purpose of producing assessable income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income) if section 32-5 would stop you deducting a loss or outgoing if you incurred it in the income year in providing the entertainment. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             349 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> **Note:** Under some provisions of this Act, in order to deduct an amount for your property, you must have used the property for the purpose of producing assessable income.
