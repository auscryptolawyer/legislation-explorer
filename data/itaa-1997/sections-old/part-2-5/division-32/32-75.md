# 32-75  Commissioner may treat you as having incurred entertainment expense

If:

> <a id="s32-75-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you incur a loss or outgoing under an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement); and

> <a id="s32-75-b"></a>
> **(b)**  someone provides [[*entertainment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-entertainment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-entertainment) under the arrangement to you or someone else; and

> <a id="s32-75-c"></a>
> **(c)**  section 32-5 would have stopped you deducting the loss or outgoing under section 8-1 (which deals with general deductions) if you had incurred it in respect of providing that entertainment; this Division applies to you as if you had incurred the loss or outgoing in providing that entertainment, to the extent (if any) that the Commissioner thinks reasonable. 358                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Rules about deductibility of particular kinds of amounts Part 2-5 Entertainment expenses Division 32 Section 32-80

> **Note:** This means that section 32-5 will prevent you from deducting the loss or outgoing under section 8-1 unless an exception applies.

> *[[*Example*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example):** A company pays $1,000 to sponsor a football game. Under the same arrangement, the company is given a viewing box at the game. To the extent the Commissioner thinks reasonable, he or she can treat the company as having incurred the $1,000 in providing entertainment.
