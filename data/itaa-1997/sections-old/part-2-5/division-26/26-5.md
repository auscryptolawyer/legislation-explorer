# 26-5  Penalties


<a id="s26-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  You cannot deduct under this Act:

> <a id="s26-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an amount (however described) payable, by way of penalty, under an [[*Australian law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-law) or a [[*foreign law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law); or

> <a id="s26-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an amount ordered by a court to be paid on the conviction of an entity for an offence against an [[*Australian law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-law) or a [[*foreign law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-law). (1A) Without limiting paragraph (1)(a), you cannot deduct under this Act the [[*general interest charge*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-interest-charge)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-interest-charge) or the [[*shortfall interest charge*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-shortfall-interest-charge)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-shortfall-interest-charge).

<a id="s26-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section does not apply to an amount payable, by way of penalty, under Subdivision 162-D of the [[*GST Act*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act).
