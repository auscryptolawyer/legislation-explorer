# 26-25  Interest or royalty


<a id="s26-25-1"></a>
**(1)**  You cannot deduct under this Act interest (within the meaning of Division 11A of Part III of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936) or a [[*royalty*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-royalty)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-royalty) if: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            171 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Section 26-25A

> <a id="s26-25-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  Subdivision 12-F in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953 requires you to withhold an amount from the interest or royalty; and

> <a id="s26-25-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  either:

> > <a id="s26-25-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you fail to withhold the amount; or

> > <a id="s26-25-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  after withholding the amount, you fail to comply with section 16-70 in that Schedule in relation to that amount.

<a id="s26-25-2"></a>
**(2)**  You cannot deduct under this Act interest (within the meaning of Division 11A of Part III of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936), or a [[*royalty*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-royalty)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-royalty), that is in the form of a *non-cash benefit if:

> <a id="s26-25-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  section 14-5 or 14-10 in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953 requires you to pay an amount to the Commissioner before providing the benefit, because of Subdivision 12-F in that Schedule; and

> <a id="s26-25-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you fail to pay the amount as required by that section.

<a id="s26-25-3"></a>
**(3)**  If:

> <a id="s26-25-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  apart from subsection (1) or (2), you can deduct interest (within the meaning of Division 11A of Part III of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936) or a [[*royalty*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-royalty)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-royalty) for an income year; and

> <a id="s26-25-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the [[*withholding tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-withholding-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-withholding-tax) payable for the interest or the royalty is paid; you can deduct the interest or royalty for that income year. 26-25A Payments to employees—labour mobility programs No deduction to extent amount not withheld

<a id="s26-25-1"></a>

> <a id="s26-25-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you fail to withhold the amount; or

> <a id="s26-25-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  after withholding the amount, you fail to comply with section 16-70 in that Schedule in relation to that amount.

> **Note:** Section 16-70 in that Schedule requires you to pay the amount to the Commissioner. Deduction to extent amount not withheld but withholding tax paid

<a id="s26-25-2"></a>
**(2)**  You can deduct, for an income year, salary, wages, commission, bonuses or allowances to the extent that:

> <a id="s26-25-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you cannot deduct the salary, wages, commission, bonuses or allowances for that income year only because of subsection (1) of this section; and

> <a id="s26-25-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the [[*labour mobility program withholding tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-labour-mobility-program-withholding-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-labour-mobility-program-withholding-tax) payable for the salary, wages, commission, bonuses or allowance is paid.
