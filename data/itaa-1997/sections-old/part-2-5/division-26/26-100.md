# 26-100  Expenditure attributable to water infrastructure improvement payments


<a id="s26-100-1"></a>
**(1)**  You cannot deduct under this Act [[*SRWUIP expenditure*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-expenditure)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-expenditure) if the matching [[*SRWUIP payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment) is, or is reasonably expected to be, [[*non-assessable non-exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income) (whether for you or for another entity) under section 59-65.

<a id="s26-100-2"></a>
**(2)**  SRWUIP expenditure, in respect of a [[*SRWUIP program*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program), is expenditure that:

> <a id="s26-100-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you incur that satisfies an obligation under an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) under the program; and

> <a id="s26-100-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is, or is reasonably expected to be, matched by a [[*SRWUIP payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment) in respect of the program. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            193 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

<a id="s26-100-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, treat the expenditure as if it had never been SRWUIP expenditure if it is no longer reasonable to expect that the expenditure will be matched by a [[*SRWUIP payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment) in respect of the program.
