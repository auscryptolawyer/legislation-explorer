# 26-80  Financing costs on loans to pay superannuation contribution


<a id="s26-80-1"></a>
**(1)**  You can only deduct under this Act a [[*financing cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financing-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financing-cost) connected with a contribution you make to a *superannuation plan if you can deduct the contribution under Subdivision 290-B. 190                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Rules about deductibility of particular kinds of amounts Part 2-5 Some amounts you cannot deduct, or cannot deduct in full Division 26 Section 26-85

<a id="s26-80-2"></a>
**(2)**  A financing cost connected with a contribution is expenditure incurred to the extent that it relates to obtaining finance to make the contribution, including:

> <a id="s26-80-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  interest, and payments in the nature of interest; and

> <a id="s26-80-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  expenses of borrowing.
