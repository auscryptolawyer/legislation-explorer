# 26-99  Excess transfer balance tax cannot be deducted

You cannot deduct under this Act an amount of [[*excess transfer balance tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-excess-transfer-balance-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-excess-transfer-balance-tax) that you pay. 192                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Rules about deductibility of particular kinds of amounts Part 2-5 Some amounts you cannot deduct, or cannot deduct in full Division 26 Section 26-99A 26-99A Division 296 tax cannot be deducted You cannot deduct under this Act any of the following:

> <a id="s26-99-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an amount of [[*Division 296 tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-division-296-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-division-296-tax) that you pay;

> <a id="s26-99-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an amount of [[*Division 296 debt account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-division-296-debt-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-division-296-debt-account) discharge liability that you pay. 26-99B Build to rent development misuse tax cannot be deducted You cannot deduct under this Act an amount of [[*build to rent development misuse tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-build-to-rent-development-misuse-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-build-to-rent-development-misuse-tax) that you pay. 26-99C Australian IIR/UTPR tax and Australian DMT tax cannot be deducted You cannot deduct under this Act an amount of *Australian IIR/UTPR tax or [[*Australian DMT tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-dmt-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-dmt-tax) that you pay.
