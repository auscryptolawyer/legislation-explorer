# 26-68  Loss from disposal of eligible venture capital investments

Partners in VCLPs and ESVCLPs

<a id="s26-68-1"></a>
**(1)**  You cannot deduct under this Act your share of a loss made from the disposal or other realisation of an [[*eligible venture capital investment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment) if:

> <a id="s26-68-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is made by a [[*VCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp), or an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp), that is [[*unconditionally registered*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered); and

> <a id="s26-68-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  were that disposal or other realisation to be a *disposal of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), your share of any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) would be disregarded under section 118-405 or 118-407. Partners in AFOFs

<a id="s26-68-2"></a>
**(2)**  You cannot deduct under this Act your share of a loss made from the disposal or other realisation of an [[*eligible venture capital investment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment) if:

> <a id="s26-68-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is made by:

> > <a id="s26-68-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  an [[*AFOF*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-afof)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-afof) that is [[*unconditionally registered*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered); or

> > <a id="s26-68-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a [[*VCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp), or an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp), that is unconditionally registered and in which an AFOF that is [[*unconditionally registered*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered) is a partner; and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            189 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s26-68-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  were that disposal or other realisation to be a *disposal of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), your share of any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) would be disregarded under section 118-410. Eligible venture capital investors

<a id="s26-68-3"></a>
**(3)**  You cannot deduct under this Act a loss made from the disposal or other realisation of an [[*eligible venture capital investment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment) if:

> <a id="s26-68-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are an [[*eligible venture capital investor*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investor)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investor); and

> <a id="s26-68-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  were that disposal or other realisation to be a *disposal of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) would be disregarded under section 118-415.
