# 27-25  GST groups and GST joint ventures


<a id="s27-25-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of a [[*GST group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-group) is to be treated, for the purposes of this Division, as if Subdivision 48-B of the [[*GST Act*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act) (other than subsections 48-45(3) and (4)) did not apply to that member.

<a id="s27-25-2"></a>
**(2)**  A [[*participant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participant) in a [[*GST joint venture*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-joint-venture)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-joint-venture) is to be treated, for the purposes of this Division, as if Subdivision 51-B of the [[*GST Act*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act) did not apply to that participant.
