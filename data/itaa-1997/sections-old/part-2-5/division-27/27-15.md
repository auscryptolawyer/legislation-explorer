# 27-15  GST payments


<a id="s27-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  You cannot deduct under this Act a loss or outgoing consisting of a payment under Division 33 of the [[*GST Act*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act).

<a id="s27-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section does not apply to the payment:

> <a id="s27-15-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  to the extent (if any) that the *net amount to which the payment relates was increased under section 21-5 of the [[*Wine Tax Act*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-wine-tax-act)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-wine-tax-act) (which allows for such increases to take account of wine equalisation tax); and

> <a id="s27-15-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  to the extent (if any) that the *net amount was increased under section 13-5 of the [[*Luxury Car Tax Act*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-luxury-car-tax-act)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-luxury-car-tax-act) (which allows for such increases to take account of luxury car tax); and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            203 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s27-15-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  to the extent (if any) that the *net amount was increased under paragraph 13-10(1)(a) of the Luxury Car Tax Act (which allows for such alterations to take account of increasing luxury car tax adjustments under that Act).

<a id="s27-15-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section does not apply to the payment of [[*assessed GST*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-assessed-gst)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-assessed-gst) (under section 33-15 of the [[*GST Act*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act)) on a [[*taxable importation*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-importation)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-importation) that:

> <a id="s27-15-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  was not a [[*creditable importation*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-creditable-importation)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-creditable-importation); or

> <a id="s27-15-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  was *partly creditable; but only to the extent that that payment of assessed GST exceeds the [[*input tax credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-input-tax-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-input-tax-credit) (if any) to which you are entitled for that importation.

<a id="s27-15-4"></a>
**(4)**  This section does not apply to the payment of an [[*assessed net amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-assessed-net-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-assessed-net-amount) under section 33-3 or 33-5 of the [[*GST Act*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst-act) to the extent that the assessed net amount includes [[*GST*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst) on a [[*taxable supply*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply) that:

> <a id="s27-15-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  exceeds the [[*input tax credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-input-tax-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-input-tax-credit) (if any) to which you are entitled for a [[*creditable acquisition*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-creditable-acquisition)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-creditable-acquisition) that relates to that supply; and

> <a id="s27-15-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is payable by you (and is not payable by the supplier of that supply) because of the operation of Division 83, 84 or 86 of the GST Act.
