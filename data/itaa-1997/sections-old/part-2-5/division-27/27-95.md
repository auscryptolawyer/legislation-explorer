# 27-95  Balancing adjustment events


<a id="s27-95-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*termination value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value) of a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) is reduced if the relevant [[*balancing adjustment event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event) is a [[*taxable supply*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply). The reduction is an amount equal to the [[*GST*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst) payable on the supply. 210                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Rules about deductibility of particular kinds of amounts Part 2-5 Effect of input tax credits etc. on deductions Division 27 Section 27-100

<a id="s27-95-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, subsection (1) does not apply if the [[*termination value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value) of the [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) is modified under Division 40 to be its *market value.

<a id="s27-95-3"></a>
**(3)**  The [[*termination value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value) of a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) is increased if the entity that [[*held*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held) the asset has a [[*decreasing adjustment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-decreasing-adjustment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-decreasing-adjustment) that relates directly or indirectly to that [[*taxable supply*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply) in the income year in which the [[*balancing adjustment event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event) occurred. The increase is the amount of the decreasing adjustment.

<a id="s27-95-4"></a>
**(4)**  The [[*termination value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value) of a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) is decreased if the entity that [[*held*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held) the asset has an [[*increasing adjustment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-increasing-adjustment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-increasing-adjustment) that relates directly or indirectly to that [[*taxable supply*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply) in the income year in which the [[*balancing adjustment event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event) occurred. The decrease is the amount of the increasing adjustment.

<a id="s27-95-5"></a>
**(5)**  An amount is included in the assessable income of the entity that [[*held*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held) the asset if the entity has a [[*decreasing adjustment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-decreasing-adjustment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-decreasing-adjustment) that relates directly or indirectly to that [[*taxable supply*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply) in a later income year. The amount included is the amount of the decreasing adjustment.

<a id="s27-95-6"></a>
**(6)**  The entity that [[*held*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held) the asset can deduct an amount if the entity has an [[*increasing adjustment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-increasing-adjustment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-increasing-adjustment) that relates directly or indirectly to that [[*taxable supply*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-supply) in a later income year. The amount it can deduct is the amount of the increasing adjustment.
