# 620-20  Disregard body’s capital gains and losses from CGT assets


<a id="s620-20-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if:

> <a id="s620-20-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the body *disposes of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) to the company because the body ceases to exist; or

> <a id="s620-20-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  another [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happens to a CGT asset of the body because the body ceases to exist.

<a id="s620-20-2"></a>
**(2)**  A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or a [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) the body makes from the [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) is disregarded.
