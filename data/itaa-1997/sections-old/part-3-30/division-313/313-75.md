# 313-75  General interest charge

If an amount of [[*assessed first home super saver tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-assessed-first-home-super-saver-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-assessed-first-home-super-saver-tax) that you are liable to pay remains unpaid after the time by which it is due to be paid, you are liable to pay the [[*general interest charge*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-interest-charge)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-interest-charge) on the unpaid amount for each day in the period that:

> <a id="s313-75-a"></a>
> **(a)**  begins on the day on which the amount was due to be paid; and

> <a id="s313-75-b"></a>
> **(b)**  ends on the last day on which, at the end of the day, any of the following remains unpaid:

> > <a id="s313-75-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the assessed first home super saver tax;

> > <a id="s313-75-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  general interest charge on any of the assessed first home super saver tax.

> **Note:** The general interest charge is worked out under Part IIA of the Taxation Administration Act 1953. 398                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 First home super saver scheme Division 313 Section 313-80
