# 313-60  First home super saver tax

You are liable to pay [[*first home super saver tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-first-home-super-saver-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-first-home-super-saver-tax) if:

> <a id="s313-60-a"></a>
> **(a)**  neither of section 313-40 or 313-50 applies to you; or

> <a id="s313-60-b"></a>
> **(b)**  section 313-40 applies to you and you do not notify the Commissioner in accordance with that section; or

> <a id="s313-60-c"></a>
> **(c)**  section 313-50 applies to you and you do not notify the Commissioner in accordance with that section.

> **Note:** The amount of the tax is set out in the First Home Super Saver Tax Act 2017. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            397 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> **Note:** Section 313-40 is about purchasing or constructing a residential premises. Section 313-50 is about making one or more non-concessional contributions.
