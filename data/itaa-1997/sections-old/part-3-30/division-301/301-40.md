# 301-40  Superannuation income stream—taxable component is assessable income, 15% offset for disability benefit


<a id="s301-40-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you are under your [[*preservation age*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-preservation-age)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-preservation-age) when you receive a [[*superannuation income stream benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit), the [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of the benefit is assessable income.

> **Note:** For taxable component, see Subdivision 307-C. 282                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Superannuation member benefits paid from complying plans etc. Division 301 Section 301-40 Offset for disability benefit

<a id="s301-40-2"></a>
**(2)**  If the benefit is a [[*superannuation income stream benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit) and a [[*disability superannuation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-disability-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-disability-superannuation-benefit), you are entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) equal to 15% of the [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of the benefit.
