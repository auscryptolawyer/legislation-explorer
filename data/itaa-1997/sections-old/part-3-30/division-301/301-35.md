# 301-35  Superannuation lump sum—taxable component taxed at 20%


<a id="s301-35-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you are under your [[*preservation age*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-preservation-age)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-preservation-age) when you receive a [[*superannuation lump sum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum), the [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of the lump sum is assessable income.

> **Note:** For taxable component, see Subdivision 307-C.

<a id="s301-35-2"></a>
**(2)**  You are entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) that ensures that the rate of income tax on the [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of the lump sum does not exceed 20%.

> **Note:** If your lump sum includes an element untaxed in the fund, see Subdivision 301-C.
