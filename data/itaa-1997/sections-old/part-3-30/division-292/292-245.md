# 292-245  Objections

If a person is dissatisfied with an [[*excess non-concessional contributions tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-excess-non-concessional-contributions-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-excess-non-concessional-contributions-tax) assessment made in relation to the person, the person may object against the assessment in the manner set out in Part IVC of the Taxation Administration Act 1953.
