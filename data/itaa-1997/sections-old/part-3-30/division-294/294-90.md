# 294-90  Payment splits


<a id="s294-90-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*transfer balance debit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-debit) arises in your [[*transfer balance account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account) if:

> <a id="s294-90-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  subsection (2) provides that the debit arises; and

> <a id="s294-90-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the Commissioner is notified in the [[*approved form*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form) that the debit has arisen. Payment splits

<a id="s294-90-2"></a>
**(2)**  A debit arises if:

> <a id="s294-90-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a *superannuation interest is subject to a [[*payment split*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-split)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-split) but remains an interest of the [[*member spouse*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member-spouse)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member-spouse); and

> <a id="s294-90-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the superannuation interest supports a [[*superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream) that is in the [[*retirement phase*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase); and

> <a id="s294-90-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  as a result of the payment split, a proportion of all [[*superannuation income stream benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit) from the income stream is to be paid to a [[*non-member spouse*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-member-spouse)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-member-spouse); and

> <a id="s294-90-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  as a result, the member spouse and the non-member spouse are both [[*retirement phase*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase) recipients of the superannuation income stream.

<a id="s294-90-3"></a>
**(3)**  The amount of the debit is:

> <a id="s294-90-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if you are the [[*member spouse*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member-spouse)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member-spouse)—the proportion mentioned in paragraph (2)(c); and

> <a id="s294-90-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if you are the [[*non-member spouse*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-member-spouse)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-member-spouse)—the remaining proportion; of the [[*value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value), on the day the debit arises, of the *superannuation interest that supports the [[*superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream) affected by the [[*payment split*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-split)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-split).

<a id="s294-90-4"></a>
**(4)**  The debit arises at the later of:

> <a id="s294-90-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the operative time (within the meaning of Part VIIIB or VIIIC (as the case may be) of the Family Law Act 1975) for the [[*payment split*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-split)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-split); and

> <a id="s294-90-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  at the start of the day you first start to have a [[*transfer balance account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account). 178                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Transfer balance cap Division 294 Section 294-95
