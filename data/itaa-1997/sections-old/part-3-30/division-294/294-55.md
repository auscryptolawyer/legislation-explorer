# 294-55  Repayment of limited recourse borrowing arrangement


<a id="s294-55-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*transfer balance credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-credit) arises in your [[*transfer balance account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account) if:

> <a id="s294-55-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a *superannuation provider makes a payment in respect of a [[*borrowing*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-borrowing)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-borrowing) under an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) that is covered by the exception in subsection 67A(1) of the Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993 (which is about limited recourse borrowing arrangements); and

> <a id="s294-55-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  as a result, there is an increase in the [[*value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value) of a *superannuation interest that supports a [[*superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream) of which you are the [[*retirement phase*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase) recipient; and

> <a id="s294-55-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the superannuation interest is in a [[*small superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-small-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-small-superannuation-fund) at the time of the payment.

<a id="s294-55-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount of the credit is the amount of the increase in [[*value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value).

<a id="s294-55-3"></a>
**(3)**  The credit arises at the time of the payment.
