# 294-45  Transfer balance account ends

The [[*transfer balance account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account) ceases when the [[*retirement phase*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase) recipient dies.
