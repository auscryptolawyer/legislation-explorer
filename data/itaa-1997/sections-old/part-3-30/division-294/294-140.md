# 294-140  Excess transfer balance—special rule for capped defined benefit income streams


<a id="s294-140-1"></a>
**(1)**  Despite section 294-30, you have excess transfer balance at a particular time if, at that time, the [[*transfer balance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance) in your [[*transfer balance account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account):

> <a id="s294-140-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  exceeds your [[*transfer balance cap*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-cap)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-cap) at that time; and

> <a id="s294-140-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  exceeds your capped defined benefit balance from subsection (3) of this section at that time.

<a id="s294-140-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount of the excess transfer balance is the lesser of the 2 excesses.

> **Note:** For modifications of the tax treatment of benefits paid from capped defined benefit income streams, see Subdivision 303-A. Your capped defined benefit balance

<a id="s294-140-3"></a>
**(3)**  You have an amount under this subsection (a capped defined benefit balance) at a time equal to:

> <a id="s294-140-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the sum of the [[*transfer balance credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-credit) in your [[*transfer balance account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account) at that time in respect of [[*capped defined benefit income streams*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capped-defined-benefit-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capped-defined-benefit-income-stream); less

> <a id="s294-140-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the sum of the [[*transfer balance debits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-debit) (if any) in your transfer balance account at that time in respect of capped defined benefit income streams.
