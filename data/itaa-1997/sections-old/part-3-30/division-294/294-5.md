# 294-5  Object of this Division

The object of this Division is to limit the total amount of an individual’s [[*superannuation income streams*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream) that receive an earnings tax exemption.
