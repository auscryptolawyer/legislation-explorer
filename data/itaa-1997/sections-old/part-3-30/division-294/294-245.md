# 294-245  When tax is payable—amended assessments

If the Commissioner amends your assessment, any extra [[*assessed excess transfer balance tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-assessed-excess-transfer-balance-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-assessed-excess-transfer-balance-tax) resulting from the amendment is due and payable 21 days after the day the Commissioner gives you notice of the amended assessment.
