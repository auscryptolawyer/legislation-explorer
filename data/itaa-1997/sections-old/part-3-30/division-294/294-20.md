# 294-20  Meaning of retirement phase recipient


<a id="s294-20-1"></a>
**(1)**  You are the retirement phase recipient of a [[*superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream) at a time if:

> <a id="s294-20-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the superannuation income stream is in the [[*retirement phase*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase) at that time; and

> <a id="s294-20-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*superannuation income stream benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit) from the superannuation income stream is payable to you at that time.

<a id="s294-20-2"></a>
**(2)**  You are also the retirement phase recipient of a [[*superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream) at a time if:

> <a id="s294-20-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the superannuation income stream is in the [[*retirement phase*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase) at that time; and

> <a id="s294-20-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the superannuation income stream is a [[*deferred superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deferred-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deferred-superannuation-income-stream); and

> <a id="s294-20-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  a [[*superannuation income stream benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit) from the superannuation income stream will be payable to you after that time.
