# 294-30  Excess transfer balance


<a id="s294-30-1"></a>
**(1)**  You have excess transfer balance at a particular time if, at that time, the [[*transfer balance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance) in your [[*transfer balance account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account) exceeds your [[*transfer balance cap*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-cap)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-cap) at that time. The amount of the excess transfer balance is the amount of the excess.

> **Note:** There is a modification for certain capped defined benefit income streams: see Subdivision 294-D.

<a id="s294-30-2"></a>
**(2)**  The transfer balance in your [[*transfer balance account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account) at a time equals:

> <a id="s294-30-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the sum of the [[*transfer balance credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-credit) in the account at that time; less

> <a id="s294-30-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the sum of the [[*transfer balance debits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-debit) (if any) in the account at that time.

> **Note:** For transfer balance debits, see Subdivision 294-C.

> **Note:** There is no consequence for having a negative transfer balance.
