# 294-15  When you have a transfer balance account


<a id="s294-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  You have a transfer balance account if you are, or have at any time been, the [[*retirement phase*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase) recipient of a [[*superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream).

<a id="s294-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  You start to have the [[*transfer balance account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transfer-balance-account) on the later of:

> <a id="s294-15-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  1 July 2017; and

> <a id="s294-15-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the day you first start to be a [[*retirement phase*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase) recipient of a [[*superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream).
