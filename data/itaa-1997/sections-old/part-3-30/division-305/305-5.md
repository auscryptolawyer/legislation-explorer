# 305-5  Tax treatment of superannuation benefits from certain Australian non-complying superannuation funds

A [[*superannuation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit) that you receive from a [[*non-complying superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-complying-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-complying-superannuation-fund) that is an [[*Australian superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-superannuation-fund) (for the income year in which the benefit is paid) is [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) if:

> <a id="s305-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the fund:

> > <a id="s305-5-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  has never been a [[*complying superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-fund); or Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                           309 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> > <a id="s305-5-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  last stopped being a complying superannuation fund for the income year in which 1 July 1995 occurred or a later income year; and

> <a id="s305-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the fund:

> > <a id="s305-5-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  has never been a [[*foreign superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-superannuation-fund); or

> > <a id="s305-5-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  last stopped being a foreign superannuation fund for the income year in which 1 July 1995 occurred or a later income year.
