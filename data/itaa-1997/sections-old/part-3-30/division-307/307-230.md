# 307-230  Total superannuation balance


<a id="s307-230-1"></a>
**(1)**  Your total superannuation balance, at a particular time, is the sum of the following:

> <a id="s307-230-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*total superannuation balance value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-total-superannuation-balance-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-total-superannuation-balance-value), at that time, of each of the following *superannuation interests (other than an interest in a *superannuation plan that, at that time, is a [[*foreign superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-superannuation-fund)):

> > <a id="s307-230-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a superannuation interest of yours;

> > <a id="s307-230-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a superannuation interest that supports a [[*superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream) of which you are a [[*retirement phase*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase) recipient because of the death of another person;

> <a id="s307-230-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the amount of each [[*roll-over superannuation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-roll-over-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-roll-over-superannuation-benefit):

> > <a id="s307-230-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  paid at or before that time; and 350                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Key concepts relating to superannuation benefits Division 307 Section 307-230

> > <a id="s307-230-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  received by the *complying superannuation plan, or the entity from which the [[*superannuation annuity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-annuity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-annuity) is being purchased, after that time; and

> > <a id="s307-230-1-c-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  not reflected in the value in paragraph (a);

> <a id="s307-230-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  if you have an LRBA amount under section 307-231 (about limited recourse borrowing arrangements) in relation to one or more *regulated superannuation funds—the LRBA amounts for each such regulated superannuation fund. Modification for structured settlement contributions

<a id="s307-230-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, if a [[*structured settlement contribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-structured-settlement-contribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-structured-settlement-contribution) is made at or before a time in respect of you, your total superannuation balance at that time is modified by reducing the sum worked out under subsection (1) by the sum of any such structured settlement contributions. Family law splits

<a id="s307-230-3"></a>
**(3)**  For the purposes of the provisions mentioned in subsection (5), you are treated as having a *superannuation interest in a *superannuation plan if:

> <a id="s307-230-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are a [[*non-member spouse*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-member-spouse)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-member-spouse) in relation to a superannuation interest that:

> > <a id="s307-230-3-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  is an interest in that superannuation plan; and

> > <a id="s307-230-3-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  is subject to a [[*payment split*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-split)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-split) but remains an interest of the [[*member spouse*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member-spouse)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member-spouse); and

> <a id="s307-230-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  circumstances prescribed by the regulations for the purposes of this paragraph exist.

<a id="s307-230-4"></a>
**(4)**  For the purposes of the provisions mentioned in subsection (5), the regulations must specify whether the *superannuation interest that you are treated as having is to be treated as being a [[*defined benefit interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-interest).

> **Note:** Regulations made for the purposes of paragraph 307-230A(1)(a) may specify the total superannuation balance value of the interest.

<a id="s307-230-1"></a>
**(1)**  The total superannuation balance value, at a particular time, of a *superannuation interest is:

> <a id="s307-230-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the regulations specify a value, or a method for determining a value, for the purposes of this paragraph—the specified value, or the value determined in accordance with the specified method; or

> <a id="s307-230-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—the total amount of the [[*superannuation benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit) that would become payable if:

> > <a id="s307-230-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the individual to whom the superannuation interest relates had the right to cause the superannuation interest to cease at that time; and

> > <a id="s307-230-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the individual voluntarily caused the superannuation interest to cease at that time.

<a id="s307-230-2"></a>
**(2)**  Regulations made for the purposes of paragraph (1)(a) may specify a value or method in different ways depending on any of the following matters:

> <a id="s307-230-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the individual to whom the *superannuation interest relates;

> <a id="s307-230-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  whether the superannuation interest is in the [[*retirement phase*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-retirement-phase);

> <a id="s307-230-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  whether the superannuation interest is or includes a [[*defined benefit interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-interest);

> <a id="s307-230-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the [[*superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream) (if any) supported by the superannuation interest;

> <a id="s307-230-2-e"></a>
> **(e)**  if the superannuation interest is an interest in a [[*superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-fund) or [[*approved deposit fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-deposit-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-deposit-fund)—the rules of the fund;

> <a id="s307-230-2-f"></a>
> **(f)**  if the superannuation interest is an interest in an [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)—the terms and conditions of the RSA; 352                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Key concepts relating to superannuation benefits Division 307 Section 307-231

> <a id="s307-230-2-g"></a>
> **(g)**  the *superannuation provider in relation to the *superannuation plan in which the individual holds the superannuation interest;

> <a id="s307-230-2-h"></a>
> **(h)**  whether the superannuation interest is subject to a [[*payment split*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-split)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment-split);

> > <a id="s307-230-2-h-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  whether the individual is treated as having the superannuation interest under subsection 307-230(3);

> <a id="s307-230-2-j"></a>
> **(j)**  the death of the individual;

> <a id="s307-230-2-k"></a>
> **(k)**  any other matter.

<a id="s307-230-3"></a>
**(3)**  Regulations made for the purposes of paragraph (1)(a) may provide for a value to be determined wholly or partly by reference to:

> <a id="s307-230-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  methods or factors that are approved by legislative instrument by a Minister:

> > <a id="s307-230-3-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  for the purposes of the regulations; or

> > <a id="s307-230-3-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  for the purposes of another Act or legislative instrument; or

> <a id="s307-230-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an [[*actuary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary)’s certificate.

<a id="s307-230-4"></a>
**(4)**  Regulations made for the purposes of paragraph (1)(a) may specify circumstances in which a [[*total superannuation balance value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-total-superannuation-balance-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-total-superannuation-balance-value) is nil.

<a id="s307-230-5"></a>
**(5)**  Subsections (2), (3) and (4) do not limit the regulations that may be made for the purposes of paragraph (1)(a).
