# 307-225  What is the crystallised segment?


<a id="s307-225-1"></a>
**(1)**  To work out the crystallised segment of a *superannuation interest, first assume that:

> <a id="s307-225-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an eligible termination payment had been made in respect of the holder of the interest just before 1 July 2007; and

> <a id="s307-225-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount of the eligible termination payment had been equal to the [[*value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value) of the interest at that time.

<a id="s307-225-2"></a>
**(2)**  The crystallised segment of the *superannuation interest is the total amount of the following components of the eligible termination payment:

> <a id="s307-225-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the concessional component;

> <a id="s307-225-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the post-June 1994 invalidity component;

> <a id="s307-225-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the undeducted contributions;

> <a id="s307-225-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the CGT exempt component;

> <a id="s307-225-2-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the pre-July 83 component. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            349 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 This section has effect subject to subsection 307-210(2).

> **Note:** This segment may be reduced if a superannuation benefit is paid from the superannuation interest: see subsection 307-210(2).

<a id="s307-225-3"></a>
**(3)**  For the purposes of paragraph (2)(e), disregard the [[*value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value) of the interest just before 1 July 2007 to the extent that it would consist, apart from this subsection, of the [[*element untaxed in the fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-untaxed-in-the-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-untaxed-in-the-fund) of the [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of a [[*superannuation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit) constituted by the eligible termination payment.

<a id="s307-225-4"></a>
**(4)**  In this section, the following terms have the same meaning as in subsection 27A(1) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (as in force just before 1 July 2007):

> <a id="s307-225-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  concessional component;

> <a id="s307-225-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  post-June 1994 invalidity component;

> <a id="s307-225-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  undeducted contributions;

> <a id="s307-225-4-d"></a>
> **(d)**  CGT exempt component;

> <a id="s307-225-4-e"></a>
> **(e)**  pre-July 83 component;

> <a id="s307-225-4-f"></a>
> **(f)**  eligible termination payment.
