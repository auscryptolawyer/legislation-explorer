# 307-285  Trustee can choose to convert element taxed in the fund to element untaxed in the fund


<a id="s307-285-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:

> <a id="s307-285-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you receive a [[*superannuation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit) from a *public sector superannuation scheme; and

> <a id="s307-285-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the trustee of the scheme gives you written notice specifying an amount as the [[*element untaxed in the fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-untaxed-in-the-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-untaxed-in-the-fund) of the [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of the benefit; and

> <a id="s307-285-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the notice is given within the time and in the manner approved by the Commissioner in writing; and

> <a id="s307-285-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the scheme came into operation on or before 5 September 2006; the taxable component consists of an element untaxed in the fund equal to the specified amount. 356                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Key concepts relating to superannuation benefits Division 307 Section 307-290

<a id="s307-285-2"></a>
**(2)**  The trustee of the scheme can give only one notice under subsection (1) in relation to a particular [[*superannuation lump sum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum).
