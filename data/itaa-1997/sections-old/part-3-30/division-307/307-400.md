# 307-400  Meaning of service period for a superannuation lump sum


<a id="s307-400-1"></a>
**(1)**  The service period for a [[*superannuation lump sum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum) consists of each day that is in the period worked out under the table or a period covered by subsection (2). Service period for superannuation lump sum types Item      For this                 The service period includes: superannuation lump sum type: 1         *Superannuation          The following: fund payment             (a) if some or all of the [[*superannuation lump sum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum) accrued while you were, or the deceased was, a member of the [[*superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-fund)—the period of membership;

> <a id="s307-400-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if some or all of the superannuation lump sum accrued while you were, or the deceased was, employed (or you or the deceased held office)— each period of employment (or of holding office) to which the lump sum relates. 2         *approved deposit        The period starting when you or the deceased first fund payment             made a deposit to the [[*approved deposit fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-deposit-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-deposit-fund) and ending when the payment is made.
