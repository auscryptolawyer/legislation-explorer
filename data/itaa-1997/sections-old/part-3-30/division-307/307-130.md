# 307-130  Superannuation guarantee payment consists entirely of taxable component

The components of a [[*superannuation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit) that is a [[*superannuation guarantee payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-guarantee-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-guarantee-payment) are as follows: 336                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Key concepts relating to superannuation benefits Division 307 Section 307-133

> <a id="s307-130-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*tax free component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-free-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-free-component) is nil;

> <a id="s307-130-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) is the amount of the benefit.
