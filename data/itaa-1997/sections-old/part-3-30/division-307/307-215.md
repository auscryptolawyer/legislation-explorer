# 307-215  Taxable component of superannuation interest

The taxable component of a *superannuation interest is the [[*value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value) of the interest less the [[*tax free component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-free-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-free-component) of the interest.
