# 307-280  Superannuation benefits from constitutionally protected funds etc.


<a id="s307-280-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of a [[*superannuation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit) paid from a [[*superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-fund) that is a [[*constitutionally protected fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-constitutionally-protected-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-constitutionally-protected-fund) consists wholly of an element untaxed in the fund.

<a id="s307-280-2"></a>
**(2)**  Despite subsection (1), if:

> <a id="s307-280-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the benefit is a [[*superannuation lump sum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum); and

> <a id="s307-280-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the benefit is attributable to one or more [[*roll-over superannuation benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-roll-over-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-roll-over-superannuation-benefit) that consisted of, or included, an [[*element taxed in the fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-taxed-in-the-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-taxed-in-the-fund); the [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of the benefit has an element taxed in the fund equal to the total of those elements taxed in the fund.

<a id="s307-280-3"></a>
**(3)**  The [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of a [[*superannuation income stream benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit) consists wholly of an element untaxed in the fund if it is paid from a [[*superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-fund) that was a [[*constitutionally protected fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-constitutionally-protected-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-constitutionally-protected-fund) on the first day of the period to which the [[*superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream) relates.
