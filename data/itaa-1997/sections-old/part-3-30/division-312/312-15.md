# 312-15  Superannuation benefits paid from complying superannuation funds to KiwiSaver schemes

A [[*superannuation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit) paid to a [[*KiwiSaver scheme provider*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-kiwisaver-scheme-provider)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-kiwisaver-scheme-provider) by the trustee of a [[*complying superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-fund) in respect of you is not assessable income of yours and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) of yours.
