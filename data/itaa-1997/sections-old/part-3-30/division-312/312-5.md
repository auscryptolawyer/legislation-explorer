# 312-5  Division implements Arrangement with New Zealand

This Division, together with the Superannuation (Unclaimed Money and Lost Members) Act 1999 and regulations made under the Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993, implement the Arrangement between the Government of Australia and the Government of New Zealand on Trans-Tasman Retirement Savings Portability, signed at Brisbane on 16 July 2009. 386                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Trans-Tasman portability of retirement savings Division 312 Section 312-10
