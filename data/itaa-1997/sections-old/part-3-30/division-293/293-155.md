# 293-155  High income threshold—effect of modification


<a id="s293-155-1"></a>
**(1)**  For the purpose of working out the extent (if any) to which the sum mentioned in subsection 293-20(1) for the individual exceeds the $250,000 threshold mentioned in that subsection, disregard section 293-150.

<a id="s293-155-2"></a>
**(2)**  To avoid doubt, the effect of subsection (1) is that the amount of the individual’s [[*taxable contributions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-contributions)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-contributions) for an income year is the lesser of:

> <a id="s293-155-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the excess (if any) mentioned in subsection 293-20(1) (worked out disregarding section 293-150) for the income year; and

> <a id="s293-155-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the individual’s [[*low tax contributions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-low-tax-contributions)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-low-tax-contributions) for the corresponding [[*financial year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year) (worked out having regard to section 293-150).
