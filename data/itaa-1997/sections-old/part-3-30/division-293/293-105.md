# 293-105  Low tax contributions—modification for defined benefit interests

Despite section 293-25, if you have a [[*defined benefit interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-interest) or interests in a [[*financial year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year), the amount of your low tax contributions for the financial year is worked out as follows: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            155 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Method statement Step 1.    Start with the low tax contributed amounts covered by section 293-30 for the [[*financial year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year), to the extent to which they do not relate to the [[*defined benefit interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-interest) or interests. Step 2.    Subtract your [[*excess concessional contributions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-excess-concessional-contributions)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-excess-concessional-contributions) for the [[*financial year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year) (if any).

> **Note:** The result of step 2 could be nil, or a negative amount. Step 3.    Add your [[*defined benefit contributions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-contributions)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-contributions) for the [[*financial year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year) in respect of the [[*defined benefit interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-defined-benefit-interest) or interests. The result (but not less than nil) is the amount of your low tax contributions for the financial year.

> **Note:** Modifications in Subdivision 293-E (about constitutionally protected State higher level office holders) and Subdivision 293-F (about Commonwealth justices) affect the amount of low tax contributions.
