# 296-25  Exception—structured settlement contributions

You are not liable to pay [[*Division 296 tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-division-296-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-division-296-tax) for an income year if a [[*structured settlement contribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-structured-settlement-contribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-structured-settlement-contribution) is made in respect of you in that year or in any earlier income year. 258                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Better targeted superannuation concessions Division 296 Section 296-30 Large superannuation balance threshold and very large superannuation balance threshold
