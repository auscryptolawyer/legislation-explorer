# 302-140  Superannuation lump sum—tax free status of tax free component

The [[*tax free component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-free-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-free-component) of a [[*superannuation lump sum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum) that you receive because of the death of a person of whom you are not a [[*death benefits dependant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-death-benefits-dependant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-death-benefits-dependant) is not assessable income and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income).
