# 302-85  Deceased died aged 60 or above or dependant aged 60 years or above—superannuation income stream: element untaxed in fund attracts 10% offset


<a id="s302-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*element untaxed in the fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-untaxed-in-the-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-untaxed-in-the-fund) of a [[*superannuation income stream benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit) that you receive because of the death of a person of whom you are a [[*death benefits dependant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-death-benefits-dependant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-death-benefits-dependant) is assessable income in either or both of the following cases:

> <a id="s302-85-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are 60 years or over when you receive the benefit; 296                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Superannuation death benefits paid from complying plans etc. Division 302 Section 302-90

> <a id="s302-85-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the deceased died aged 60 or above.

<a id="s302-85-2"></a>
**(2)**  You are entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) equal to 10% of the [[*element untaxed in the fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-untaxed-in-the-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-untaxed-in-the-fund) of the benefit.

> **Note:** If your superannuation income stream benefit is defined benefit income, see Subdivision 303-A.
