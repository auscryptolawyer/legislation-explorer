# 302-145  Superannuation lump sum—element taxed in the fund taxed at 15%, element untaxed in the fund taxed at 30%


<a id="s302-145-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you receive a [[*superannuation lump sum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum) because of the death of a person of whom you are not a [[*death benefits dependant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-death-benefits-dependant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-death-benefits-dependant), the [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of the lump sum is assessable income.

> **Note:** For taxable component, see Subdivision 307-C.

<a id="s302-145-2"></a>
**(2)**  You are entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) that ensures that the rate of income tax on the [[*element taxed in the fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-taxed-in-the-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-taxed-in-the-fund) of the lump sum does not exceed 15%.

<a id="s302-145-3"></a>
**(3)**  You are entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) that ensures that the rate of income tax on the [[*element untaxed in the fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-untaxed-in-the-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-element-untaxed-in-the-fund) of the lump sum does not exceed 30%.
