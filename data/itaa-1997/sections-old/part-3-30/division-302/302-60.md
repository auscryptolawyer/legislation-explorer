# 302-60  All of superannuation lump sum is tax free

A [[*superannuation lump sum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum) that you receive because of the death of a person of whom you are a [[*death benefits dependant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-death-benefits-dependant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-death-benefits-dependant) is not assessable income and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income). 294                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Superannuation death benefits paid from complying plans etc. Division 302 Section 302-65 Superannuation income stream—either deceased died aged 60 or above or dependant aged 60 or above
