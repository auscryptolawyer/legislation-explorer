# 302-75  Superannuation income stream—taxable component attracts 15% offset


<a id="s302-75-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of a [[*superannuation income stream benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream-benefit) that you receive because of the death of a person of whom you are a [[*death benefits dependant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-death-benefits-dependant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-death-benefits-dependant) is assessable income if: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            295 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s302-75-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are under 60 when you receive the benefit; and

> <a id="s302-75-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the deceased died aged under 60.

> **Note:** For taxable component, see Subdivision 307-C.

<a id="s302-75-2"></a>
**(2)**  You are entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) equal to 15% of the [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of the benefit.
