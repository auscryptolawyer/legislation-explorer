# 302-5  Division applies to superannuation death benefits paid from complying plans etc.

This Division applies to [[*superannuation death benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-death-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-death-benefit) that:

> <a id="s302-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  are paid from a *complying superannuation plan; or 292                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Superannuation death benefits paid from complying plans etc. Division 302 Section 302-10

> <a id="s302-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  are [[*superannuation guarantee payments*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-guarantee-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-guarantee-payment), [[*small superannuation account payments*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-small-superannuation-account-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-small-superannuation-account-payment), [[*unclaimed money payments*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unclaimed-money-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unclaimed-money-payment), *superannuation co-contribution benefit payments or [[*superannuation annuity payments*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-annuity-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-annuity-payment).

> **Note:** For the tax treatment of superannuation member benefits paid from complying plans, see Division 301. Superannuation benefits paid from superannuation plans that are not complying superannuation plans are dealt with in Division 305.
