# 295-410  Amount credited to RSA

> <a id="s295-410-a"></a>
> **(a)**  multiplying the amount by the number of days in the income year for which the pension covered by section 295-407 was paid; and 232                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Taxation of superannuation entities Division 295 Section 295-460

> <a id="s295-410-b"></a>
> **(b)**  dividing the result by the number of days in the income year that the RSA existed.
