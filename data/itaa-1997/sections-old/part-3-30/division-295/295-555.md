# 295-555  Components of taxable income—RSA providers


<a id="s295-555-1"></a>
**(1)**  The taxable income of an [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa) provider is split into:

> <a id="s295-555-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an [[*RSA component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa-component); and

> <a id="s295-555-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  a [[*standard component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-standard-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-standard-component).

> **Note:** The RSA component is taxed at the same concessional rate that applies to the low tax component of complying superannuation entities (see section 23 of the Income Tax Rates Act 1986). The standard component is taxed at the standard company rate. 248                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Taxation of superannuation entities Division 295 Section 295-555

<a id="s295-555-2"></a>
**(2)**  The RSA component for an income year is worked out in this way: Method statement Step 1.     Add these amounts included in the provider’s assessable income for the income year:

> <a id="s295-555-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  amounts included under Subdivision 295-C; and

> <a id="s295-555-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  other amounts credited during the year to *RSAs that it provides. Step 2.     Subtract from the step 1 amount amounts paid from those *RSAs (except benefits for the RSA holders or tax). Step 3.     The result is the RSA component.

<a id="s295-555-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, if the [[*RSA component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa-component) is more than the [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa) provider’s taxable income:

> <a id="s295-555-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the provider’s taxable income is equal to that sum; and

> <a id="s295-555-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  this Act applies to the provider as if it had a [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss for the income year of an amount that would have been that loss if the RSA component were not [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income) or [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income).

<a id="s295-555-4"></a>
**(4)**  The standard component is the remaining part (if any) of the [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa) provider’s taxable income for the income year after subtracting the [[*RSA component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa-component).
