# 295-680  Amount of the tax offset

The amount of the [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) is the sum of each amount of tax that counts towards the offset for the provider for the current year.
