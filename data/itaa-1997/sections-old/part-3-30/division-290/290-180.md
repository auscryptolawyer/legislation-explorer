# 290-180  Notice may be varied but not revoked or withdrawn


<a id="s290-180-1"></a>
**(1)**  You cannot revoke or withdraw a valid notice in relation to the contribution (or a part of the contribution).

<a id="s290-180-2"></a>
**(2)**  You can vary a valid notice, but only so as to reduce the amount stated in relation to the contribution (including to nil). You do so by giving notice to the trustee or the [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa) provider in the [[*approved form*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form).

<a id="s290-180-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, you cannot vary a valid notice after:

> <a id="s290-180-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if you have lodged your [[*income tax return*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return) for the income year in which the contribution was made on a day before the end of the next income year—the end of that day; or

> <a id="s290-180-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—the end of the next income year. (3A) The variation is not effective if, when you make it:

> <a id="s290-180-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you were not a member of the fund or the holder of the [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa); or 100                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Contributions to superannuation funds Division 290 Section 290-230

> <a id="s290-180-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the trustee or [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa) provider no longer holds the contribution; or

> <a id="s290-180-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the trustee or RSA provider has begun to pay a [[*superannuation income stream*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-income-stream) based in whole or part on the contribution.

<a id="s290-180-4"></a>
**(4)**  Subsection (3) does not apply to a variation if:

> <a id="s290-180-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you claimed a deduction for the contribution (or a part of the contribution); and

> <a id="s290-180-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the deduction is not allowable (in whole or in part); and

> <a id="s290-180-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the variation reduces the amount stated in relation to the contribution by the amount not allowable as a deduction. Application to successor funds

<a id="s290-180-5"></a>
**(5)**  Subsections (2) and (3A) apply as if:

> <a id="s290-180-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the reference in subsection (3A) to the fund or [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa) were a reference to a *successor fund; and

> <a id="s290-180-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  references in those subsections to the trustee or [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa) provider were references to the trustee or RSA provider of the successor fund; if, after a valid notice is given under section 290-170 in relation to the contribution, all of the *superannuation interest to which the notice relates is transferred to the successor fund.
