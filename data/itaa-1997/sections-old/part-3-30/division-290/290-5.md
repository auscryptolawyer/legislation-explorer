# 290-5  Non-application to roll-over superannuation benefits etc.

This Division does not apply to a contribution that is any of the following:

> <a id="s290-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*roll-over superannuation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-roll-over-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-roll-over-superannuation-benefit);

> <a id="s290-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*superannuation lump sum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum) that is paid from a [[*foreign superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-superannuation-fund);

> <a id="s290-5-c"></a>
> **(c)**  an amount transferred to a [[*complying superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-fund) or an [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa) from a scheme for the payment of benefits in the nature of superannuation upon retirement or death that: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            83 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> > <a id="s290-5-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  is not, and never has been, an [[*Australian superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-australian-superannuation-fund) or a [[*foreign superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-superannuation-fund); and

> > <a id="s290-5-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  was not established in Australia; and

> > <a id="s290-5-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  is not centrally managed or controlled in Australia.
