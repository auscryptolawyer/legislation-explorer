# 290-240  Tax file number

If you are entitled to the [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for the contribution, you may, with your *spouse’s consent, quote your spouse’s [[*tax file number*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-file-number)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-file-number) to the trustee (or [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa) provider) of the [[*superannuation fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-fund) (or [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)) to which the contribution is made.
