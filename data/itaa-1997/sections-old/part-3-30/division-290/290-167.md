# 290-167  Contribution must not be a downsizer contribution

You cannot deduct the contribution if it is a contribution that is covered under section 292-102 (about downsizer contributions). Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             97 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026
