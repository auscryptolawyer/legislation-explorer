# 303-10  Superannuation lump sum member benefit paid to member having a terminal medical condition


<a id="s303-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies to a [[*superannuation member benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-member-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-member-benefit) that:

> <a id="s303-10-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  is a [[*superannuation lump sum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-lump-sum); and 304                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-30 Superannuation benefits paid in special circumstances Division 303 Section 303-15

> <a id="s303-10-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is:

> > <a id="s303-10-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  paid from a *complying superannuation plan; or

> > <a id="s303-10-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a [[*superannuation guarantee payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-guarantee-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-guarantee-payment), a [[*small superannuation account payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-small-superannuation-account-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-small-superannuation-account-payment), an [[*unclaimed money payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unclaimed-money-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unclaimed-money-payment), a *superannuation co-contribution benefit payment or a [[*superannuation annuity payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-annuity-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-annuity-payment).

<a id="s303-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  The lump sum is not assessable income and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) if a [[*terminal medical condition*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-terminal-medical-condition)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-terminal-medical-condition) exists in relation to you when you receive the lump sum or within 90 days after you receive it.

> **Note:** For a lump sum you receive in the 2007-08 financial year, the period of 90 days may be extended until 30 June 2008: see section 303-10 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997.
