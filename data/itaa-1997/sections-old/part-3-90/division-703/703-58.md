# 703-58  Notice of choice to consolidate


<a id="s703-58-1"></a>
**(1)**  If a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) comes into existence on the day specified in a choice under section 703-50, the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the group must give the Commissioner a notice in the [[*approved form*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form) containing the following information:

> <a id="s703-58-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the identity of the head company;

> <a id="s703-58-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the day specified in the choice on which the [[*consolidatable group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidatable-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidatable-group) is taken to be consolidated;

> <a id="s703-58-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the identity of each [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group on that day;

> <a id="s703-58-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the identity of each entity that was a subsidiary member of the group on that day but was not such a subsidiary member when the notice is given; 68                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 Consolidated groups and their members Division 703 Section 703-60

> <a id="s703-58-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the identity of each entity that was not a subsidiary member of the group on that day but was such a subsidiary member when the notice is given;

> <a id="s703-58-1-f"></a>
> **(f)**  the identity of each entity that became a subsidiary member of the group after that day but was not such a subsidiary member when the notice is given.

<a id="s703-58-2"></a>
**(2)**  The notice must be given no later than:

> <a id="s703-58-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) is required to give the Commissioner its [[*income tax return*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return) for the income year during which that day occurs—the day on which the company gives the Commissioner that income tax return; or

> <a id="s703-58-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—the last day in the period within which the head company would be required to give the Commissioner such a return if it were required to give the Commissioner such a return.
