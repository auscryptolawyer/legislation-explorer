# 703-40  Treating entities held through non-fixed trusts as wholly-owned subsidiaries


<a id="s703-40-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section operates to ensure that an entity (the test entity) is not prevented from being a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) or [[*consolidatable group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidatable-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidatable-group) just because there is a trust that is Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             65 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 not a [[*fixed trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fixed-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fixed-trust) interposed between the test entity and the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the group.

<a id="s703-40-2"></a>
**(2)**  This Part (except Division 719) operates as if the test entity were a *wholly-owned subsidiary of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) if the test entity would have been a wholly-owned subsidiary of the head company had the interposed trust been a [[*fixed trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fixed-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fixed-trust) and all its objects been beneficiaries.
