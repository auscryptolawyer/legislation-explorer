# 713-510  Certain subsidiaries of life insurance companies cannot be members of consolidated group


<a id="s713-510-1"></a>
**(1)**  An entity cannot be a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) or [[*consolidatable group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidatable-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidatable-group) of which a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) is a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) if:

> <a id="s713-510-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the life insurance company owns, either directly or indirectly through one or more interposed entities, all the *membership interests in the entity and either:

> > <a id="s713-510-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  some, but not all, of the membership interests described in subsection (3) (the key interests) are [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset) of the life insurance company; or

> > <a id="s713-510-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  some, but not all, of the key interests are *segregated exempt assets of the life insurance company; or

> <a id="s713-510-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the life insurance company owns, either directly or indirectly through one or more interposed entities, only some of the membership interests in the entity and any of the key Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            267 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Section 713-510A interests are complying superannuation assets or segregated exempt assets of the life insurance company.

> **Note:** The entity could, however, be a member of another consolidated group or consolidatable group.

<a id="s713-510-2"></a>
**(2)**  An entity cannot continue to be a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) of which a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) is a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) if:

> <a id="s713-510-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the life insurance company owns, either directly or indirectly through one or more interposed entities, all the *membership interests in the entity and, had the entity not been a subsidiary member of the group, either:

> > <a id="s713-510-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  some, but not all, of the membership interests described in subsection (3) (the key interests) would be [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset) of the life insurance company; or

> > <a id="s713-510-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  some, but not all, of the key interests would be *segregated exempt assets of the life insurance company; or

> <a id="s713-510-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the life insurance company owns, either directly or indirectly through one or more interposed entities, only some of the membership interests in the entity and, had the entity not been a subsidiary member of the group, any of the key interests would be complying superannuation assets or segregated exempt assets of the life insurance company.

<a id="s713-510-3"></a>
**(3)**  The key interests are the *membership interests the [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) owns directly in:

> <a id="s713-510-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity; or

> <a id="s713-510-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an interposed entity. 713-510A Disregard single entity rule in working out certain amounts in respect of life insurance company

<a id="s713-510-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, if the [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) is a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group, this section does not apply:

> <a id="s713-510-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  for the purposes of working out the [[*tax cost setting amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount) of an asset of the life insurance company when it becomes a subsidiary member of the group; and

> <a id="s713-510-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  for the purposes of working out the tax cost setting amount of a *membership interest in the life insurance company if it ceases to be a subsidiary member of the group.

<a id="s713-510-3"></a>
**(3)**  Disregard section 701-1 (the single entity rule) in working out any of the following for the purposes of Division 320 in relation to the [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company):

> <a id="s713-510-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  amounts of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)’s ordinary income and statutory income derived from *segregated exempt assets that are not assessable income and are not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) under paragraph 320-37(1)(a);

> <a id="s713-510-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the head company’s taxable income of the [[*complying superannuation class*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-class)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-class) (see section 320-137);

> <a id="s713-510-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the head company’s [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss of the complying superannuation class (see section 320-141);

> <a id="s713-510-3-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the total *transfer value of the head company’s [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset) (see paragraph 320-175(1)(a));

> <a id="s713-510-3-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the amount of the head company’s *complying superannuation liabilities (see paragraph 320-175(1)(b));

> <a id="s713-510-3-f"></a>
> **(f)**  the total transfer value of the head company’s segregated exempt assets (see paragraph 320-230(1)(a));

> <a id="s713-510-3-g"></a>
> **(g)**  the amount of the head company’s [[*exempt life insurance policy*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-life-insurance-policy)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-life-insurance-policy) liabilities (see paragraph 320-230(1)(b)).
