# 713-505  Head company treated as a life insurance company

This Act, and the Income Tax Rates Act 1986, apply to the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) as if it were a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) for an income year if one or more life insurance companies are [[*subsidiary members*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group at any time during that year.
