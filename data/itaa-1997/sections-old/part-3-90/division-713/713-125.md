# 713-125  Object of this Subdivision


<a id="s713-125-1"></a>
**(1)**  The main object of this Subdivision is to provide, by the means described in subsections (2) and (3), for certain unit trusts to be treated like companies, and therefore like *head companies of [[*consolidated groups*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group), with consequent effects on other entities including:

> <a id="s713-125-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the trustees; and

> <a id="s713-125-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  *members of the trusts; and

> <a id="s713-125-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  entities the trustees hold *membership interests in.

<a id="s713-125-2"></a>
**(2)**  The first means is letting a [[*public trading trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-public-trading-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-public-trading-trust), that could become the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) if the trust were a company, choose to form such a group (with other entities as [[*subsidiary members*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)).

<a id="s713-125-3"></a>
**(3)**  The second means is changing the way in which the law relating to income tax applies on and after the time the choice takes effect, so that law (with some modifications) applies in relation to the trust or 248                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 Rules for particular kinds of entities Division 713 Section 713-130 the trustee (as appropriate) in a way corresponding to the way in which that law applies in relation to a company.
