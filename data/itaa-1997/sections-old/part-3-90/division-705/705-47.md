# 705-47  Reduction in tax cost setting amount for some privatised assets

Object

<a id="s705-47-1"></a>
**(1)**  The object of this section is to limit appropriately the amount the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the joined group can deduct for a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) it starts to [[*hold*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold) because the joining entity becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group, by reference to the direct or indirect effect of the following provisions on the amount the joining entity could deduct for the asset:

> <a id="s705-47-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  former section 61A of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (about depreciation deductions for tax-exempt entities that become taxable);

> <a id="s705-47-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  former Subdivision 57-I, and Subdivision 57-J, in Schedule 2D to the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (about depreciation and capital allowance deductions);

> <a id="s705-47-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  Division 58 of this Act (as that Division applies to a transition time or acquisition time mentioned in that Division before, on or after 1 July 2001). Reduction of tax cost setting amount

<a id="s705-47-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*tax cost setting amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount) for a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) is reduced to the joining entity’s [[*terminating value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-terminating-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-terminating-value) for the asset if:

> <a id="s705-47-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  at a time before the joining entity became a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the joined group, the asset was [[*held*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held) by an entity (whether the joining entity or another entity) that, at that time, was: 88                                     Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 Tax cost setting amount for assets where entities become subsidiary members of consolidated groups Division 705 Section 705-47

> > <a id="s705-47-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  an *exempt Australian government agency; or

> > <a id="s705-47-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  another entity whose [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income) and [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income) were exempt from income tax; and

> <a id="s705-47-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  any of the following provisions directly or indirectly affected the amount the joining entity could deduct for the asset:

> > <a id="s705-47-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  former section 61A of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (about depreciation deductions for tax-exempt entities that become taxable);

> > <a id="s705-47-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  former Subdivision 57-I, and Subdivision 57-J, in Schedule 2D to the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (about depreciation and [[*capital allowance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-allowance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-allowance) deductions);

> > <a id="s705-47-2-b-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  Division 58 of this Act (as that Division applies to a transition time or acquisition time mentioned in that Division before, on or after 1 July 2001); and

> <a id="s705-47-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  apart from this section, the tax cost setting amount for the asset would exceed the joining entity’s terminating value for the asset.

> **Note:** Unlike the position with a reduction in tax cost setting amount under section 705-40, the amount of the reduction is not re-allocated among other assets.

> **Note:** Former section 61A of, or former Subdivision 57-I or Subdivision 57-J in Schedule 2D to, the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 or Division 58 of this Act may, for example, have indirectly affected the amount the joining entity could deduct for the asset because:

> <a id="s705-47-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  that section, Subdivision or Division affected the amount that could be deducted by an entity that held the asset before the joining entity and that effect extended to the joining entity because of a previous application of this subsection, roll-over relief or section 701-40 (the exit history rule); or

> <a id="s705-47-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  this subsection affected the amount the joining entity could deduct for the asset (either directly or because of section 701-40).

> **Note:** Subsection (2) has effect even if, just before the joining time, the joining entity was:

> <a id="s705-47-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an exempt Australian government agency; or

> <a id="s705-47-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  another entity whose ordinary income and statutory income were exempt from income tax. <a id="s705-47-3"></a>
**(3)**  Subsection (2) does not apply if:

> <a id="s705-47-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  just before the joining time, the joining entity was neither an *exempt Australian government agency nor another entity whose [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income) and [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income) were exempt from income tax; and

> <a id="s705-47-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a condition in subsection (4) or (5) is met in relation to the period (the pre-joining taxable period) between the last time for which the condition in paragraph (2)(a) is met and the joining time.

<a id="s705-47-4"></a>
**(4)**  One condition for subsection (2) not to apply is that an amount was included in an entity’s assessable income, or an entity could deduct an amount, because of a [[*balancing adjustment event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event) that occurred for the asset during the pre-joining taxable period.

<a id="s705-47-5"></a>
**(5)**  Another condition for subsection (2) not to apply is that:

> <a id="s705-47-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  for at least some of the pre-joining taxable period, the asset was [[*held*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held) by the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) (the earlier group) for the period (the earlier group period):

> > <a id="s705-47-5-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  starting when (and because) an entity that had previously held the asset became a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the earlier group or when the asset started to be held by that company because of an asset sale situation described in subsection 58-5(4) involving a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the earlier group as the purchaser mentioned in that subsection; and

> > <a id="s705-47-5-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  ending when (and because) an entity ceased to be a subsidiary member of the earlier group or when the earlier group ceased to exist; and

> <a id="s705-47-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the company that was the head company of the earlier group just before the end of the earlier group period was not: 90                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 Tax cost setting amount for assets where entities become subsidiary members of consolidated groups Division 705 Section 705-55

> > <a id="s705-47-5-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  an [[*associate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate) of the head company of the joined group just before the joining time; or

> > <a id="s705-47-5-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the same company as the head company of the joined group; and

> <a id="s705-47-5-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the earlier group period was at least 24 months.
