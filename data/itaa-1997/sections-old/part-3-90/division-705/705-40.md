# 705-40  Tax cost setting amount for reset cost base assets held on revenue account etc.


<a id="s705-40-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*tax cost setting amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount) for a reset cost base asset that is [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock), a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset), a [[*registered emissions unit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-registered-emissions-unit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-registered-emissions-unit) or a [[*revenue asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset) must not exceed the greater of:

> <a id="s705-40-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the asset’s *market value; and

> <a id="s705-40-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the joining entity’s [[*terminating value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-terminating-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-terminating-value) for the asset.

<a id="s705-40-2"></a>
**(2)**  If subsection (1) reduces the asset’s [[*tax cost setting amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount), the amount of the reduction is allocated among the other reset cost base assets (including other [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock), [[*depreciating assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset), [[*registered emissions units*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-registered-emissions-unit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-registered-emissions-unit) and [[*revenue assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)), so as to increase their tax cost setting amounts, in accordance with the principles set out in subsection (3).

> **Note:** If any of the amount of the reduction cannot be allocated, it is instead treated as a capital loss of the head company: see CGT event L8.

<a id="s705-40-3"></a>
**(3)**  These are the principles:

> <a id="s705-40-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the allocation is to be in proportion to the *market values of the assets;

> <a id="s705-40-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount allocated to an item of [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock), to a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset), to a [[*registered emissions unit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-registered-emissions-unit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-registered-emissions-unit) or to a [[*revenue asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset) must not cause its [[*tax cost setting amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount) to contravene subsection (1);

> <a id="s705-40-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  any of the amount that cannot be allocated is to be reallocated, to the maximum extent possible, among the remaining reset cost base assets by applying this subsection a further one or more times. 86                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 Tax cost setting amount for assets where entities become subsidiary members of consolidated groups Division 705 Section 705-45
