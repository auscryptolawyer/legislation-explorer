<a id="s701-60-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies for the purpose of working out the [[*tax cost setting amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount) of an asset if:

> <a id="s701-60-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity (the leaving entity) ceases to be a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) (the old group) at a time (the leaving time); and

> <a id="s701-60-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the asset’s tax cost is set under section 701-45 because it consists of a liability (the corresponding liability) owed to the leaving entity.

<a id="s701-60-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*tax cost setting amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-cost-setting-amount) is: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                              41 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Section 701-60A

> **(a)**  unless subsection (3) or (4) applies—the *market value of the asset at the leaving time; or

> <a id="s701-60-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if subsection (3) applies—nil; or

> <a id="s701-60-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if subsection (4) applies—the least of the following amounts:

> > <a id="s701-60-2-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the tax cost setting amount mentioned in paragraph (4)(c);

> > <a id="s701-60-2-c-ii"></a>

> > <a id="s701-60-2-c-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  the market value of the asset at the leaving time.

<a id="s701-60-3"></a>
**(3)**  This subsection applies if:

> <a id="s701-60-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the corresponding liability is not a debt; and

> <a id="s701-60-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  either:

> > <a id="s701-60-3-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  at the time the corresponding liability arose, the entity to whom the corresponding liability was owed and the entity owing the corresponding liability were both *members of the old group; or

> > <a id="s701-60-3-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if subparagraph (i) does not apply—after the time the corresponding liability arose, a member of the old group *acquired the asset or started to have the corresponding liability.
