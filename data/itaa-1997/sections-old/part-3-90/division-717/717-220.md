# 717-220  FIF surpluses


<a id="s717-220-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section operates for the purposes of sections 23AK and 23B of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 if: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            393 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s717-220-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a company (the joining company) becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) at a time (the joining time); and

> <a id="s717-220-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  just before the joining time there was a post FIF abolition surplus for a FIF attribution account entity in relation to the joining company for the purposes of those sections; and

> <a id="s717-220-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  just before the joining time, the joining company’s FIF attribution account percentage in relation to the FIF attribution account entity for the purposes of those sections was more than nil. Credit in relation to the head company

<a id="s717-220-2"></a>
**(2)**  A post FIF abolition credit arises at the joining time for the FIF attribution account entity in relation to the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the group. The credit is equal to the post FIF abolition surplus. Debit in relation to the joining company

<a id="s717-220-3"></a>
**(3)**  A post FIF abolition debit arises at the joining time for the FIF attribution account entity in relation to the joining company. The debit is equal to the post FIF abolition surplus. Definitions

<a id="s717-220-4"></a>
**(4)**  In this section: FIF attribution account entity has the same meaning as in former Part XI of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936. FIF attribution account percentage has the same meaning as in former Part XI of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936. post FIF abolition credit has the same meaning as in the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936. post FIF abolition debit has the same meaning as in the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936. 394                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 International tax rules Division 717 Section 717-227 post FIF abolition surplus has the same meaning as in the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936.
