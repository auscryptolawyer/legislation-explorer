# 717-10  Head company taken to be liable for subsidiary member’s foreign income tax


<a id="s717-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section operates if:

> <a id="s717-10-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity was a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) for all or part of an income year; and

> <a id="s717-10-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an amount was included in the [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income) or [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income) of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the group for that income year; and

> <a id="s717-10-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity paid [[*foreign income tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax) (except [[*credit absorption tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-credit-absorption-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-credit-absorption-tax), [[*unitary tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unitary-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unitary-tax), [[*foreign IIR tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-iir-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-iir-tax) or [[*foreign UTPR tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-utpr-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-utpr-tax)) in respect of the amount.

<a id="s717-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  Division 770 operates as if:

> <a id="s717-10-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) had paid the [[*foreign income tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-foreign-income-tax); and

> <a id="s717-10-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity had not paid the foreign income tax.

> **Note:** Division 770 provides a tax offset for foreign income tax paid.

<a id="s717-10-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section does not limit the operation of Division 770. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            391 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026
