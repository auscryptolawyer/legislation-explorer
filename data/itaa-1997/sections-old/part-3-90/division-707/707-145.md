# 707-145  Cancelling the transfer of the loss


<a id="s707-145-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the joined group may choose to cancel the transfer of the loss.

<a id="s707-145-2"></a>
**(2)**  If the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the joined group does so, this Act (except this section) operates for all income years ending after the transfer as if it had not occurred under section 707-120.

<a id="s707-145-3"></a>
**(3)**  The choice cannot be revoked. 172                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 Losses for head companies when entities become members etc. Division 707 Section 707-150 What happens if the loss is not transferred?
