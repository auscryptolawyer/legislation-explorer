# 715-105  Second choice: head company’s final RUNL applied in reducing adjustable values of leaving assets that are loss assets


<a id="s715-105-1"></a>
**(1)**  The second choice is to reduce under this section the [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) of each leaving asset (a loss asset) for which the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) would have had a notional capital loss, or notional revenue loss, under section 165-115F at the time (the test time) just before the leaving time if the test time had been a *changeover time for the head company. The choice has effect accordingly.

> **Note:** The consequences of the choice are worked out under this section and section 715-145.

<a id="s715-105-2"></a>
**(2)**  If: 302                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 Interactions between this Part and other areas of the income tax law Division 715 Section 715-110

> <a id="s715-105-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  2 or more entities cease to be [[*subsidiary members*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) at the leaving time; and

> <a id="s715-105-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  2 or more of them make the second choice; the choices have effect in whichever order the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) determines.

<a id="s715-105-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section applies to each of the loss assets in order, according to their respective [[*adjustable values*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) (apart from this section) at the test time: from largest to smallest. (If an asset has more than one such adjustable value, use the greater or greatest of them.)

<a id="s715-105-4"></a>
**(4)**  At the test time, the [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) of the loss asset is reduced to the asset’s *market value at that time.

<a id="s715-105-5"></a>
**(5)**  However, if the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)’s [[*final RUNL*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-final-runl)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-final-runl) at the leaving time (as reduced by any previous reductions under this section) is less than the difference between:

> <a id="s715-105-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) of the loss asset (apart from this section) at the test time; and

> <a id="s715-105-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the asset’s *market value at the test time; the adjustable value is instead reduced at the test time by that final RUNL.

<a id="s715-105-6"></a>
**(6)**  That [[*final RUNL*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-final-runl)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-final-runl) is reduced by the amount of the reduction under subsection (4) or (5). If 2 or more such reductions are made for the same asset (because it has 2 or more different characters), that final RUNL is reduced by the greater or greatest of the reductions.
