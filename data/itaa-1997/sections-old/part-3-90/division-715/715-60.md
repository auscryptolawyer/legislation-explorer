# 715-60  Assets that the head company already owns


<a id="s715-60-1"></a>
**(1)**  At the time (the formation time) when a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) comes into existence under paragraph 703-5(1)(a), a loss denial pool of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) is created if:

> <a id="s715-60-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the formation time is not a *changeover time for the head company; and

> <a id="s715-60-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  at the formation time, the head company owns a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset):

> > <a id="s715-60-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  that is a *165-CC tagged asset of the head company at that time; and

> > <a id="s715-60-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  that it owned at the *changeover time; and

> > <a id="s715-60-1-b-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  that is not a *membership interest in a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the group; and

> > <a id="s715-60-1-b-iv"></a>
> > **(iv)**  that is not a right or option (including a contingent right or option), created or issued by a member of the group, to acquire such a membership interest; and

> > <a id="s715-60-1-b-v"></a>
> > **(v)**  that is not constituted by a liability owed to the head company by a member of the group; 296                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 Interactions between this Part and other areas of the income tax law Division 715 Section 715-70 or 2 or more such assets; and

> <a id="s715-60-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the head company’s [[*final RUNL*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-final-runl)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-final-runl) just before the formation time (as reduced by any reductions under section 715-50 or 715-55) was greater than nil; and

> <a id="s715-60-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the head company does not satisfy the [[*business continuity test*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-business-continuity-test)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-business-continuity-test) for:

> > <a id="s715-60-1-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the period (the business continuity test period) consisting of the head company’s [[*trial year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trial-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trial-year); and

> > <a id="s715-60-1-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the time (the test time) just before the *changeover time.

> **Note:** Paragraph (1)(c) has the effect that if the head company has 165-CC tagged assets that are affected by section 715-50 or 715-55 (because they are membership interests in, or accounting liabilities owed by, another group member), those sections are applied before this section.

<a id="s715-60-2"></a>
**(2)**  When it is created, the pool consists of the one or more [[*CGT assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) referred to in paragraph (1)(b), and its loss denial balance is equal to the [[*final RUNL*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-final-runl)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-final-runl) referred to in paragraph (1)(c).

> **Note:** The pool is distinct from any other loss denial pool of the head company, for example, one created at the formation time under section 715-70.

> **Note:** 170-D deferred losses on 165-CC tagged assets of the head company may be added to the pool by subsection 715-355(1).
