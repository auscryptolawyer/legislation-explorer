# 715-925  Effect on restructures—original entity ceases being a subsidiary member

If, as a result of an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) to which section 124-784A applies, an original entity (within the meaning of that section):

> <a id="s715-925-a"></a>
> **(a)**  ceases to be a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) after the completion time (within the meaning of that section) for the arrangement; and

> <a id="s715-925-b"></a>
> **(b)**  does not become a member of another consolidated group; then, for the purposes of section 124-784B, the completion time for the arrangement is taken to happen at the time of the cessation.

> **Note:** Sections 124-784A and 124-784B apply to arrangements for restructures. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            357 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026
