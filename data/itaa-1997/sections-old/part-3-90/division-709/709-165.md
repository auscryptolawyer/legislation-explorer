# 709-165  Subsidiary member is former exempting entity


<a id="s709-165-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section operates if:

> <a id="s709-165-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) is neither an exempting entity nor a [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity); and

> <a id="s709-165-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group at a time (also the joining time); and

> <a id="s709-165-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity is a [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity) at the joining time.

<a id="s709-165-2"></a>

> **Note:** Any surplus in the subsidiary’s franking account will be transferred to the head company’s franking account: see subsection 709-60(2).

> **Note:** If the subsidiary’s franking account is in deficit, it will be liable for franking deficit tax: see subsection 709-60(3). This deficit may be increased by item 4 in the table in subsection (2).

> **Note:** The subsidiary’s franking account does not operate while it is a member of the group: see section 709-65.
