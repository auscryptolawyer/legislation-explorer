# 709-65  Subsidiary member’s franking account does not operate

The [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) of an entity that is a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) does not operate during the period:

> <a id="s709-65-a"></a>
> **(a)**  beginning just after the entity becomes a subsidiary member of the group; and

> <a id="s709-65-b"></a>
> **(b)**  ending when the entity ceases to be a subsidiary member of the group. 198                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 Other rules applying when entities become subsidiary members etc. Division 709 Section 709-70 Treatment of head company’s franking account
