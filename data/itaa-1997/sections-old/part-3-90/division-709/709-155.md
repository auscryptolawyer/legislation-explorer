# 709-155  Testing consolidated groups


<a id="s709-155-1"></a>
**(1)**  To determine whether a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) is an [[*exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity) or [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity), the tests in Division 208 are applied to the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the group. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            203 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

<a id="s709-155-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, there are some additional rules that can alter the way that Division 208 applies to a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group). These are set out in sections 709-160 to 709-175.

<a id="s709-155-3"></a>
**(3)**  In applying those rules to an entity that is a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group):

> <a id="s709-155-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  Division 208 is to be applied before those rules; and

> <a id="s709-155-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  that Division is to be applied just after the entity became a member of the group but, for a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member), it is to be applied on the assumption that the subsidiary was not a member of the group at that time.

<a id="s709-155-4"></a>
**(4)**  Except as mentioned in paragraph (3)(b), Division 208 has no application to a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group).
