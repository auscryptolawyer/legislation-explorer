# 709-95  Payment of group liability by former subsidiary member


<a id="s709-95-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section operates if: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            201 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s709-95-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity (the former subsidiary) ceases to be a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) (the old group) at a particular time (the leaving time); and

> <a id="s709-95-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  at or after the leaving time, the former subsidiary:

> > <a id="s709-95-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  [[*pays a PAYG instalment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pays-a-payg-instalment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pays-a-payg-instalment) for which it was jointly and severally liable under subsection 721-15(1) because it was a subsidiary member of the old group; or

> > <a id="s709-95-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  [[*pays income tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pays-income-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pays-income-tax) for which it was jointly and severally liable under that subsection because it was a subsidiary member of the old group; and

> <a id="s709-95-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  apart from this section, a [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) would arise under section 205-15 in the [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) of the former subsidiary at a time (the crediting time) because of that payment.

<a id="s709-95-2"></a>
**(2)**  The credit:

> <a id="s709-95-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  does not arise at the crediting time in the [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) of the former subsidiary; and

> <a id="s709-95-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  instead, arises at the crediting time in the franking account of the entity that was the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the old group at the leaving time.
