# 709-75  Debits arising in head company’s franking account


<a id="s709-75-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section operates if a debit would arise in the [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) of a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) at a time (the debiting time) apart from section 709-65.

<a id="s709-75-2"></a>
**(2)**  A debit arises in the [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the group at the debiting time.

> **Note:** A debit can also arise in the head company’s franking account at any time under section 205-30.

<a id="s709-75-3"></a>
**(3)**  The amount of the debit is the same as the amount of the debit that would arise in the [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) of the [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member). Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            199 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

<a id="s709-75-4"></a>
**(4)**  This section does not apply to a debit arising in the [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)’s [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) under paragraph 709-60(2)(a).

> **Note:** Such a debit arises if the entity that became the subsidiary member had a surplus in its franking account just before the time it became the subsidiary member.
