# 709-55  Object of this Subdivision

The object of this Subdivision is for each [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) to operate what is in substance a single [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account), by ensuring that:

> <a id="s709-55-a"></a>
> **(a)**  there is a nil balance in the franking accounts of entities becoming [[*subsidiary members*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group; and

> <a id="s709-55-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the franking accounts of those subsidiary members do not operate while they are subsidiary members; and

> <a id="s709-55-c"></a>
> **(c)**  debits or credits that would otherwise arise in the franking accounts of the subsidiary members arise instead in the franking account of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the group; and

> <a id="s709-55-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the head company is the only [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the group that can frank distributions.
