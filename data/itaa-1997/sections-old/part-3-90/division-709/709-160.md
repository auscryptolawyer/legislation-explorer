# 709-160  Subsidiary member is exempting entity


<a id="s709-160-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section operates if:

> <a id="s709-160-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) is neither an exempting entity nor a [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity); and

> <a id="s709-160-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group at a time (the joining time); and

> <a id="s709-160-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity is an [[*exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity) at the joining time.
