# 709-175  Head company is former exempting entity


<a id="s709-175-1"></a>
**(1)**  Subsection (2) operates if:

> <a id="s709-175-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) is a [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity); and

> <a id="s709-175-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group at a time (also the joining time); and

> <a id="s709-175-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity is an [[*exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity) at the joining time.

<a id="s709-175-2"></a>
**(2)**  These rules apply to the [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group). Rules applying to [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) Item      Rule 1         There is no change to the status of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) 2         If the subsidiary member’s [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) has a [[*franking surplus*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-surplus)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-surplus) at the joining time:

> <a id="s709-175-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a debit equal to that surplus arises in that account at the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-175-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a credit equal to that surplus arises in the [[*exempting account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account) of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) at the joining time 3         Subsection 709-60(2) (about franking surplus) does not apply to the [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)

> **Note:** If the subsidiary’s franking account is in deficit, it will be liable for franking deficit tax: see subsection 709-60(3). Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                                   207 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> **Note:** The subsidiary’s franking account does not operate while it is a member of the group: see section 709-65.

<a id="s709-175-3"></a>
**(3)**  Subsection (4) operates if:

> <a id="s709-175-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) is a [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity); and

> <a id="s709-175-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group at a time (also the joining time); and

> <a id="s709-175-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity is a [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity) at the joining time.

<a id="s709-175-4"></a>
**(4)**  These rules apply to the [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group). Rules applying to [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) Item      Rule 1         There is no change to the status of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) 2         If the [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)’s [[*exempting account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account) has an [[*exempting surplus*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-surplus)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-surplus) at the joining time:

> <a id="s709-175-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a debit equal to that surplus arises in that account at the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-175-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a credit equal to that surplus arises in the exempting account of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) at the joining time 3         If the [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)’s [[*exempting account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account) has an [[*exempting deficit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-deficit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-deficit) at the joining time:

> <a id="s709-175-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a credit equal to that deficit arises in that account at the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-175-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a debit equal to that deficit arises in the subsidiary’s [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) just before the joining time 4         The [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)’s [[*exempting account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account) does not operate during the period:

> <a id="s709-175-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  starting just after the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-175-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  ending when the entity ceases to be a subsidiary member of the group

> **Note:** If the subsidiary’s franking account is in deficit, it will be liable for franking deficit tax: see subsection 709-60(3). This deficit may be increased by item 3 in the table in subsection (4).

<a id="s709-175-5"></a>
**(5)**  There is no change to the status of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) if:

> <a id="s709-175-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the head company is a [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity); and

> <a id="s709-175-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group; and

> <a id="s709-175-5-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity is neither an [[*exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity) nor a former exempting entity at the joining time.

> **Note:** If the subsidiary’s franking account is in surplus, that surplus will be transferred to the head company’s franking account: see subsection 709-60(2).

> **Note:** If the subsidiary’s franking account is in deficit, it will be liable for franking deficit tax: see subsection 709-60(3).

> **Note:** The subsidiary’s franking account does not operate while it is a member of the group: see section 709-65.
