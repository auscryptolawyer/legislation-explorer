# 709-80  Subsidiary member’s distributions on employee shares and certain preference shares taken to be distributions by the head company


<a id="s709-80-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section operates if:

> <a id="s709-80-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) makes a [[*frankable distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution); and

> <a id="s709-80-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the distribution is made because an entity (the shareholder) owns a [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share) in the subsidiary member; and

> <a id="s709-80-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the share must be disregarded under subsection 703-35(4) or 703-37(4); and

> <a id="s709-80-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the distribution is made to the shareholder, or to another entity because the shareholder owns the share; and

> <a id="s709-80-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the entity to which the distribution is made is not a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the group.

> **Note:** Subsection 703-35(4) requires certain shares acquired under employee share schemes to be disregarded.

> **Note:** Subsection 703-37(4) requires certain preference shares to be disregarded following an ADI restructure.

<a id="s709-80-2"></a>
**(2)**  Part 3-6 operates as if the *distribution were a [[*frankable distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution) made by the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the group to a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the head company.
