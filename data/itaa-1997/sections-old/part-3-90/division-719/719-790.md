# 719-790  Exception for interests in entity leaving MEC group

Membership interests in leaving entity

<a id="s719-790-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:

> <a id="s719-790-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the realised interest is a *membership interest; and

> <a id="s719-790-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  during the ownership period the first entity ceased to be a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group); the first condition in section 719-775 cannot be satisfied, because of that MEC group, at a time when the first entity was a member of the group, unless the interest needed to be disregarded under section 719-30 (about employee shares) in order for the first entity to be a member of the group at that time. Liabilities owed by leaving entity

<a id="s719-790-2"></a>
**(2)**  If the realised interest:

> <a id="s719-790-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  consists of a liability owed by the first entity to the owner; and

> <a id="s719-790-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  became an asset of the owner because subsection 701-1(1) (the single entity rule) ceased to apply to the first entity when it ceased to be a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group); Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            477 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 the first condition in section 719-775 cannot be satisfied, because of that MEC group, at a time when the first entity was a member of the group.
