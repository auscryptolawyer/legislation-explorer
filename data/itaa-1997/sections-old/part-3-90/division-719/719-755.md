# 719-755  Effect on MEC group cost setting rules if head company is losing entity or gaining entity for indirect value shift


<a id="s719-755-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section has effect for the purposes of working out the consequences (if any) of an [[*indirect value shift*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-value-shift)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-value-shift) if the *losing entity or *gaining entity is the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of a [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group). (Subsection (3) has effect in addition to section 727-455.) 474                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 MEC groups Division 719 Section 719-775

<a id="s719-755-2"></a>
**(2)**  An [[*equity or loan interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest) can be an [[*affected interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affected-interest) in the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) only if it is:

> <a id="s719-755-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an [[*equity or loan interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-equity-or-loan-interest) in the [[*top company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-top-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-top-company) for the MEC group; or

> <a id="s719-755-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an [[*indirect equity or loan interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-equity-or-loan-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-equity-or-loan-interest) in the top company.

<a id="s719-755-3"></a>
**(3)**  Subdivision 719-K (MEC group cost setting rules: pooling cases) applies to the [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group), in relation to the first time referred to in that Subdivision as a trigger time that happens at or after the [[*IVS time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ivs-time), on the basis that:

> <a id="s719-755-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  what would, apart from this section, be the pooled cost amount for the purposes of the formulas in subsections 719-570(1) and (2) is:

> > <a id="s719-755-3-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  if the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) is the *losing entity—reduced; or

> > <a id="s719-755-3-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if the head company is the gaining entity—increased; by the amount of the indirect value shift; and

> <a id="s719-755-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  paragraph (a) of this subsection also affects the application of those formulas because of subsection 719-570(3) (to work out the *reduced cost base of a *membership interest). Cancelling loss on realisation event for direct or indirect interest in a subsidiary member of a MEC group
