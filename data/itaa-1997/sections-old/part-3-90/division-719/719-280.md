# 719-280  Assumptions about the test company failing to meet the conditions in section 165-12


<a id="s719-280-1"></a>
**(1)**  Assume that the test company fails to meet the conditions in section 165-12 at the time an event described in subsection (2), (3) or (4) happens after the start of the [[*ownership test period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-test-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-test-period) for the focal company in relation to:

> <a id="s719-280-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group) whose [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) was the focal company; or

> <a id="s719-280-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the [[*potential MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-potential-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-potential-mec-group) whose membership was the same as the membership of that MEC group.

> **Note:** If the test company is assumed to fail to meet the conditions in section 165-12 for the claim year and the loss, the focal company is taken (under section 719-260) to have failed to meet those conditions.

<a id="s719-280-2"></a>
**(2)**  One event is the [[*potential MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-potential-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-potential-mec-group) ceasing to exist.

<a id="s719-280-3"></a>
**(3)**  Another event is something happening that meets these conditions:

> <a id="s719-280-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the thing happens at a time in relation to *membership interests in one or more of these entities:

> > <a id="s719-280-3-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a company that was just before that time a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group) and an [[*eligible tier-1 company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company) of the [[*top company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-top-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-top-company) for the MEC group;

> > <a id="s719-280-3-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  an entity interposed between a company described in subparagraph (i) and the company that was the top company for the group just before that time;

> <a id="s719-280-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the thing does not cause the [[*potential MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-potential-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-potential-mec-group) to cease to exist but does cause a change in the identity of the top company for the potential MEC group.

<a id="s719-280-4"></a>
**(4)**  Another event is the [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group) ceasing to exist because there ceases to be a *provisional head company of the group. 450                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 MEC groups Division 719 Section 719-285 Other causes of failure to meet conditions in section 165-12

<a id="s719-280-5"></a>
**(5)**  To avoid doubt, this section does not limit the circumstances in which the test company would have failed to meet the conditions in section 165-12 on the relevant assumptions set out in sections 719-270 and 719-275. Business continuity test and change of head company
