# 719-435  Distributions by subsidiary members of MEC group taken to be distributions by head company


<a id="s719-435-1"></a>
**(1)**  Part 3-6 operates as if a [[*frankable distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution) made by an [[*eligible tier-1 company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company) that:

> <a id="s719-435-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  is a member of a [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group); and

> <a id="s719-435-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is not the *provisional head company of the group; had been made by the provisional head company of the group to a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the provisional head company.

> **Note:** Part 3-6 deals with imputation.

<a id="s719-435-2"></a>
**(2)**  Part 3-6 operates as if a [[*frankable distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution) made by a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group) (the foreign-held subsidiary) that is not an [[*eligible tier-1 company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company) were a frankable distribution made by the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the group to a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the head company if:

> <a id="s719-435-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the foreign-held subsidiary meets the set of requirements in section 703-45, section 701C-10 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997 or section 701C-15 of that Act; and

> <a id="s719-435-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the frankable distribution is made to a foreign resident. 458                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 MEC groups Division 719 Section 719-450
