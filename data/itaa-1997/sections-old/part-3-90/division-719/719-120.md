# 719-120  Application


<a id="s719-120-1"></a>
**(1)**  This Subdivision applies if, at a particular time (the conversion time):

> <a id="s719-120-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) (the new group) is [[*created*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-created)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-created) from a [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group) (the old group); or

> <a id="s719-120-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a MEC group (the new group) is created from a consolidated group (the old group). 430                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 MEC groups Division 719 Section 719-125

<a id="s719-120-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, sections 719-130 and 719-135 apply only in relation to entities that:

> <a id="s719-120-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  were *members of the old group just before the conversion time; and

> <a id="s719-120-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  are members of the new group at that time.
