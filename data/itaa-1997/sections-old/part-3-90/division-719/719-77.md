# 719-77  Notice in relation to new eligible tier-1 members etc.


<a id="s719-77-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if:

> <a id="s719-77-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group) consists of the members of a [[*potential MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-potential-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-potential-mec-group) derived from one or more *eligible tier-1 companies of a [[*top company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-top-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-top-company); and

> <a id="s719-77-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  one or more other companies become eligible tier-1 companies of the top company at a time because of a choice under subsection 719-5(4).

<a id="s719-77-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group) must give the Commissioner a notice in the [[*approved form*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form) containing the following information:

> <a id="s719-77-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the identity of the head company;

> <a id="s719-77-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the time mentioned in paragraph (1)(b);

> <a id="s719-77-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the identity of each entity that became an [[*eligible tier-1 company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company) of the [[*top company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-top-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-top-company) in relation to the MEC group at that time because of the choice;

> <a id="s719-77-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the identity of each entity that became a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of the group at that time because of the choice;

> <a id="s719-77-2-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the identity of each entity that was a subsidiary member of the group at that time but was not such a subsidiary member when the notice is given. 424                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 MEC groups Division 719 Section 719-78

<a id="s719-77-3"></a>
**(3)**  The notice must be given no later than:

> <a id="s719-77-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) is required to give the Commissioner its [[*income tax return*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return) for the income year during which that time occurs—the day on which the head company gives the Commissioner that income tax return; or

> <a id="s719-77-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—the last day in the period within which the head company would be required to give the Commissioner such a return if it were required to give the Commissioner such a return.
