# 719-560  Pooled interests


<a id="s719-560-1"></a>
**(1)**  A pooled interest in an [[*eligible tier-1 company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-tier-1-company) that is a member of a [[*MEC group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mec-group) is a *membership interest in the eligible tier-1 company that is held by an entity that is not a member of the group. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            465 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> **Note:** A membership interest in the head company of a MEC group can be a pooled interest.

<a id="s719-560-2"></a>
**(2)**  Despite subsection (1), a *membership interest is not a pooled interest if it is:

> <a id="s719-560-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share) that is disregarded under subsection 719-30(2); or

> <a id="s719-560-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  held by an entity only as a nominee of one or more other entities each of which is a member of the group.
