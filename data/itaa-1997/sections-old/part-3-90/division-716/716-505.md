# 716-505  History for entitlement to tax offset: joining entity

If:

> <a id="s716-505-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a company becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group); and

> **(b)**  apart from this section, things happening in relation to the company before it became a subsidiary member would, because of section 701-5 (the entry history rule), be taken into account as things happening in relation to the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) for working out the head company’s *aggregated 384                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 Miscellaneous special rules Division 716 Section 716-510 turnover for the purposes of section 355-100 (tax offsets for R&D); the things happening are not to be taken into account as mentioned in paragraph (b).
