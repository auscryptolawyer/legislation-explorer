# 716-300  Prime cost method of working out decline in value


<a id="s716-300-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section has effect if:

> <a id="s716-300-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity (the joining entity) becomes a [[*subsidiary member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary-member) of a [[*consolidated group*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-consolidated-group) at a time (the joining time); and

> <a id="s716-300-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  because of subsection 40-80(1), the joining entity could (or did) deduct for a period before the joining time the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) of a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) that became an asset of the [[*head company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-head-company) of the group at the joining time because 370                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Consolidated groups Part 3-90 Miscellaneous special rules Division 716 Section 716-300 section 701-1 (Single entity rule) applied to the joining entity; and

> <a id="s716-300-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the joining entity could not deduct an amount under Subdivision 40-B (except because of subsection 40-80(1)) for the income year that includes the joining time for that cost.

> **Note:** Subdivision 40-B allows deductions for the decline in value of depreciating assets. Subsection 40-80(1), which is in that Subdivision, provides that the decline in value of certain assets used for exploration and prospecting equals their cost.

<a id="s716-300-2"></a>
**(2)**  Subsection 701-55(2) has effect as if the [[*prime cost method*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-prime-cost-method)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-prime-cost-method) for working out the decline in value of the [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) applied just before the joining time.

> **Note:** This may affect both the method of working out the decline in value of the asset and the asset’s effective life.
