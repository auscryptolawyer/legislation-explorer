# 59-70  List of SRWUIP programs


<a id="s59-70-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*Water Secretary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-secretary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-secretary) must keep a list of [[*SRWUIP programs*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program). The list must:

> <a id="s59-70-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  specify the days for which each program is on the list; and

> <a id="s59-70-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  be published on the [[*Water Department*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-department)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-department)’s website.

> *[[*Example*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-example):** A program could be listed for each day on or after 1 July 2011. Entering SRWUIP programs on the list

<a id="s59-70-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*Water Secretary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-secretary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-secretary) must enter on the list each [[*SRWUIP program*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program) (and its days) in accordance with a direction under subsection (3).

<a id="s59-70-3"></a>
**(3)**  The Minister and the [[*Water Minister*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-minister)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-minister) may jointly direct the [[*Water Secretary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-secretary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-secretary) to enter a program (and its days) on the list only if the Water Minister has notified the Minister in writing that the Water Minister is satisfied that the program:

> <a id="s59-70-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  is a [[*SRWUIP program*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program); and

> <a id="s59-70-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  will generate efficiencies in water use through infrastructure improvements. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             387 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

<a id="s59-70-4"></a>
**(4)**  A direction under subsection (3) must be in writing and specify the days for which the [[*SRWUIP program*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program) is to be on the list. Some or all of those days may be before the day the direction is given. Changing the days for which a SRWUIP program is listed

<a id="s59-70-5"></a>
**(5)**  The Minister and the [[*Water Minister*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-minister)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-minister) may jointly direct the [[*Water Secretary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-secretary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-secretary) to change the list to specify:

> <a id="s59-70-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  additional days (including days before the day the direction is given) for which a [[*SRWUIP program*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program) is on the list; or

> <a id="s59-70-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the final day (which must be after the day the direction is given) for which a SRWUIP program is on the list. The [[*Water Secretary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-secretary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-secretary) must change the list accordingly.

<a id="s59-70-6"></a>
**(6)**  A direction under subsection (5) must be in writing. Giving directions

<a id="s59-70-7"></a>
**(7)**  The Minister and the [[*Water Minister*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-minister)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-minister) must have regard to the policies and budgetary priorities of the Commonwealth Government in deciding whether to give a direction under subsection (3) or (5).
