# 59-15  Mining payments


<a id="s59-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  These are not assessable income and are not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income):

> <a id="s59-15-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*mining payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-payment) made to a [[*distributing body*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distributing-body)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distributing-body);

> <a id="s59-15-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a mining payment made to one or more [[*Indigenous persons*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indigenous-person)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indigenous-person), or applied for their benefit.

<a id="s59-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  A payment:

> <a id="s59-15-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  made to a [[*distributing body*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distributing-body)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distributing-body); or

> <a id="s59-15-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  made to one or more [[*Indigenous persons*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indigenous-person)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indigenous-person), or applied for their benefit; is not assessable income and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) if the payment is made by a [[*distributing body*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distributing-body)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distributing-body) out of a [[*mining payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-payment) that it has received.

<a id="s59-15-3"></a>
**(3)**  A payment made to a [[*distributing body*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distributing-body)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distributing-body) by another distributing body, out of a [[*mining payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-payment) received by the other distributing body, is taken to be a mining payment for the purposes of: 378                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Non-assessable income Part 2-15 Particular amounts of non-assessable non-exempt income Division 59 Section 59-20

> <a id="s59-15-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  any further applications of subsection (2); and

> <a id="s59-15-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  any further applications of this subsection.

<a id="s59-15-4"></a>
**(4)**  Subsection (2) does not apply to a payment by a [[*distributing body*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distributing-body)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distributing-body) for the purposes of meeting its administrative costs.

<a id="s59-15-5"></a>
**(5)**  This section does not apply to an amount paid to or applied for the benefit of a person if it is remuneration or consideration for goods or services provided by that person.
