# 59-65  Water infrastructure improvement payments


<a id="s59-65-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*SRWUIP payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment), in respect of a [[*SRWUIP program*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program), to an entity that is a participant in the program is not assessable income and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) if:

> <a id="s59-65-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity has made a choice under subsection (2) for the program; and 384                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Non-assessable income Part 2-15 Particular amounts of non-assessable non-exempt income Division 59 Section 59-65

> <a id="s59-65-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if the payment is an [[*indirect SRWUIP payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-srwuip-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-indirect-srwuip-payment)—the entity *derives the payment because it owns an asset (otherwise than under a [[*financial arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-arrangement)) to which the program relates.

> **Note:** One of the requirements for a SRWUIP payment is for the SRWUIP program to be on the published list of SRWUIP programs for the day the payment is made (see subsection 59-67(5)).

<a id="s59-65-2"></a>
**(2)**  An entity may make a choice for a [[*SRWUIP program*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program) under this subsection if, in an income year:

> <a id="s59-65-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity *derives a [[*SRWUIP payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment) in respect of the program but has not, in an earlier income year:

> > <a id="s59-65-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  derived a SRWUIP payment in respect of the program; or

> > <a id="s59-65-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  incurred [[*SRWUIP expenditure*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-expenditure)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-expenditure) in respect of the program; or

> <a id="s59-65-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity incurs SRWUIP expenditure in respect of the program but has not, in an earlier income year:

> > <a id="s59-65-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  derived a SRWUIP payment in respect of the program; or

> > <a id="s59-65-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  incurred SRWUIP expenditure in respect of the program. Disregard subsection 26-100(3) (about expenditure that is never SRWUIP expenditure) for the purposes of this subsection.

<a id="s59-65-3"></a>
**(3)**  The choice must be:

> <a id="s59-65-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  made in the [[*approved form*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form); and

> <a id="s59-65-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  made:

> > <a id="s59-65-3-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  unless subparagraph (ii) or (iii) applies—on or before the day the entity lodges its [[*income tax return*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return) for the income year; or

> > <a id="s59-65-3-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if the Commissioner makes an assessment of the entity’s taxable income for the income year before the entity lodges its income tax return for the income year, and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             385 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 subparagraph (iii) does not apply—on or before the day the Commissioner makes that assessment; or

> > <a id="s59-65-3-b-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  within such further time as the Commissioner allows. The choice cannot be revoked. Integrity rule

<a id="s59-65-4"></a>
**(4)**  Subsection (1) does not apply if, at the time the entity *derives the [[*SRWUIP payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-payment) in respect of a [[*SRWUIP program*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-srwuip-program), it is reasonable to conclude that:

> <a id="s59-65-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity will not incur expenditure at least equal to the payment on works required by the program; and

> <a id="s59-65-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  despite not incurring such expenditure, the entity will comply with the program because an [[*associate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate) of the entity will incur expenditure on those works; and

> <a id="s59-65-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the associate has not made, and will not make, a choice under subsection (2) for the program.
