# 59-40  Issue of rights


<a id="s59-40-1"></a>
**(1)**  The *market value, as at the time of issue (the issue time), of rights issued to you:

> <a id="s59-40-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  by a company to [[*acquire*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquire) *shares in that company; or

> <a id="s59-40-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  by a trustee of a unit trust to acquire units in that trust; is not assessable income and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) as at the issue time if the conditions in subsection (2) are satisfied.

<a id="s59-40-2"></a>
**(2)**  The conditions are as follows:

> <a id="s59-40-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  at the issue time, you must already own *shares in the company or units in the unit trust (the original interests); 380                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Non-assessable income Part 2-15 Particular amounts of non-assessable non-exempt income Division 59 Section 59-50

> <a id="s59-40-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the rights must be issued to you because of your ownership of the original interests;

> <a id="s59-40-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the original interests and the rights must not be [[*revenue assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset) or [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock) at the issue time;

> <a id="s59-40-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  if you acquired a beneficial interest in the rights under an [[*employee share scheme*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme)—neither Subdivision 83A-B nor 83A-C (about employee share schemes) applies to the beneficial interest;

> <a id="s59-40-2-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the original interests and the rights must not be *traditional securities;

> <a id="s59-40-2-f"></a>
> **(f)**  the original interests must not be [[*convertible interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-convertible-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-convertible-interest).
