# 54-45  Personal injury lump sum exemption for injured person

A payment of a [[*personal injury lump sum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-injury-lump-sum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-injury-lump-sum) that is made to the [[*injured person*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-injured-person)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-injured-person) is exempt from income tax if:

> <a id="s54-45-a"></a>
> **(a)**  there is at least one [[*personal injury annuity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-injury-annuity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-injury-annuity) (provided under the same [[*structured settlement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-structured-settlement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-structured-settlement) or [[*structured order*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-structured-order)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-structured-order)) that satisfies the conditions in Subdivision 54-B; and

> <a id="s54-45-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the other conditions in this Subdivision are satisfied.
