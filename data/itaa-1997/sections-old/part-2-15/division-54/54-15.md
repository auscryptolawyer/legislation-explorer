# 54-15  Personal injury annuity exemption for injured person

A payment of a [[*personal injury annuity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-injury-annuity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-injury-annuity) that is made to the [[*injured person*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-injured-person)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-injured-person) is exempt from income tax if the conditions in this Subdivision are satisfied.

> **Note:** Section 54-70 provides a tax exemption if the payment is instead made to the trustee of a trust.
