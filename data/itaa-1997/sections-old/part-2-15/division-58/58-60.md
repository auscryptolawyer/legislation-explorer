# 58-60  Purpose of rules in this Subdivision

This Subdivision sets out rules that affect the way in which the [[*transition entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transition-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transition-entity) or the purchaser work out the decline in value of, and balancing adjustments for, [[*privatised assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-privatised-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-privatised-asset) under Division 40 after the [[*transition time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transition-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-transition-time) or the [[*acquisition time*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquisition-time)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-acquisition-time).
