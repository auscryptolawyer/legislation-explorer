# 51-54  Gain or profit from disposal of eligible venture capital investments

Partners in VCLPs and ESVCLPs

<a id="s51-54-1"></a>
**(1)**  An entity’s share of any gain or profit made from the disposal or other realisation of an [[*eligible venture capital investment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment) is exempt from income tax if:

> <a id="s51-54-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is made by a [[*VCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp), or an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp), that is [[*unconditionally registered*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered); and

> <a id="s51-54-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  were that disposal or other realisation to be a *disposal of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), the entity’s share of any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) would be disregarded under section 118-405 or 118-407. (1A) An entity’s share of any gain or profit made:

> <a id="s51-54-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  by an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp) that is [[*unconditionally registered*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered); and

> <a id="s51-54-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  from the disposal or other realisation of an [[*eligible venture capital investment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment); is exempt from income tax to the extent that, were that disposal or other realisation to be a *disposal of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), the equivalent [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) arising from the [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) would be disregarded because of a partial exemption from the CGT event under section 118-408. Partners in AFOFs

<a id="s51-54-2"></a>
**(2)**  An entity’s share of any gain or profit made from the disposal or other realisation of an [[*eligible venture capital investment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment) is exempt from income tax if:

> <a id="s51-54-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is made by:

> > <a id="s51-54-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  an [[*AFOF*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-afof)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-afof) that is [[*unconditionally registered*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered); or

> > <a id="s51-54-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a [[*VCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-vclp), or an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp), that is unconditionally registered and in which an AFOF that is [[*unconditionally registered*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unconditionally-registered) is a partner; and 282                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Non-assessable income Part 2-15 Exempt amounts Division 51 Section 51-55

> <a id="s51-54-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  were that disposal or other realisation to be a *disposal of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), the entity’s share of any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) would be disregarded under section 118-410. Eligible venture capital investors

<a id="s51-54-3"></a>
**(3)**  Any gain or profit made from the disposal or other realisation of an [[*eligible venture capital investment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investment) is exempt from income tax if:

> <a id="s51-54-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are an [[*eligible venture capital investor*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investor)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-venture-capital-investor); and

> <a id="s51-54-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  were that disposal or other realisation to be a *disposal of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) would be disregarded under section 118-415.
