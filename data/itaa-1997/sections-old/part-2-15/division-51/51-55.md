# 51-55  Gain or profit from disposal of venture capital equity

Any gain or profit made from the disposal or other realisation of [[*venture capital equity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-equity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-equity) in a [[*resident investment vehicle*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-resident-investment-vehicle)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-resident-investment-vehicle) is exempt from income tax if:

> <a id="s51-55-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is made by a [[*venture capital entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-entity) or a *limited partnership referred to in subsection 118-515(2); and

> <a id="s51-55-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if that disposal or other realisation were a *disposal of a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset), any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) would be disregarded under Subdivision 118-G.
