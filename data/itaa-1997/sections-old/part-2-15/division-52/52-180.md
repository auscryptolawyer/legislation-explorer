# 52-180  National Disability Insurance Scheme amounts are exempt

An [[*NDIS amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ndis-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ndis-amount) *derived by a participant (within the meaning of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013) is exempt from income tax.
