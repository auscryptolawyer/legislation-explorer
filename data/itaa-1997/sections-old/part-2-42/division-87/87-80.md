# 87-80  Revoking personal services business determinations

The Commissioner must, by giving written notice to the individual or [[*personal services entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-entity) on whose application a [[*personal services business determination*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-business-determination)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-business-determination) was made, revoke the determination if the Commissioner is no longer satisfied that there are grounds on which the determination could be made.
