# 87-85  Review of decisions

A person who is dissatisfied with;

> <a id="s87-85-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a decision of the Commissioner to make, vary or revoke a [[*personal services business determination*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-business-determination)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-business-determination); or

> <a id="s87-85-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the Commissioner’s refusal of an application for a personal services business determination or for a variation of a personal services business determination; may object against the decision in the manner set out in Part IVC of the Taxation Administration Act 1953. 128                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Capital gains and losses: general topics Part 3-1 A Guide to capital gains and losses Division 100 Section 100-1 Chapter 3—Specialist liability rules
