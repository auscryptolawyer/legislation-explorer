# 87-40  Application of this Division to certain agents

Object of this section

<a id="s87-40-1"></a>
**(1)**  The object of this section is to modify the operation of this Division for *agents who bear entrepreneurial risk in the way they provide services. 118                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Personal services income Part 2-42 Personal services businesses Division 87 Section 87-40 Agent rules do not apply (1A) The rules in section 960-105 (Certain entities treated as agents) do not apply to this section. Agents covered by this section

<a id="s87-40-2"></a>
**(2)**  Subsection 87-15(3) and section 87-20 apply, in the manner specified in this section, to an individual or [[*personal services entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-entity) if:

> <a id="s87-40-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the individual or personal services entity is an [[*agent*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-agent)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-agent) of another entity (the principal) but not the principal’s employee; and

> <a id="s87-40-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the agent receives income from the principal that is for services that the agent provides to other entities (customers) on the principal’s behalf; and

> <a id="s87-40-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  at least 75% of that income is commissions, or fees, based on the agent’s performance in providing services to the customers on the principal’s behalf; and

> <a id="s87-40-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the agent actively seeks other entities to whom the agent could provide services on the principal’s behalf; and

> <a id="s87-40-2-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the agent does not provide any services to the customers, on the principal’s behalf, using premises:

> > <a id="s87-40-2-e-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  that the principal or an [[*associate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate) of the principal owns; or

> > <a id="s87-40-2-e-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  in which the principal or an associate of the principal has a leasehold interest; unless the agent uses the premises under an arrangement entered into at *arm’s length. Whether personal services income is from one source

<a id="s87-40-3"></a>
**(3)**  If the [[*agent*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-agent)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-agent) is an individual, in applying subsection 87-15(3) to the [[*personal services income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-income) of the agent during an income year, any part of the agent’s personal services income from the principal that:

> <a id="s87-40-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the agent gains or produces during the income year; and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            119 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s87-40-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is for services that the agent provided to a customer on the principal’s behalf in the income year or an earlier income year; is treated as if it were personal services income from the customer, and not personal services income from the principal.

<a id="s87-40-4"></a>
**(4)**  If the [[*agent*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-agent)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-agent) is a [[*personal services entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-entity), in applying subsection 87-15(3) to an individual’s [[*personal services income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-income) that is included in the entity’s [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income) or [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income) during an income year, any part of the individual’s personal services income from the principal that:

> <a id="s87-40-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the agent gains or produces during the income year; and

> <a id="s87-40-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is for services that the individual or the agent provided to a customer on the principal’s behalf in the income year or an earlier income year; is treated as if it were personal services income from the customer, and not personal services income from the principal. The unrelated clients test for a personal services business

<a id="s87-40-5"></a>
**(5)**  In determining whether, during an income year, the [[*agent*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-agent)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-agent) meets the unrelated clients test under section 87-20, any services the agent provided in the income year or an earlier income year:

> <a id="s87-40-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  for which the agent gains or produces, during the income year, personal services income from the principal; and

> <a id="s87-40-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  that were provided to a customer on the principal’s behalf; are treated for the purposes of paragraph 87-20(1)(a) as if the agent, and not the principal, provided them to the customer.
