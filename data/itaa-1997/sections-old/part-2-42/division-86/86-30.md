# 86-30  Assessable income etc. of the personal services entity

[[*Ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income) or [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income) of the [[*personal services entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-entity) is neither assessable income nor [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) of the entity, to the extent that it is [[*personal services income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-income) included in your assessable income under section 86-15.

> **Note:** Subsection 118-20(4) prevents this income being treated as a capital gain. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                           101 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026
