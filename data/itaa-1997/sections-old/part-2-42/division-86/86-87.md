# 86-87  Personal services entity cannot deduct net personal services income loss

The total amount of the deductions to which a [[*personal services entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-entity) is entitled for an income year is reduced by the amount of any deduction that an individual, whose [[*personal services income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-personal-services-income) is ordinary or statutory income of the entity for that income year, is entitled to under section 86-27.
