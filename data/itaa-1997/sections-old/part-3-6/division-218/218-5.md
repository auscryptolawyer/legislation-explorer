# 218-5  Application of imputation rules to co-operative companies


<a id="s218-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*imputation system*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-imputation-system)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-imputation-system) applies to a *co-operative company in the same way as it applies to any other company but with the modifications set out in this section.

<a id="s218-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  Each reference to a *distribution is taken to include a reference to an amount distributed as mentioned in paragraph 120(1)(a) or (b) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936.

<a id="s218-5-3"></a>
**(3)**  Despite subsection 202-75(1) (about giving distribution statements), a *co-operative company does not have to give the recipient of a [[*frankable distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution) a [[*distribution statement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distribution-statement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distribution-statement) unless the [[*franking percentage*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-percentage)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-percentage) for the distribution is greater than zero.
