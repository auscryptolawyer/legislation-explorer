# 220-605  Effect on exempting account if NZ franking choice ceases to be in force


<a id="s220-605-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section has effect if:

> <a id="s220-605-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a company has made an [[*NZ franking choice*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-nz-franking-choice)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-nz-franking-choice); and

> <a id="s220-605-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the choice is revoked or cancelled at a time (the end time); and

> <a id="s220-605-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  immediately before the end time:

> > <a id="s220-605-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the company is a foreign resident; and

> > <a id="s220-605-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the company is a [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity). Exempting debit if exempting surplus just before end time

<a id="s220-605-2"></a>
**(2)**  An [[*exempting debit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-debit) arises in the company’s [[*exempting account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account) at the end time if the account was in [[*surplus*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-surplus)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-surplus) immediately before that time. The amount of the debit equals the [[*exempting surplus*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-surplus)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-surplus). If exempting deficit just before end time

<a id="s220-605-3"></a>
**(3)**  If the company’s [[*exempting account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account) was in [[*deficit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit) immediately before the end time:

> <a id="s220-605-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*franking debit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit) equal to that deficit arises in the company’s [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) immediately before the end time; and

> <a id="s220-605-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an [[*exempting credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-credit) equal to that deficit arises in the company’s exempting account at the end time.
