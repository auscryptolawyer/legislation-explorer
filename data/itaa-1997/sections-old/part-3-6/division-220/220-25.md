# 220-25  Application of provisions of Part 3-6 outside this Division


<a id="s220-25-1"></a>
**(1)**  The provisions of Part 3-6 outside this Division apply in relation to a company that is an [[*NZ franking company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-nz-franking-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-nz-franking-company) at a time as if it were an Australian resident at that time.

<a id="s220-25-2"></a>
**(2)**  They apply with the modifications made by the other sections of this Division. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            241 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026
