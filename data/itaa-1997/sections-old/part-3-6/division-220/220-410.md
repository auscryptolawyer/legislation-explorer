# 220-410  Franking credit reduced if tax offset reduced


<a id="s220-410-1"></a>
**(1)**  If, under section 220-400 or 220-405, a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)’s [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) (the reduced tax offset) for the [[*franked distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution) described in that section is less than it would be apart from that section, the [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) arising in that entity’s [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) because of the *distribution is equal to the reduced tax offset.
