# 203-25  Benchmark rule

An entity must not make a [[*frankable distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution) whose [[*franking percentage*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-percentage)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-percentage) differs from the entity’s [[*benchmark franking percentage*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-benchmark-franking-percentage)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-benchmark-franking-percentage) for the [[*franking period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-period) in which the distribution is made. This is the benchmark rule.

> **Note:** If a corporate tax entity franks a distribution in breach of this rule, the distribution will still be a franked distribution, although consequences will flow under section 203-50. 20                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 The imputation system Part 3-6 Benchmark rule Division 203 Section 203-30
