# 208-125  Exempting surplus and deficit


<a id="s208-125-1"></a>
**(1)**  An entity’s [[*exempting account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account) is in surplus at a particular time if, at that time, the sum of the [[*exempting credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-credit) in the account exceeds the sum of the [[*exempting debits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-debit) in the account. The amount of the exempting surplus is the amount of the excess. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            147 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

<a id="s208-125-2"></a>
**(2)**  An entity’s [[*exempting account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account) is in deficit at a particular time if, at that time, the sum of the [[*exempting debits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-debit) in the account exceeds the sum of the [[*exempting credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-credit) in the account. The amount of the exempting deficit is the amount of the excess.
