# 208-195  Division 207 does not generally apply

Division 207 does not apply to a *distribution by an [[*exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity), unless expressly applied under this Subdivision.
