# 208-60  Franking with an exempting credit

An entity franks a *distribution with an exempting credit if:

> <a id="s208-60-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity is a [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity) when the distribution is made; and

> <a id="s208-60-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity is a [[*franking entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity) that satisfies the [[*residency requirement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-residency-requirement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-residency-requirement) when the distribution is made; and

> <a id="s208-60-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the distribution is a [[*frankable distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution); and 134                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 The imputation system Part 3-6 Exempting entities and former exempting entities Division 208 Section 208-65

> <a id="s208-60-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the entity allocates an [[*exempting credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-credit) to the distribution.

> **Note:** The residency requirement for an entity making a distribution is set out in section 202-20.
