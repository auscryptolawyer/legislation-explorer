# 208-235  Distributions to employees acquiring shares under eligible employee share schemes

Division 207 also applies to a *distribution *franked with an exempting credit made by a [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity) as if it were a [[*franked distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution) if:

> <a id="s208-235-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the distribution is made to an individual who, at the time the distribution is made, is an employee of:

> > <a id="s208-235-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the former exempting entity; or

> > <a id="s208-235-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a [[*subsidiary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-subsidiary) of the former exempting entity; and

> <a id="s208-235-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the employee acquired a beneficial interest in the [[*share*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share) on which the distribution is made:

> > <a id="s208-235-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  under an [[*employee share scheme*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme); and

> > <a id="s208-235-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  in circumstances specified as relevant in section 208-215; and

> <a id="s208-235-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the employee does not hold that beneficial interest as a trustee.
