# 208-80  Additional information to be included by a former exempting entity or exempting entity


<a id="s208-80-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*former exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-former-exempting-entity) that makes a *distribution *franked with an exempting credit must include in the [[*distribution statement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distribution-statement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distribution-statement) given to the recipient, a statement that there is an [[*exempting credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-credit) of a specified amount on the distribution.

<a id="s208-80-2"></a>
**(2)**  An [[*exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity) that makes a [[*frankable distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution) to a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) must include in the [[*distribution statement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distribution-statement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distribution-statement) given to the member, a statement to the effect that members who are Australian residents are not entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) or [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) as a result of the distribution, except for certain *corporate tax entities, and employees who receive the distribution in connection with certain [[*employee share schemes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme).

<a id="s208-80-3"></a>
**(3)**  If, under subsection (1) or (2), a statement must be included in a [[*distribution statement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distribution-statement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distribution-statement), the distribution statement is taken not to have been given unless the statement is included. 136                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 The imputation system Part 3-6 Exempting entities and former exempting entities Division 208 Section 208-85
