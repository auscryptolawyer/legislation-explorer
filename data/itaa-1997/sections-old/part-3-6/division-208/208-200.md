# 208-200  Distributions to exempting entities


<a id="s208-200-1"></a>
**(1)**  Division 207 applies to a [[*franked distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution) made by an [[*exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity) to another exempting entity if the distribution gives rise to a [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) for the other exempting entity under item 5 or 6 of the table in section 208-130.

<a id="s208-200-2"></a>
**(2)**  Division 207 applies to a [[*franked distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution) that is made by an [[*exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity) and [[*flows indirectly*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-flows-indirectly)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-flows-indirectly) to another exempting entity if the distribution gives rise to a [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) for that other entity under item 7 of the table in section 208-130.
