# 202-47  Distributions of certain ADI profits following restructure


<a id="s202-47-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies to an amount paid by a body corporate if:

> <a id="s202-47-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the body corporate is a non-operating holding company within the meaning of the Financial Sector (Transfer and Restructure) Act 1999; and

> <a id="s202-47-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a restructure instrument under Part 4A of that Act is in force in relation to the body; and

> <a id="s202-47-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  because of the restructure to which the instrument relates, an *ADI becomes a subsidiary (within the meaning of that Act) of the body; and

> <a id="s202-47-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the amount is sourced, directly or indirectly, from the profits of the ADI before the restructure instrument came into force; and 10                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 The imputation system Part 3-6 Franking a distribution Division 202 Section 202-50

> <a id="s202-47-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the amount would have been a [[*frankable distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution) if it had been distributed by the ADI before the restructure instrument came into force.

<a id="s202-47-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount:

> <a id="s202-47-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  is taken to be a dividend paid by the body, for the purposes of this Act (and so is a *distribution by the body); and

> <a id="s202-47-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is not taken to be an [[*unfrankable*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unfrankable)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-unfrankable) distribution by the body just because of paragraph 202-45(e) (which makes distributions from [[*share capital accounts*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share-capital-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-share-capital-account) unfrankable).
