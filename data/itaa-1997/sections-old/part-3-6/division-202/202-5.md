# 202-5  Franking a distribution

An entity franks a *distribution if:

> <a id="s202-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity is a [[*franking entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity) that satisfies the [[*residency requirement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-residency-requirement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-residency-requirement) when the distribution is made; and

> <a id="s202-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the distribution is a [[*frankable distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution); and

> <a id="s202-5-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity allocates a [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) to the distribution. 6                                     Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 The imputation system Part 3-6 Franking a distribution Division 202 Section 202-10

> **Note:** Division 205 deals with a corporate tax entity’s franking account and sets out when credits, known as franking credits, and debits, known as franking debits, arise in that account.

> **Note:** The mechanism by which an entity allocates a franking credit to a distribution (for example, whether it is done by resolution or some other means) is determined by the entity.
