# 210-140  Effect of a liability to pay venture capital deficit tax on franking deficit tax


<a id="s210-140-1"></a>
**(1)**  If an entity is liable to pay [[*venture capital deficit tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-deficit-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-deficit-tax) under subsection 210-135(2) because its [[*venture capital sub-account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-sub-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-sub-account) is in [[*deficit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit) at the end of an income year, the amount (if any) of [[*franking deficit tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-deficit-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-deficit-tax) that the entity would otherwise be liable to pay under subsection 205-45(2) because its [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) is in [[*deficit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit) at that time is reduced by the amount of the liability for venture capital deficit tax.

<a id="s210-140-2"></a>
**(2)**  If an entity is liable to pay [[*venture capital deficit tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-deficit-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-deficit-tax) under subsection 210-135(3) because it ceases to be a *PDF during an income year, the amount (if any) of [[*franking deficit tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-deficit-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-deficit-tax) that the entity would otherwise be liable to pay under subsection 205-45(3) because it ceases to be a [[*franking entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity) at that time is reduced by the amount of the liability for [[*venture capital deficit tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-deficit-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-deficit-tax).
