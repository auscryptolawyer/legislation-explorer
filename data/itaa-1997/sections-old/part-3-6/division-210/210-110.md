# 210-110  Determining the extent to which a franking credit is reasonably attributable to a particular payment of tax

In determining the extent to which a [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) is reasonably attributable to a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) in relation to a [[*qualifying SME investment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-qualifying-sme-investment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-qualifying-sme-investment) of the *PDF, have regard to:

> <a id="s210-110-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the extent to which the credit can reasonably be attributed to the [[*payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payment) of a PAYG instalment or the payment of 182                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 The imputation system Part 3-6 Venture capital franking Division 210 Section 210-115 income tax by the PDF in relation to its *section 124ZZB SME assessable income for an income year; and

> <a id="s210-110-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the extent to which the section 124ZZB SME assessable income can reasonably be attributed to the CGT event.
