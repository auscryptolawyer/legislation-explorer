# 210-125  Venture capital debit where CGT limit is exceeded


<a id="s210-125-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*venture capital debit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-debit) arises for a *PDF where the PDF’s net venture capital credits for the income year exceed whichever is the lesser of:

> <a id="s210-125-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the PDF’s CGT limit for that income year; and

> <a id="s210-125-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the tax paid by the PDF on its *SME income component for that income year. Net venture capital credits
