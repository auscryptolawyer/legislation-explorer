# 214-105  Further return as a result of a refund affecting a franking deficit tax liability


<a id="s214-105-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:

> <a id="s214-105-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*franking assessment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-assessment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-assessment) for a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) for an income year has been made; and 202                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 The imputation system Part 3-6 Administering the imputation system Division 214 Section 214-110

> <a id="s214-105-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  on a particular day (the further return day) the entity gives the Commissioner a further [[*franking return*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-return)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-return) for the income year under subsection 214-45(1) (because the entity has *received a refund of income tax that affects its liability to pay [[*franking deficit tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-deficit-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-deficit-tax)); the Commissioner is taken to have amended the entity’s franking assessment on the further return day, and to have assessed:

> <a id="s214-105-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity’s [[*franking account balance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account-balance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account-balance) at a particular time as that stated in the further return as the balance at that time; and

> <a id="s214-105-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the entity’s [[*venture capital sub-account balance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-sub-account-balance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-sub-account-balance) (if any) at a particular time as that stated in the further return as the balance at that time; and

> <a id="s214-105-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the amounts (if any) of [[*franking tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-tax) payable by the entity because of events that have occurred, or are taken to have occurred, during that income year as those stated in the further return.

<a id="s214-105-2"></a>
**(2)**  The further return is taken to be notice of the amended assessment signed by the Commissioner and given to the entity on the further return day.
