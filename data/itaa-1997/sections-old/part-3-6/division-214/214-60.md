# 214-60  Commissioner may make a franking assessment


<a id="s214-60-1"></a>
**(1)**  The Commissioner may make an assessment of:

> <a id="s214-60-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) is a [[*franking entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity) at the end of the income year—its [[*franking account balance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account-balance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account-balance) at the end of the income year; and

> <a id="s214-60-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if a corporate tax entity ceased to be a franking entity during the income year—its franking account balance immediately before it ceased to be a franking entity; and

> <a id="s214-60-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if a corporate tax entity is a *PDF at the end of the income year—its [[*venture capital sub-account balance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-sub-account-balance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-venture-capital-sub-account-balance) at the end of the income year; and

> <a id="s214-60-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  if a corporate tax entity ceased to be a PDF during the income year—its venture capital sub-account balance immediately before it ceased to be a PDF; and

> <a id="s214-60-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the amounts (if any) of [[*franking tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-tax) which the entity is liable to pay because of events that have occurred, or are taken to have occurred, during the income year. This is a franking assessment for the entity for the income year. (1A) However, the Commissioner must not make an assessment under subsection (1) for an entity for an income year if:

> <a id="s214-60-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity is not required under Subdivision 214-A to give the Commissioner a [[*franking return*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-return)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-return) for the income year; and

> <a id="s214-60-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity is not required under Division 214 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997 to give the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            199 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Commissioner a franking return for the balancing period ending within the income year; and

> <a id="s214-60-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity was required to lodge an [[*income tax return*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return) for the income year by a particular time; and

> <a id="s214-60-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the entity has lodged that income tax return; and

> <a id="s214-60-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  3 years have passed since the later of the following:

> > <a id="s214-60-1-e-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the time mentioned in paragraph (c);

> > <a id="s214-60-1-e-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the time when the entity lodged that income tax return.

<a id="s214-60-2"></a>
**(2)**  The Commissioner must give the entity notice of the assessment as soon as practicable after making the assessment.
