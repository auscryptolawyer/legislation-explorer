# 215-15  Non-share dividends are unfrankable if profits are unavailable


<a id="s215-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:

> <a id="s215-15-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) pays a [[*non-share dividend*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-share-dividend)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-share-dividend); and

> <a id="s215-15-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  immediately before the payment, the amount of the [[*available frankable profits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-available-frankable-profits)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-available-frankable-profits) of the entity is nil, or less than nil; the non-share dividend is unfrankable.

<a id="s215-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  If:

> <a id="s215-15-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) pays a [[*non-share dividend*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-share-dividend)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-share-dividend) that is not one of a number of non-share dividends paid at the same time; and

> <a id="s215-15-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  immediately before the payment, the amount of the [[*available frankable profits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-available-frankable-profits)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-available-frankable-profits) of the entity, although greater than nil, are less than the amount of the non-share dividend; the entity is taken to have made a frankable distribution equal to the amount of the available frankable profits. The remainder of the dividend is taken to be an unfrankable distribution.

<a id="s215-15-3"></a>

> <a id="s215-15-3-a"></a>

> <a id="s215-15-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  immediately before the payment, the amount of the [[*available frankable profits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-available-frankable-profits)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-available-frankable-profits) of the entity, although greater than nil are less than the sum of the amounts of the non-share dividends; the entity is taken to have made a frankable distribution equal to the amount worked out using the formula: Amount of the [[*non-share dividend*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-share-dividend)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-share-dividend) ´ [[*Available frankable profits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-available-frankable-profits)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-available-frankable-profits) Sum of the amounts of all the non-share dividends The remainder of the dividend is taken to be an unfrankable distribution.
