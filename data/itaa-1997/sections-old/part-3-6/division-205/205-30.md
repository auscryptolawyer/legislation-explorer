<a id="s205-30-2"></a>
**(2)**  Despite item 2 of the table in subsection (1), no debit arises on that part of the refund that is attributable to any of the following:

> <a id="s205-30-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a payment of income tax in relation to an [[*RSA component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa-component);

> <a id="s205-30-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) that is subject to the refundable tax offset rules because of section 67-30 (about R&D).

<a id="s205-30-3"></a>
**(3)**  The proportion is the standard corporate tax rate (within the meaning of Part IVA of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936) divided by 40%.
