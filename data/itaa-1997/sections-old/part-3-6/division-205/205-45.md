# 205-45  Franking deficit tax

Object

<a id="s205-45-1"></a>
**(1)**  While recognising that an entity may anticipate [[*franking credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) when *franking *distributions, the object of this section is to prevent those credits from being anticipated indefinitely by requiring the entity to reconcile its [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) at certain times and levying tax if the account is in [[*deficit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit). Franking deficit at end of income year

<a id="s205-45-2"></a>
**(2)**  An entity is liable to pay franking deficit tax imposed by the New Business Tax System (Franking Deficit Tax) Act 2002 if its [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) is in [[*deficit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit) at the end of an income year. Corporate tax entity ceases to be a franking entity

<a id="s205-45-3"></a>
**(3)**  An entity is liable to pay [[*franking deficit tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-deficit-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-deficit-tax) imposed by the New Business Tax System (Franking Deficit Tax) Act 2002 if:

> <a id="s205-45-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it ceases to be a [[*franking entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity); and

> <a id="s205-45-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  immediately before it ceases to be a franking entity, its [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) is in [[*deficit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-deficit).

> **Note:** The tax is imposed in the New Business Tax System (Franking Deficit Tax) Act 2002 and the amount of the tax is set out in that Act. Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             63 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026
