# 205-40  Franking surplus and deficit


<a id="s205-40-1"></a>
**(1)**  An entity’s [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) is in surplus at a particular time if, at that time, the sum of the [[*franking credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) in the account exceeds the sum of the [[*franking debits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit) in the account. The amount of the franking surplus is the amount of the excess.

<a id="s205-40-2"></a>
**(2)**  An entity’s [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) is in deficit at a particular time if, at that time, the sum of the [[*franking debits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit) in the account exceeds the sum of the [[*franking credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) in the account. The amount of the franking deficit is the amount of the excess.
