# 207-124  Entity may be ineligible if other money or property also acquired

Subject to section 207-128, this section applies to an entity (the ineligible entity) to whom a [[*franked distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution) is made, or [[*flows indirectly*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-flows-indirectly)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-flows-indirectly) under subsection 207-50(3) or (4), if:

> <a id="s207-124-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the ineligible entity or another entity has entered into an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement) as part of, or in association with:

> > <a id="s207-124-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the distribution; or 100                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 The imputation system Part 3-6 Effect of receiving a franked distribution Division 207 Section 207-126

> > <a id="s207-124-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if the distribution flows indirectly to the ineligible entity—the ineligible entity’s [[*trust share amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trust-share-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trust-share-amount) in relation to the distribution; and

> <a id="s207-124-b"></a>
> **(b)**  because of the arrangement, the ineligible entity or another entity has acquired or will acquire (whether directly or indirectly) money or property, other than money or property comprising the distribution or the ineligible entity’s trust share amount, from:

> > <a id="s207-124-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the entity making the distribution; or

> > <a id="s207-124-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  an entity through which the distribution flows indirectly to the ineligible entity; or

> > <a id="s207-124-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  an [[*associate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-associate) of any of those entities (other than the ineligible entity).
