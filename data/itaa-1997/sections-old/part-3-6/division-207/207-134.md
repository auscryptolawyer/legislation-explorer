# 207-134  Entity’s present entitlement disregarded in certain circumstances

The present entitlement of a beneficiary of a trust to a share of trust income is disregarded for the purposes of Division 6 of Part III of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 if:

> <a id="s207-134-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the beneficiary has claimed a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) under section 207-45 or 207-110 of this Act on the basis that the beneficiary was an *exempt institution that was eligible for a refund in relation to a [[*trust share amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trust-share-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trust-share-amount) that is that share of trust income; but

> <a id="s207-134-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the beneficiary was not entitled to that tax offset because of the operation of section 207-120, 207-122, 207-124 or 207-126 in respect of a [[*distribution event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distribution-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-distribution-event), or an [[*arrangement*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arrangement), to which the trust share amount is related.

> **Note:** This means that the trustee of the trust is liable to pay income tax on that share of the trust income. 108                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 The imputation system Part 3-6 Effect of receiving a franked distribution Division 207 Section 207-136
