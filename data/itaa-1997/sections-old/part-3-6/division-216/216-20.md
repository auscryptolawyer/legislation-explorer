# 216-20  Cum dividend sale—statement by securities dealer

If:

> <a id="s216-20-a"></a>
> **(a)**  section 216-5 applies in relation to a [[*franked distribution*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution) or a *distribution franked with an exempting credit (cum dividend sales); and

> <a id="s216-20-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*securities dealer*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-securities-dealer)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-securities-dealer) has acted for a particular party to the contract concerned; the securities dealer must, as soon as practicable after the making of the distribution, give to the other party to the contract a statement in the [[*approved form*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form) setting out such information in relation to the distribution as is required by the approved form.
