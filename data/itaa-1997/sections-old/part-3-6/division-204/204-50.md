# 204-50  Assessment and notice of determination


<a id="s204-50-1"></a>
**(1)**  A determination under subsection 204-30(3) does not form part of an assessment.

<a id="s204-50-2"></a>
**(2)**  The Commissioner must give notice in writing of the determination:

> <a id="s204-50-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  in a case where the Commissioner determines that a [[*franking debit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-debit) is to arise in the [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) of an entity under paragraph 204-30(3)(a)—to the entity; and

> <a id="s204-50-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  in a case where the Commissioner determines that an [[*exempting debit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-debit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-debit) is to arise in the [[*exempting account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-account) of an entity under paragraph 204-30(3)(b)—to the entity; and

> <a id="s204-50-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  in a case where a favoured member is denied an [[*imputation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-imputation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-imputation-benefit) under paragraph 204-30(3)(c)—to the favoured member.

<a id="s204-50-3"></a>
**(3)**  If the Commissioner makes a determination denying an [[*imputation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-imputation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-imputation-benefit) under paragraph 204-30(3)(c) on a *distribution made by a [[*listed public company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-listed-public-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-listed-public-company), the Commissioner is taken to have served notice in writing of the determination on the favoured member if the Commissioner causes a notice to be published in a manner that results in the notice being accessible to the public and reasonably prominent. The notice is taken to have been served on the day on which the publication takes place.
