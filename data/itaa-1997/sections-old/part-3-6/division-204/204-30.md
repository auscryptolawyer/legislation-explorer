# 204-30  Streaming distributions

Commissioner’s power to make a determination when distributions or distributions and other benefits are streamed

<a id="s204-30-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section empowers the Commissioner to make determinations if an entity streams one or more *distributions (or one or more distributions and the giving of other benefits), whether in a single [[*franking period*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-period)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-period) or in a number of franking periods, in such a way that:

> <a id="s204-30-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an [[*imputation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-imputation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-imputation-benefit) is, or apart from this section would be, received by a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the entity as a result of the distribution or distributions; and

> <a id="s204-30-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the member would *derive a *greater benefit from franking credits than another member of the entity; and

> <a id="s204-30-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the other member of the entity will receive lesser imputation benefits, or will not receive any imputation benefits, whether or not the other member receives other benefits. The member that derives the greater benefit from franking credits is the favoured member. The member that receives the lesser imputation benefits is the disadvantaged member. Examples of other benefits

<a id="s204-30-2"></a>
**(2)**  These are examples of the giving of other benefits:

> <a id="s204-30-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  issuing bonus *shares;

> <a id="s204-30-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  returning *paid-up share capital;

> <a id="s204-30-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  *forgiving a debt;

> <a id="s204-30-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the entity or another entity making a payment of any kind, or giving any property, to a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) or to another person on a member’s behalf. Nature of the determination that the Commissioner may make

<a id="s204-30-5"></a>
**(5)**  The Commissioner may specify the *distribution under paragraph (3)(a), (b) or (c) by specifying:

> <a id="s204-30-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the date on which the distribution was made, or the period during which the distribution was made; and

> <a id="s204-30-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the member, or class of members, to whom the distribution was made. What is an imputation benefit?

<a id="s204-30-7"></a>
**(7)**  The following subsection lists some of the cases in which a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of an entity *derives a greater benefit from franking credits than another member of the entity. It is not an exhaustive list.

<a id="s204-30-8"></a>
**(8)**  A [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of an entity *derives a greater benefit from franking credits than another member of the entity if any of the following circumstances exist in relation to the other member in the income year in which the distribution giving rise to the benefit is made, and not in relation to the first member:

> <a id="s204-30-8-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the other member is a foreign resident;

> <a id="s204-30-8-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the other member would not be entitled to any [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) under Division 207 because of the distribution;

> <a id="s204-30-8-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the amount of income tax that, apart from this Division, would be payable by the other member because of the distribution is less than the tax offset to which the other member would be entitled;

> <a id="s204-30-8-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the other member is a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) at the time the distribution is made, but no [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) arises for the entity as a result of the distribution;

> <a id="s204-30-8-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the other member is a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity) at the time the distribution is made, but cannot use [[*franking credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) received on the distribution to *frank distributions to its own members because:

> > <a id="s204-30-8-e-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  it is not a [[*franking entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-entity); or Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             35 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> > <a id="s204-30-8-e-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  it is unable to make [[*frankable distributions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-frankable-distribution);

> <a id="s204-30-8-f"></a>
> **(f)**  the other member is an [[*exempting entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempting-entity).

**(9)**  A [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of an entity *derives a greater benefit from franking credits than another member of the entity if any of the following circumstances exist in relation to the first member in the income year in which the *distribution giving rise to the benefit is made, and not in relation to the other member:

**(10)**  A [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of an entity *derives a greater benefit from franking credits than another member if the first member is entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) under section 210-170 as a result of the *distribution, and the other member is not.
