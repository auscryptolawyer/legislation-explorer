# 204-1  Objects

The objects of this Division are to ensure that:

> <a id="s204-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity and its *members cannot avoid the effect of the *benchmark rule by exploiting the [[*benchmark franking percentage*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-benchmark-franking-percentage)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-benchmark-franking-percentage) of another entity; and

> <a id="s204-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an entity does not stream [[*franked distributions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franked-distribution) and [[*tax-exempt bonus shares*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-exempt-bonus-share)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-exempt-bonus-share); and

> <a id="s204-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  an entity does not stream *distributions to members of the entity who *derive a *greater benefit from franking credits than other members.
