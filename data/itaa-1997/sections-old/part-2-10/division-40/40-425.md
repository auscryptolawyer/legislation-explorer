# 40-425  Allocating assets to a low-value pool


<a id="s40-425-1"></a>
**(1)**  You may choose to allocate a *low cost asset you [[*hold*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold) to a low-value pool for the income year in which you start to use it, or have it [[*installed ready for use*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-installed-ready-for-use)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-installed-ready-for-use), for a [[*taxable purpose*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose).

<a id="s40-425-2"></a>
**(2)**  A low-cost asset is a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) (except a [[*horticultural plant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-horticultural-plant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-horticultural-plant)) whose [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) as at the end of the income year in which you start to use it, or have it [[*installed ready for use*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-installed-ready-for-use)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-installed-ready-for-use), for a [[*taxable purpose*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose) is less than $1,000.

<a id="s40-425-3"></a>
**(3)**  You may also choose to allocate a [[*low-value asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-low-value-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-low-value-asset) to a low-value pool.

<a id="s40-425-4"></a>
**(4)**  You cannot allocate a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) to a low-value pool if:

> <a id="s40-425-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  its [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) does not exceed $300; and

> <a id="s40-425-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you use the asset predominantly for the [[*purpose of producing assessable income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income) that is not income from carrying on a *business; and

> <a id="s40-425-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the asset is not part of a set of assets that you started to hold in that income year where the total cost of the set of assets exceeds $300; and

> <a id="s40-425-4-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the total cost of the asset and any other identical, or substantially identical, asset that you start to hold in that income year does not exceed $300.

<a id="s40-425-5"></a>
**(5)**  A low-value asset is a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset), except a [[*horticultural plant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-horticultural-plant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-horticultural-plant), you [[*hold*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold): Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            97 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s40-425-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if you have deducted or can deduct amounts for it under this Division for a previous income year—for which you used the [[*diminishing value method*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-diminishing-value-method)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-diminishing-value-method); and

> <a id="s40-425-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  that has an *opening adjustable value for the current year of less than $1,000 (worked out using the diminishing value method); and

> <a id="s40-425-5-c"></a>
> **(c)**  that is not a [[*low-cost asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-low-cost-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-low-cost-asset).

<a id="s40-425-6"></a>
**(6)**  A [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset):

> <a id="s40-425-6-a"></a>
> **(a)**  to which Division 58 (about assets previously owned by an exempt entity) applied for an entity sale situation; and

> <a id="s40-425-6-b"></a>
> **(b)**  for which you used the [[*diminishing value method*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-diminishing-value-method)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-diminishing-value-method); and

> <a id="s40-425-6-c"></a>
> **(c)**  whose [[*adjustable value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) as at the end of the income year before the [[*current year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-current-year) is less than $1,000; is also a low-value asset. Exception: small business entities

<a id="s40-425-7"></a>
**(7)**  You cannot allocate a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) to a low-value pool if you deduct amounts for it under Subdivision 328-D (about capital allowances for small business entities). Exception: medium sized businesses (7A) You cannot allocate a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) to a low-value pool if the decline in value of the asset for any income year is determined by section 40-82 (about assets costing below a threshold). Exception: R&D

<a id="s40-425-8"></a>
**(8)**  You cannot allocate a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) to a low-value pool if you are entitled under section 355-100 to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for a deduction under section 355-305 for the asset for an income year starting before, or at the same time as, the allocation has effect.

> **Note:** A similar rule applies if you deducted or could have deducted amounts under former 73BA of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (see section 40-430 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997). 98                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Capital allowances: rules about deductibility of capital expenditure Part 2-10 Capital allowances Division 40 Section 40-430
