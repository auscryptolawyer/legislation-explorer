# 40-225  Adjustment: acquiring a car at a discount


<a id="s40-225-1"></a>
**(1)**  You must increase the first element of the cost of a [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car) designed mainly for carrying passengers you acquire at a discount if:

> <a id="s40-225-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is reasonable to conclude that any portion (the discount portion) of the discount is referable to you or another entity selling another asset for less than its *market value; and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             65 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s40-225-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you, or another entity, has deducted or can deduct an amount for the other asset for any income year; and

> <a id="s40-225-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the sum of the cost of the car and the discount portion exceeds the [[*car limit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-limit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-limit) for the [[*financial year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year) in which you first use the car for any purpose.

<a id="s40-225-2"></a>
**(2)**  The first element of the cost of the [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car) is increased by the discount portion.

<a id="s40-225-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section does not apply to a [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car) that is excluded from the [[*car limit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-limit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-limit) by subsection 40-230(2).
