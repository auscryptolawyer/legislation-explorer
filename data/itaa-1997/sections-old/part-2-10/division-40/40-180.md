# 40-180  First element of cost


<a id="s40-180-1"></a>
**(1)**  The first element is worked out as at the time when you began to [[*hold*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold) the [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) (except for a case to which item 3, 4 or 14 of the table in subsection (2) applies). It is:

> <a id="s40-180-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if an item in that table applies—the amount specified in that item; or

> <a id="s40-180-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—the amount you are taken to have paid to hold the asset under section 40-185.

> **Note:** The first element of the cost may be modified by a later provision in this Subdivision.

> **Note:** Section 230-505 provides special rules for working out the amount of consideration for an asset if the asset is a Division 230 financial arrangement or a Division 230 financial arrangement is involved in that consideration.

<a id="s40-180-3"></a>
**(3)**  The first element of [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) includes an amount you paid or are taken to have paid in relation to starting to [[*hold*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold) the [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) if that amount is directly connected with holding the asset.

<a id="s40-180-4"></a>
**(4)**  The first element of [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) of a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) does not include an amount that forms part of the second element of cost of another depreciating asset.

> **Note:** The first element of cost may be reduced under section 40-1130 to account for exploration benefits received under farm-in farm-out arrangements.
