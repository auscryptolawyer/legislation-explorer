# 40-230  Adjustment: car limit


<a id="s40-230-1"></a>
**(1)**  The first element of the cost of a [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car) designed mainly for carrying passengers (after applying section 40-225 and Subdivision 27-B) is reduced to the [[*car limit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-limit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-limit) for the [[*financial year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year) in which you started to [[*hold*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold) it if its cost exceeds that limit.

<a id="s40-230-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, the [[*car limit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-limit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car-limit) does not apply to a [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car):

> <a id="s40-230-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  fitted out for transporting disabled people in wheelchairs for profit; or

> <a id="s40-230-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  whose first element of [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) exceeds that limit only because of modifications made to enable an individual with a disability to use it for a [[*taxable purpose*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose).

<a id="s40-230-3"></a>
**(3)**  The car limit for the 2000-01 [[*financial year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year) is $55,134. The limit is indexed annually.

> **Note:** Subdivision 960-M shows you how to index amounts.

<a id="s40-230-4"></a>
**(4)**  If you [[*hold*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hold) a [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car) that is also held by one or more other entities, subsection (1) applies to the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) of the car despite section 40-35. Then section 40-35 applies to the cost of the car as reduced under subsection (1). 66                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Capital allowances: rules about deductibility of capital expenditure Part 2-10 Capital allowances Division 40 Section 40-235
