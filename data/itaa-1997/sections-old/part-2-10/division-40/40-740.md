# 40-740  Meaning of ancillary mining activities and mining building site


<a id="s40-740-1"></a>
**(1)**  Any of the following are ancillary mining activities:

> <a id="s40-740-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  preparing a site for you to carry on [[*mining and quarrying operations*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-and-quarrying-operations)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-and-quarrying-operations);

> <a id="s40-740-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  providing water, light or power for, access to, or communications with, a site on which you carry on, or will carry on, mining and quarrying operations;

> <a id="s40-740-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  [[*minerals treatment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-minerals-treatment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-minerals-treatment) of *minerals or minerals treatment of quarry materials, obtained by you in carrying on mining and quarrying operations;

> <a id="s40-740-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  storing (whether before or after minerals treatment) such minerals, [[*petroleum*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-petroleum)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-petroleum) or quarry materials in relation to the operation of a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) for use primarily and principally in treating such minerals or quarry materials;

> <a id="s40-740-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  liquefying natural gas obtained from mining and quarrying operations you carry on.

<a id="s40-740-2"></a>
**(2)**  A mining building site is a site, or a part of a site, where there are [[*depreciating assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) that are or were necessary for you to carry on [[*mining and quarrying operations*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-and-quarrying-operations)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-mining-and-quarrying-operations). However, a mining building site does not include anything covered by the definition of housing and welfare.
