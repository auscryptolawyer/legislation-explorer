# 40-53  Alterations etc. to certain depreciating assets


<a id="s40-53-1"></a>
**(1)**  These things are not the same [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) for the purposes of section 40-50 and Subdivision 40-F:

> <a id="s40-53-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a depreciating asset; and

> <a id="s40-53-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a repair of a capital nature, or an alteration, addition or extension, to that asset that would, if it were a separate depreciating asset, be a [[*water facility*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-facility)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-water-facility), [[*fodder storage asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fodder-storage-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fodder-storage-asset) or [[*fencing asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fencing-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-fencing-asset).

<a id="s40-53-2"></a>
**(2)**  These things are not the same [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) for the purposes of section 40-50 and Subdivision 40-G:

> <a id="s40-53-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a depreciating asset; and

> <a id="s40-53-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a repair of a capital nature, or an alteration, addition or extension, to that asset that would, if it were a separate depreciating asset, be a [[*landcare operation*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-landcare-operation)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-landcare-operation).
