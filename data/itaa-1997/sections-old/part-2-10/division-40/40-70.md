# 40-70  Diminishing value method


<a id="s40-70-1"></a>
**(1)**  You work out the decline in value of a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) for an income year using the diminishing value method in this way: Days held           150% Base value                 365       Asset’s * effective life where: base value is:

> <a id="s40-70-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  for the income year in which the asset’s *start time occurs— its [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost); or

> <a id="s40-70-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  for a later year—the sum of its *opening adjustable value for that year and any amount included in the second element of its cost for that year. days held is the number of days you [[*held*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held) the asset in the income year from its *start time, ignoring any days in that year when you did not use the asset, or have it [[*installed ready for use*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-installed-ready-for-use)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-installed-ready-for-use), for any purpose.

> **Note:** If you recalculate the effective life of a depreciating asset, you use that recalculated life in working out your deduction. <a id="s40-70-2"></a>
**(2)**  You cannot use the [[*diminishing value method*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-diminishing-value-method)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-diminishing-value-method) to work out the decline in value of:

> <a id="s40-70-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  *in-house software; or

> <a id="s40-70-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an item of [[*intellectual property*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-intellectual-property)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-intellectual-property) (except copyright in a [[*film*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-film)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-film)); or

> <a id="s40-70-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  a [[*spectrum licence*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-spectrum-licence)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-spectrum-licence); or

> <a id="s40-70-2-e"></a>
> **(e)**  a [[*telecommunications site access right*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-telecommunications-site-access-right)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-telecommunications-site-access-right). Limit on decline

<a id="s40-70-3"></a>
**(3)**  The decline in value of a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) under this section for an income year cannot be more than the amount that is the asset’s *base value for that income year.
