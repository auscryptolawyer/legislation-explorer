# 40-55  Use of the “cents per kilometre” car expense deduction method

You cannot deduct any amount for the decline in value of a [[*car*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-car) for an income year if you use the “cents per kilometre” method for the car for that year. 18                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Capital allowances: rules about deductibility of capital expenditure Part 2-10 Capital allowances Division 40 Section 40-60

> **Note:** See Subdivision 28-C for that method.
