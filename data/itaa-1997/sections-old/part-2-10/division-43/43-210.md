# 43-210  Deduction for capital works begun after 26 February 1992

Step 1 Calculate the amount worked out using the formula: Portion of your CE  Days used  0.04 365 where: portion of your CE is the portion of [[*your construction expenditure*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-construction-expenditure)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-construction-expenditure) that is attributable to the part of [[*your area*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area) that you used in the *4% manner. days used is the number of days in the income year that:

> <a id="s43-210-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you owned or were the lessee of that part of [[*your area*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area) and used it in the *4% manner; or

> <a id="s43-210-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you were the holder of that part of [[*your area*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area) under a *quasi-ownership right over land granted by an *exempt Australian government agency or an *exempt foreign government agency, and used that part of your area in the 4% manner. Step 2 Reduce the Step 1 amount by the extent to which the part referred to in Step 1 was used only partly for the [[*purpose of producing assessable income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income).

> **Note:** This Step applies if: •    part of your income from the part referred to in Step 1 is exempt income; or •    part of the part referred to in Step 1 was not used for the purpose of producing assessable income or was not available for that use; or •    the part of the part referred to in Step 1 was not used for such a purpose during a part of the days used period. 218                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Capital allowances: rules about deductibility of capital expenditure Part 2-10 Deductions for capital works Division 43 Section 43-210 Step 3 Calculate the amount worked out using the formula: Portion of your CE  Days used  0.025 365 where: portion of your CE is the portion of [[*your construction expenditure*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-construction-expenditure)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-construction-expenditure) that is attributable to the part of [[*your area*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area) that you did not use in the *4% manner but was used as described in Table 43-140 (Current year use). days used is the number of days in the income year that:

> <a id="s43-210-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you owned or were the lessee of that part of [[*your area*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area) and used it in that manner; or

> <a id="s43-210-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you were the holder of that part of [[*your area*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-your-area) under a *quasi-ownership right over land granted by an *exempt Australian government agency or an *exempt foreign government agency, and used that part of your area in that manner. Step 4 Reduce the Step 3 amount by the extent to which the part referred to in Step 3:

> <a id="s43-210-a"></a>
> **(a)**  for a [[*hotel building*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hotel-building)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hotel-building) or [[*apartment building*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-apartment-building)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-apartment-building)—was used only partly for the [[*purpose of producing assessable income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-purpose-of-producing-assessable-income); or

> <a id="s43-210-b"></a>
> **(b)**  for any other capital works—was used only partly for the purpose of *producing assessable income or conducting *R&D activities.

> **Note:** This Step applies if: •    part of your income from the part referred to in Step 3 is exempt income; or •    part of the part referred to in Step 3 was not used for the purpose of producing assessable income (or R&D activities) or was not available for that use; or •    the part of the part referred to in Step 3 was not used for such a purpose during a part of the days used period. Step 5 Add the Step 2 and Step 4 amounts.
