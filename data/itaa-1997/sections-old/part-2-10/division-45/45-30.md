# 45-30  Reduction for certain plant acquired before 21.9.99


<a id="s45-30-1"></a>
**(1)**  The amount included in your assessable income under subsection 45-5(2) or 45-10(2) is reduced if:

> <a id="s45-30-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you acquired the [[*plant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-plant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-plant) at or before 11.45 am, by legal time in the Australian Capital Territory, on 21 September 1999 and you disposed of the plant or an interest in it after that time; and

> <a id="s45-30-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the sum of the amounts (your proceeds) referred to in paragraph 45-5(1)(e) or 45-10(1)(f) is more than the plant’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost), or that part of it that is attributable to the interest you disposed of.

<a id="s45-30-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount included is reduced by the lesser of:

> <a id="s45-30-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the amount (if any) by which the [[*plant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-plant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-plant)’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base exceeds its [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost), or that part of the excess that is attributable to the interest you disposed of; and

> <a id="s45-30-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the difference between your proceeds and the plant’s cost, or that part of its cost that is attributable to the interest you disposed of.

<a id="s45-30-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, the amount is not reduced under this section if:

> <a id="s45-30-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*plant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-plant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-plant) was a [[*pre-CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pre-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pre-cgt-asset) at the time of the [[*balancing adjustment event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event); or

> <a id="s45-30-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) from the plant or interest would be disregarded because of a provision listed in the table in this subsection if:

> > <a id="s45-30-3-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you had made the gain or loss from [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) A1; and
