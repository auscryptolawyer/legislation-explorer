# 70-50  Valuation if trading stock obsolete etc.

You may elect to value an item of your [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock) below all the values in section 70-45 if: 10                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Trading stock Part 2-25 Trading stock Division 70 Section 70-55

> <a id="s70-50-a"></a>
> **(a)**  that is warranted because of obsolescence or any other special circumstances relating to that item; and

> <a id="s70-50-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the value you elect is reasonable.
