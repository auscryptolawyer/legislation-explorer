# 405-1  What this Division is about

Significant fluctuations can occur in the professional incomes of authors, inventors, performing artists, production associates and sportspersons. To lessen the impact of these fluctuations on your marginal tax rates, special tax rates apply if your professional income is above your average. This Division explains how the scheme works and sets out the rules for working out your above-average special professional income.
