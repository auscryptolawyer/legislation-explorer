# 355-305  When notional deductions for decline in value arise


<a id="s355-305-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:

> <a id="s355-305-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an *R&D entity is registered under section 27A of the Industry Research and Development Act 1986 for an income Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            173 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 year (the present year) for one or more *R&D activities that are activities to which section 355-210 (conditions for R&D activities) applies; and

> <a id="s355-305-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  while a tangible [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) is [[*held*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held) by the R&D entity during the present year, the asset is used for the purpose of conducting one or more of those R&D activities; and

> <a id="s355-305-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the R&D entity could deduct an amount under section 40-25 for the asset for the present year if Division 40 applied with the changes described in section 355-310; and

> <a id="s355-305-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the R&D entity cannot deduct an amount for the asset for:

> > <a id="s355-305-1-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  an earlier income year under Subdivision 328-D (capital allowances for small business entities); or

> > <a id="s355-305-1-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  an earlier income year under Division 40 (as that Division applies apart from this Division), in a case where section 40-440 (low-value pools) applied; the R&D entity can deduct the amount referred to in paragraph (c) for the present year.

<a id="s355-305-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect subject to subsection 355-580(4) (CRC contributions).
