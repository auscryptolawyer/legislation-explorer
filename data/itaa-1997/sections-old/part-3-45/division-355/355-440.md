# 355-440  R&D recoupments


<a id="s355-440-1"></a>
**(1)**  The *R&D entity has an amount under this section if:

> <a id="s355-440-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity, or another entity mentioned in subsection (5), receives or becomes entitled to receive a [[*recoupment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-recoupment) from either of the following (otherwise than under the [[*CRC program*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-crc-program)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-crc-program)):

> > <a id="s355-440-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  an *Australian government agency;

> > <a id="s355-440-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  an STB (within the meaning of Division 1AB of Part III of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936); and

> <a id="s355-440-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the recoupment is received, or the entitlement to receive the recoupment arises, during the present year; and

> <a id="s355-440-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  either:

> > <a id="s355-440-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the recoupment is of expenditure incurred on or in relation to certain activities; or Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            183 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> > <a id="s355-440-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the recoupment requires expenditure (the project expenditure) to have been incurred, or to be incurred, on certain activities.

> **Note:** Paragraph (c) includes expenditure incurred in purchasing a tangible depreciating asset to be used when conducting R&D activities.

<a id="s355-440-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount is equal to the sum of:

> <a id="s355-440-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  so much of the expenditure referred to in subsection (1) that is deducted under this Division; and

> <a id="s355-440-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  for each asset (if any) for which expenditure referred to in subsection (1) is included in the asset’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)—each amount (if any) equal to the asset’s decline in value that is deducted under this Division; that is taken into account in working out [[*tax offsets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) under section 355-100 obtained by the *R&D entity for one or more income years.

> **Note:** Paragraphs (a) and (b) of this subsection refer to amounts notionally deducted under this Division (see section 355-105). Amount is reduced by any repayments of the recoupment
