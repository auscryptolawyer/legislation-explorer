# 394-40  Payments under forestry managed investment scheme

For the purposes of this Division, do not treat the following payments as payments under a [[*forestry managed investment scheme*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forestry-managed-investment-scheme)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-forestry-managed-investment-scheme) by a [[*participant*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participant)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participant) in the scheme:

> <a id="s394-40-a"></a>
> **(a)**  payments for [[*borrowing*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-borrowing)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-borrowing) money;

> <a id="s394-40-b"></a>
> **(b)**  payments of interest and payments in the nature of interest;

> <a id="s394-40-c"></a>
> **(c)**  payments of stamp duty;

> <a id="s394-40-d"></a>
> **(d)**  payments of [[*GST*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst); Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            397 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s394-40-e"></a>
> **(e)**  payments that relate to one or more of the matters mentioned in paragraphs 394-45(4)(a), (b) or (c).
