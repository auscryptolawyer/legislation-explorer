# 380-32  Amended certificates

A reference in this Subdivision to an *NRAS certificate in relation to an *NRAS year is to be treated as a reference to an amended NRAS certificate in relation to the NRAS year, if the [[*Housing Secretary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-housing-secretary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-housing-secretary) issues such an amended certificate.
