**(4)**  Treat the references in subsection (2) to the *NRAS year as being references to a period that occurs during the NRAS year, if the *NRAS certificate is apportioned for the period. Amount of tax offset—rent that passes through NRAS approved participant

<a id="s380-17-5"></a>
**(5)**  For the purposes of the references in the definitions in subsection (2) to rent *derived from the *NRAS dwelling during the *NRAS year, disregard *NRAS rent derived by a [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of the *NRAS consortium from the NRAS dwelling during a period in the NRAS year, to the extent that another member derives rent from the NRAS dwelling during the period because:

> <a id="s380-17-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the first member is the *NRAS approved participant of the NRAS consortium throughout the period; and

> <a id="s380-17-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the first member, in accordance with the contractual *arrangements that established the NRAS consortium, passes the NRAS rent on to the other member.

> **Note:** There may be more than one NRAS approved participant during an NRAS year. The electing member may be the NRAS approved participant for only part of the NRAS year.

<a id="s380-17-6"></a>
**(6)**  For the purposes of paragraph (5)(b), treat any *NRAS rent retained by the first [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) under the *arrangements as management fees or commission as having been passed on to the other member.
