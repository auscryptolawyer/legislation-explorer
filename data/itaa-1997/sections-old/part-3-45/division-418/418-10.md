# 418-10  Who is entitled to the tax offset—ordinary case

You are entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for an income year if:

> <a id="s418-10-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an [[*exploration credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit) is issued to you under Subdivision 418-E for the income year; and

> <a id="s418-10-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you are not:

> > <a id="s418-10-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a [[*corporate tax entity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-entity); or 458                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Rules for particular industries and occupations Part 3-45 Exploration for minerals Division 418 Section 418-15

> > <a id="s418-10-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a trust (other than a trust in relation to which some or all of the liability of the trustee to tax is provided under subsection 98(1) or (2) or 99(2) or (3) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936); or

> > <a id="s418-10-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  a partnership; or

> > <a id="s418-10-iv"></a>
> > **(iv)**  an *exempt entity (other than an *exempt institution that is eligible for a refund); and

> <a id="s418-10-c"></a>
> **(c)**  you are an Australian resident during the whole of that income year.
