# 418-101  Determination by the Commissioner

Determination allocating exploration credits

<a id="s418-101-1"></a>
**(1)**  The Commissioner may make a written determination allocating [[*exploration credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit) of an amount specified in the determination to an entity for an income year. Circumstances in which the Commissioner must not make a determination

<a id="s418-101-2"></a>
**(2)**  The Commissioner must not make a determination allocating [[*exploration credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit) to an entity for an income year if the Commissioner is not satisfied that:

> <a id="s418-101-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  there is a reasonable possibility that the entity will have:

> > <a id="s418-101-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  [[*greenfields minerals expenditure*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-greenfields-minerals-expenditure)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-greenfields-minerals-expenditure) of the amount estimated by the entity in the application, or greater; and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            473 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> > <a id="s418-101-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss of the amount estimated by the entity in the application, or greater; and

> > <a id="s418-101-2-a-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  the [[*corporate tax rate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-rate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-rate) estimated by the entity in the application; and

> <a id="s418-101-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity meets any other requirement prescribed under the regulations. Amount of the exploration credits allocated

<a id="s418-101-3"></a>
**(3)**  The amount of the [[*exploration credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit) specified in the determination must be the smallest of the following amounts:

> <a id="s418-101-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity’s estimated [[*greenfields minerals expenditure*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-greenfields-minerals-expenditure)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-greenfields-minerals-expenditure) for the income year multiplied by the entity’s estimated [[*corporate tax rate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-rate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-rate) for the income year;

> <a id="s418-101-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity’s estimated [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss for the income year multiplied by the entity’s estimated corporate tax rate for the income year;

> <a id="s418-101-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  either:

> > <a id="s418-101-3-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  5% of an amount equal to the *annual exploration cap for the income year; or

> > <a id="s418-101-3-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if another amount, or a method for working out another amount, is prescribed—the other amount. Determination not a legislative instrument

<a id="s418-101-4"></a>
**(4)**  A determination made under subsection (1) is not a legislative instrument.
