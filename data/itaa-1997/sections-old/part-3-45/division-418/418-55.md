# 418-55  Junior minerals exploration incentive franking credit—life insurance company


<a id="s418-55-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) arises in the [[*franking account*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-account) of a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) if:

> <a id="s418-55-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an [[*exploration credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit) is issued to the life insurance company under Subdivision 418-E during an income year; and

> <a id="s418-55-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  paragraph 418-15(1)(d) does not apply in relation to the exploration credit; and

> <a id="s418-55-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if that paragraph were to apply in relation to the credit, the life insurance company would be entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) under Subdivision 418-B in relation to the exploration credit.

<a id="s418-55-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount of the [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) is the amount of the [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) to which the [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) would be entitled under Subdivision 418-B if no other [[*exploration credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit) were issued to the life insurance company during the income year.

<a id="s418-55-3"></a>
**(3)**  The [[*franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) arises at the same time the [[*exploration credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit) is issued. 464                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Rules for particular industries and occupations Part 3-45 Exploration for minerals Division 418 Section 418-70
