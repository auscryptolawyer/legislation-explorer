# 418-100  Applying for an exploration credits allocation


<a id="s418-100-1"></a>
**(1)**  An entity may apply to the Commissioner for a determination under section 418-101 allocating [[*exploration credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit) to the entity for an income year. 472                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Rules for particular industries and occupations Part 3-45 Exploration for minerals Division 418 Section 418-101

<a id="s418-100-2"></a>
**(2)**  The application must be made within 1 month before the start of the [[*financial year*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-financial-year) corresponding to the income year for which the allocation is sought.

<a id="s418-100-3"></a>
**(3)**  The application must:

> <a id="s418-100-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  be *lodged electronically; and

> <a id="s418-100-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  be in the [[*approved form*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-approved-form); and

> <a id="s418-100-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  include an estimate of:

> > <a id="s418-100-3-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the entity’s [[*greenfields minerals expenditure*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-greenfields-minerals-expenditure)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-greenfields-minerals-expenditure) for the income year; and

> > <a id="s418-100-3-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the entity’s [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss for the income year; and

> > <a id="s418-100-3-c-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  the entity’s [[*corporate tax rate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-rate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-corporate-tax-rate) for the income year.

<a id="s418-100-4"></a>
**(4)**  The Commissioner must give the entity:

> <a id="s418-100-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the Commissioner makes a determination under section 418-101—a copy of the determination; or

> <a id="s418-100-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if the Commissioner decides to refuse the application— notice of that decision.
