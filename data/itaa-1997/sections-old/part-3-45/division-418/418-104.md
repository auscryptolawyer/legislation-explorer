# 418-104  Failure to comply with this Subdivision does not affect allocation

A failure by the Commissioner to comply with this Subdivision does not invalidate a determination allocating [[*exploration credits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exploration-credit) to an entity for an income year.
