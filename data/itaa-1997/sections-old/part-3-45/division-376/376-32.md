# 376-32  Minister may require information


<a id="s376-32-1"></a>
**(1)**  Either:

> <a id="s376-32-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  before determining a company’s *qualifying Australian production expenditure on a [[*film*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-film)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-film) under subsection 376-30(1) for the purposes of the location offset; or 242                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Rules for particular industries and occupations Part 3-45 Films generally (tax offsets for Australian production expenditure) Division 376 Section 376-35

> <a id="s376-32-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  before issuing a certificate to the company for the film under section 376-20 (certificate for the location offset); the [[*Arts Minister*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister) may, by written notice given to the company, require that the company provide to the Arts Minister information specified in the notice.

<a id="s376-32-2"></a>
**(2)**  The information specified in the notice must be information that the [[*Arts Minister*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister) considers relevant to:

> <a id="s376-32-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  determining the company’s *qualifying Australian production expenditure or issuing the certificate to the company; or

> <a id="s376-32-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  assessing the benefit of the film to the Australian screen industry.

<a id="s376-32-3"></a>
**(3)**  The notice must specify the time by which the information is to be provided (which must be at least 30 business days after the notice is given).

<a id="s376-32-4"></a>
**(4)**  The [[*Arts Minister*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister) may, on request by the company, extend the time by written notice given to the company.

<a id="s376-32-5"></a>
**(5)**  If the information is not provided by the specified time (including any extensions), the [[*Arts Minister*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister) may refuse to determine the company’s *qualifying Australian production expenditure or issue the certificate to the company.
