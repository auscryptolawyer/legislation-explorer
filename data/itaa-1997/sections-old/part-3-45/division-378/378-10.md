# 378-10  Company entitled to refundable tax offset for Australian expenditure incurred in developing digital games


<a id="s378-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  A company is entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) under this section (the digital games tax offset) for an income year if:

> <a id="s378-10-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*Arts Minister*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister) has issued one or more certificates to the company for the income year under section 378-25 (certificate for the digital games tax offset); and

> <a id="s378-10-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the company claims the offset in its [[*income tax return*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-return) for the income year; and

> <a id="s378-10-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the company:

> > <a id="s378-10-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  is an Australian resident that has an [[*ABN*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-abn)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-abn); or

> > <a id="s378-10-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  is a foreign resident that has a [[*permanent establishment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-permanent-establishment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-permanent-establishment) in Australia and an ABN; when the company lodges the income tax return and when the tax offset is due to be credited to the company.

> **Note:** The digital games tax offset is a refundable tax offset: see section 67-23. 286                                    Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Rules for particular industries and occupations Part 3-45 Digital games (tax offset for Australian expenditure on digital games) Division 378 Section 378-15

<a id="s378-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  The claim referred to in paragraph (1)(b) may be varied to take account of a variation under subsection 378-15(5) of a notice given under subsection 378-15(3) by the company in relation to the income year. Otherwise, the claim is irrevocable.
