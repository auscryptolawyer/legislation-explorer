# 378-110  Delegation by Arts Minister


<a id="s378-110-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*Arts Minister*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister) may, in writing, delegate all or any of the Arts Minister’s powers under this Division, other than under section 378-100 or section 378-105, to:

> <a id="s378-110-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*Arts Secretary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-secretary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-secretary); or

> <a id="s378-110-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an SES employee, or acting SES employee, in the Department administered by the Arts Minister.

<a id="s378-110-2"></a>
**(2)**  In exercising powers under a delegation, the delegate must comply with any directions of the [[*Arts Minister*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-arts-minister).
