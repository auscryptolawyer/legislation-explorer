# 419-90  Amendment of assessments

Section 170 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 does not prevent the amendment of an assessment given to a company for the purposes of giving effect to this Division for an income year if the registration of a [[*registered CMPTI processing activity*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-registered-cmpti-processing-activity)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-registered-cmpti-processing-activity) for the company is transferred, varied, suspended or revoked.
