# 419-30  Expenditure to be worked out excluding GST

In determining an amount of expenditure for the purpose of this Division, the expenditure is taken to exclude [[*GST*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-gst).
