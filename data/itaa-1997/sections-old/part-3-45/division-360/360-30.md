# 360-30  Amount of the tax offset—members of trusts or partnerships


<a id="s360-30-1"></a>
**(1)**  If subsection 360-15(2) applies, the amount of the [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)’s [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for the income year is as follows: Determined share of notional tax offset  Notional tax offset amount where: determined share of notional tax offset is the percentage determined under subsection (2) for the [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member). (1A) However, reduce the amount worked out under subsection (1) to the extent necessary to ensure that the sum of the following does not exceed $200,000:

> <a id="s360-30-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the sum of the [[*tax offsets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) under this Subdivision for the income year for which the member and the member’s *affiliates (if any) are entitled;

> <a id="s360-30-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the sum of the tax offsets under this Subdivision that the member and the member’s affiliates (if any) carry forward to the income year.

<a id="s360-30-2"></a>
**(2)**  The trustee or partnership may determine the percentage of the notional tax offset that is the [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)’s share of the notional tax offset.

<a id="s360-30-3"></a>
**(3)**  If, under the terms and conditions under which the trust or partnership operates, the [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) would be entitled to a fixed proportion of any [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) from a *disposal:

> <a id="s360-30-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  relating to the trust or partnership; and

> <a id="s360-30-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  of the *shares that gave rise to the notional tax offset; and

> <a id="s360-30-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  happening at the end of the income year to which the notional tax offset relates; the percentage determined under subsection (2) must be equivalent to that fixed proportion, and a determination of any other percentage has no effect.

<a id="s360-30-4"></a>
**(4)**  The trustee or partnership must give the [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) written notice of the determination. The notice:

> <a id="s360-30-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  must enable the member to work out the amount of the member’s [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) by including enough information to enable the member to work out the member’s share of the notional tax offset; and 218                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Rules for particular industries and occupations Part 3-45 Early stage investors in innovation companies Division 360 Section 360-35

> <a id="s360-30-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  must be given to the member within 3 months after the end of the income year, or within such further time as the Commissioner allows.

<a id="s360-30-5"></a>
**(5)**  The sum of all the percentages determined under subsection (2) in relation to the *members of the trust or partnership must not exceed 100%.
