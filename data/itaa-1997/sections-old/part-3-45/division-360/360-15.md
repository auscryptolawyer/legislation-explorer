# 360-15  Entitlement to the tax offset

General case

<a id="s360-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  You are entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for an income year if:

> <a id="s360-15-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are none of the following:

> > <a id="s360-15-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a trust or a partnership;

> <a id="s360-15-1-ia"></a>
> **(ia)**  an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp);

> > <a id="s360-15-1-ia-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a *widely held company or a *100% subsidiary of a widely held company; and

> <a id="s360-15-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  at a particular time during the income year, a company issues you with *equity interests that are *shares in the company; and

> <a id="s360-15-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  subsection 360-40(1) (about early stage innovation companies) applies to the company immediately after that time; and

> <a id="s360-15-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  neither you nor the company is an [[*affiliate*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affiliate)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-affiliate) of each other at that time; and

> <a id="s360-15-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the issue of those shares is not an *acquisition of *ESS interests under an [[*employee share scheme*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employee-share-scheme); and

> <a id="s360-15-1-f"></a>
> **(f)**  immediately after the issue of those shares, you do not hold equity interests in the company, or in an entity [[*connected with*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-connected-with)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-connected-with) the company, that carry the right to: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            215 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> > <a id="s360-15-1-f-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  receive more than 30% of any distribution of income by the company or the entity; or

> > <a id="s360-15-1-f-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  receive more than 30% of any distribution of capital by the company or the entity; or

> > <a id="s360-15-1-f-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  exercise, or control the exercise of, more than 30% of the total voting power in the company or the entity. Members of trusts or partnerships

<a id="s360-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  A [[*member*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-member) of a trust or partnership (other than a partnership that is an [[*ESVCLP*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-esvclp)) at the end of an income year is entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for the income year if:

> <a id="s360-15-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the trust or partnership would be entitled to a tax offset, under this section, for the income year if the trust or partnership were an individual; and

> <a id="s360-15-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the member is not a *widely held company or a *100% subsidiary of a widely held company. Trustees

<a id="s360-15-3"></a>
**(3)**  A trustee of a trust is entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) for an income year if:

> <a id="s360-15-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the trustee would be entitled to a tax offset, under subsection (1), for the income year if the trustee were an individual; and

> <a id="s360-15-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the trustee is liable to be assessed or has been assessed, and is liable to pay [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax), on a share of, or all or a part of, the trust’s [[*net income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-income) under section 98, 99 or 99A of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 for the income year.
