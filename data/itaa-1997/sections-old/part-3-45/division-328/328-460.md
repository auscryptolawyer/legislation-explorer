# 328-460  Effect of small business restructures on acquisition times of pre-CGT assets

For the purposes of applying subsection 328-455(1) to the asset as a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) (other than a [[*revenue asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)) that is a [[*pre-CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pre-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pre-cgt-asset), a transferee is taken to have *acquired the asset before 20 September 1985.
