# 328-470  Membership interests affected by transfers of assets

If:

> <a id="s328-470-a"></a>
> **(a)**  section 328-455 applies in relation to the transfer of an asset under a transaction; and

> <a id="s328-470-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an entity holds, either directly or indirectly: 156                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Rules for particular industries and occupations Part 3-45 Small business entities Division 328 Section 328-475

> > <a id="s328-470-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a *membership interest in the transferor or a transferee; or

> > <a id="s328-470-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a membership interest that was issued as provided for by the transaction; disregard a [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) from a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) that arises in relation to the membership interest after the transaction takes effect, except to the extent that the entity can demonstrate that the loss is attributable to a matter other than the transaction.
