# 328-200  Closing pool balance

You work out the closing pool balance of your [[*general small business pool*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-small-business-pool)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-small-business-pool) for an income year in this way: Method statement Step 1.   Add to the [[*opening pool balance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-opening-pool-balance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-opening-pool-balance) of the pool for the income year:

> <a id="s328-200-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the sum of the [[*taxable purpose proportions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose-proportion)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose-proportion) of the [[*adjustable values*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-adjustable-value) of [[*depreciating assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) you started to use, or have [[*installed ready for use*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-installed-ready-for-use)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-installed-ready-for-use), for a [[*taxable purpose*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose) during the income year and that are allocated to the pool; and

> <a id="s328-200-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the taxable purpose proportion of any cost addition amounts (see subsection 328-190(3)) for the income year for assets allocated to the pool. Step 2.   Subtract from the step 1 amount:

> <a id="s328-200-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*taxable purpose proportions*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose-proportion)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose-proportion) of the [[*termination values*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value) of [[*depreciating assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) allocated to the pool and for which a [[*balancing adjustment event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event) occurred during the income year; and 128                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Rules for particular industries and occupations Part 3-45 Small business entities Division 328 Section 328-205

> <a id="s328-200-b"></a>
> **(b)**  your deduction under subsection 328-190(1) for the pool for the income year; and

> <a id="s328-200-c"></a>
> **(c)**  your deductions under subsection 328-190(2) for [[*depreciating assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) you started to use, or have [[*installed ready for use*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-installed-ready-for-use)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-installed-ready-for-use), for a [[*taxable purpose*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose) during the income year and that are allocated to the pool; and

> <a id="s328-200-d"></a>
> **(d)**  your deductions under subsection 328-190(3) for the income year for cost addition amounts for assets allocated to the pool. Step 3.      The result is the closing pool balance of the pool for the income year.

> **Note:** A transferor does not subtract anything for certain balancing adjustment events under paragraph (a) of step 2 if roll-over relief under section 40-340 is chosen: see sections 328-243 and 328-245.
