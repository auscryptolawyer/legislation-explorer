# 328-243  Roll-over relief (1A) There is roll-over relief under subsection 40-340(1) (as affected by

subsection 40-340(2)) if:

> <a id="s328-243-a"></a>
> **(a)**  [[*balancing adjustment events*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event) occur for [[*depreciating assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) on a day (the BAE day) because an entity (the transferor) disposes of the assets in an income year to another entity (the transferee); and

> <a id="s328-243-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the disposal involves a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event); and

> <a id="s328-243-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the conditions in item 1, 2, 3 or 8 of the table in subsection 40-340(1) are satisfied; and

> <a id="s328-243-d"></a>
> **(d)**  deductions for the assets are calculated under this Subdivision; and

> <a id="s328-243-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the transferor and the transferee jointly choose the roll-over relief; and

> <a id="s328-243-f"></a>
> **(f)**  the condition in subsection (2) is met.

<a id="s328-243-1"></a>
**(1)**  Roll-over relief can be chosen under subsection 40-340(3) if:

> <a id="s328-243-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  [[*balancing adjustment events*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event) occur for [[*depreciating assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) on a day (the BAE day) because of subsection 40-295(2); and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                            137 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s328-243-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  deductions for the assets are calculated under this Subdivision; and

> <a id="s328-243-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity or entities that had an interest in the assets just before the balancing adjustment events occurred (the transferor) and the entity or entities that have an interest in the assets just after the events occurred (the transferee) jointly choose the roll-over relief; and

> <a id="s328-243-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the condition in subsection (2) is met.

<a id="s328-243-2"></a>
**(2)**  All of the [[*depreciating assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) that, just before the [[*balancing adjustment events*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event) occurred, were:

> <a id="s328-243-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  [[*held*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-held) by the transferor; and

> <a id="s328-243-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  allocated to the transferor’s [[*general small business pool*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-small-business-pool)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-small-business-pool); must be held by the transferee just after those events occurred.
