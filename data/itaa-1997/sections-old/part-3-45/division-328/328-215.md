# 328-215  Disposal etc. of depreciating assets


<a id="s328-215-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section sets out adjustments you may have to make if a [[*balancing adjustment event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-balancing-adjustment-event) occurs for a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset) for which you calculate your deductions under this Subdivision.

<a id="s328-215-2"></a>
**(2)**  If the asset is allocated to your [[*general small business pool*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-small-business-pool)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-small-business-pool) and:

> <a id="s328-215-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*closing pool balance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-closing-pool-balance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-closing-pool-balance) of the pool for the income year in which the event occurred is less than zero; or

> <a id="s328-215-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount worked out under subsection 328-210(2) for that income year is less than zero; the amount by which that balance or amount is less than zero is included in your assessable income for that year.

<a id="s328-215-3"></a>
**(3)**  In that case, the [[*closing pool balance*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-closing-pool-balance)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-closing-pool-balance) of the pool for that income year then becomes zero. 132                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Rules for particular industries and occupations Part 3-45 Small business entities Division 328 Section 328-220

<a id="s328-215-4"></a>
**(4)**  If the asset was one for which you deducted an amount under section 328-180 (about assets costing less than $1,000), you include the [[*taxable purpose proportion*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose-proportion)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-purpose-proportion) of the asset’s [[*termination value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-termination-value) in your assessable income.
