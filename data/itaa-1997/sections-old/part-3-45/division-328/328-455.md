# 328-455  Effect of small business restructures on transferred cost of assets


<a id="s328-455-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*income tax law*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-law)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-tax-law) applies to an entity in relation to the transfer of an asset by the entity, or to the entity, as if the transfer takes place for the asset’s [[*roll-over cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-roll-over-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-roll-over-cost) if:

> <a id="s328-455-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the transfer occurs under a transaction in relation to which section 328-430 applies; and

> <a id="s328-455-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a roll-over under this Subdivision is available under that section in relation to the asset.

<a id="s328-455-2"></a>
**(2)**  The asset’s roll-over cost is whichever of the following amounts is applicable in relation to the transfer:

> <a id="s328-455-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  in relation to the application of subsection (1) to the asset as a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) (other than [[*trading stock*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-trading-stock), a [[*revenue asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-revenue-asset) or a [[*depreciating asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-depreciating-asset))—the transferor’s [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) base for the asset just before the transfer takes effect;

> <a id="s328-455-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  in relation to the application of subsection (1) to the asset as trading stock—the amount equal to:

> > <a id="s328-455-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the [[*cost*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cost) of the item for the transferor; or

> > <a id="s328-455-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if the transferor held the item as trading stock at the start of the income year—the [[*value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value) of the item for the transferor then;

> <a id="s328-455-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  in relation to the application of subsection (1) to the asset as a revenue asset—the amount that would give rise to the transferor not making a profit or a loss on the transfer.
