# 393-17  Tax consequences of liabilities reducing because of farm management deposits


<a id="s393-17-1"></a>
**(1)**  To avoid doubt, if amounts of interest payable by the [[*owner of a *farm management deposit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-owner-of-a-farm-management-deposit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-owner-of-a-farm-management-deposit), or by a partnership of which the owner is a partner, to the *FMD provider in respect of loans or other debts of the owner or partnership fall short of what they otherwise would be because the owner holds the farm management deposit, then:

> <a id="s393-17-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  any income of the owner or partnership comprising the shortfall is neither assessable income nor [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) of the owner or partnership; and

> <a id="s393-17-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  any amount that any person:

> > <a id="s393-17-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  is not liable to pay because of the shortfall; and

> > <a id="s393-17-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  could have, apart from this section, deducted under this Act; is not deductible.

<a id="s393-17-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, this section applies only to the extent that the loans or other debts relate to a [[*primary production business*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-primary-production-business)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-primary-production-business) that the *owner or partnership carries on. 374                                 Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Rules for particular industries and occupations Part 3-45 Farm management deposits Division 393 Section 393-20
