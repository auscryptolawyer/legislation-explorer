# 83-235  Termination payments tax free—foreign resident period

A payment received by you is not assessable income and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) if:

> <a id="s83-235-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it was received in consequence of the termination of your employment in a foreign country; and

> <a id="s83-235-b"></a>
> **(b)**  it is not a [[*superannuation benefit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-benefit); and

> <a id="s83-235-c"></a>
> **(c)**  it is not a payment of a pension or an *annuity (whether or not the payment is a superannuation benefit); and

> <a id="s83-235-d"></a>
> **(d)**  it relates only to a period of employment when you were not an Australian resident. 56                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Rules affecting employees and other taxpayers receiving PAYG withholding payments Part 2-40 Other payments on termination of employment Division 83 Section 83-240
