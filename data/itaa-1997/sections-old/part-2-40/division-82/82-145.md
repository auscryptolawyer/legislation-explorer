# 82-145  Taxable component of an employment termination payment

The taxable component of an [[*employment termination payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employment-termination-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-employment-termination-payment) is the amount of the payment less the [[*tax free component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-free-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-free-component) of the payment (see section 82-140). 38                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Liability rules of general application Chapter 2 Rules affecting employees and other taxpayers receiving PAYG withholding payments Part 2-40 Employment termination payments Division 82 Section 82-150
