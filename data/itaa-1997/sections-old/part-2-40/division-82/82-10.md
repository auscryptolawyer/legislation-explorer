# 82-10  Taxation of life benefit termination payments

Tax free component

<a id="s82-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [[*tax free component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-free-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-free-component) of a [[*life benefit termination payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-benefit-termination-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-benefit-termination-payment) you receive is not assessable income and is not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income). Taxable component

<a id="s82-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of the payment is assessable income.

<a id="s82-10-3"></a>
**(3)**  You are entitled to a [[*tax offset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax-offset) that ensures that the rate of income tax on the amount mentioned in subsection (4) does not exceed:

> <a id="s82-10-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if you are your [[*preservation age*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-preservation-age)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-preservation-age) or older on the last day of the income year in which you receive the payment—15%; or

> <a id="s82-10-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—30%.

> **Note:** The remainder of the taxable component is taxed at the top marginal rate in accordance with the Income Tax Rates Act 1986.

<a id="s82-10-4"></a>
**(4)**  The amount is so much of the [[*taxable component*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-component) of the payment as does not exceed the smallest of the following:

> <a id="s82-10-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [[*ETP cap amount*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-etp-cap-amount)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-etp-cap-amount) reduced (but not below zero) by:

> > <a id="s82-10-4-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  if the payment is a payment of a kind referred to in subsection (6) (an excluded payment)—the amount worked out under this subsection for each [[*life benefit termination payment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-benefit-termination-payment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-benefit-termination-payment) you have received earlier in the income year to the extent that it is an excluded payment; or
