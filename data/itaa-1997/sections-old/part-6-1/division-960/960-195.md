# 960-195  Non-portfolio interest test

An interest held by an entity (the holding entity) in another entity (the test entity) passes the non-portfolio interest test at a time if the sum of the [[*direct participation interests*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-participation-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-direct-participation-interest) held by the holding entity and its *associates in the test entity at that time is 10% or more.
