# 960-240  Abnormal trading—suspected acquisition or merger

There is an abnormal trading in *shares in a company, or in units in a unit trust, if a *trading in those shares or units happens which the company or trustee knows or reasonably suspects is part of an Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             79 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 acquisition or merger of the company with another company, or of the trust with another trust.
