# 960-61  Functional currency for calculating capital gains and losses on indirect Australian real property interests


<a id="s960-61-1"></a>
**(1)**  Subsection (2) applies if: Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             51 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> <a id="s960-61-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are a foreign resident; and

> <a id="s960-61-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a [[*CGT event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-event) happens in relation to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) that is an *indirect Australian real property interest for you; and

> <a id="s960-61-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the sole or predominant currency in which you keep your accounts at the time of the CGT event is a currency other than Australian currency.

<a id="s960-61-2"></a>
**(2)**  You must use the [[*applicable functional currency*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-applicable-functional-currency)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-applicable-functional-currency) to work out the amount of your [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) (if any).
