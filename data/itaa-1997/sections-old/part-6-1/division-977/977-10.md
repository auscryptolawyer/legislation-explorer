# 977-10  Loss realised for income tax purposes


<a id="s977-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  A loss is realised for income tax purposes by a [[*realisation event*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realisation-event) that happens to a [[*CGT asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-cgt-asset) if, and only if, an entity makes a [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) from the event. That capital loss is the loss realised by the event. 156                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 The Dictionary Chapter 6 Concepts and topics Part 6-1 Realisation events, and the gains and losses they realise for income tax purposes Division 977 Section 977-15

<a id="s977-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  If a provision of this Act reduces the loss that would, apart from that provision, be [[*realised for income tax purposes*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realised-for-income-tax-purposes)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-realised-for-income-tax-purposes) by the event, the [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) is reduced by the same amount.
