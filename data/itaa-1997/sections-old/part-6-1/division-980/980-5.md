# 980-5  Providing affordable housing

A [[*dwelling*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-dwelling) is used to provide affordable housing on a particular day (the test day) if:

> <a id="s980-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  on the test day, the dwelling is [[*taxable Australian real property*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-real-property)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-taxable-australian-real-property) and is *residential premises that:

> > <a id="s980-5-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  are tenanted or available to be tenanted; and

> > <a id="s980-5-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  are not *commercial residential premises; and 162                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 The Dictionary Chapter 6 Concepts and topics Part 6-1 Affordable housing Division 980 Section 980-10

> <a id="s980-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  on the test day, the tenancy or prospective tenancy of the dwelling is exclusively managed by an [[*eligible community housing provider*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-community-housing-provider)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-eligible-community-housing-provider); and

> <a id="s980-5-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the eligible community housing provider has given each entity that holds an [[*ownership interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) in the dwelling a certificate under section 980-15 that covers the dwelling for the test day; and

> <a id="s980-5-d"></a>
> **(d)**  no entity is entitled to receive an incentive, under the Scheme prescribed for the purposes of Part 2 of the National Rental Affordability Scheme Act 2008, for the dwelling for the NRAS year (within the meaning of that Scheme) that includes the test day; and

> <a id="s980-5-e"></a>
> **(e)**  in the case of a [[*managed investment trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-managed-investment-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-managed-investment-trust) holding an [[*ownership interest*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ownership-interest) in the dwelling on the test day—none of the tenants or occupants of the dwelling on that day holds an interest in the trust that passes the [[*non-portfolio interest test*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-portfolio-interest-test)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-portfolio-interest-test) at any time during that day.
