# 976-1  Franked part of a distribution

The franked part of a *distribution is an amount worked out using the formula: [[*Franking credit*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-franking-credit) on the distribution ´ Applicable gross-up rate where: applicable gross-up rate means the *corporate tax gross-up rate of the entity making the distribution for the income year in which the distribution is made.
