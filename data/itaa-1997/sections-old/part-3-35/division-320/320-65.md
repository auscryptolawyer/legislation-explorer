# 320-65  Deduction for life insurance premiums in respect of life insurance policies that provide for participating or discretionary benefits

A [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) can deduct the amounts of *net premiums received in respect of *life insurance policies (other than *complying superannuation life insurance policies or *exempt life insurance policies) that provide for [[*participating benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participating-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participating-benefit) or [[*discretionary benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discretionary-benefits)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discretionary-benefits).
