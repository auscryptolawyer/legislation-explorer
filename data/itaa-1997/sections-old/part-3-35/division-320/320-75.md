# 320-75  Deduction for ordinary investment policies


<a id="s320-75-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies to a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) in respect of *ordinary investment policies issued by the company. 48                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Insurance business Part 3-35 Life insurance companies Division 320 Section 320-80

<a id="s320-75-2"></a>
**(2)**  The company can deduct, in respect of *life insurance premiums received in the income year for those policies:

> <a id="s320-75-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the sum of the *net premiums; less:

> <a id="s320-75-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  so much of the net premiums as an [[*actuary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary) determines to be attributable to fees and charges charged in that income year.

<a id="s320-75-3"></a>
**(3)**  In making a determination under subsection (2), an [[*actuary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary) is to have regard to:

> <a id="s320-75-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the changes over the income year in the sum of the *net current termination values of the policies; and

> <a id="s320-75-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the movements in those values during the income year.

<a id="s320-75-4"></a>
**(4)**  In addition, if an [[*actuary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary) determines that:

> <a id="s320-75-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  there has been a reduction in the income year (the current year) of exit fees that were imposed in respect of those policies in a previous income year; and

> <a id="s320-75-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the reduction (or a part of it) has not been taken into account in a determination under subsection (2) for the current year; the company can deduct so much of that reduction as has not been so taken into account.
