# 320-195  Transfer of assets and payment of amounts from a complying superannuation asset pool otherwise than as a result of a valuation under section 320-175


<a id="s320-195-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:

> <a id="s320-195-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a *life insurance policy issued by a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) becomes an [[*exempt life insurance policy*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-life-insurance-policy)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-life-insurance-policy); and

> <a id="s320-195-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  immediately before the policy became an exempt life insurance policy, the policy was a policy referred to in subsection 320-190(1); the company can transfer from a [[*complying superannuation asset pool*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset-pool)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset-pool), to its *segregated exempt assets, assets of any kind whose total *transfer value does not exceed the company’s liabilities in respect of the policy.

<a id="s320-195-2"></a>
**(2)**  A [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) can at any time transfer an asset from a [[*complying superannuation asset pool*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset-pool)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset-pool) in exchange for an amount of money equal to the *transfer value of the asset at the time of the transfer.

<a id="s320-195-3"></a>
**(3)**  If a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company):

> <a id="s320-195-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  imposes any fees or charges in respect of [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset); or

> <a id="s320-195-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  imposes any fees or charges in respect of *complying superannuation life insurance policies other than policies:

> > <a id="s320-195-3-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  that provide [[*superannuation death benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-death-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-superannuation-death-benefit), [[*disability superannuation benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-disability-superannuation-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-disability-superannuation-benefit) or temporary disability benefits of a kind referred to in paragraph 295-460(c), that are [[*participating benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participating-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participating-benefit); and

> > <a id="s320-195-3-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the liabilities under which are to be discharged out of the company’s [[*complying superannuation asset pool*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset-pool)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset-pool); or 72                                  Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Insurance business Part 3-35 Life insurance companies Division 320 Section 320-200

> <a id="s320-195-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  determines, at a time other than a *valuation time, that the total *transfer value of the company’s complying superannuation assets as at that time exceeds the sum of:

> > <a id="s320-195-3-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the company’s *complying superannuation liabilities at that time; and

> > <a id="s320-195-3-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  any reasonable provision made by the company at that time in its accounts for liability for income tax in respect of those assets; the company must, when the fees or charges are imposed or the excess is determined, as the case may be, transfer, from the [[*complying superannuation asset pool*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset-pool)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset-pool), assets having a total transfer value equal to the fees, charges or excess, as the case may be.

<a id="s320-195-4"></a>
**(4)**  If:

> <a id="s320-195-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  any liabilities arise for the discharge of which a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)’s [[*complying superannuation asset pool*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset-pool)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset-pool) is established; or

> <a id="s320-195-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  any expenses are incurred by a life insurance company directly in respect of [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset) in relation to a period during which the assets are complying superannuation assets; or

> <a id="s320-195-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  any liabilities to pay [[*PAYG instalments*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payg-instalment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-payg-instalment), or income tax, that are attributable to the company’s [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset); the life insurance company must pay, from the complying superannuation asset pool, any amounts required to discharge the liabilities, or amounts equal to the expenses (as appropriate).
