# 320-5  Object of Division


<a id="s320-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  The object of this Division is to provide for the taxation of *life insurance companies in a broadly comparable way to other entities that *derive similar kinds of income.

<a id="s320-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  To achieve this object, the Division:

> <a id="s320-5-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  identifies certain amounts that are included in the assessable income, or are [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) or [[*non-assessable non-exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-non-assessable-non-exempt-income), of a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company); and

> <a id="s320-5-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  identifies certain amounts that a life insurance company can deduct; and

> <a id="s320-5-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  enables a life insurance company to have taxable incomes and [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) losses of the following classes for the purposes of working out its income tax for an income year:

> > <a id="s320-5-2-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the [[*complying superannuation class*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-class)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-class);

> > <a id="s320-5-2-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the *ordinary class; and

> <a id="s320-5-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  contains other provisions necessary to enable the income tax on the taxable income of a life insurance company to be worked out.
