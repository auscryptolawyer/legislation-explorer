# 320-85  Deduction for increase in value of liabilities under net risk components of life insurance policies


<a id="s320-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) can deduct the amount (if any) by which the [[*value*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-value), at the end of the income year, of its liabilities under the *net risk components of *life insurance policies exceeds the value, at the end of the previous income year, of those liabilities.

> **Note:** Where the value at the end of the income year is less than the value at the end of the previous income year, the difference is included in assessable income: see paragraph 320-15(1)(h).

> **Note:** Section 320-85 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997 makes special provision in respect of the calculation of the value of a life insurance company’s liabilities under the net risk components of life insurance policies at the end of the income year immediately preceding the income year in which 1 July 2000 occurs.

<a id="s320-85-2"></a>
**(2)**  Subsection (1) does not cover any liabilities under:

> <a id="s320-85-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a *life insurance policy that provides for [[*participating benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participating-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participating-benefit) or [[*discretionary benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discretionary-benefits)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discretionary-benefits); or

> <a id="s320-85-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an [[*exempt life insurance policy*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-life-insurance-policy)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-life-insurance-policy); or 50                                     Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Insurance business Part 3-35 Life insurance companies Division 320 Section 320-87

> <a id="s320-85-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  a [[*funeral policy*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-funeral-policy)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-funeral-policy).

<a id="s320-85-3"></a>
**(3)**  If a *life insurance policy is a [[*disability policy*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-disability-policy)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-disability-policy) (other than a [[*continuous disability policy*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-continuous-disability-policy)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-continuous-disability-policy)), the value at a particular time of the liabilities of the [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) under the *net risk component of the policy is the *current termination value of the component at that time (calculated by an [[*actuary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary)).

<a id="s320-85-4"></a>
**(4)**  In the case of *life insurance policies other than policies to which subsection (3) applies, the value at a particular time of the liabilities of the [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) under the *net risk components of the policies is the amount calculated by an [[*actuary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary) to be:

> <a id="s320-85-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the sum of the policy liabilities (as defined in the [[*Valuation Standard*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-valuation-standard)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-valuation-standard)) in respect of the net risk components of the policies at that time; less

> <a id="s320-85-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the sum of any cumulative losses (as defined in the Valuation Standard) for the net risk components of the policies at that time.
