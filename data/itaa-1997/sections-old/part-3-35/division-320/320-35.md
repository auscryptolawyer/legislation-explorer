# 320-35  Exempt income

These amounts *derived by a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) are exempt from income tax: 42                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Insurance business Part 3-35 Life insurance companies Division 320 Section 320-37

> <a id="s320-35-a"></a>
> **(a)**  amounts of [[*ordinary income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-ordinary-income) and [[*statutory income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-statutory-income) accrued before 1 July 1988 that were derived from assets that have become [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset);

> <a id="s320-35-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if the company is an [[*RSA*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-rsa) provider—any amounts that are disregarded because of paragraph 320-137(3)(d) or (e) in working out the company’s taxable income of the [[*complying superannuation class*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-class)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-class).
