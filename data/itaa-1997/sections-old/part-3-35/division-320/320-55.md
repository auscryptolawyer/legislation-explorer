# 320-55  Deduction for life insurance premiums where liabilities under life insurance policies are to be discharged from complying superannuation assets


<a id="s320-55-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies to a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) in respect of *life insurance policies where the company’s liabilities under the policies are to be discharged out of [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset).

<a id="s320-55-2"></a>
**(2)**  The company can deduct:

> <a id="s320-55-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the amounts of the *life insurance premiums received in respect of the policies that are transferred to its [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset) in the income year; less:

> <a id="s320-55-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  so much of those amounts as relate to the company’s liability to pay amounts on the death or disability of a person.

<a id="s320-55-3"></a>
**(3)**  For the purposes of subsection (2) only, the amount of a *life insurance premium that relates to the company’s liability to pay amounts on the death or disability of a person is:

> <a id="s320-55-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the policy provides for [[*participating benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participating-benefit)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-participating-benefit) or [[*discretionary benefits*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discretionary-benefits)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-discretionary-benefits)—nil; or

> <a id="s320-55-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if paragraph (a) does not apply and the policy states that the whole or a specified part of the premium is payable in respect of such a liability—the whole or that part of the premium, as appropriate; or

> <a id="s320-55-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if neither paragraph (a) nor (b) applies:

> > <a id="s320-55-3-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  if the policy is an [[*endowment policy*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-endowment-policy)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-endowment-policy)—10% of the premium; or

> > <a id="s320-55-3-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  if the policy is a [[*whole of life policy*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-whole-of-life-policy)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-whole-of-life-policy)—30% of the premium; or

> > <a id="s320-55-3-c-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  otherwise—so much of the premium as an [[*actuary*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-actuary) determines to be attributable to such a liability.
