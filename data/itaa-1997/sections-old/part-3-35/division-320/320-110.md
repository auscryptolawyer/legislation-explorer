# 320-110  Deduction for interest credited to income bonds


<a id="s320-110-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) that is a *friendly society can deduct interest credited in the income year to the holders of [[*income bonds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-bond)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-bond) issued after 31 December 2002 where the interest accrued on or after 1 January 2003.
