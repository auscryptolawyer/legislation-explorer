# 320-37  Non-assessable non-exempt income


<a id="s320-37-1"></a>
**(1)**  These amounts *derived by a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) are not assessable income and are not [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income):

> <a id="s320-37-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  amounts of ordinary income and statutory income derived from *segregated exempt assets, being income that relates to the period during which the assets were segregated exempt assets;

> <a id="s320-37-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  amounts of ordinary income and statutory income derived from the *disposal of units in a [[*pooled superannuation trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pooled-superannuation-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-pooled-superannuation-trust);

> <a id="s320-37-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if an *Australian/overseas fund or an [[*overseas fund*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-overseas-fund)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-overseas-fund) established by the company derived foreign establishment amounts—the foreign resident proportion of the foreign establishment amounts;

> <a id="s320-37-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  if the company is a *friendly society:

> > <a id="s320-37-1-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  amounts derived before 1 July 2001 that are exempt from income tax under section 50-1; and

> > <a id="s320-37-1-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  amounts derived on or after 1 July 2001 but before 1 January 2003, that are attributable to [[*income bonds*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-bond)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-income-bond), *funeral policies or *sickness policies; and

> > <a id="s320-37-1-d-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  amounts derived on or after 1 July 2001 but before 1 January 2003, that are attributable to [[*scholarship plans*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-scholarship-plan)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-scholarship-plan) and would have been exempt from income tax under section 50-1 if they had been received before 1 July 2001; and

> > <a id="s320-37-1-d-iv"></a>
> > **(iv)**  amounts derived on or after 1 January 2003 that are attributable to income bonds, funeral policies or Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             43 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 *sickness policies, that were issued before 1 January 2003; and

> > <a id="s320-37-1-d-v"></a>
> > **(v)**  amounts derived on or after 1 January 2003 that are attributable to scholarship plans issued before 1 January 2003 and that would have been exempt from income tax if they had been received before 1 July 2001.

> **Note:** The effect of this section is modified when the life insurance business of a life insurance company is transferred to another life insurance company: see section 320-325. (1A) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c), foreign establishment amounts for the [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company) means the total amount of assessable income that was *derived in the income year:

> <a id="s320-37-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  in the course of the carrying on by the company of a business in a foreign country at or through a [[*permanent establishment*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-permanent-establishment)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-permanent-establishment) of the company in that country; and

> <a id="s320-37-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  from sources in that or any other foreign country; and

> <a id="s320-37-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  from assets that:

> > <a id="s320-37-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  are attributable to the permanent establishment; and

> > <a id="s320-37-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  are held to meet the liabilities under the *life insurance policies issued by the company at or through the permanent establishment.
