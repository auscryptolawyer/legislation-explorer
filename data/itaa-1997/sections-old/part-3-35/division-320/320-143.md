# 320-143  Tax loss—ordinary class

Working out a tax loss of the ordinary class

<a id="s320-143-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)’s [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss of the ordinary class is a tax loss worked out under this Act on the basis of only:

> <a id="s320-143-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  assessable income of the company that is not covered by subsection 320-137(2); and

> <a id="s320-143-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  amounts (other than tax losses) that the company can deduct and are not covered by subsection 320-137(4); and

> <a id="s320-143-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  [[*net exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-exempt-income) of the company that is not attributable to [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) *derived:

> > <a id="s320-143-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  from the company’s [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset); and Income Tax Assessment Act 1997                             63 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026

> > <a id="s320-143-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  in relation to the period during which those assets were complying superannuation assets.

> **Note:** For the usual way of working out a tax loss: see section 36-10. For other ways of working out a tax loss: see section 36-25. Deducting a tax loss of the ordinary class

<a id="s320-143-2"></a>
**(2)**  A [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)’s [[*tax*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-tax) loss of the ordinary class can be deducted under this Act only from:

> <a id="s320-143-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  [[*net exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-exempt-income) of the company that is not attributable to [[*exempt income*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-exempt-income) *derived:

> > <a id="s320-143-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  from the company’s [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset); and

> > <a id="s320-143-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  in relation to the period during which those assets were complying superannuation assets; and

> <a id="s320-143-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  assessable income of the company that is not covered by subsection 320-137(2), reduced by amounts (other than tax losses) that the company can deduct and are not covered by subsection 320-137(4).

> **Note:** For the usual way of deducting a tax loss: see section 36-17. For other ways of deducting a tax loss: see section 36-25.
