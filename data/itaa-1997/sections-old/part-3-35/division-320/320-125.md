# 320-125  Capital losses from complying superannuation assets


<a id="s320-125-1"></a>
**(1)**  In working out a [[*life insurance company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-life-insurance-company)’s [[*net capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-gain) or [[*net capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-net-capital-loss) for the income year, *capital losses from [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset) can be used only to reduce [[*capital gains*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) from complying superannuation assets. 54                                   Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 Authorised Version C2026C00122 registered 10/04/2026 Specialist liability rules Chapter 3 Insurance business Part 3-35 Life insurance companies Division 320 Section 320-130

<a id="s320-125-2"></a>
**(2)**  If some or all of a [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) from a [[*complying superannuation asset*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset) cannot be applied in an income year, the unapplied amount can be applied in the next income year in which the company’s [[*capital gains*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) from [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset) exceed the company’s capital losses (if any) from complying superannuation assets.

<a id="s320-125-3"></a>
**(3)**  If the company has 2 or more unapplied *net capital losses from [[*complying superannuation assets*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-complying-superannuation-asset), the company must apply them in the order in which they were made.

> **Note:** This section affects the amount of assessable income that is to be taken into account in working out a taxable income or tax loss of the complying superannuation class: see sections 320-137 and 320-141.
