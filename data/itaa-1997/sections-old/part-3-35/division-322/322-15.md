# 322-15  Certain capital gains and capital losses disregarded

A [[*capital gain*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-gain) or [[*capital loss*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-capital-loss) you make because you assign a right under or in relation to a [[*general insurance policy*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-insurance-policy)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-general-insurance-policy) you held with an [[*HIH company*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hih-company)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hih-company) to the Commonwealth, the trustee of the [[*HIH Trust*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hih-trust)*](/itaa-1997/s995-1#s995-1-hih-trust) or a prescribed entity is disregarded.
