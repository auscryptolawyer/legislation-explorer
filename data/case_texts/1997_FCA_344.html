In the matter of Australian Company Number 007 764 249 Pty Ltd (in liquidation); Anthony Stevens Smith v Deputy Commissioner of Taxation & Ors [1997] FCA 344 (9 May 1997)

CATCHWORDS

CORPORATIONS - liquidator's claim to recover payments of arrears of tax instalment deductions and sales tax as voidable transactions under s 588FF - whether priority under s 221P of Income Tax Assessment Act for tax instalment deductions made before 31 May 1993 protects payment of those amounts from being voidable transactions.

CORPORATIONS - liquidator's claim to recover payments of arrears of tax instalment deductions and sales tax as voidable transactions under s 588FF - whether defence under s 588FF established - whether payment received in good faith - whether absence of reasonable grounds for suspecting insolvency - onus of proof.

CORPORATIONS - liquidator's claim to recover payments of arrears of tax instalment deductions and sales tax as voidable transactions under s 588FF - effect of statutory notices to solicitors for company given under s 218 of Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 and under s 74 of Sales Tax Assessment Act 1992 - whether monies paid at settlement of sale of company's business held by solicitors 'for or on behalf of company' - bank cheques presented at settlement payable to secured creditors of company and handled by solicitors for company - whether oral waiver of notices possible - effect of arrangement made as to payment inconsistent with notices.

CORPORATIONS - liquidator's claim to recover payments of arrears of tax instalment deductions and sales tax as voidable transactions under s 588FF - claim successful - cross-claim against directors of company under s 588FGA in respect of tax instalment deductions repayable - whether directors took all reasonable steps to prevent payment - defence under s 588FGA(6) not made out.

MINUTES OF ORDER

CORAM: Mansfield J

PLACE: Adelaide

DATE: 9 May 1997

THE COURT DECLARES THAT:

1. The payment by Australian Company Number 007 764 249 Pty Ltd ("the company") to the respondent and the receipt by the respondent of the sums of

a) $194,000 on 3 November 1994, and

b) $41,000 on or about 6 February 1995 were each an unfair preference and an insolvent transaction and a voidable transaction pursuant to ss588FA, 588FC and 588FE of the Corporations Law.

2. The cross claimant be, and is entitled to be, indemnified by the cross respondents in respect of such part of the sum of $180,999.07 as the cross claimant does not recover from the company in its liquidation, and being proportion of the sum the subject of the declaration in par1 hereof, together with the interest on the amount so declared to be payable for which the respondent is liable to the applicant.

THE COURT ORDERS THAT:

1. The respondent do pay to the applicant, as liquidator of the company, the sum of $235,000.
