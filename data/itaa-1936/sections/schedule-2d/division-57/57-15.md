# 57-15  Time when income derived

<a id="s57-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  To the extent that income derived by the transition taxpayer before the transition time is in respect of:

> <a id="s57-15-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  services rendered; or

> <a id="s57-15-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  goods provided; or

> <a id="s57-15-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the doing of any other thing; at or after the transition time, the income is treated for the purposes of this Act as having been derived at the time the services were rendered, the goods were provided or the thing was done, as the case requires.

<a id="s57-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  To the extent that income derived by the transition taxpayer at or after the transition time is in respect of:

> <a id="s57-15-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  services rendered; or

> <a id="s57-15-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  goods provided; or

> <a id="s57-15-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the doing of any other thing; before the transition time, the income is treated for the purposes of this Act as having been derived before that time.
