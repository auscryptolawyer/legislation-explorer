# 13 A resolution is passed to proceed with the No franking credit arises for the company or the subsidiary in relation to the demutualisation of a mutual insurance company or payment of the dividend on or after the demutualisation resolution day. both a mutual insurance company and a mutual affiliate company. A dividend that was declared before the demutualisation resolution day is paid on or after the demutualisation resolution day to:

> <a id="s13-a"></a>
> **(a)** in the case of any demutualisation method—the mutual insurance company or any wholly-owned subsidiary of the mutual insurance company; or

> <a id="s13-b"></a>
> **(b)** in the case of demutualisation method 7—the mutual affiliate company, a wholly-owned subsidiary of the mutual affiliate company, or a company all of whose shares are beneficially owned by the general insurance company and the mutual affiliate company.
