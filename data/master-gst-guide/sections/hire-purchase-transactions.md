# Hire-purchase transactions ¶7-438

A hire-purchase agreement is normally treated in the same way as a purchase by instalments. This requires taxpayers remitting GST on an accruals basis to account for the whole GST liability in the earlier of the tax period in which the first payment is received or an invoice is issued (¶7-205). Correspondingly, purchasers can claim input tax credits (ITCs) in the earlier of the tax period in which they make the first payment or receive a tax invoice.

In contrast, cash basis taxpayers would normally attribute their GST liabilities and ITCs progressively, in the tax periods in which they make or receive each instalment. To overcome the cash flow differential between cash and accrual remitters as between hire-purchase and other forms of financing, s 158-5 (¶12-110) enables cash basis taxpayers to account for hire-purchase transactions pursuant to agreements entered into from 1 July 2012, as if they were accruals basis taxpayers.

Illustration: Accounting for GST on hire-purchase transactions by cash remitters A taxpayer who accounts on a cash basis enters into a hire-purchase agreement. The total GST-inclusive purchase price of $5,500 th is payable in 10 instalments of $550. Although the taxpayer remits GST on a cash basis, it can claim the full ITC of $500 (1/11 × $5,500) in the tax period in which the invoice is originally issued, in the same way had the taxpayer been remitting GST on an accruals basis.

See ¶4-020 for early termination of a hire-purchase agreement.
