# Postponing credit claim to later BAS ¶5-125

A business may sometimes fail to claim an input tax credit (ITC) in the GST return for the relevant tax period in which the entitlement arose. This may arise from cut-off procedures around period end, or where a taxpayer remits GST on an accrual basis, and an invoice has not been processed before payment. In such situations, the ITC for the acquisition is postponed to the first tax period (subject to the general 4-year limit on claims for ITCs (¶5-010)) for which a return is lodged taking it into account (s 29-10(4)). This removes the need to lodge an amended GST return for the earlier tax period.

Illustration: Claiming an ITC in a later period During a tax period, a business orders and buys a piece of equipment. Although it has received a tax invoice, no claim for an ITC is claimed in the GST return for that tax period. The credit may instead be claimed in any subsequent GST return and will be attributable to the tax period covered by that return.

Where this occurs, the normal 5-year period for keeping records starts from the time the ITC is claimed, rather than from when the acquisition was made (Administration Act Sch 1 s 382-5). For general rules on correction of BAS mistakes, see ¶8-045.

*Refs: s 29-10(4)), s 382-5)*
