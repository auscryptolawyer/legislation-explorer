# Transitional rules ¶1-200

The general rule is that GST is payable only on supplies and importations made on or after 1 July 2000, and that sales tax does not apply to those transactions. Similarly, input tax credits can only be claimed on acquisitions and importations made on or after 1 July 2000 (¶19-100).

However, there were qualifications to this rule. For example, supplies made on or after 1 July 2000 could be GST-free in certain situations, where the contract was entered into before the GST officially became law (8 July 1999).

- redeemable vouchers (Transition Act s 11)

- progressive or periodic supplies (¶19-210)

- construction contracts (¶19-230)

- life memberships (¶19-220)

- prepaid funerals (¶13-380)

- stocks of second-hand goods on hand at 1 July 2000 (¶16-130).
