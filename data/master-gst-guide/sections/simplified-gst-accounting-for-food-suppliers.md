# Simplified GST accounting for food suppliers ¶13-210

Suppliers may sell a range of food products that are taxable, GST-free, composite or mixed, or a combination thereof. To assist taxpayers to correctly determine the application of GST to food, a range of resources are available including a Detailed food list comprising thousands of items. In addition, the National Product Catalogue (replacing the GS1net) of GS1 Australia, the Australian bar code provider, may be used to determine the GST classification of specific food and beverage products (Practice Statement PS LA 2012/2 (GA); ATO GST food classification guidance webpage).

Certain food retailers may also be eligible to use a Simplified Accounting Method (¶13-215).
