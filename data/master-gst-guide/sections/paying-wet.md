# Paying WET ¶22-300

WET is collected in the same way as GST, and is taken into account in determining the net BAS (Business Activity Statement) amount of an entity for the tax period (WET Act s 21-5); see ¶8-100.

The WET on a dealing which is a taxable supply is attributed to the same period to which the supply would be attributed under the normal GST rules (¶7-100). If the dealing involves a non-taxable supply, the WET is attributed to the tax period in which the supply would have been attributed if it had been taxable. In the case of AOUs (¶22-170) — which do not involve a supply — the WET is attributed to the tax period in which the application to own use is made (WET Act s 21-10). For the attribution of WET where a contract for sale includes a retention of title clause and the purchaser sells or uses the wine before title passes, see Practical Compliance Guideline PCG 2019/3.

Where a member of a GST group is liable for WET, that WET is payable by the representative member (WET Act s 21-40). Similarly, where WET is payable on a taxable dealing that a joint venture operator makes on behalf of another participant, that WET is payable by the operator, not the participant (WET Act s 21-70).

Unlike GST, there is no exclusion from WET for intra-group taxable supplies of wine.

A taxpayer company remains liable for dealings made before the appointment of an administrator even though the consideration for the dealing is received after the appointment (Interpretative Decision ID 2003/992).

Correction of earlier errors In certain circumstances, where a taxpayer has made an error in a WET calculation in an earlier tax period, they may correct it in a BAS for a later period (A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) (Correcting Wine Equalisation Tax Errors) Determination 2025 (F2025L00928)). This option is not available where an ATO tax audit is under way, and special time restrictions apply to correction of debit errors.
