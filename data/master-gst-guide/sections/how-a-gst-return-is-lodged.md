# How a GST return is lodged ¶8-043

Entities with GST turnover (¶3-030) of at least $20 million (¶7-110) must lodge their GST return electronically in a format approved by the ATO, unless the ATO approves some other method (s 31-25).

In other cases, an entity may lodge hard copy (by mail) or electronically.

Electronic lodgments can be made through ATO online services for business. Businesses lodging quarterly returns electronically may be entitled to an additional 2 weeks to lodge and pay their BAS (¶8-005; ATO Due dates for lodging and paying your BAS webpage).

An automated phone service allows for lodging of nil BAS (ie nil amounts at all labels) (s 31-15; ATO How to lodge your BAS website). This may apply, for example, where a business is seasonal, intermittent or has no staff or turnover activity in the period. earlier BAS
