# Basis of accounting ¶1-130

The GST and the input tax credits that belong to each period are worked out according to attribution rules, which vary according to whether you are on a cash basis or an accruals basis of accounting.

If you are on the cash basis, you work out your GST and input tax credits for each tax period on the basis of amounts actually received and paid out. You can use the cash basis if:

- you satisfy a small business test

- you account on a cash basis for income tax purposes

- you are a charity, or

- you can convince the ATO that it is appropriate (¶7-300).

If you use the accruals basis, you work out your GST and input tax credits for each tax period on the basis of your entitlement to be paid and your obligation to pay. This will often be when you give or receive an invoice (¶7-205).
