# Imported second-hand goods ¶9-070

Imported second-hand goods do not qualify for the special input tax credit which applies where goods are acquired from an unregistered supplier (¶16-110). However, if you had imported second-hand goods on hand at 1 July 2000, you could have claimed a separate input tax credit equal to the amount of sales tax you paid when you imported the goods (Transition Act, s 16).
