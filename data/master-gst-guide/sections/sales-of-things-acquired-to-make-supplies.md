# Sales of things acquired to make supplies ¶10-060

If you acquire something for the purpose of making financial supplies, you will either not be entitled to an input tax credit or be entitled to a reduced credit. If you later sell that thing, you may be entitled to a decreasing adjustment. For details, see ¶6-310.
