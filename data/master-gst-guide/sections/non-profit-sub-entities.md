# Non-profit sub-entities ¶18-235

The GST obligations of a non-profit sub-entity (¶15-080) are also imposed on each person who is responsible to third parties for the management of the sub-entity. These persons are liable for offences committed by the sub-entity, subject to the statutory defence (Administration Act, Sch 1, s 444-85), and are jointly and severally liable for any amount of GST payable by the sub-entity.

*Refs: s 444-85)*
