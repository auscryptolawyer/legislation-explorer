# Leases entered into before 1 July 2000 ¶11-340

If a commercial lease spanned 1 July 2000, only the part from 1 July 2000 is subject to GST (¶19-210).

A lease may set the rent according to the amount of the turnover of the lessee. Where the contract extends beyond 1 July 2000, it will need to be made clear whether that turnover amount includes GST. Turnover clauses are not treated as constituting opportunities to review.
