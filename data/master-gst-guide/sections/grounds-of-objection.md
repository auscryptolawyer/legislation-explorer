# Grounds of objection ¶18-620

An objection will only be valid if it is framed so that the Commissioner can appreciate the grounds on which the decision is disputed. Stating the grounds of objection fully is also important because if the objection later becomes the subject of administrative review or appeal to a court, the review will generally be limited to the grounds stated in the objection.
