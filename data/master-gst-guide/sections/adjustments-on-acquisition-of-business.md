# Adjustments on acquisition of business ¶6-420

An increasing adjustment will apply if you acquire a business GST-free as a going concern but intend to use it wholly or partly in making private or input taxed supplies (eg providing credit facilities). This type of adjustment is explained at ¶11-520.
