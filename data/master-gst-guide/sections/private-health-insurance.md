# Private health insurance ¶13-370

Private health insurance with a registered health insurance organisation is GST-free (s 38-55). Insurance that provides against liability to pay for ambulance services is also GST-free. This exemption does not apply to Overseas Student Health Cover policies (ATO GST Industry Issues: Health Industry Partnership). For the position of the insurance company, see ¶10-140.

*Refs: s 38-55)*
