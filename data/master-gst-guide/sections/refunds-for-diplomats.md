# Refunds for diplomats ¶12-160

Diplomatic missions, consular posts and their employees may be entitled to refunds of GST and luxury car tax on the purchase of locally manufactured or imported vehicles. The refund is made under the Indirect Tax Concession Scheme (¶16-230). Precise entitlements vary from country to country, and the position held.

For details, see the ATO Fact Sheet Motor Vehicle Sales to the Diplomatic Community (available online at www.ato.gov.au).
