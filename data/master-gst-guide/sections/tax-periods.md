# Tax periods ¶1-120

Your liability is worked out at the end of each of your tax periods. These periods are normally monthly or quarterly. Monthly tax periods must be used if:

- your GST turnover is $20m or more, or

- you have a bad tax history.

If you use quarterly tax periods they will normally end on 31 March, 30 June, 30 September and 31 December (¶7-100).

Annual tax periods may apply where GST is being paid by instalments (¶8-037), or where voluntarily- registered taxpayers so elect (¶8-040).
