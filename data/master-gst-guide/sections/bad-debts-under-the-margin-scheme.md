# Bad debts under the margin scheme ¶11-130

If the buyer defaults, there may be a GST bad debt adjustment for the supplier (¶6-200). This adjustment th is limited to 1/11 of the margin (s 75-25). There is no adjustment for the purchaser because a purchaser under the margin scheme is not eligible for input tax credits.

*Refs: s 75-25)*
