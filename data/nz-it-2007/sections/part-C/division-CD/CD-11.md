# CD 11  Avoidance arrangements

An amount treated as a dividend under any of the following sections is a dividend:

- (a) section GB 1 (Arrangements involving dividend stripping):

- (b) section GB 23(7) (Excessive remuneration to relatives):

- (c) section GB 25 (Close company remuneration to shareholders, directors, or relatives).

*Defined in this Act: amount, close company, director, dividend, relative, shareholder*
