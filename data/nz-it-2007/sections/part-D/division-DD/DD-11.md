# DD 11  Some definitions

In this subpart,— business includes any recurring income-earning activity business contacts— (a) includes, for a person,— (i) their clients, customers, shareholders, other financiers, and suppliers: (ii) the clients, customers, shareholders, other financiers, and suppliers of an associated person: (b) if the person is in partnership, does not include other partners in the partnership. business premises [Repealed]

*Defined in this Act: associated person, business, business contacts, business premises, shareholder*
