# HD 4  Treatment of principals

Despite section HD 3,—

- (a) a principal remains liable for their tax obligations, and is not released from them merely through the existence of the agency; and

- (b) if the Commissioner agrees, the principal and the agent may decide that the principal is to undertake the duties set out in section HD 3(2).

*Defined in this Act: agent, Commissioner, tax*
