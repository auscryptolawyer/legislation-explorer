# HA 28  Elections by trustee shareholders

If a shareholder referred to in section HA 5 is acting as trustee, they must make an election together with—

- (a) 1 or more beneficiaries of the trust who are natural persons with legal capacity; or

- (b) if no beneficiary has legal capacity, a natural person, who may also be the trustee, who assumes liability on behalf of beneficiaries.

*Defined in this Act: shareholder, trustee*
