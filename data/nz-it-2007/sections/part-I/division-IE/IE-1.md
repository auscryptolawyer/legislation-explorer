# IE 1  When this subpart applies

This subpart applies if, in an amalgamation,—

- (a) either the amalgamating company or the amalgamated company has, before the date of the amalgamation, a tax loss component or ring-fenced tax loss:

- (b) a company that is part of a group of companies has a tax loss for the tax year of amalgamation that may be made available to the amalgamated company to subtract from its net income for the tax year.

*Defined in this Act: amalgamated company, amalgamating company, amalgamation, group of companies, net income, ring-fenced tax loss, tax loss, tax loss component, tax year*
