# IA 1  What this subpart does

This subpart—

- (a) defines the relationship between the core provisions of this Act, the provisions of this Part, and other provisions in this Act that allow a person with a tax loss to use the amount of the loss; and

- (b) establishes the general rules for using a tax loss.

*Defined in this Act: amount, tax loss*
