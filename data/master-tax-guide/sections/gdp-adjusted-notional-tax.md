# GDP-adjusted notional tax ¶27-470

The Commissioner calculates GDP-adjusted notional tax in much the same way as notional tax is calculated (¶27-460). However, the adjusted taxable income for the base year is increased by the GDP adjustment and the adjusted tax is calculated based on this increased adjusted taxable income. Further, the adjusted withholding income for the base year is also increased by the GDP adjustment and the adjusted tax is calculated based on this increased adjusted withholding income.

The GDP adjustment factor is 6% for 2023–24 and 2024–25.
