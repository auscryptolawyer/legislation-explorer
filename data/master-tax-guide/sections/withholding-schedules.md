# Withholding schedules ¶42-100

The ATO issues various Schedules setting out the weekly, fortnightly or monthly amounts required to be withheld from withholding payments covered by the PAYG withholding system (¶26-130). PAYG withholding rates on other types of withholding payments are also explained at ¶26-130.
