# CGT: exemption for PST units ¶11-890

A capital gain is exempt from CGT and a capital loss is disregarded if it is made in relation to units in a PST and the entity is:

- the trustee of a complying superannuation fund, a complying ADF or a PST

- a life insurance entity and, just before the event happened, the unit was included in a tax-

  advantaged insurance fund of the entity, or

- a registered organisation and, just before the event happened, the unit was owned by the entity

  solely for carrying on a tax-advantaged business of the entity (s 118-350).

*Refs: s 118-350)*
