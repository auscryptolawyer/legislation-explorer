# Other acquisition rules ¶11-470

There are several other acquisition rules which apply for non-CGT situations (s 109-60) but which also apply for CGT purposes:

- a taxpayer stops holding an item as trading stock (¶9-245)

- a trust ceases to be a resident trust and there is an attributable taxpayer (¶21-290)

- a CGT event happens to an asset in connection with the demutualisation of an insurance

  company (¶12-650)

- a CGT event happens to a pre-1 July 1988 asset of a complying superannuation fund, a

  complying ADF or a PST (¶13-130)

- a CFC owned an asset on its commencing day (¶21-200)

- an asset is owned by a tax-exempt entity that becomes taxable (¶10-630).

*Refs: s 109-60)*
