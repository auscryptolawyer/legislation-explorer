# Collection of Medicare levy and surcharge ¶2-370

The levy and surcharge are collected in conjunction with, and in the same way as, income tax (ITAA36 s 251R(7)). Taxpayers can elect to pay Medicare levy and surcharge through the PAYG system by giving notice to the employer to vary the PAYG amounts withheld from salary or wages (TAA sch 1 s 15-50) (¶26-350).
