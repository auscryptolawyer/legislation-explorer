# Deductions for COVID-19 test expenses ¶16-490

From 1 July 2021, expenses incurred by an individual taxpayer in respect of a COVID-19 test taken for the purpose of determining whether the taxpayer can attend or remain at their place of work are deductible under

- a polymerase chain reaction (PCR) test, or

- a test that is included in the Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods for testing COVID-19, such

  as an approved rapid antigen test.

Where the COVID-19 test expense is partially incurred in gaining or producing assessable income, the amount of the deduction must be reasonably apportioned. For example, where one test from a 2-pack rapid antigen test is used for the purpose of leisure travel and the other for work purposes, the amount of the expense is halved for the purpose of claiming the deduction.

The ancillary costs of acquiring the tests, including the costs of travelling and parking to purchase a testing kit, are not deductible. However, incidental costs such as vendor credit surcharge fees at the time of purchase and postage and handling for online purchases are deductible.
