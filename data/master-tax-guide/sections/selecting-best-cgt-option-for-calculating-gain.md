# Selecting best CGT option for calculating gain ¶31-125

Individuals, complying superannuation entities and trusts may have a choice as to how to calculate the capital gain on disposal of assets — the frozen indexation option or the discounted basis. For factors relevant to this decision, see ¶11-038.
