# Withholding PAYG amounts from payments ¶3-840

Clubs may be required to withhold PAYG amounts from payments made to entertainers appearing at the club where the entertainer is an employee (¶26-150) or, even if the entertainer is not an employee, if no ABN is quoted (¶26-220).
