# TFN withholding tax on non-residents ¶22-050

Under the TFN system (¶33-000), an investment body is required to withhold tax from any income that it becomes liable to pay in connection with certain investments in respect of which the investor has not quoted a TFN (¶33-130). However, non-resident investors are exempt from the TFN quotation rules provided the investment body is required to withhold tax from the relevant interest or dividend payments (ITAA36 s 202EE(1)).
