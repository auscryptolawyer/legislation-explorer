# Consequences of the debt/equity rules ¶4-880

The imputation system applies to a non-share equity interest in the same way as it applies to a membership interest. It also applies to a non-member equity holder in an entity in the same way as it applies to a member of the entity (ITAA97 s 215-1).

*Refs: ITAA97 s 215-1)*
